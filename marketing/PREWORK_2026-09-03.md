# The pre-work — the Superpower Quiz, wired to the launch

**Wendell, 2026-09-03:** *"What would we want people to have done before they start the class that
will give us the information we need to actually help them… so valuable that even if they don't
make it to the class they will still be left with an impression of wanting to explore more."* And:
*"the superpower quiz… is already built and I believe even takes an email in order to save it."*

**Checked against the code, 2026-09-04. It is built, and your memory is right.** The pre-work is the
**Superpower Quiz** at `/superpower` in bars-engine. This doc was first drafted from the Appendix G
beliefs; the live quiz uses a different, better mechanism, so the sections below are corrected to
match what actually ships. **Nothing here is a new form to build — the diagnostic, the reveal, and
the email capture all exist.**

## What the quiz actually is (from the code)

- **12 forced-choice items plus one orientation question** (`SuperpowerQuiz.tsx`,
  `lib/superpowers/quiz/items`). Not a self-rated belief pick — indirect items it scores for you,
  which is more honest than asking someone to diagnose themselves.
- **Scored deterministically, offline, no AI** (`resolveSuperpowerIntake`), to **one of seven
  superpowers** — connector, storyteller, strategist, disruptor, alchemist, escape_artist, coach —
  plus an **orientation** (internal / external), your **home Face**, and the **Face you ranked
  last** (the "avoided Face").
- **The reveal shows the whole result with no gate** (`SuperpowerReveal.tsx`). You see your
  superpower first. *Then* an optional **email capture**: *"Want this kept? I will send your result
  and the Face you ranked last."* → `captureSuperpowerLead({ email, name, homeFace, avoidedFace })`
  saves a lead tagged by superpower and **emails the result** (`sendSuperpowerResultEmail`).
- **It already links to the character sheet** — *"that is one line of your character sheet; here
  are the other twelve"* → `/mastering-allyship/sheet`. The deeper diagnostic is one click on.

**So the two hard things are done:** the mirror (a real, scored result) and the email capture (save
+ send). Your recollection was correct — "NO email gate" in the code just means the result is
readable first, and the email is the optional save, not a wall.

## What it already gives us to help them

- the **superpower** (one of seven),
- the **orientation** (internal / external),
- the **home Face** and the **avoided Face**,
- **email + name**, saved as a lead tagged by superpower.

That is already most of the read. The character-sheet link is the path to the rest.

## What the launch adds — the delta, and only the delta

**Reconciled against the live quiz. Build only this.**

| for the launch | status | where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Delivery choices** — PDF tonight / pre-order / replay / course preview / Seattle first-word | **not captured today** | **the real work.** Extend the reveal's `EmailCaptureForm` and `captureSuperpowerLead` to carry them (checkboxes → lead tags). This is what closes the Partiful email leak. |
| **The launch links in the result** — the PDF/pre-order destination, a real `/course` route, the Seattle stop | not there | wire into the reveal and into `sendSuperpowerResultEmail` |
| **"One of the original 361 backers?"** | not there | one checkbox → a lead tag |
| **The situation** (free text — the harm they keep fixing) | not there | optional; the richest field, the highest friction — add only if it does not cost completions |

**The one addition that matters is the delivery choices.** Everything else is a nice-to-have. The
quiz already captures the email; the launch needs to capture *what this person wants sent*, because
that choice is what turns a Partiful RSVP into a deliverable.

## How it dovetails with `/course` (already native)

**The quiz and the course share one home.** The reveal already writes to a logged-in player's
`CampaignMembership` and loadout, and already routes toward a campaign (its built-in
"Take this move in The Crossing" CTA). So the course handoff is not a marketing link to bolt on —
it is a route the engine already has. **The launch delta is just pointing that existing handoff at
the launch's course-preview and pre-order, and adding the delivery choices to the lead.**

## Notes before it ships

- **Do not rebuild the quiz, and do not re-author the reveal copy.** The live reveal is the source
  of truth. The Appendix-G belief copy from this doc's first draft does **not** match the seven-
  superpower engine — it was the wrong model and has been removed.
- **The delta is a small extension to `captureSuperpowerLead` and its form**, done in bars-engine:
  add the delivery-choice checkboxes, carry them as lead tags, and thread the launch links into the
  reveal and the result email.
- **Point the Partiful invite and the text blasts at `/superpower`.** That is the pre-work link the
  launch page (`VIRTUAL_LAUNCH_PAGE`) refers to.
- **Confirm one product decision** the code cannot make: the PDF / pre-order destination (the same
  KDP-vs-direct choice `LAUNCH_SEQUENCING` flags) and the `/course` preview route the reveal should
  send people to.

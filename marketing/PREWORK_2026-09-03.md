# The pre-work — *Find Your Allyship Superpower*

**Wendell, 2026-09-03:** *"What would we want people to have done before they start the class that
will give us the information we need to actually help them… so valuable that even if they don't
make it to the class they will still be left with an impression of wanting to explore the work
more."*

**The pre-work is the book's own diagnostic, run once.** It uses the two engines already built —
the **character sheet** (`instruments/character_sheet.html`) and the **belief-to-superpower trace**
(Appendix G). It hands the person a true, generous mirror of themselves and hands us the read we
need to meet them. It is the same trace the course runs in full, so **the pre-work is the front
door to `/course`**, not a separate exercise.

**What makes it worth doing even if they never show:** it tells someone the belief that has been
stopping them is the exact shape of their gift. That is the book's whole emotional payload,
delivered in seven minutes. Nobody forgets being told that.

**What it gives us to actually help them** — the four fields off the character sheet that let
Wendell meet anyone at the event or in the course:

1. the **self-sabotaging belief** they run,
2. the **home face** they reach for,
3. the **channel they skip**,
4. the **harm they keep fixing** — one real situation, in their words.

## The form — *Find Your Allyship Superpower*

**Intro line.** Seven minutes. By the end you will have named the one belief that has been stopping
you — and seen what it becomes when you turn it around. Bring the result to the launch; we will run
it live.

### 1 · The situation *(short text, optional but encouraged)*

Think of one person or group you wanted to show up for, where it did not go the way you hoped. You
do not have to explain it. In a line or two, what was the situation?

> *Why we ask: this is the harm you keep fixing, and it is what we help you with — live at the
> event, or inside the course.*

### 2 · The belief *(single select — the core of the trace)*

When you hesitate to step in for someone, which voice is closest to the one in your head?

- I'm not ready.
- I'm not capable.
- I'm not worthy.
- I'm not good enough.
- I'm insignificant.
- I don't belong.

### 3 · The move that is most you *(single select — your home face)*

When you *do* show up, which of these is most you?

- I read what a group is feeling and tend to what is underneath. **(Shaman)**
- I say the hard truth nobody else will. **(Challenger)**
- I carry what was handed to me and try to pass it on well. **(Regent)**
- I fix the system so the problem stops happening to the next person. **(Architect)**
- I make sure the person at the edge is actually included. **(Diplomat)**
- I see the whole board and where it is heading. **(Sage)**

### 4 · The feeling you skip *(single select — the channel)*

Which feeling do you tend to *manage* instead of *feel*?

- Fear
- Sadness
- Anger
- Joy
- Numbness — I go flat instead

### 5 · Deliver the result

**Email** *(required — where your result and what you choose below get sent)*

**Send me:** *(checkboxes)*

- The book PDF, tonight
- A pre-order link for the printed edition
- The replay
- A preview of the course
- First word on the Seattle in-person launch

**Are you one of the original 361 backers?** *(checkbox)*

## The result screen — *your superpower, named*

**Generated from the belief in Q2. Every version follows one shape:** name the belief, turn it, and
point at the course. The superpower lines are the book's own, from Appendix G.

**If they chose *"I'm not ready":***
> Your belief built the most prepared person in any situation. Aimed outward, that means you can
> hold space for others to be unprepared — you move before others feel ready, while they are still
> gathering courage. **That is your superpower.** The course is thirty days of turning *not ready*
> into that, until it is usable on purpose.

**If they chose *"I'm not capable":***
> Your belief built someone who can work in the mess. Aimed outward, you ally in the
> under-resourced, too-early, nobody-knows-what-is-needed situations that others abandon — and you
> figure it out from the inside. **That is your superpower.**

**If they chose *"I'm not worthy":***
> Your belief built someone who can help without needing to be seen doing it. Aimed outward, your
> allyship is precise: it delivers what is actually needed, not what looks most helpful from the
> outside, and it does not require credit. **That is your superpower.**

**If they chose *"I'm not good enough":***
> Your belief built the person who asks *but did it actually help?* — and insists on the answer.
> Aimed outward, you catch harm before it happens and refuse the performative version. Your
> standard protects the people the work is supposed to serve. **That is your superpower.**

**If they chose *"I'm insignificant":***
> Your belief built someone who notices who has been erased before anyone else names it. Aimed
> outward, you bring in the need no one else names, the person at the edge who stopped expecting to be
> included. **That is your superpower.**

**If they chose *"I don't belong":***
> Your belief built someone who reads the relational temperature before it is spoken. Aimed
> outward, you move *before* exclusion consolidates — you see the person about to be edged out and
> step in while there is still time. **That is your superpower.**

**The shared close, under every result:**
> This is the trace at the center of *Mastering the Game of Allyship* — your belief, turned into
> the exact way you were built to show up. At the launch we run it live on the situation you
> named. The course is where you run it in full, thirty days, until the superpower is yours on
> purpose. ‹course preview link›

## How it dovetails with `/course` (bars-engine)

**The pre-work is Chapter 2's trace, shortened.** Appendix G: *"return here before Chapter 2,
choose your next belief. The Chapter 2 work runs the same trace; only the belief changes."* The
belief they pick in Q2 **becomes their opening course campaign** — the course runs the full
metabolization on it. So the handoff is seamless: the pre-work names the superpower, the course
makes it usable.

**Data flows one direction, into helping them.** The four fields (belief, face, skipped channel,
situation) are the start of their character sheet. Carried into the course, they mean a returning
participant is not a stranger — the course already knows the belief they are working and the
feeling they avoid. **This is the read the course needs to personalize, captured before day one.**

## Notes before it ships

- **Build it in whatever runs the logic** — a Typeform or a route in bars-engine can branch the
  result off Q2; a Google Form can send all six results and tell them which to read. Bars-engine is
  the better home, because the result already wants to hand off to the course.
- **The result is the product.** Do not bury it behind a *thanks for submitting*. Show the
  superpower on screen the second they finish, then email it.
- **Keep Q1 optional.** The situation is the richest field for us and the highest-friction for
  them; encouraged, never required, so it does not cost completions.

## 1. THE ARCHITECTURE

One hub. Multiple doors. Each door speaks one language to one person and sends them somewhere specific.

**The financial hub:** Ko-fi page. Everything either points here or to a product sales page. Nothing points nowhere.

**The lead generation engine:** Chapter 1 of the book. Free. The best argument that the rest of the book is worth having. Every channel that isn't asking for money is offering Chapter 1.

**The connective tissue:** The Ideal Reader Matrix. Every door is pre-tuned to a specific reader before they arrive. Without the matrix, copy is generic. With it, each channel is speaking directly to one person.

**The product layer:** Four products, four sales pages, all discoverable independently. Ko-fi tiers are the fundraiser entry point. Sales pages are the evergreen entry point.

# Audience research — studying who we want to reach

**Wendell, 2026-09-03:** *"each dream 100 has 2 goals. Outreach list and marketing research
(studying audiences we'd also like to engage with). I think we can do the latter as research and
I'll keep populating the former with people I actually know."*

**So the Dream 100 splits.** The **outreach list** (`DREAM_100`) is people Wendell knows and can
DM — his to fill. **This file is the other half: the research** — the aligned public figures whose
audiences would vibe with the work, what those audiences care about, and where MTGOA sits among
them. These are *candidates to vet*, not asserted allies, and the audience reads are qualitative:
the searches gave positioning, not follower counts, so nothing here claims a number it cannot back.

---

## The headline: MTGOA's audience already has a home

**Two findings reframe the whole launch.**

**1 · The book's real neighborhood is somatics-meets-social-justice, and it is a crowded, warm
one.** MTGOA's core move — reading fear and anger in the body and using that to show up for other
people — is not a novel niche. It is the exact territory of **Resmaa Menakem** (*Somatic
Abolitionism*, *My Grandmother's Hands*) and **Prentis Hemphill** (The Embodiment Institute,
*What It Takes To Heal*). Their audiences already believe the premise MTGOA has to argue from
scratch elsewhere: that the body is where this work happens. **This is the core audience, and the
positioning writes itself — MTGOA adds a six-role game layer on top of a somatic layer that
audience already lives in.**

**2 · Layla Saad already ran MTGOA's exact funnel, to a NYT bestseller.** *Me and White Supremacy*
began as a **28-day Instagram challenge**, became a **free PDF workbook**, then a bestseller. That
is the 30-day-challenge → PDF → book template MTGOA has been building, **validated by a book one
lane over.** Her funnel is worth studying move by move before the launch.

## The lanes, researched

### Somatics / embodiment / racial healing — *the core overlap*

The nearest neighbors, and the warmest audience.

- **Resmaa Menakem** — the leading voice on racialized trauma; coined *Somatic Abolitionism*;
  audience is practitioners of embodied anti-racist work. **Overlap:** his nervous-system-and-race
  material is MTGOA's fear/anger channels aimed at allyship. **Position MTGOA as the game layer on
  his somatic layer**, not a competitor to it.
- **Prentis Hemphill** — therapist and somatic facilitator, founder of The Embodiment Institute,
  host of the *Finding Our Way* podcast. **Audience:** embodiment plus social change. A guest
  candidate *and* a podcast cross-promo target (a *Finding Our Way* swap reaches exactly the crowd).

### Allyship / DEI — *the message audience, and the playbook*

- **Layla Saad** — *Me and White Supremacy*, the *Good Ancestor* podcast. **Study the funnel**
  (challenge → PDF → book) as the proven version of MTGOA's own. Her audience is the direct
  message-match: people who want the practice, not the lecture.
- **Rachel Cargle** — writer and lecturer, *The Great Unlearn*; a large Instagram platform built on
  critical unlearning around race and womanhood. **Audience:** self-directed learners already
  paying to do the work.

### Integral / sensemaking — *the opening arc's wider world*

The audience Ike, Marin and Regis come from, one ring out.

- **The Daily Evolver** (Jeff Salzman, with Ankur Delight) — the integral-community hub, Institute
  for Cultural Evolution, a monthly Developmental Alliance Zoom and a YouTube following. **The most
  directly-aligned integral audience**, and the lineage the opening arc already speaks to.
- **John Vervaeke** (*the meaning crisis*, applied metatheory) and **Daniel Schmachtenberger** (the
  metacrisis, civilization design) — larger, adjacent sensemaking audiences. **Overlap:** MTGOA is
  integral theory made practical and personal, which is the gap these thinkers name and rarely
  fill. Finale-tier reach for this lane, not opening-arc.

## What the research changes

**The unique position, triangulated.** MTGOA sits where three audiences overlap and few authors
stand: **embodiment** (Resmaa, Prentis) + **integral/developmental** (Salzman, Vervaeke) +
**allyship-as-practice** (Saad, Cargle). **Nobody else is clearly at all three corners.** That
intersection is the one-line pitch the launch has been missing: *the game layer on the somatic
work, built on an integral frame, aimed at actually showing up.*

**The funnel is validated.** Layla Saad's challenge → PDF → book proves the 30-day-challenge
repurpose is a real path, not a hope. Model the launch's PDF-and-pre-order ask on what worked
there.

**The clip strategy follows the audience.** These crowds respond to *embodied practice plus
framework clarity* — a concrete body-level move plus the six-role map that explains it. That is
the shape the podcast clips should take.

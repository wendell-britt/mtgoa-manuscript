### Body

Okay party people. It's finally time.

The book is here, and before it goes fully out into the world I want to gather everyone who has
been watching this come together — the people who backed it, the people who have been waiting on
the sidelines, the smartypants friends who need to kick the tires before they believe it — and
show you what six years of work actually produced.

Think of it as my thesis defense. Except the orals board is you, and I want you loaded with
questions.

I'll walk you through the book, the Allyship Deck, the RPG, and the app. I'll tell you the whole
story of how we got here — the course that under ten percent of people finished, and what I built
out of that failure. Then I open it up: anything you want to know about allyship, about the
framework, about what comes next. Bring the hard questions. That is what this is for.

If you have a friend who has been stuck in the guilt and the shame and the *I want to help but I
don't know how* — bring them. This is exactly who the night is for.

### What you leave with

- **The PDF of the book, that night.** You do not have to wait for the print run to start reading.
- **A pre-order of the printed edition**, so your copy ships off the first run.
- **The replay**, in case life happens and you can't be there live.
- **A standing invitation to the Seattle launch** — the first in-person stop, coming in November.

### What it costs

**Free to attend, donation-based.** No ticket, no barrier. If the work has meant something to you
and you want to put something behind it, your contribution funds the print run for the 361 people
who backed this in 2023, the podcast, and Campaign Zero scholarships. Give what feels right, or
give nothing and just come. ‹link: what your contribution funds — bars-engine page›

### After they register — the confirmation

You're in. Watch your inbox for the Zoom link and the replay afterward. If you want a head start,
your PDF and pre-order link are on their way. Bring someone who needs this.

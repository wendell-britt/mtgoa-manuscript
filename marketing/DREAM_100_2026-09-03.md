# The Dream 100 — who goes on the podcast, and in what order

**Wendell, 2026-09-03:** *"I do think we should have a dream 100 for the podcast guests. People I
can reach out to on instagram and twitter if they are aligned and have audiences that would vibe
with my work."*

**The Dream 100 is a relationship list, not a booking list.** The idea (Brunson) is that your
audience already exists — it is gathered around a hundred other people. You do not buy your way in;
you build real relationships with those hundred, and their audiences meet you through them. For the
podcast, each name is someone whose crowd would vibe with the work and who might trade audiences by
sitting for a conversation.

**This file is the scaffold and the confirmed names. The rest is yours to fill** — from the people
you already follow and admire on IG and Twitter, because *aligned* is a judgment only you can make.
I have seeded the four that are set and described each lane so the blanks are easy to fill.

## The test every name passes

From `MARKETING_STRATEGY §10`, three questions plus the one Wendell added:

1. **Aligned** — the work and the values actually fit. Your call, nobody else's.
2. **Has an audience that would vibe** — a real following that overlaps yours.
3. **Serves a level / lane** — they bring a specific angle, so the feed reads as a body of work.
4. **Generates content** — the conversation clips well for every channel.

## The outreach method — for IG and Twitter

**Relationship first, ask second. Never a cold pitch.**

1. **Follow and engage for two to three weeks.** Substantive comments, real shares of their work,
   no agenda visible. You become a familiar name in their mentions.
2. **The warm DM.** Reference something specific they made, say what your conversation would open
   that theirs have not, and make it an invitation to a conversation rather than a booking request.
3. **Give before you take.** Offer the clip, the audience, the cross-post. A Dream 100 relationship
   is worth more than one episode.

**Pipeline stages** — the status column below uses these: `Identified` → `Warming` (engaging on
socials) → `Invited` → `Booked` → `Recorded` → `Live`.

## How to work it

- **Aim for ten per lane, but do not wait for a hundred to start.** Four are set; the opening arc
  can record while the list fills.
- **Warm ten at a time**, not a hundred. The engaging is the work, and it does not scale past a
  handful at once.
- **Book against the arc:** integral first, the broad names later, AJ last.
- **Review the list monthly** — the review date on this file is the trigger.

## 10. FUNDRAISER STORY ARC — THE CROSSING

> Source: bars-engine Obsidian library (THE_CROSSING_CAR_FUNDRAISER_QUEST.md + INBOX_2026-04-30_STORY-ARC-THE-CROSSING.md). Canonical. All fundraiser copy must honor this frame.

### The Frame
This is not a charity ask. Not a rescue story. It is the story of **learning to ask real** — inviting people to accompany the finish of something already built, not asking for help to start something new.

### The Pattern (What the Audience Is Actually Watching)
1. Build something in crisis → underbaked → can't steward it → drops
2. Something breaks → repeat
3. **The break:** Feb–April 2026, hermit mode. Stopped starting new things. Finished what already existed — the book, the deck, the nonprofit design.
4. **The interrupt:** April 22, car explodes. Breaks hermit mode before completion.
5. **The ask:** Help me finish it. Not build it. Finish it.

### The Timeline (For Copy Use)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Feb/March 2025 | First birthday ask — CYOA built in crisis, underbaked, dropped |
| April 2025 | Shoe store job |
| Feb 16, 2026 | Fired by text — birthday season again |
| Feb–April 2026 | Hermit mode — finishing the book, deck, nonprofit design |
| April 22, 2026 | Car engine blows. Hermit mode broken. |
| June 2026 | Third ask. Different kind. |

### The Evolution of the Ask
| Ask | Energy |
|---|---|
| First (2025) | Rescue + start something new |
| Second (Feb 2026) | Help me build |
| **Third (now)** | **Here's what I've been building. Come with me while I finish it.** |

### What the Story IS NOT
- Not charity. Not rescue. Not "I haven't figured it out yet."

### What the Story IS
- Asking for accompaniment, not rescue
- Finishing instead of starting
- The ICA watching someone practice real asking — and recognizing themselves in it

### The Epiphany (Wendell's)
The fallapart was necessary. The stability was the cage. The pattern keeps breaking because it needs to.

### The Epiphany (For the Reader)
I thought I was watching someone ask for rescue. The story I'm actually in is someone learning to stop starting things in crisis — and offering what they've made instead of asking for help to make it.

### The CTA Energy
Not "give me money for the car." Not "help me build."
→ *"Here's what I've been building. Here's the game. Playing the game is how you get better at helping. Come watch me try to finish it."*

### The Car as Symbol
The 2018 Kia Sportage carried Wendell through 8 years of the work — Portland and back, every chapter. He paid it off himself. First time. It was the literal vehicle of the chapter that produced MTGOA. Its death closes that chapter. Getting back on the road opens the next one. The fundraiser isn't just about a car — it's about what comes next.

### Pathos Asset
Wendell has photos of the blown engine. Use them. This is not metaphor — it happened.

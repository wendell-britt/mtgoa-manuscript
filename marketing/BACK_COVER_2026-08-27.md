# Back cover copy

**160 words.** A 6×9 back cover carrying a barcode holds roughly 150–200 before it stops being
readable at arm's length in a shop, so this is written to the ceiling rather than trimmed to it.

---

You started this work for a reason. It's just gotten hard to hear that reason over all the noise
of doing the work.

You have read the books that explain what is wrong. Every one of them points at the problem. Not
one of them hands you a move.

This is a field guide. It argues that allyship is a game most well-meaning people are losing
without knowing the rules, and then it teaches the rules: six roles a group needs, five channels
the feeling runs through, and thirty moves you can practice, each with a test for whether it
worked.

Burnout here is a design flaw that looks exactly like a character flaw. You can change a design.

Wendell Britt built a course on this material in 2020. Under ten percent of the people who started
it finished. This book is what he rebuilt from that.

Not for you if you are looking to be told to care more.

---

## What it is doing, paragraph by paragraph

**The opening two sentences are Wendell's, verbatim, from his own Gumroad draft.**
`ANALYSIS_SALES_PAGE_2026-08-13.md` §2 measured them as almost word-for-word the Green reader's
**Crack** as `IDEAL_READER_MATRIX` defines it, and its verdict was *"Do not touch it."* They are
not touched.

**A back cover confirms rather than convinces.** Whoever reads this is already holding the book,
so the job is to tell them quickly whether it is theirs. That is why the disqualifier is the last
line and not a softer one.

**The lineup paragraph is the whole competitive claim** and it names no competitor. *Every one of
them points at the problem* concedes the genre its real achievement before ranking against it,
which is the difference between a ranking and a sneer.

**The specifics are the offer.** Six roles, five channels, thirty moves, and a test per move. The
sales-page analysis found the single weakest thing on the Gumroad page was an offer section
reading *"The full digital book, instantly"* — a back cover has the same decision point and the
same obligation to say what is actually inside.

**The failure story is the credential.** For this reader an author admitting a sub-ten-percent
completion rate outranks any endorsement, because `IDEAL_READER_MATRIX` says what loses her is
*"pure armor"* and *"anything that sounds like a pitch."*

**The disqualifier closes it.** *"Not for you if you are looking to be told to care more"* is from
Wendell's draft and is on-strategy rather than merely brave — a page that turns somebody away
reads as neither a funnel nor armour.

## The review pass

**Step 0 · ELI5, written first.** *You keep trying to help and it keeps costing you. That isn't
because you're bad at caring — nobody taught you the moves. Here are six ways of helping and
thirty things to actually do.* The register version adds the genre claim, the specifics and the
credential, and nothing else. Every word past the ELI5 was checked against it.

**Step 1 · gate** clean. **Step 0b · voice** clean.

**Step 2 · diet.** `passive` **0.00** · `zombie` 0.00 · `expletive` 0.00 · `empty` 0.00 ·
`be` 0.99. Two counters read heavy and both are held deliberately:

| counter | | |
|---|---|---|
| `copula` **1.32** | *This is a field guide* · *Burnout here is a design flaw* | Both definitional. A back cover has to say what the object is, and at 160 words two copulas swing the ratio past a ceiling calibrated on chapter-length prose |
| `waste` **1.55** | four pronouns | Every one has a clear antecedent, and `waste` has a **floor** as well as a ceiling — the skill's own note is that a passage which never says *it* has stopped pointing at things |

**One real defect was found and fixed.** The draft read *"A design can be changed"* — `passive`
2.02, and the doer hidden was the reader. Now *"You can change a design."* **The fix is right on
the hero-and-guide constraint before it is right on the counter**, which is the only reason to
make it.

**Step 3 · slop, by hand, against `eval.md` check 1.** No invented claims, examples or statistics.
Six roles, five channels and thirty moves are canon; the test per move is the `*You're winning
when:*` frame; the completion rate and the 2020 course are Chapter 9. *"Not one of them hands you
a move"* is a **ranking**, not a denial — the negated thing stays true at the sentence's end.

**Step 3.5 · stance.** Person — no first-person plural, address holds on *you*. Doer — no
get-passives. Back-pointer — one opener, *"This is a field guide"*, kept because a back cover is
the one surface where *this* resolves to the physical object in the reader's hand. Membrane — no
fiction present. Borrowed move — none performed unnamed.

**Step 7 · empty head** — 0 hard, 0 clause, 3 soft: *the work* twice inside Wendell's own untouched
sentence, and *a move*, which is canon vocabulary rather than a placeholder.

## One thing to settle before this goes to print

**The subtitle exists in three versions**, open since `HANDOFF_ANNOUNCE_2026-08-10.md` §5:

| surface | says |
|---|---|
| the cover | *A Field Guide* |
| `front_matter/title_page.md:3` | *How to Build an Allyship Practice That Lasts* |
| the site metadata | neither |

**This copy leans on *field guide*, because that is what is printed on the front** and a back
cover that disagrees with its own front reads as two books. **A retailer listing takes exactly
one, so the three have to become one.**

**Also still open from that same section:** the cover's tagline, *Stuckness is data, not failure*,
**appears nowhere in the manuscript.** This copy does not use it. A reader who buys on that line
will go looking for it.

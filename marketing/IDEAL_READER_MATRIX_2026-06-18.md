## How to Use This

Each row is one reader. Each reader is a face of the Game Master. The job of every piece of copy is to speak directly to one row before that reader arrives at the Ko-fi page.

The "Crack" column is the most important. It's where Wendell's story and the reader's story touch. Without the crack, there's no bridge. Without the bridge, there's no conversion.

The "ICA Source" column tracks which FriendCraft profile populates the row. As profiles are completed, pull from them to deepen the texture here.

# Podcast invites — four conversations, in order

**These are warm invitations, not pitches.** Per `MARKETING_STRATEGY §10`: *personalized outreach —
not asks, invitations to a conversation. These are not interviews. They are the conversations that
have been waiting to happen.* Send each however you and that person actually talk — text, DM,
email. The register is you-to-a-friend, not author-to-booking.

**The order:** Ike (Ep 1), Marin (Ep 2), Regis (Ep 3) open the integral arc; **AJ Cohen is the
finale, and her invite goes first** because a centerpiece guest needs the longest lead.

**Two of these I could write; two I could only frame.** Ike and AJ have a specific conversation on
record in the strategy. For Marin and Regis I left the guest-specific hook as a `‹fill›` — the
thread you two have been circling is yours to name, and inventing it would make the invite ring
false.

---

## 1 · Ike — Episode 1 *(the integral arc opens)*

**Ike — I'm finally starting the MTGOA podcast, and the first conversation I want on it is the one
you and I have been circling for years.**

You brought integral to Blue Sky, you have been in the lineage longer than I have, and you are the
one person who can say out loud what I can't say about my own book without sounding defensive:
what makes it stand on its own rather than Wilber with the serial numbers filed off. I want that
conversation on the record — honor the lineage, and name exactly where MTGOA departs from it.

It is the opening episode on purpose. I'm starting with the people who already get it before I go
out to the people who don't, and you are at the top of that list.

Loose and long — Zoom, or in person if you're up for it, about an hour. You get the clips to use
however you want. No prep; it's a conversation, not an interview.

You in? I'd love to record in the next couple of weeks.

## 4 · AJ Cohen — the season finale *(send this one first — longest lead)*

**AJ — I'm starting the MTGOA podcast, and I want the biggest conversation on it to be with you.**

Everyone in this space critiques power from the outside. You have actually been inside it — the
industry, the tables where it gets decided — and you know what it takes to show up for people when
you have something real to lose. That is the conversation the book earns and almost nobody can
have: allyship from inside a power structure, not as an outsider throwing rocks.

I'm building the first season toward it. The early episodes are with my integral people; I want
yours to be the one that lands the whole arc. Which is exactly why I'm reaching out now — I know
the calendar is the hard part, and I would rather hold the right slot for you than rush it.

However's easiest for you: Zoom or in person, an hour or so, and you get the clips for your own
channels. It's a conversation, not an interview — no prep, just the talk we've been meaning to
have.

Can we find a time that works in the next month or two?

## Before you hit send

- **Fill Marin's and Regis's hooks.** The `‹fill›` lines are the whole point of a warm invite.
- **Personalize the sign-off** to how you each actually talk. These are drafts of the substance,
  not the salutations.
- **Attach nothing.** These are friends; a one-sheet on a friend invite reads as a pitch. Save the
  attachment for the field and speaking sends.
- **Send AJ's today.** The finale needs the longest runway, and the opening-arc three can follow
  this week.

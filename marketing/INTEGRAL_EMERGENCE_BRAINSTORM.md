## What We Know

- Two teams, one goal: create the strategic plan for the MTGOA nonprofit
- It's a brainstorming session in the form of a game
- Quests are directed toward the nonprofit design challenge, not open-ended
- A deck of cards that people draw from and send to each other
- Sign up ahead of time — character creation happens before arrival
- Small group. Daytime. Intentional.

## What Needs To Be Designed

### The Mission Frame
Both teams are working toward the same artifact: a strategic plan for the MTGOA nonprofit.
The competition is not about winning against each other — it's about what kind of plan emerges from the tension between two approaches, two value systems, two teams who want to get there differently.

**Open question:** What are the two teams? Two archetypes? Two developmental levels? Two orientations toward the nonprofit mission? This needs a decision before character creation can be designed.

### The Quest Architecture
Quests need to be directed toward nonprofit strategy, not just fun/connection.
Draft quest types:
- Resource quests — find someone in the room who has X skill/connection/knowledge the nonprofit needs
- Vision quests — get another player to articulate what they believe the nonprofit's first year should look like
- Defection quests — make a case for your team's strategic approach compelling enough that someone switches
- Salad Bowl quests — wild cards that introduce constraints or provocations into the planning process

**Open question:** How do completed quests translate into actual strategic content? Is there a scribe? A board? A shared document that gets built in real time?

### Character Creation (Pre-Arrival)
Players sign up ahead of time and complete character creation before they arrive.
Character creation probably includes:
- Which of the six Game Master archetypes they're playing from
- Their stated superpower (what they're bringing to the nonprofit design)
- Their stated shadow (what they know they need to watch out for)
- Their team assignment (or discovered on arrival?)

**Open question:** Is team assignment pre-determined or revealed at the event?

### The Digital Version Dream
Wendell wants this game playable digitally — not just in-room.
This is an app feature, not a July 20 deliverable.
File it: the quest architecture above is the prototype for the bars-engine campaign mechanic.
The July 20 game generates the design spec for the digital version.

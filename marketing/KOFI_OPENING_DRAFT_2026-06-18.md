To those who have been quietly waiting in the audience for Wendell to return to the stage,

The last few months have been quite a lot. I've tried to write this a few times and I couldn't be bothered. Not because y'all didn't deserve it but because I really wanted to bring something good to the stage for y'all.

In February I got fired from a job I loved two days before my birthday. It was something that was distracting me from the finish line, like all the jobs I've taken since starting this journey. And so I had time and energy to finally finish the book. And as is always the case when I reach a major milestone on this book, car trouble wasn't far behind. April 22nd the engine of my car exploded. This happened literally the day after I finished the manuscript. I am so tired of this wretched pattern. Three times. Always at the threshold. Van stolen in 2022. Same car stolen and damaged in 2023. Engine explodes the day after the manuscript is done in 2026. This book is my vehicle to the life I want to live. And it seems to be a jealous vehicle.

This time I stayed put. I didn't want the devastation of losing my primary means of transportation to set me back like it did before. I kept pushing. And here is what I built while nobody was watching:

A digital deck of allyship prompts — ready to ship, playable today, designed to help people show up as effective allies even before the full book arrives. A completed manuscript. A digital RPG companion edition of the book, fully laid out, early access ready. And an app — the thing I wish I'd had when I was wrangling artists at Blue Sky, trying to help build a dream studio before the Disney overlords handed us the unceremonious boot — a place where people can capture inspiration and turn it into aligned action toward actually helping people.

These aren't separate things. They're the same system at different depths.

I've been waiting to come to y'all with good news. I kept thinking if I just finished the thing I'd have earned the right to ask. I know how that sounds. It's actually what the book is about.

Over 400 people backed this on Kickstarter. They were promised delivery three years ago. That's not a footnote — that's the reason August 1 is not a soft goal. It's the date I get to keep my word.

To do a book tour I need a car. So I'm here asking for help raising money to get one. I'm also opening pre-sales for the book. And I have some additional things I think are pretty exciting. I wish I could've announced these without the added flavor of personal tragedy but that doesn't seem to be the kind of story I'm the protagonist of.

### The AJ Letter
Hey AJ. It's been a while. If I'm being 100% I've been wanting to reach out but I've been terrified. Life has been not as great as I'd like and I keep holding onto this insane dream that I'll figure everything out on my own and be able to come to you with good news. Even as I write this I hear the version of you that lives in my head roll their eyes as they are laughing at me for putting that weird pressure on myself you've never asked me to hold. And hold it I did because I've wanted to do right by the tremendous gift of presence and opportunity you gave me all those years and that somehow I'd be able to "pay you back" in personal success. Well I've become aware of that enough that I'm writing this letter at the behest of the robots who kept asking me what kind of person do you want to read this book.

I'm in the actual final stages of writing the book and as is always the case when I make a significant step toward finishing it car troubles have beset me. When I finished the Manuscript in April my car exploded and I am hosting a fundraiser to help me to get a new car. That's the REASON I'm reaching out but its not the purpose. I'm writing because I owe you a response and an apology for thinking that if I was "good enough" I could somehow be more deserving of connection. Hilariously this is something I talk about at length in the book and have yet to put into personal practice. I'd love to catch up when you've got the time. In the meantime feel free to check out what I've been working on. It's not perfect just yet, but I am proud of what I've been able to pull off. I've got a digital deck of allyship prompts that can help people show up as effective allies in a pinch even if they haven't read the book. I'm also developing a digital notetaking app that's a companion to the book that will allow people to capture inspiration and turn it into aligned action towards actually helping people. It's the thing that I wish I'd had when I was at blue sky wrangling all the artists and trying to help build a dream studio that we were trying to make before getting the unceremonial boot by the disney overlords.

I very much hope you're well and very much interested in hearing how your journey has been in the last few years.

### AJ Character Notes
There was this weird guy that I took on as my assistant during my final days at blue sky. he had a magical way of seeing the world and connecting with people. I took a chance on him because I liked his vibe and Maggie gave him glowing recommendation. He took joy in talking to people and learning their stories and how he could help them. He was so neurotic though. He was terrified of doing a bad job and he'd take so many things personally. He was so very green and didn't really know what it was like to work in a studio. In a lot of ways this is what made him so good at his job. He was also willing to fight and push back. It's not something I was used to. Though he was terrified he would hold to his conviction and his care of others even as it was obvious he wanted to run the other way. he has BIG HUMANITY. He knew there was something called power, but he believed he was able to contend to it even though he was essentially born yesterday as far as the industry goes. But he did give me hope. there are many ways in which I was stuck in my own thinking and he opened my eyes. Though I rarely wanted to give him the satisfaction. I'm a little too old to be having this youth telling me what's up.

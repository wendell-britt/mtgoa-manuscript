# About the Author

**Wendell Britt** spent fifteen years in customer service at Apple, Airbnb and T-Mobile, which is where he learned allyship. Before that he ran alumni relations and employee engagement at an animation company. For most of his life he has been the meddling kid trying to fix institutions that never asked him to, which he now does on purpose and with permission.

He left to integrate the two things he knows best, roleplaying games and personal development. This book is that. He also wrote *Igniting Joy*, on building a healthier and more fun relationship with anger, whose practices appear in Appendix D.

His friends come to him for a light shone into whatever they have been refusing to look at, for genre-savvy takes on the state of the world, for rad parties, and to run their roleplaying games. Chapter 1 tells you that you are a Game Master. He has been one since long before he wrote it down.

He coaches one-on-one, and he speaks to companies and organizations that would like the game named out loud.

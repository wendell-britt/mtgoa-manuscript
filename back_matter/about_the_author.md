# About the Author

**Wendell Britt** builds practices for people who want to be useful to each other
and keep finding that the work costs more than it should. He is the author of
*Igniting Joy*, whose emotional-body practices appear in Appendix D, and the
builder of bars-engine, the app this book routes to. He coaches the six Faces
one-on-one and certifies others to run them.

The Chapter Engine

Every Face chapter runs one machine: a Face, working its Daemon through a Polarity, across the four Domains (with the parable going deep on one), to dismantle one Allyship Myth — and it spotlights one of the seven Superpowers in the world where that power is most vivid.

Note: Superpowers are a separate axis from the six faces — the reader keeps her own across every world. Each chapter spotlights one to teach it; it is not "the face's superpower."

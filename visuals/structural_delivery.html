The order, and what it costs

First — pays for itselfClose the term debt by cutting, not adding: define altitude at ch3:167, resolve the colour ladder at ch8:161, resolve the Vulnerable Child at ch8:169. Net −60 to −120 words.
Second — cheapest yieldThe character-sheet thread. Seven insertions, +250 words, closes the book's one broken promise to itself.
Third — quality parityCh8's two bare moves (+240), Ch7's missing performable block (+220). Ch5's two bare moves (+330) are already queued in the Ch5 rebuild.
Fourth — the actual gapThe transfer drill. Six situations, or one consolidated set in Ch9 where all six faces are available to choose between. +900 words.

Roughly +1,500 words net, against a cut mandate. Only the first item pays for itself. Everything after it is a trade, and the trade has to be made against the line-level compression already scheduled for the last four days.

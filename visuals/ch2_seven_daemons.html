The Seven Daemons

The cast the reader meets walking into the Forest — seven daemons around the Player, who holds the joystick.

Concept only — brand style not yet locked. Each daemon is half-lit: its ally form in the light (upper band), its demon form in the shadow (lower band). Same part throughout; what changes is who holds the joystick. Grayscale-safe: ally/demon read by band + label, not color.

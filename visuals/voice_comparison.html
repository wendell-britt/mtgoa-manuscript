Twelve sites: 182 words in, 161 words out. Net −21. Mean −1.75 per site.

The register repair is a voice move that returns some words. The detector finds 464 adjacent pairs of this shape across the nine chapters, of which 67 are true restatements. At −1.75 per site the whole pool returns about 810 words. A wider pass reaching past the detector into the 1,961 sentences of six words or fewer might reach 1,500 to 2,000. That is below the 1,600–2,600 band I quoted earlier, and the reason to run the pass is what it does to the register rather than what it does to the count.

How the fourth column was built

The 121-page PDF was re-extracted from source. Reading order was rebuilt line by line, so the bold runs sit back inside the sentences they belong to instead of falling below the paragraph. Three pages set in two columns are ordered by column rather than by height, which repairs the openers of chapters 4, 5 and 7, where two columns of body text had been interleaved a line at a time. The Type3 font ships a broken character map that renders fi as y and fl as z, which is where conzict and difycult came from; a frequency-gated repair fixed 328 instances across 71 token types, and the words it left alone are your own coinages, an email address, and Genpo Merzel's surname.

That yields 24,735 words of correctly ordered manuscript. The measured sample strips headings, bullets, the per-chapter BARs-challenge apparatus and the 52-card appendix, which leaves 20,077 words of running prose — four times the text every number in the previous version of this page rested on. Measuring the narrative chapters alone, with the eight-tool chapter set aside, moves the mean to 19.2 and the six-words-or-fewer share to 4.0%, so the toolkit is holding the average down rather than propping it up.

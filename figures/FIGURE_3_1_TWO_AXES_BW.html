Two axes, and a word that sits on both

Operation 1 — Transmute

one row · dissatisfied ──▶ satisfied

Stay in the same channel and mature its quality. Frustration to triumph. Grief to poignance. Apathy to peace.

It has a channel, because a row is a channel.

Operation 2 — Translate

row to row · metal ──▶ fire

Move the energy into a different channel. Fear becomes anger when preparation becomes action. Anger becomes peace once the boundary holds.

It names two channels, because it crosses rows.

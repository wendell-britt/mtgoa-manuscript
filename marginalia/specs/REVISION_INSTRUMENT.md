# 06 — The Revision Instrument

**Built:** 2026-07-28. Purpose: turn the synthesis into something that produces prose rather
than verdicts.

The detector ("who's in this paragraph?") stays — it is cheap and it finds dead paragraphs
fast. But it diagnoses one failure and generates nothing. This is the generative half.

---

## Part 1 — The palette

Fifteen moves, sorted by what they actually are. **Only the second group is paint.**

### Constraints — always on, never chosen

These govern every revision and are not stylistic options.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hero / guide** | reader is the hero, author is the guide who already walked it. Never narrate her interior. (A0 + Snicket + Miller, three independent sources) |
| **Ranking not denying** | a negation is legal only if the negated thing is still true when the sentence ends |
| **Mechanism visible** | simple language for the promise, exact language for the mechanism |
| **No Orange substrate** | no urgency, scarcity, achievement, or attention-anxiety as the engine |
| **Beat placement** | fragments carry beats, never claims, and only in landing position |

### The colors — chosen, one per revision, committed to fully

| # | Color | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Testimony** | author's own specific instance, first person, dated and concrete | Ch8 opener; Ch1:236 |
| 2 | **Clown** | self as the butt, own reaction exaggerated to absurdity | *Igniting Joy* |
| 3 | **Jerk** | a named behavior inside a scene, teased — never a class of person | *Igniting Joy*; Adams' "You're a jerk, Dent" |
| 4 | **Cult Leader** | satire at the system, distance held, nobody personally blamed | *Igniting Joy*; Adams' Vogons |
| 5 | **Parable** | a person, a choice, a cost, ends on the image, never glossed | Ch1:240 poker cards; Snicket's Devil's Tongue |
| 6 | **Deadpan** | absurd content in a flat, sober, reportorial register | Adams |
| 7 | **Conceit** | one word run 4-6× in a sentence, or a rule held for a paragraph | Adams' "alien"; Snicket's "stop" |
| 8 | **Reversed order** | name the effect as an established routine, reveal the cause second | Adams' opening line |
| 9 | **Bathos** | build ceremony, then drop register hard | Adams' silver ship |
| 10 | **Symmetry** | two parties, identical logic, opposite verdicts, no side taken | Adams' dolphins |
| 11 | **Reassignment** | her significant private suffering reclassified as standard issue | Adams' "perfectly normal paranoia" |
| 12 | **Unreconciled** | true cause and false feeling in one sentence, left unresolved | Snicket's Violet |
| 13 | **Ladder** | stacked standalone declaratives with marked dependency | Naval; = a Gate walk rendered |
| 14 | **Break frame** | confess in the open what the text is doing to her | Ch1:236; Snicket |

**Rule of commitment.** One color per revision, run all the way. Blending two produces the
average, and the average is the dead prose you started with. Blends happen at the *chapter*
level — alternating paragraphs — never inside one.

## Part 2 — Diagnosis, before any rewriting

Six checks. Mechanical, fast, and they select the color rather than just condemning.

1. **Who is in it?** Named person acting, or nobody.
2. **Which color is running?** Name it, or write NONE.
3. **Demonstrate or describe?** Is something happening, or is something being explained?
4. **Abstraction nouns in subject position** — count them.
5. **Sentence lengths** — list them. Flag three consecutive over 25.
6. **Negations** — for each, is the negated thing still true at the end?

**The diagnosis picks the colors.** It is not decoration:

| Diagnosis | Reach for |
|---|---|
| nobody present, concept is about the author's own failure | Testimony, Clown |
| nobody present, concept is about a system or institution | **Cult Leader**, Symmetry |
| present but flat, reader may feel singled out | Reassignment, Clown |
| correct but inert, no music | Conceit, Reversed order, Deadpan |
| a claim she'll resist | Parable, Unreconciled |
| stacked concepts, lecture creep | Ladder |
| she's about to feel accused | Break frame, Clown |

## Part 3 — The pitch

For each paragraph, three revisions. Each one:

- **commits to one named color**, cited to the excerpt it's imitating
- is **full prose**, not a sketch or a description of a fix
- carries a **COST** line — what this choice loses, honestly

Then Wendell picks one, or takes a line from each, and a line-level pass follows.

**Why three and not one.** One revision is a verdict wearing a draft's clothes. Three
different colors on the same content make the choice visible, and the choice is his.

**Why the cost line is mandatory.** Every color spends something. Cult Leader spends warmth.
Testimony spends words and can tip a chapter into memoir. Conceit fixes the music but can
leave the paragraph still empty of people. Without the cost line the pitch is advocacy.

## Part 4 — The dials

The palette picks a color for a *fresh* revision. The dials adjust a draft that already
exists. Called by name, with an intensity: *a touch / more / a lot.*

They split along the locked column: some change sentences, some change thinking.

### Prose dials — applied to a draft, change the sentences

| Call | What it turns up |
|---|---|
| **+SNICKETY** | direct address; narrator with visible limits; inline definition of a word; formal courtesy about dreadful things; warning her away; one conceit held to the end |
| **+ADAMSY** | deadpan against scale; invented specifics delivered flat; ceremony then register drop; subverted comparison; the throwaway last |
| **+CLOWN** | author as the butt; own reaction exaggerated past plausibility |
| **+JERK** | one named behavior inside a scene, teased. Never a category of person |
| **+CULT LEADER** | satire at the system; institutional and liturgical vocabulary; distance held; nobody personally blamed |
| **+NAVAL** | compress to stacked standalone declaratives; Gate walk rendered as a ladder |

### Analysis dials — change what is understood, then output in house voice

| Call | What it does | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| **LIKE WILBER** | name the altitude; mark what is genuinely contested; be exact about the distinction being drawn | never returns Wilber's *prose* — the finding comes back written as Wendell |
| **LIKE RAO** | find the shape; coin the term that carries the load | same |
| **LIKE MILLER** | restate as her internal problem rather than her external one; guide not hero | drop the survival substrate on the way in |

### Rules of use

- Dials **stack with a color** but never with each other at high intensity. +SNICKETY on a
  Cult Leader paragraph works. +SNICKETY +ADAMSY at "a lot" produces pastiche.
- An analysis dial always round-trips: it changes the *understanding*, and the sentence comes
  back in house voice. `LIKE WILBER` is never an instruction to write like Wilber.
- After any dial, re-run the constraints in Part 1. Dials do not get to break them.

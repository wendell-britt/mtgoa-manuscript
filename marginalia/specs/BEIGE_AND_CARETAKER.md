## 1. Beige enters as Level 0 — CHEAP

**Checked:** Beige appears in no chapter of the manuscript and in no row of
`MTGOA_LEVEL_MAP_CANONICAL`. The map runs Magenta→Turquoise, numbered 1–7.

**Add it as Level 0, not by renumbering.** Every existing cross-reference stays valid. Three
table rows total: the level map, the Power Allergy table, the Allyship Transformation table.

**Why it never fit before:** you were looking for a Face to give it. It does not want one. Beige
is the substrate — the body, survival, the hull. It wants **staff, not faculty.**

**And it produces a symmetry the map was missing.** Turquoise/Player is already flagged *not yet
defined*. With Beige at the bottom, the ladder is bookended by two levels that are not Faces:
what you are before you have any Face, and what you are when you can hold all of them. Below
and above the faculty.

| Row | Draft content |
|---|---|
| Level map | **0 · Beige · *(no Face — the Caretaker)* · Survival — the body's own power, older than will, exercised before it can be argued with** |
| Power allergy | **Beige — allergic to the body's own authority. Shadow of survival power: it does not negotiate, so it is easy to mistake for something that must be overridden** |
| Allyship transformation | **Beige — allyship looks like staying in the room, staying alive, and being resourced enough that someone else's crisis is survivable** |

**Ch2's Protector text is already written at Beige** — *it knows you are in danger before you
could prove it to anyone*, *machinery built for a predator*, *for most of human history those
were one event.* Nothing to rewrite. The level was in the book without a name.

## 2. The Caretaker — CHEAP, and the strongest item here

Not faculty. Staff. Knows how the ship actually runs, cares about survival and about the
daemons, keeps the things everyone else manages at a distance.

**Structural job:** every Head teaches an altitude. Nobody in the cast represents *the ship
staying alive.* The Caretaker does, which is Beige with a person attached.

**He authors Chapter 2.** This replaces the unsigned-orientation-document solve in
`CH2_MARGINALIA_SET.md` — better, because an authored Ch2 keeps the byline convention unbroken
from the first chapter that has one. **Note 1 in that set needs rewriting** (it was a joke about
authorlessness); notes 2–5 are unaffected.

**His daemon is the ship.**

This makes a line already in the Ch2 draft literal: *"The Protector is the hull of the ship."*
Written years before there was a ship. He is bonded to the hull of a vessel that cannot go home
— the Protector at ship scale — and it gives the Headmaster's grief a second body walking
around, which the Ch8 reveal can use without a word of setup.

**Name:** wants the Heads' register (short, dignified, no apostrophes) pitched rougher and
warmer — crew rather than faculty. *Bram Tull. Odger Fen. Sull.* Wendell's call.

## 4. Daemons as guardian spirits — FLAGGED

**The expensive version:** His Dark Materials proper. External, animal-formed, settling,
inseparable. Famous and specific enough that borrowing it incurs seven daemon forms plus bonding
lore, and invites the comparison on every page. **Not a four-day job.**

**The risk beyond schedule:** if the Protector becomes an animal companion, Jordan may stop
reading it as *her*. Pullman's dæmons *are* the person — that is the whole point of the system —
but "guardian spirit" invoked casually reads as separate, and the book cannot afford that.

**The free version — register, not system.** Speak of daemons as companions with their own will
rather than parts to be integrated. No forms, no animals, no bonding lore, no new rules.

Your Ch2 text already does this: the daemons are *"a part of you to be faced, understood, and
befriended."* The reframe is sitting in the manuscript. It needs a register pass, not an
invention.

**Recommendation:** free version now, expensive version in the RPG, where forms and bonding are
mechanics rather than metaphor and where the comparison is a feature.

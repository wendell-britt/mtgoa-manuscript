## The Caretaker — five fields

**Name: Bram Tull.** Swappable. Built to sit next to Voss, Ash, Quill, Vale, Cross, Orr — same
register, pitched rougher. *Tull* sounds like something you would name a piece of equipment,
which is the point.

**Title: Caretaker.** No epithet. The Heads each have one, chosen with ceremony —
*Patternwright*, *Keeper of the Far Game*. Bram has a job. **That contrast is his
characterisation and it is free**: the one man aboard who is not faculty does not get a poetic
title and does not want one.

**Stance.** The body is the only honest witness on the ship. He is impatient with people who
treat their own fear as a character flaw, and he has said so to Heads.

**Tic.** Understates. Short sentences. Describes dangerous things in maintenance vocabulary.

**Daemon coloring.** His daemon is the ship. The Protector at hull scale. He cannot reliably
tell the difference between the ship being fine and himself being fine, and he stopped trying
some time ago.

**Wrong about.** He let a student go planetside who was not ready, because the student asked and
Bram believes in letting people find out. It went badly. He would do it again, and says so
without any particular defensiveness, which is somehow worse.

**W. on him.** The Headmaster is slightly afraid of him and entirely dependent on him.

## NOTE 6 — NEW — *clarify / gossip*
**Placement:** Section 6, at the head of "The Seven," before the list.

> *You will not meet all seven. Most students finish with two, perhaps three, and go home
> entirely competent. The full set arrives only if you take all six schools, which almost nobody
> does, and which is not a recommendation.*
>
> *You already have the first one. It came with the body. Bram is fond of observing that it is
> the only member of the party that has never once had to be persuaded to turn up.*

*Carries new canon the reader needs — daemons are met through the schools, not possessed by
default — and installs it as gossip rather than exposition. **"Which is not a recommendation"
foreshadows Chapters 8 and 9 without reading as foreshadowing**, which is the only way this arc
is allowed to work.*

### *The Forest · Protector · orientation text*

**2026-07-28.** Five notes, ~560 words of new prose against a 7,272-word chapter. **No body
text changed.** Notes are unsigned and set as inset blocks.

**Arc beat:** pure Snicket. Wry, distant, **no cost visible anywhere.** Ch2's only job in the

## NOTE 1 — *humanize / gossip*
**Placement:** immediately under the header, before "Section 1: Urgency."

> *This one has no author because it has had eleven. It is revised each intake by whoever is
> least able to say no to the Deputy Headmistress, and the result is a document that has been
> improved well past the point where anyone can improve it further.*
>
> *I have stopped fighting about it. I kept one sentence. You will not be able to tell which
> one, and I intend to keep it that way.*

*Does the most work of any note in the chapter. Establishes that the margin is a person with
standing, that the institution is funny, and that reading is a game — and the last line will
have her reading the whole chapter hunting for it.*

---

## NOTE 2 — *clarify*
**Placement:** Section 1, after "The feeling is this: *this isn't working.*"

> *Every world we put in at has a version of this paragraph. The wording varies. Once it was a
> song.*
>
> *You are not being told something new — you are being told it in a room where it is finally
> allowed to be said out loud, which is a different service and worth more than it sounds. I
> have heard it delivered by a harbour official who had been awake for two days, and by a child
> who was entirely calm about it. The child was more accurate.*

---

## NOTE 3 — *amend*
**Placement:** Section 3, directly after the Decision question in Forest vs Village.

> *Skip the decision question. It is a good question and you will answer it flatteringly,
> because it is phrased as a choice between being shallow and being brave, and nobody picks
> shallow at a trailhead.*
>
> *Better: think of the last time you did not say the true thing. Do not judge it. Only notice
> whether you knew, at the time, that you were not saying it. If you knew, you were in the
> village. If you did not know until afterwards, you were already in the forest and the forest
> was working.*

*The Prince move — a practitioner's shortcut that improves the textbook without disrespecting
it. Also the chapter's only genuinely useful note, which is the right ratio at one in five.*

---

## NOTE 4 — *gossip / humanize*
**Placement:** Section 6, after the first paragraph of "The Protector, up close" — right below
the hull image.

> *The hull image is not one the school invented for teaching purposes.*
>
> *You are shortly going to be asked to thank a part of yourself, the request will sound
> sentimental, and I would like you to have a picture of what it looks like when an institution
> does it in earnest. Ask anyone in engineering. They will tell you the hull is the only part of
> this ship that has never once sat in a meeting about its own performance. It braces. It has no
> opinion. It is thanked annually, by tradition, on a day nobody can attend, because we are
> always underway.*

*Grounds the "thank the Protector" move institutionally so it lands as practice rather than
woo. The hull line was already in the manuscript years before the ship existed — the note is
claiming ground the chapter already holds.*

---

## NOTE 5 — *confess, weightless*
**Placement:** Section 8, after the five threshold moves.

> *I have taught these five for a long time and I still cannot do the second one without
> rehearsing it in a corridor first.*
>
> *I am told this is common. I am told this by people who are being kind.*

*Confession with no cost attached — exactly Ch2's brief. She learns the annotator implicates
himself and that it is safe to laugh when he does. The arc uses that habit in Ch6 and breaks it
in Ch8.*

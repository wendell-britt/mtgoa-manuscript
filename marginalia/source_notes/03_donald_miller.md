# 03 — Donald Miller, *Building a StoryBrand*

**Added:** 2026-07-28, from a 20-quote collection.
**Bucket:** admired-but-wrong, with one stance import.

**Form caveat, stated once.** This is a quote collection, not passages. It cannot supply
rhythm, escalation, or mouthfeel — the corpus's own standing objection to quote-based
sourcing applies. What it can supply is **stance**, and stance is what Wendell asked it for.

---

## THE IMPORT: reader is hero, author is guide

*"The customer is the hero, not your brand. When we position our customer as the hero and
ourselves as the guide, we will be recognized as a trusted resource."*

**Why this is now load-bearing rather than optional.** Three independent sources have landed
on the same constraint from different directions:

| Source | Route |
|---|---|
| **A0** (`CH1_VOICE_AND_LINE_EDIT_CHECKLIST`) | ethical — don't narrate the reader back to herself |
| **Snicket** (passage 1) | craft — the narrator's *limitation* is the source of his authority |
| **Miller** | commercial — the guide position is what earns trust |

Three frames, one rule. That is the signature of a structural constraint rather than a
stylistic preference, and it should be stated at the top of the voice doc, not buried in the
tics list.

## Second import: internal problem over external problem

*"Almost all companies try to sell solutions to external problems, but customers are much
more motivated to resolve their inner frustrations."*

Directly applicable. The ICA's external problem is that she wants to be a better ally. Her
internal problem is that she is exhausted, guilty, and suspects her care is performance. The
manuscript already knows this. The **copy** does not consistently.

## Third: "life with the dull parts taken out"

Miller quoting Hitchcock. Useful as a cutting standard for the revision pass, and it is the
argument for the workhorse/flagship ratio rather than against it — dull parts removed, not
every part heightened.

---

## THE TRAP: the substrate is survival

*"help them survive and thrive"* · *"burning calories to process the information"* · *"they
will tune us out"* · *"get ahead in life"* · *"survive or thrive, or they will tune us out"*

This is scarcity and threat as the engine of attention. It is Orange, and `BRAND_FOUNDATION`
bans it by name — no achievement, competition, urgency, or scarcity.

**The repair is already yours, stated in your own prior work:** keep the structural skeleton
of direct-response mechanics; swap the psychological substrate from fear/superiority to
recognition/belonging.

Applied here: the reason to be clear is **not** that she will tune you out if you burn her
calories. It is that clarity is how she recognizes herself in the page. Same craft
instruction, opposite engine.

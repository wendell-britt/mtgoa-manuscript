# The Six School Handbooks, draft 1

**2026-07-30.** Written to `specs/SPEC_SCHOOL_HANDBOOKS_2026-07-30.md` §4 (the six required
clauses), `marginalia/specs/SEVEN_VOICES.md` (genre), and
`marginalia/specs/HEAD_VOICE_DIAL.md` (the six dials).

**The premise doing the work:** the institution requires every school to file the same
document to the same form. Six temperaments comply. The form is identical; what leaks through
is personality.

**Clause 6 carries the learning edge, not the formative fact.** The fact is already seated in
each treatise as of today. A prospective student needs to know their teacher is still working
on something, which is a different disclosure and lands in a different document.

Not in the manuscript. Referral map and clause set both proposed rather than approved.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE BODY
### *Admissions. Filed as required.*

**1. Who we are for.** Two conditions. The first is that you have known something in a
gathering before anybody said it, and said nothing, and been right. The second is that you
have watched somebody else know something and say nothing, and you saw them decide not to.
Applicants with only the second condition are declined. You cannot return an instrument you
have never held.

**2. What you will be able to do when you leave.** Name where a thing landed in your body
before you name what it means. That is the whole capability and it sounds small. What follows
from it is not small: the people around you begin saying things earlier, because somebody in
the gathering is visibly not waiting for proof.

**3. What it costs.** You will be wrong out loud, on a schedule, in front of the same people
each time. Students who arrange to be wrong only in private have suffered as a result: the
instrument does not recalibrate in private, and three of them have come back after a decade
to start again.

**4. What we do not teach.** What to do about it. We can get you the reading. We cannot get
you the sentence that changes the situation, and the two are separate trainings by separate
hands. Send it to the School of the Line.

**5. Entry.** None. Everyone begins here, including the ones who arrive certain they are past
it. Especially those.

**6. A word from the Head.** The reading is not the difficulty any more. What I am working on
is what it costs a person to be read accurately by somebody they did not ask, and I have not
solved it, and I teach here anyway.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE LINE
### *Admissions. Filed as required.*

**1. Who we are for.** You have said no and had it not take. You said it in a meeting,
pleasantly, and the thing went ahead; you said it again six weeks later and were told you had
raised that already. You have also watched it happen to somebody with less standing than you,
and done nothing, and known at the time you were doing nothing. Both. Applicants with one are
declined.

**2. What you will be able to do when you leave.** State a limit in one sentence and not take
it back in the four seconds afterward. The specific thing stops. Then the second thing, which
students underestimate for about a year: the people watching learn that limits can be stated
here, and some of them start stating their own.

**3. What it costs.** You will be unwelcome in small ways, more often than you have budgeted
for, and somebody whose opinion you value will call you difficult where you can hear it.
Students who wait until they feel ready have suffered as a result!

**4. What we do not teach.** Repair. You will object that a limit without repair is cruelty.
It is not, it is incomplete, and incomplete is a different problem with a different address.
Send it to the School of the Bridge. Failing to send it is the error here, not sending it.

**5. Entry.** You must be able to say what you felt in the last moment you said nothing. Not
what you thought. What you felt, and where. If you cannot, the School of the Body takes
first-years every season and we will still be here.

**6. A word from the Head.** The lines land now. What I am working is the four seconds after a
line drawn on somebody else's behalf, when they go on living in the place where I said it and
I do not.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE OATH
### *Admissions. Filed as required, under clause one.*

**Clause one.** Each school shall produce an account of itself for those considering entry.
The clause as written requires an account. The clause as kept, since Merrow, has produced a
recruitment notice. This is an account.

**Clause two, who we are for.** Those who have found a practice they belong to altered in the
part that concerned them, without notice, and learned of it afterward and from somebody else.
Applicants must also have kept a thing they knew was costing someone. Both conditions. The
first without the second produces a student who breaks well and cannot hold; we have admitted
several and it is recorded.

**Clause three, the capability conferred.** To pass a practice on intact and named. Named is
the part that is difficult and the part that is the whole point: a practice handed over
without its record is handed to somebody who must now guess what it was for, and guessing is
how the alterations start.

**Clause four, the cost.** Time, at a scale students do not anticipate. Nothing here resolves
inside a season. Those who required resolution inside a season have suffered as a result, and
two are named in the ledger with their consent.

**Clause five, what is not taught.** The repair of the machine that produced the alteration.
We hold and we hand on. We do not redesign, and a school that redesigns while claiming to
preserve is the failure this school exists downstream of. Send it to the School of the
Pattern.

**Clause six, entry.** Admission requires prior standing at the School of the Line. One must
be able to refuse a thing before one can be trusted to keep it.

**A note from the Head, appended outside the clauses.** I can pass an inheritance intact and
named, which is the whole of what is claimed above. I have not learned to hand one to somebody
who will change it without entering the change as damage. My own ledgers are the reason that
is hard.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE PATTERN
### *Admissions. Specification 0.1.*

**1. Who we are for.** Specification: an applicant has (a) taken a path that cost them more
than it cost the person beside them, for reasons neither of them chose, and (b) watched
somebody else take that path and thought *the process is fine, they are struggling.* Both
required. Applicants presenting only (b) are declined and told why, which takes an afternoon
and saves four years.

**2. Capability conferred.** You will locate the point at which a system produces the outcome
it produces. Not the person at that point. The point. In practice the consequence arrives
about a season later and it is this: people stop apologising to you for things that were
never theirs, because you have stopped accepting the apology.

**3. Cost.** Tolerance, stated: this training holds up to roughly the third redesign. Past
that, students begin producing designs to correct designs, each further from anybody living
inside them, and the drift is the mechanism rather than the error. Students who did not stop
at three have suffered as a result. See figure two.

**4. Not taught.** What to do with the person the redesign will not reach in time. There is
always one. The specification does not cover them and I decline to pretend otherwise. Send it
to the School of the Horizon.

**5. Entry.** Prior standing at the School of the Oath. You must have kept something before
you are permitted to change something, and the order is not stylistic.

**6. A word from the Head.** The designs hold. I ask first now, which I did not for nineteen
years. In practice I am still slower at asking than at designing, and the asking takes an
afternoon.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE BRIDGE
### *Admissions. Filed as required.*

**1. Who we are for.** Two positions, and we record both. The applicant who says *I have no
leverage and I stayed at the table anyway*: admitted. The applicant who says *I watched
somebody sit at a table where their only leverage was somebody else's goodwill, and I said
nothing because it was not my table*: also admitted, and only in combination with the first.
One without the other produces a translator with nothing at stake, and we have trained those
and it did not hold.

**2. What you will be able to do when you leave.** Name what each party is protecting, out
loud, including the party you think is wrong. Consequence: the conversation stops being about
who is right roughly four minutes earlier than it used to, and four minutes is more than it
sounds.

**3. What it costs.** You will not be thanked. I want to be exact, because students hear this
as modesty. The terms you write will hold, the people they hold for will not connect the
holding to you, and there is no version of this training that fixes that. Students who came
for the acknowledgement have suffered as a result.

**4. What we do not teach.** How to make terms outlast the people who agreed to them. We get
the agreement. We cannot get the century. Send it to the School of the Oath, which can, and
which will be dry with you about it.

**5. Entry.** Prior standing at the School of the Pattern. You must be able to see the design
producing the pressure before you sit down inside the pressure, or you will negotiate with a
person about a machine.

**6. A word from the Head.** The terms hold. Whether wanting the sentence is a defect in me or
a condition of the work is the open question here, and I am the wrong person to settle it.

## THE SCHOOL OF THE HORIZON
### *Admissions. Filed as required, and late.*

**1. Who we are for.** Maera would say the person who has stopped registering their own
signal, and Corin would say the person who cannot spend a line, and both are describing the
same applicant from inside their own school, which is the difficulty with this document and
with this school. We take the person who has sat with something that will not resolve and
did not try to close it, and who has also been sat with, once, by somebody who fixed nothing,
and can still describe the afternoon.

**2. What you will be able to do when you leave.** Tell which school a moment belongs to
before you act in it, and hand the moment over, which is most of what we do and reads as
doing nothing. The consequence is that the people you send arrive at the right school on the
first attempt rather than the third, and Irix will tell you what the first two cost.

**3. What it costs.** Range, which sounds like a gift. You will stop being simply at home in
any one school, including the one you came from, and I have watched that land as loss in
students who expected it to land as freedom. Those who came here to be above the other five
have suffered as a result, and left, and one has come back.

**4. What we do not teach.** Any of it. We do not teach the reading, the line, the keeping,
the design, or the terms. We teach which of the five a moment is asking for, and then we send
you, and the sending is the entire curriculum. All five schools take our referrals and Sera
is dry about ours in particular.

**5. Entry.** Standing at all five. There is no way to shorten this and students ask every
year.

**6. A word from the Head.** I have read all five schools for thirty years and nobody has read
me, which I arranged, and which I am now told is a course I never took. I am the oldest
first-year at this school. That is a joke and it is also the timetable.

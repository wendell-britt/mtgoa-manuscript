## VOICE CALIBRATION SUMMARY

### What Wendell's voice does well (mechanically):
1. **Stacks 2-3 satisfaction states in a single paragraph** — the reader finishes a section feeling multiple things at once
2. **Names the mechanic AND demonstrates it** — "When metabolized, fear's scanning becomes excitement's foresight" is both the teaching and the example
3. **Uses somatic metaphor** — "weather," "flood," "lightning," "storming" — the body is always present
4. **Uses parentheticals for voice** — "(I checked)" signals wit, "(and yes, this is awkward)" signals embodied honesty
5. **Uses rhythmic repetition** — the W.A.V.E. passage, the element table, the "fear shows X / anger points to X" structure

### What the voice avoids:
- Hedging ("it might seem like," "you could argue")
- Excessive warmth (it's warm but it doesn't nuzzle)
- Explanation when demonstration is possible

### Register shifts in the book:
- **Warm prose** — narrative, vignettes, exercises
- **Sharp reference** — tables, definitions, canonical lists
- **Liturgical** — moments meant to be spoken aloud (epiphany moments, WAVE passages)

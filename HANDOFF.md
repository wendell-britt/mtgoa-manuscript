## The locked spine (do not contradict)

- **Macro journey:** Villager → Player. Reader descends through 8 gates. Reaches Vulnerable Child at center. Returns as Player.
- **Campaign mechanic:** Reader selects one self-sabotaging belief in Ch0 and runs it as her campaign for the whole book. Six beliefs, re-readable — one campaign per belief, six campaigns deep.
- **The six beliefs:** I'm not ready / not capable / not worthy / not good enough / insignificant / don't belong.
- **The Effective Allyship Formula:** (1) Identify your superpower Ch2, (2) who needs it Ch3, (3) enroll allies Ch4, (4) show up consistently Ch6.
- **Gate sequence:** Each chapter's gate comes BEFORE the Face teaching. Gate = internal precondition. Face = external capacity. These are distinct registers — see the Face vs. Gate register principle in the master doc.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)

- Never soften. The manuscript's voice is direct, processed, earned.
- Flag teaching mode (explaining the framework rather than using it).
- Flag "you already know this in your body" and all variants — logged as a global sweep item.
- Somatic language: invite, never tell. Reader's body, not author's body.
- Work from Teal or within Green. Never Orange.
- Visualizations are primary for the ideal reader — suggest and develop proactively.

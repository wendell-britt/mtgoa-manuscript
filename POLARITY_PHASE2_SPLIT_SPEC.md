## Part B — Ch4 encounter (Phase 1.5)

**File:** `chapters/ch4-REGENT/CHAPTER4_REGENT_FULL_DRAFT.md`  
**Placement:** **Option A (preferred):** New subsection after Section 3 closing (`Real stewardship includes the possibility of reform…`), before Section 4.  
**Alternative:** Insert after **Move 2: Honor What Still Serves**, before **Move 3: Reform Without Erasing** — ties polarity to Reformer channel.

**Section title:** `### Polarity Encounter — Honor ↔ Reform`  
**Mode:** Practice mode — explicit entry: *Try this now.*  
**Target length:** ~300–400 words + boxed exercise (~150 words)

### Prose frame (~250 words)

**Must include:**

- Callback to Ch2: *You met the Polarity Map in Chapter 2. Here is the Regent's pair.*
- **Pair definition (behavioral, not moral):**
  - **Pole A — Honor what was passed:** keep the form, protect what works, stay loyal to the inheritance  
  - **Pole B — Reform what no longer serves:** update the form, name what broke, change what the essence requires  
- **Ideal reader catch:** coalition norms, DEI vocabulary, land acknowledgments-as-cargo-cult — *without* naming DEI explicitly; use "the practices you inherited from the work"  
- **Regent thesis link:** *Traditions worth protecting and worth breaking* — this is that tension made drawable  
- **Solo scope:** map **your** inheritance, not a conflict between two people  
- **No app handoff** — paper/notes sufficient  

**Must not include:**

- Care ↔ Impact (reserved for Ch6)  
- Full four-quadrant / 2×2 matrix (appendix only)  
- Moral language (good/bad, courage/avoidance) per `FSR/Polarity Rules.md`  

### Boxed exercise — `Draw the Inheritance Axis`

```
Try this now.

1. Name one inheritance you carry in allyship work — a practice, norm, or loyalty
   you didn't invent but keep performing. (One sentence.)

2. Draw the axis:
   HONOR WHAT WAS PASSED  ←——●——→  REFORM WHAT NO LONGER SERVES
   Mark where you are today.

3. Write one integrated sentence — both poles in the same statement:
   "I stay with __________ because it still __________,
    and I need __________ to change before I can keep showing up honestly."

4. Sit 30 seconds. Notice: heaviness (pole A stuck) or heat (pole B stuck) or ground (integrated).

Back to the chapter.
```

- [ ] Completable in **5–10 minutes**  
- [ ] No other new Phase-2 tool in Ch4 (gates walk already exists)  
- [ ] Reader can map **honor ↔ reform** without naming another person as villain

## Part C — Ch6 first full practice (Phase 2, merged with Move 3)

**File:** `chapters/ch6-diplomat/CHAPTER6_DIPLOMAT_FULL_DRAFT_MASTER.md`  
**Placement:** **Replace/expand** `**Move 3: Close with Honest Terms**` — polarity map **precedes** close; close **is** the integrated action.  
**Mode:** Practice mode — one boxed exercise, one somatic exit.  
**Target length:** Move 3 total ~600–750 words (prose + box); **single** sit-down.

### Prose frame (~350 words)

**Must include:**

- Ch4 callback (bridge sentence above)  
- **Pair definition:**
  - **Pole A — Care:** attunement, harm reduction, presence that protects dignity  
  - **Pole B — Impact:** material change, costly presence, decisions that stick  
- **Governing tension** (from `POLARITY-TYPES-CANON.md`): *People care too much in ways that remove their impact*  
- **First-person / coalition scope:** a meeting, partnership, or working group **she** is in — not abstract culture war both-sides  
- **Discernment note (Challenger):** equal **hearing** ≠ equal **endorsement**; map **her** over-care, not "both camps valid"  
- **Integrative Negotiator link:** Close with Honest Terms = **integrated move** on the axis, not separate ultimatum  
- **Lexicon:** stakes, terms, agreement — not price, walk-away  

**Retire as standalone structure:** Move 3 prose that teaches close **without** map first — merge into single flow.

### Boxed exercise — `Map Care ↔ Impact, Then Close`

```
Try this now.

Prerequisite: a real coalition tension where you've been "caring" more than moving.

1. CHARGE — One sentence. The scene. (Meeting, thread, partnership.)

2. POLES — Label the axis:
   CARE (attunement, harm reduction)  ←——●——→  IMPACT (change that costs)

3. DOT — Where have you been living on this axis? What did over-care protect you from?

4. INTEGRATED TERMS — Draft one sentence naming what the field must hold for BOTH:
   "I care about __________, and I also need us to __________ by __________."

5. CLOSE — Say the integrated sentence (out loud if possible). Not a threat. An offering
   of information. Then stop. Let the field respond.

→ app: Capture what landed. /polarity

What it feels like: stillness + heat with ground — not molasses, not bracing for exit.
```

### Move 3 structure after merge

1. Short prose — why close requires map first (~200 words)  
2. **Boxed exercise** (above)  
3. **Example line** — revise existing example to show care+impact in same sentence  
4. **The test** — keep existing honest-terms test; add: *Did the map change what you were willing to say?*  
5. **Somatic** — keep stillness vs bracing; add molasses shadow (endless care, no close)

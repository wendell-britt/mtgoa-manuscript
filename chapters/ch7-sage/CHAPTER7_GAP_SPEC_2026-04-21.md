## P1 Gaps (Must Have)

### G1: S4 Practice — 5 EA Transcend Moves (P1)
**What:** Each of the 5 modes needs an explicit transcend move with:
- Format: `[DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION]` Transcend [X] — **Emotion Name** → *Alchemical Outcome*
- Written in Wendell's voice, grounded in Sage-specific content

**Where to insert:** After each mode's existing practice paragraph, before the next mode

**Content:**
1. **Panoramic Seer:** [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend fear-of-blankness — **Fear** → *Excitement*
   - The fear: what if I see something I can't unsee? What if I see how small the room actually is?
   - The alchemy: the contraction becomes expansion — what you see, you can hold

2. **Altitude-Switcher:** [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend altitude-arrogance — **Anger** → *Triumph*
   - The anger: *I'm at Teal, you're at Green, this is beneath me*
   - The alchemy: the judgment becomes the capacity to meet them where they are

3. **Diagnostician:** [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend superiority — **Sadness** → *Peace*
   - The dissatisfaction: grief at what lower altitudes miss; superiority about knowing
   - The alchemy: naming becomes service, not spectacle

4. **Liberator:** [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend identity-attachment — **Anger** → *Bliss*
   - The anger: *I am the person who holds this together* — and the rage when it doesn't hold
   - The alchemy: the game stops being who you are and starts being what you chose

5. **Returner:** [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend altitude-loss — **Sadness** → *Poignancy*
   - The sadness: grief at leaving the view, at coming back down
   - The alchemy: the poignancy is the point — the distance between what you see and where they are is where the love lives

### G2: S4 Practice — 5 Control Moves (P1)
**What:** Each mode needs an explicit Control (Neutral Channel) move, labeled as behavioral pattern + cost.

**Content:**
1. **Panoramic Seer → narrating:** seeing becomes a performance of knowing
2. **Altitude-Switcher → inconsistency:** switching without anchoring, never present long enough to be useful
3. **Diagnostician → lecturing:** naming altitudes as a way to be right, not to be useful
4. **Liberator → abandoning:** putting games down before they're done teaching
5. **Returner → retreating:** coming back down as escape, not as gift

### G3: S5 Journey — The 8 Gates Walk (P1)
**What:** A named, guided walk through the 8 Gates at Teal altitude — the WAVE-Spiral applied to Sage's specific terrain.

**Structure:** Gate name → What it says → What the Sage carries → What it costs → What it gives back

**From SPEC (already written, needs expansion into full walk):**
| Gate | Says | Carrying | Job |
|------|------|----------|-----|
| **Protector** | Not safe to share what you see. People won't understand. | **Fear** | Keep the Sage's vision hidden so they don't get ostracized. |
| **Controller** | If you see everything, you'll never act. Seeing is the enemy of deciding. | **Fear** | Contain the panoramic vision so you stay functional. |
| **Skeptic** | Is this "seeing" just me constructing a flattering narrative? | **Fear** | Doubt the integration before someone else can. |
| **Fixer** | Stop watching. Act. Seeing without doing is decadence. | **Anger** | Convert vision into action prematurely. |
| **Fear** | If you see all of this, you become responsible for all of it. | **Fear** | Rehearse the burden of the meta-view. |
| **Victim** | I see too much because I've been hurt too many times. | **Sadness** | Keep the story alive that the seeing is damage. |
| **Damaged Self** | There's something wrong with how I process the world. | **Fear** | Turn panoramic capacity into defect. |
| **Vulnerable Child** | I want to be in the game AND see the game. | **Joy** | Hold the superpower. |

**Format:** Each gate gets 3-5 sentences of walking prose — not a table row, a paragraph that walks the reader through the gate's territory.

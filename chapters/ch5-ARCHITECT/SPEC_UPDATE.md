# SPEC_UPDATE.md — Architect Chapter Revision Spec
## *Based on competitive gap analysis — 2026-04-17*

---

## What the Gap Analysis Revealed

The Architect chapter as drafted follows the universal template with Wendell's voice. It is well-structured. It is also **undifferentiated** — it could live in any gamification book because it doesn't yet sound like what MTGOA's Architect actually does.

The competitive gap analysis surfaced 5 specific things MTGOA does that no other source does. **Those 5 differentiators are the revision targets.**

---

## 5 Structural Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

These are the things the Architect chapter MUST demonstrate that no other gamification/design source can claim:

### D1 — Emotional alchemy + game mechanics COMBINED
**Gap:** 10K HP gamifies behavior. Igniting Joy alchemizes energy. MTGOA does both.
**What this means for the chapter:**
- Section 3 (Concept) must explain that the Architect's game design IS emotional alchemy practice — not a separate overlay
- Section 4 (Practice) must show the energy economy of design decisions — not just the mechanics
- The "why it works" must reference the feeling/energy layer, not just the behavioral layer

### D2 — Person-as-ecology
**Gap:** Laloux builds organizations. MTGOA treats the individual AS an organization.
**What this means for the chapter:**
- The Architect designs the person, not just the system around them
- Section 3 must make clear: the Architect's client is the individual's internal design system
- Section 6 (Game) moves should include at least one that designs the SELF (not just external quests)

### D3 — 6 GM Faces as architectural voices
**Gap:** No source uses a multi-voice persona system.
**What this means for the chapter:**
- The Architect doesn't speak alone — the Architect's voice is in dialogue with the other 5 faces
- At least one moment in Section 3 or Section 4 should reference another face explicitly
- Example: "The Architect designs the quest. But the Shaman knows whether the reward feeds or Starves the player."

### D4 — Quest grammar + BARs
**Gap:** Sources use goals or habits. MTGOA uses fork-first design and artifact blooms.
**What this means for the chapter:**
- Section 4 (Practice) must make the quest grammar distinct from "set a goal and track it"
- The fork-first principle must be named and explained
- BARs must appear as a named MTGOA mechanic (not just "milestones")

### D5 — Structural generosity
**Gap:** Sources design FOR users. MTGOA designs so the design becomes unnecessary.
**What this means for the chapter:**
- The capstone of Section 3 (Concept): "The Architect's job is to design themselves out of a job"
- Section 6 (Game) must include at least one move that is about the HANDOVER, not just the build
- This is the most emotionally resonant differentiator — it needs the most voice attention

## Voice Diversification

The current chapter uses Wendell's calibrated voice (from Shaman). That voice is correct for the Shaman. It is NOT correct for all 6 faces.

**Voice per face (6-face architecture):**

| Face | Voice quality | Key word patterns | What it sounds like |
|------|-------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Shaman** | Warm, embodied, mythic | *feel, body, signal, data* | Sitting by a fire. Listening. |
| **Challenger** | Direct, confrontational, clean | *line, boundary, no, done* | Sharp. No decoration. |
| **Regent** | Measured, principled, weighty | *duty, lineage, honor, serve* | Someone who has carried this a long time. |
| **Architect** | Precise, generative, builder | *lever, design, system, elegant, handoff* | Slightly more cerebral. Shows the structure. Uses "you can" not just "you should." |
| **Diplomat** | Relational, curious, bridging | *field, bridge, hold, together* | Asking questions. Making connections. |
| **Sage** | Panoramic, liberating, calm | *game, altitude, perspective, release* | Someone who has seen all of it and came back. |

**For Architect specifically:** The voice should feel like someone who genuinely enjoys the puzzle of design. Not cold. Not academic. Enjoys it. Phrases like "the elegant solution," "the satisfying mechanism," "here's the piece that makes it click." Warm precision.

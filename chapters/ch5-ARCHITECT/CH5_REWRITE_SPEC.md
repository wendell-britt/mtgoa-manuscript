## The Correct Architecture — How Emotional Alchemy Shapes Each Face

**The rule:** The Shaman (Ch2) is the foundation. Emotional alchemy lives there. Each subsequent face is an **emergent type** that arises from how emotional alchemy influences the role as it develops at altitude.

**What "emergent" means:**
- The Shaman's 5 channels are the base taxonomy (Fear, Sadness, Joy, Anger, Neutrality).
- As each face develops at its altitude (Challenger at Red, Regent at Green, Architect at Orange, Diplomat at Teal, Sage at Magenta), the emotional alchemy channels SHAPE what the role becomes.
- The Architect doesn't use the Shaman's 5 channels directly. The Architect has their own 5 emergent types (Mapmaker, Engineer, Strategist, Inventor, Optimizer) — and those types exist BECAUSE emotional alchemy has been layered into the system.
- Each face's channels are shaped BY the emotional alchemy influence — they're not the same channels wearing different labels.

**The architectural relationship:**
```
Shaman (Ch2) — base emotional alchemy
  ↓ (emotional alchemy shapes and influences)

  ↓

  ↓
Diplomat (Ch6) — emerges as the relational-face, channels are: [TBD per chapter]
```

**Key implication:** The Architect's 5 channels (Mapmaker, Engineer, Strategist, Inventor, Optimizer) are emergent types that reflect how a structural design role develops when shaped by emotional alchemy. They are NOT: a direct mapping to Metal/Water/Wood/Fire/Earth, nor a translation of the Shaman's channel system. They are the Architect's own forms, and the Shaman's framework explains WHERE those forms came from.

## The Rewrite Plan

### S4 Rewrite — Key Changes

**4.1 — Reframe the 5 channels as emergent types:**
The Architect's 5 channels (Mapmaker, Engineer, Strategist, Inventor, Optimizer) are emergent structural design types shaped by emotional alchemy — not direct mappings of the Shaman's 5 channels. Write them as the Architect's own forms, with the Shaman framework explaining the origin.

Each channel has an EA move (Dissatisfaction → Satisfaction) using the Architect's native material (logic). Example format:

- **Mapmaker** — sees the whole system at once. EA move: [Confusion → Clarity] Transcend — **{Confusion}** → **{Systemic map}**. The Architect encounters the messy multi-variable situation and produces a clean model.

**4.2 — Remove all energy economy notation:**
Strip +2/+1/-1 from all transcend, translate, and control move labels. Replace with general "this move energizes / costs" phrasing in the prose if needed, but no accounting.

**4.3 — Integrate the 5 differentiators into S4:**
- **D1 (EA + mechanics combined):** When describing each channel move, include what happens in the game system (quest grammar, fork-first design, BARs).
- **D2 (Person-as-ecology):** At least one move references designing the self, not just external systems.
- **D3 (6 GM Faces):** At least one moment where the Architect's design is checked against another face. Example: "The Architect designs the fork. The Shaman checks: does this reward feed or starve?"
- **D4 (Quest grammar + BARs):** Reference fork-first and BARs by name.
- **D5 (Structural generosity):** The flow cycle must end with the handoff principle as the capstone.

**4.4 — Fix the control moves:**
Control moves address behavioral patterns (over-design, scheming, systematization-for-comfort, modelitis). State the underlying dissatisfaction emotion explicitly before each transformation. Format: [DISSATISFACTION → SATISFACTION] Transcend X — **{Emotion Name}** → *{Alchemical Outcome}*

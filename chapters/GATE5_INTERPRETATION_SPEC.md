## Three Interpretations of "The Emotional Body" (Gate 5)

### Interpretation A — Gate Identity (structural)

Gate 5 is the structural label for the fifth gate in the allyship walk. It is about the *emotional body* — the capacity to feel and process emotion without suppression or flooding. The EA channel Metal/Fear is one possible signal that can pass through this gate, but the gate itself is not about Fear.

**Evidence for A:** Ch1 uses "Emotional Body" as the gate label. This aligns with the EA framework where each gate has a corresponding emotional channel. The emotional body is the container; the EA channel is what flows through it.

**Challenge:** Ch1 is the only chapter that uses this label. If this is the canonical meaning, all other chapters have it wrong.

---

### Interpretation B — WAVE Charge State (tactical)

Gate 5 is the moment in the allyship walk where the practitioner enters WAVE Charge state — the moment when emotional charge is elevated and the person is most likely to act from reactivity rather than choice. "The Fear" is a descriptor of what the Charge state *feels like* in the context of allyship work — specific fear about failure, harm, or inadequacy.

**Evidence for B:** Ch2, Ch3, and Ch4 all describe Gate 5 as fear-based. The language is consistent across these three chapters: something gets dark, narrow, specific. The fear is about taking on something too large.

**Challenge:** This interpretation treats "Fear" as a WAVE state descriptor, not a gate label. But the chapter structure uses Gate 5 as a structural identity, not a state description. Mixing the two creates the confusion we're seeing.

---

### Interpretation C — Face-Native Emergent (per chapter)

Each face has an emergent interpretation of Gate 5 that reflects the face's specific work. The emotional body manifests differently depending on the altitude/color of the face doing the work.

- Ch1 Forest: "Emotional Body" — direct, unfiltered feeling
- Ch2 Shaman: "Fear" — instinct-level alarm signal
- Ch3 Challenger: "The Fear" — boundary violation detection
- Ch4 Regent: "The Fear" — tradition-dying fear
- Ch5 Architect: "Fear — Fear" — collapse/failure fear
- Ch6 Diplomat: "Already Alone" — price-naming devastation
- Ch7 Sage: TBD
- Ch8 Player: TBD

**Evidence for C:** The six faces all describe Gate 5 differently, which could reflect genuine emergent multiplicity OR could be the propagation of an original labeling error.

**Challenge:** If every face has a different label for the same structural gate, the reader cannot build a consistent internal map. "Which gate am I in?" becomes unanswerable when each chapter renames the same gate.

## Required Decision

**Wendell needs to answer:** Which interpretation is correct?

1. **Interpretation A** — Gate 5 = "Emotional Body" always. The EA channel Metal/Fear is one of five possible signals through this gate. All other chapters (Ch2–Ch6) need their Gate 5 labeled "Emotional Body" and their fear-language moved into the content of the section, not the label.

2. **Interpretation B** — Gate 5 = the WAVE Charge state. "Fear" is the correct descriptor for what happens at this gate in allyship contexts. The label can vary by face if the *type* of fear differs. Ch1's "Emotional Body" label is the exception, not the rule.

3. **Interpretation C** — Gate 5 = emergent per face. Each face gets to define Gate 5 in its own voice. The structural identity is "Gate 5" and the content label is face-native. No fix needed; this is intentional variation.

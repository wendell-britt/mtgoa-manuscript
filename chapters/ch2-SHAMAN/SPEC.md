## Core thesis

Before strategy, structure, before any move in the world — there is pattern.

Emotional alchemy is the practice of being in relationship with the full spectrum of human feeling, and letting those feelings teach you something that changes who you are. The Shaman's superpower is naming the feeling the room is having before anyone else can.

## The 5 channels (emotional elements)

1. **Metal / Fear** — detection. What risk is present?
2. **Water / Sadness** — loss. What was real?
3. **Wood / Joy** — vitality. What aligned?
4. **Fire / Anger** — boundary. What matters enough to fight for?
5. **Earth / Neutrality** — perspective. Can I see clearly?

---

## The stage sequence (WAVE)

**Wake → Alchemize → Voice → Embody**

1. **Wake:** Feel the signal. Don't override it.
2. **Alchemize:** Let the feeling complete its cycle. Don't fix it — let it teach.
3. **Voice:** Speak from the teaching, not from the feeling.
4. **Embody:** The response is now informed by the lesson.

## Vulnerable Child's superpower

**Emotional truth.** The capacity to feel fully and let feeling teach. The Shaman's gift is not the absence of pain — it is the willingness to let pain be the teacher.

---

## The 5 Game moves (Section 6)

1. **Read the Field** — Before you respond, feel the room.
2. **Name the Unsaid** — Say what nobody in the room is saying.
3. **Create Safety** — Hold another person's emotions without fixing them.
4. **Be Real** — Don't perform. Show up actual.
5. **Stay** — Breathe through the difficulty instead of leaving.

---

## Bridge to next chapter (Challenger)

The Shaman can feel what is true. The Shaman cannot act on it without the Challenger. Feeling without action is paralysis. Once you know what you feel — the next question is: what do you do about it?

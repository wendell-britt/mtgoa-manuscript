## Part D — Boxed exercise — `Reclaim the Projected Line`

```
Try this now.

Pick the figure you call "oppressor energy" — someone who draws lines, says no,
takes space without apologizing. NOT someone you're allied with. The one who
provokes the thought: that's domination.

1. STEP 3 — FACE IT (third person)
   Describe them like a journalist. Body, voice, what they do that tightens your jaw.
   Two short paragraphs. This is the figure in the next room — not the real person.

2. STEP 2 — TALK TO IT (second person)
   At least three exchanges. Suggested opens:
   — "Why do you get to say no when I can't?"
   — "What are you protecting?"
   — "What would happen if I had your certainty?"
   Let it answer. Don't polite the dialogue.

3. STEP 1 — OWN IT (first person)
   Speak AS the part. One paragraph. No "I notice a part of me."
   Example shape: "I say no because I can't afford to keep paying for harmony
   with my disappearance. I draw the line because someone has to."

4. RECEIPT — Sit 30 seconds. Did the charge move? Up, down, into clarity?
   That's fuel returning — available for a clean line, not a explosion.

→ app: Capture what landed. /shadow/321

Back to the chapter. You didn't become the villain. You recovered the capacity
you split off.
```

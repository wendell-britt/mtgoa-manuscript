## The Three Core Patterns

The 6-face analysis of Chapter 8 identified three structural patterns that are not unique to Chapter 8 — they apply to every chapter in the second half of the book (Ch5 Architect through Ch8 Player). These are not style preferences. They are structural requirements that the book-level template demands.

---

### Pattern A: Somatic "Try It Now" Practice

**What it is:** A concrete practice within the Journey section that the reader can run in the moment — not a prompt, not a reflection, an actual short iteration of the chapter's core loop.

**Why it applies to every chapter:** Every chapter teaches a Face. Every Face has a stage sequence. The stage sequence must be demonstrated, not just described. Telling the reader about the WAVE is different from letting them run it. Telling the reader about altitude-switching is different from letting them feel what it means to switch.

**Where it applies:**

| Chapter | Practice location | Practice type |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| Ch5 Architect | Journey section, after "The Walk" | Contradiction Codex iteration — run one contradiction you contain |
| Ch6 Diplomat | Journey section, after "The Walk" | Walk-Away Price naming — one real price you actually hold |
| Ch7 Sage | Journey section, after "The Walk" | Altitude name — name the altitude you're in right now, out loud, to yourself |
| Ch8 Player | Already in REWRITE_SPEC as Try It Now | Review → Discern → Design → Build → Pass On, sixty-second iteration |

**Implementation standard:**
- 60–120 seconds of reader time
- Must be runnable without any materials or preparation
- Must directly use the chapter's core tool or move
- Must have a somatic (body-level) component, not just cognitive
- Must be placed before the reader is asked to do extended reflection

---

### Pattern B: "What If You Don't Have X?" Honest Subsection

**What it is:** A short subsection (one page max) that directly addresses the reader who doesn't have the chapter's core superpower in them. The one who goes through the practice and finds they have no Founder move, no Panoramic Vision, no Crossroad Charter, no Contradiction Codex.

**Why it applies to every chapter:** The book teaches tools. Tools are for everyone. But not everyone who reads the book will discover they have the tool the chapter points toward. The chapter must be honest about this — and must give the reader who doesn't have the tool a way to stay in the walk without feeling like they've failed.

**Where it applies:**

| Chapter | Subsection location | Core question addressed |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Ch5 Architect | Journey section, before "The walk ends" | What if you're a builder but not a system designer? |
| Ch6 Diplomat | Journey section, before "The walk ends" | What if you don't have a walk-away price? |
| Ch7 Sage | Journey section, before "The walk ends" | What if you can't put a game down? |
| Ch8 Player | Already in REWRITE_SPEC as "What If You Don't Have a Game?" | What if you have no Founder move? |

**Key principle:** The answer is always the same structure — the walk is what you have if you don't have the specific tool. The WAVE still runs. The village still needs you. The practice continues. Do not let the reader leave empty-handed.

### Pattern C: bars-engine as Concrete Proof (Cross-Reference Pattern)

**What it is:** In Chapter 8, bars-engine is the concrete proof that the theory of self-authorship is real. In earlier chapters, the concrete proof will be different things — but every chapter needs a concrete, specific, named example of the chapter's theory working in real life.

**Why it applies to every chapter:** The book teaches abstract concepts (the 6 Faces, the WAVE, emotional alchemy) through narrative and instruction. But abstraction without concrete proof produces understanding without transformation. Every chapter needs at least one moment where the reader sees the theory working in a specific, named, real-life example.

**Where it applies:**

| Chapter | Concrete proof type | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| Ch5 Architect | bars-engine system design | The token economy as Architect practice — designing for the next person |
| Ch6 Diplomat | bars-engine field holding | Running emotional undertow detection during a session |
| Ch7 Sage | bars-engine altitude-switching | Using panoramic vision to name which game is being played mid-session |
| Ch8 Player | bars-engine itself | Already in REWRITE_SPEC — three placement expansion |

**Implementation standard:**
- Named concretely, not as "a client" or "someone I knew" — as the real thing, real iteration
- Appears at least twice in the chapter — in the Practice section (as the Founder move example) and in the Journey section (as what the walk actually looked like)
- Not as a credential — as a demonstration of the pattern the reader can extract

bars-engine named but role ambiguous → now CONFIRMED as the village common ground, post-book playground, non-profit practice ground. Six mentions in Ch8 is correct — bars-engine is the gift the book hands to the reader. Not a product. Not a credential. The common ground.

**Pattern C — Revised:** Every chapter needs bars-engine or an equivalent concrete proof — but "bars-engine" is now confirmed as the correct reference for Ch5, Ch6, Ch7, and Ch8. In earlier chapters (Ch1–Ch4), the proof will be the Origin Story or the Infinite Arcade framing. The concrete proof requirement stands; its form varies by chapter position.

## The WAVE Loop — Every Chapter Must Complete It

Every chapter must end with a Come Back moment. The WAVE is:

**Wake up → Clean up → Grow up → Show up → Come back**

The first four stages are covered across the chapter. The Come Back is the one most consistently underweighted across Ch5–Ch8.

**Come Back checklist by chapter:**

| Chapter | Come Back moment present? | Word count |
|---------|-------------------------|------------|
| Ch5 Architect | ⚠️ Needs check | ~50-100 words |
| Ch6 Diplomat | ⚠️ Needs check | ~50-100 words |
| Ch7 Sage | ⚠️ Needs check | ~50-100 words |
| Ch8 Player | ✅ In REWRITE_SPEC (#5) | ~90 words |

**Come Back structure standard:**
- One breath pause
- Recognition: *I have been through this. I know what I know.*
- Readiness statement: *I am ready to be useful.*
- Transition to reflection prompts or final line

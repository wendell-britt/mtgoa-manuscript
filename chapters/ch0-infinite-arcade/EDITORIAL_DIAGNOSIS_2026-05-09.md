## Structural Diagnosis

### What is working

- The portal/opening does the emotional job described in [CH0_UNPACKING.md](/home/workspace/manuscripts/chapters/ch0-infinite-arcade/CH0_UNPACKING.md:16): it moves the reader from skepticism toward recognition.
- The `Game Master` section gives the chapter a strong moment-of-discovery frame instead of pure concept exposition.
- The chapter has real forward motion into Chapter 1.

### What is off-spec

1. The chapter thesis in [SPEC.md](/home/workspace/manuscripts/chapters/ch0-infinite-arcade/SPEC.md:7) is token economics.
The current draft foregrounds portal/game/GM recognition first and delays the renewable vs non-renewable mechanic until much later.

2. The token section does not yet match the spec's core distinction.
[SPEC.md](/home/workspace/manuscripts/chapters/ch0-infinite-arcade/SPEC.md:31) requires renewable vs non-renewable tokens.
[CHAPTER0_DRAFT.md](/home/workspace/manuscripts/chapters/ch0-infinite-arcade/CHAPTER0_DRAFT.md:175) currently frames tokens as `emotional resources` plus `time`, which is adjacent but not the same mechanic.

3. The chapter spends too much space on pre-mechanics orientation before the reader gets the book's actual claim.
The opening promise is strong; the mechanism arrives late.

4. `Build Your Allyship Character` likely belongs after the chapter's mechanics are more firmly established, or in Chapter 1.
Right now it increases cognitive load before the core system finishes teaching.

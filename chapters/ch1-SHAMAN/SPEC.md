## Core thesis

Most allyship fails because people start in the village (performance, strategy, optics) instead of the forest (inner terrain, parts, grief, fear, and truth).

Chapter 1 reframes the work: your history is not disqualifying. It is the map. Your superpower is hidden in what you've survived and what you've learned to protect.

- **Stage name:** Threshold / Orientation
- **Native material:** Urgency, diagnosis, and entry language

- **Superpower introduced:** The reader can locate their origin story as the root of their allyship capacity

## Chapter objective

By the end of Chapter 1, the reader should:

1. Feel the insufficiency of old allyship framing (urgency lands).
2. Understand the Forest vs Village distinction.
3. Meet the 8 Gates as a real inner map (preview + Gate 1 depth).
4. Reframe their personal damage/history as potential superpower material.
5. Leave with a first concrete BARs loop.

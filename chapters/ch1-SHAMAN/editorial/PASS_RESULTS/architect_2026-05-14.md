## Source Text

"We see a problem. We respond. We post, we protest, we donate, we sign, we share, we speak up, we show up. Then we wonder why three months later the problem is still there, or worse, is bigger, or has transformed into something that our original response can't touch. We wonder why we're exhausted. Why we're burned out. Why the allyship feels like more than we can carry. And if we're honest with ourselves, we've also been the one who couldn't hold it. Who froze. Who said the wrong thing. Who meant well and made it worse."

## Transformation Arc

**Start:** behavioral catalog (post, protest, donate — public, visible, culturally endorsed)
**Cross:** exhaustion articulation → shadow confession (couldn't hold it, froze, made it worse)
**End:** self-indictment without resolution

The arc earns its landing. Moves from collective ("we see a problem") → particular ("we're exhausted") → personal ("the one who couldn't hold it"). Reader is gradually pulled inward. Transformation logic holds.

---

## Structural Breaks

1. **Stealth pronoun handover** (not a break, a flag): Sentences 3–5 start with collective "we wonder" but by sentence 5 the implied subject is second person "you've also been the one who." The text is pulling the reader from "we" to "you" without announcing it. Structurally significant — worth noting.

2. **Missing Bridge**: No somatic marker between "we're burned out" and "we've also been the one who couldn't hold it." Text moves from exhaustion state to shadow confession without naming the transition. *What did the burnout feel like in the body before the shadow showed up?* This is where the somatic signal could lock in.

## Annotations (plain language)

This block is doing the right structural work — moving the reader from "we do stuff" to "we are depleted" to "we are also the ones who fail." Transformation logic is sound.

**The one structural vulnerability:** the leap from exhaustion to shadow confession. The reader needs a somatic anchor there — what did it feel like *in the body* the moment before you couldn't hold it? Right now that beat is implied but not written. Adding one physical sensation before the shadow admission would make the whole block land harder.

---

## Suggested Changes

1. **Add somatic bridge** (between "Why the allyship feels like more than we can carry" and "And if we're honest with ourselves"): One physical sensation — e.g., "Your jaw tightens. You set the phone down and the room goes quiet and the quiet is worse." The somatic anchor before the shadow confession would ground the reader in their own body before the self-indictment lands.

2. **Flag the pronoun handover** (sentence 5): If intentional, make it explicit ("You've also been the one"). If unintentional, tighten to "We've also been the ones who couldn't hold it."

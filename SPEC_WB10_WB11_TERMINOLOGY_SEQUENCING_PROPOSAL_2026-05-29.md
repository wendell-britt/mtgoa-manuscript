## A. Diagnosis (from the live manuscript, 2026-05-29)

### A1. "Shadow" carries four jobs
Verified across chapters:

| Sense | Meaning | Loci (verified) |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** Disowned / projected material (Elliott) — **framework #2 proper** | The pattern you've disowned and project onto the person who makes your jaw tighten. What shadow *work* operates on. | Ch1 L33–35; Ch2 3-2-1 L517–529 |
| **(b)** A gate's **cost** | The downside each gate exacts when its material stays unexamined. | Ch1 L268–303 ("Shadow: permanent guardedness"), L313+ ("Protector's shadow cost") |
| **(c)** A **Face in distortion** | What a Face looks like when it runs from disowned material instead of being chosen. | Ch1 L546 ("The shadow: the Vulnerable Child in exile"); Ch3 L308 ("The shadow Challenger…"); Ch8 L40–46 ("a shadow Player…") |
| **(d)** **"The Distortion"** | Formal section name for sense (c). | Ch2 §2 title |

A reader cannot answer "what is *my* shadow?" because the word points at four things. Every later framework that "depends on recognizing Shadows" depends on an ungripped term.

### A2. "Village" splits in two
- **Exile-village (myth):** the mythic home the Face was driven out of. Dominant. Every §1 opener: Ch3 L7+, Ch4 L7+, Ch5 L7+, Ch6 L14+, Ch7 L39+, Ch8 L12+ ("Why the [Face] Left the Village").
- **Action-Village (arena):** the present-day world where allyship actually costs something — audience, roles, image. Load-bearing but under-built. Ch1 L152–178 already renders it well ("In the Village, the core move is impression management… protect your image in the Village, or grow your capacity in the Forest").

The action-Village is exactly WB-1's conversion target ("alchemized charge has to leave the Forest and cost something in the Village"). It must be built up, not just the exile-village.

### A3. "Game" and "Forest" (lower-severity drift)
- **Game:** the spine — *you're already playing, but for bullshit prizes* — is sound, but "game" also names the Carse finite/infinite taxonomy, §6's title, and the product ("design your own game," Ch8 L20). Risk: a redefinition reads as scope-creep.
- **Forest:** clean in Ch1 (L176–178, the *place* interior work happens; gates run through it). Mild drift toward "Forest" = the gate-walk itself (Ch2 §5 subtitle "A Walk Through Your Own Forest").

### A4. Dependency inversions (WB-11)
- **Ch2 §4 before §5:** WAVE/EA mechanism (§4, L143–513) and the 3-2-1 shadow method (L517) precede the 8-gate shadow walk (§5, L571+). EA is taught before the shadow material it operates on.
- **Framework #2 ungripped before EA leans on it:** the Shadow is introduced (Ch1 L35) but never elevated to a definition the reader can hold before Ch2's 3-2-1 and gate walk depend on it.
- **Orphaned spiral-color header:** Ch2 L1 "`THE SHAMAN — Magenta — Survival/Instinct`" deploys an un-taught (and WB-9-submerged) altitude label.

## B. WB-10 — Proposed canonical terminology (the decision to approve)

**One word, one referent.** Split the four "shadow" jobs into three distinct, nested terms:

| Term | Referent | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| **The Shadow** (capital S, singular) | Disowned / projected material (Elliott). The raw stuff. The answer to "what is my shadow?" — *the specific pattern I've disowned and project onto the person who makes my jaw tighten.* | sense (a) — keep as-is |
| **Distortion** / "the [Face] in distortion" | What a Face looks like when it runs **from** the Shadow instead of being chosen. | sense (c) + (d) — absorbs "shadow Player/Challenger/etc." AND reconciles with Ch2 §2 "The Distortion" |
| **Cost** / "the gate's cost" | The price a gate exacts when its material stays disowned. | sense (b) — drop "shadow" from the cost labels |

**The model nests cleanly (state it once, early):**
> Every Face has a gift. When that Face runs from disowned material — **the Shadow** — instead of being chosen, you get the Face **in distortion**. And every gate you walk has a **cost** if you pass it without meeting what it guards.

This gives the reader one gripable referent for "my Shadow," and makes Distortion and Cost *consequences* of it rather than synonyms.

### B1. "Village" — keep one word, disambiguate by case + build-up
- **lowercase "village"** = the mythic origin frame in the §1 "Why the [Face] Left the Village" openers. Leave these; they're origin myth and read as such.
- **Capital "the Village"** = the present-day arena of exterior action (Ch1 L152–178 usage). **Build it up** so WB-1's conversion beat ("cost something in the Village") has a real referent. This is the load-bearing fix; coordinate with WB-1.

### B2. "Game" — hold the spine, mark the rest as extensions
- Keep *"the game you're already playing / for bullshit prizes"* as the canonical spine (reinforces WB-5/WB-12).
- Where "game" means Carse's finite/infinite taxonomy or "design your own game," signal it as a *lens/extension* of the spine, not a redefinition. No new term needed — just don't let an extension silently overwrite the spine.

### B3. "Forest" — keep it the *place*
- Forest = the place of interior work; the gate-walk happens *in* it, isn't *it*. Light touch: ensure no passage uses "the Forest" as a bare synonym for "the gate-walk."

## C. WB-11 — Proposed dependency-sequencing fixes

### C1. Define the Shadow before EA leans on it
**Two-stage plant (decided 2026-05-29):**
1. **Ch0 one-liner** — plant the term so "the Shadow" exists before Ch1 (locus TBD in the Ch0 WB pass; natural home near the existing Faces/gate framing).
2. **Ch1 L33–35** — elevate the existing Elliott introduction to the **clean, gripable definition** of the Shadow (sense a), using the nested model in §B.

This is the earliest load-bearing definition; it must precede Ch2's 3-2-1 (L517) and the gate walk (§5).

### C2. Resolve the Ch2 §4-before-§5 inversion (recommend option b)
Two options from the parent spec:
- **(a)** Surface more of the shadow/gate encounter before the WAVE mechanism.
- **(b) [recommended]** Frame §4 explicitly as *"the tool you'll use on what you meet in §5."* §1 (Exile), §2 (Distortion), §3 (Concept) already precede §4, so the wound and the distortion are established; the order only *reads* as inverted because §4 doesn't say what it's for. Cheapest fix, no restructuring: add a forward-pointing seam at the open of §4 and a back-reference at the open of §5.

*(Recommend (b) because it's a seam, not a restructure, and the WB-6 gate-walk pass will be editing §5 anyway — cleaner to land both in one Ch2 pass.)*

### C3. Strip the orphaned spiral-color header
Remove "`— Magenta — Survival/Instinct`" from Ch2 L1 and the equivalent color labels in other chapter headers. **In-scope now:** these are orphaned labels, not the §7 developmental-handoff carriers. **Out-of-scope / WB-9:** the deeper altitude submersion (esp. Ch7) stays in WB-9 behind its dependency guard (strengthen the §7 Face-to-Face handoffs *before* cutting altitude language). This proposal only removes the header tags.

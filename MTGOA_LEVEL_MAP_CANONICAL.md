## Core principles

1. **Levels emerge from Kegan stage transitions** — the trauma of being inadequate to the next level's problems is what creates the structural artifact/wound pattern
2. **Power allergy** — each level is allergic to the power of the level below it, because it can't see its own shadow from its own frame. The allyship wound is *specific to each level's blind spot*
3. **Holographic book** — The whole book is a Sage (Teal) book. Talking about any level from within its frame activates Sage consciousness. The book could have been 6 books — but one book teaching Sage mastery over all levels is the choice made
4. **Sage meta** — Sage is the first stage that can consciously use the moves of all lower levels. It is also the stage that can see ALL levels clearly because it has been at each one and integrated it

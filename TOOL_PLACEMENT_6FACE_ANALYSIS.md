# 6-Face GM Analysis — Tool Placement: Option A vs B (+ Hybrid)
## *Where do 3-2-1, Polarity, and the inner toolkit live?*

**Created:** 2026-05-24  
**Question:** Option A (thin Ch2, fat appendix) vs Option B (Ch2 toolkit chapter) — with Wendell's pull toward **techniques introduced in Ch2, practiced one per chapter**  
**Context:** Ch2 gained ~2,034 words (3-2-1 + Polarity full drafts). Conflicts with `SPEC_BOOK_TOOL_PLACEMENT.md` and `SPEC_READER_FACING_TOOLS.md` (one Phase-2 tool per chapter).  
**Companion:** `SPEC_MANUSCRIPT_INTEGRATION.md`, `SPEC_BOOK_TOOL_PLACEMENT.md`, `SPEC_READER_FACING_TOOLS.md`

## The three positions

| Option | Ch2 job | Later chapters | Appendix/workbook |
|--------|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| **A — Thin body** | WAVE + Gates + *routing only* (name tools, don't teach) | Phase 3 references | Full 3-2-1, polarity, gate map |
| **B — Toolkit chapter** | WAVE + full 3-2-1 + full Polarity + Gates (~14k) | Face narrative only; no new tool teaches | Reference sheets only |
| **A+B Hybrid — Technique Academy** | WAVE full + **catalog** (encounter all inner tools + routing) + Gates | **One Phase-2 practice per chapter** at that Face's altitude | Full procedures + logs |

**Wendell's compelling idea:** Ch2 introduces the *family* of inner techniques; each subsequent chapter gives the reader **one sit-down practice** — not a new tool every time, but the **first real do** of a tool introduced as concept in Ch2.

This is not pure A or pure B. It is **A's placement discipline + B's Ch2 as technique hub + distributed Phase 2 across the arc**.

---

## Anchor (design intent)

The book is a **reclamation project** for the ideal reader: she already has Green relational tools but has split off Shaman felt-sense, Challenger line, Regent stewardship, Architect systems. 

**Ch2's irreducible job:** teach her that charge is real, movable, and hers — WAVE + Gates.  
**The open question:** do 3-2-1 and Polarity belong *inside* that job as full teaches, or as **named tools** whose first practice is **timed** to the chapter where she needs that capacity back?

## 🧠 Architect — Structure and integrity

**Option A — Working:** Matches existing specs. Alpha/Beta/Gamma/Delta zones stay clean. Ch2 word budget recoverable (~10–11k). Appendix earns its existence.

**Option A — Cost:** Reader may never open appendix; 3-2-1 stays conceptual for skimmers. Book-to-app flywheel weaker if full process isn't in body.

**Option B — Working:** One place to learn inner work; no hunt across chapters. Current drafts are usable.

**Option B — Breaking:** Ch2 becomes an **engine dump** before the reader has met any Face. Violates "one Phase-2 tool per chapter." Section 6 Ch2 still written as if only WAVE + Gates exist. Ch1 Forest threshold → Ch2 **manual** whiplash. Final book word budget: +2k in Ch2 with nowhere to trim without losing Gates.

**Hybrid — Working:** Preserves **spine integrity**:
- Ch2 = **Technique Academy** (WAVE full + tool *catalog* + routing table + Gates)
- Ch3–Ch8 = **Face lab** (one Beta exercise each, tool × altitude)
- Appendix = **rulebook** (full steps, edge cases, recursive 3-2-1)

**Hybrid — Structure:**

```
Ch2 INTRODUCE (Phase 1)     Ch3–Ch8 PRACTICE (Phase 2)     Appendix REFERENCE (Gamma)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAVE-Spiral        ────────► Ch2 (full — already there)
3-2-1              ────────► Ch3 Challenger (project oppressor / clean shadow)
Polarity Map       ────────► Ch4 Regent OR Ch7 Sage (see Regent/Sage below)
Token check        ────────► Ch0 (framing — already there)
BAR capture        ────────► Ch1 Forest (threshold BAR)
Dojo / domain      ────────► Ch3 or Ch5 (one paragraph + exercise)
Close with terms   ────────► Ch6 (game move — not a new tool)
Player loop        ────────► Ch8 (synthesis exercise)
```

**Architect verdict:** Reject pure B. **Hybrid wins** if Ch2 drafts compress to Phase 1 + one-page routing. Pure A only if appendix/app capture rate is proven high.

## 🏛 Regent — What to preserve

**Preserve:** Chapter arc (Ch0 orient → Ch1 threshold → Ch2 inner engine → Ch3–7 Faces → Ch8 Player). Gate walk as Ch2's **sacred center** — don't cannibalize for methodology.

**Preserve:** Revenue architecture — book complete without workbook; workbook for repetition.

**Option A:** Protects Regent's **stewardship of reader attention**. Doesn't waste the one heavy inner chapter on duplicate teaching.

**Option B:** Risks **tradition-as-cage in reverse** — treating every TEAL tool as equally mandatory in Ch2 because it was in the source corpus. That's archive stewardship, not reader stewardship.

**Hybrid:** Regent-friendly. **Introduce in Ch2, steward practice across the journey** — like a dojo curriculum: white belt meets all forms on the wall; each month one kata deepens. Honors "you can't change the village if you won't enter the forest" (Ch1) before "here's how charge moves" (Ch2) before "here's how Red shows up" (Ch3).

**Regent verdict:** Hybrid. Compress Ch2 full teaches; **assign** first practices to chapters where the Face owns the failure mode.

## ⚔️ Challenger — What gets wrong

**Option A shadow:** Never makes the reader **do** 3-2-1 in the book — she stays comfortable in concept. Challenger says: *if it matters, put a line in the sand.*

**Option B shadow:** **Overwhelms before the first fight.** Reader hits Ch3 Challenger already exhausted from Ch2 homework. She skips to Diplomat (her home Face) without inner tools embodied.

**Hybrid — Challenger's clean move:**

| Chapter | Face | First practice | Why here |
|---------|------|----------------|----------|
| Ch3 | Challenger | **3-2-1** on oppressor/villain projection | Reclaims Red/"no" energy she demonized |
| Ch6 | Diplomat | **Close with Honest Terms** (game move) | Native negotiation — not imported Price-Namer |

Challenger ** rejects** Polarity first practice in Ch3 — polarity is "both sides legitimate"; Challenger chapter needs **ownership**, not balance. 3-2-1 first.

**Challenger verdict:** Hybrid with **3-2-1 Phase 2 in Ch3**, not Ch2. Cut full 3-2-1 teach from Ch2 to ~1 encounter paragraph + forward pointer.

## 🎭 Diplomat — What bridges

**Option A:** Keeps Green reader from feeling **lectured at** in Ch2 — she hates hierarchy and teaching mode. Thin Ch2 + distributed practice = inclusion across the arc.

**Option B:** Feels like **one chapter doing all the emotional labor** while Face chapters stay performatively accessible. Uneven hospitality.

**Hybrid — Diplomat's bridge pattern:**

Ch2 says: *Here are the inner tools. You don't have to master them today. You'll practice each where it belongs.*

That sentence is **Green-native** — no rush to closure, but also not endless process (Ch6 shadow). Each later chapter: *Now you've met the Challenger — let's 3-2-1 the figure you've been calling oppressor.*

**Polarity placement (Diplomat input):** First full polarity practice fits **Ch6** (integrative negotiation IS polarity management in the field) OR **Ch4** (Regent: tradition ↔ renewal). Diplomat prefers **Ch6** — polarity pair *care ↔ impact* already drafted; practice = close with terms exercise, not a second tool.

**Diplomat verdict:** Hybrid. Ch2 = **encounter + routing**. Polarity Phase 2 → **Ch6** (compress current Ch2 polarity section to concept + pointer). Avoid polarity + 3-2-1 both full in Ch2.

## 🌊 Shaman — Felt sense

**Option A somatic:** Relief — shorter Ch2, faster reach to Gates walk (the real inner journey). Risk: **unfinished** — reader names shadow in appendix she never opens.

**Option B somatic:** **Density fatigue** before Gate 1. Body bracing at "another exercise." Gates walk lands as homework stacked on homework.

**Hybrid somatic:** Ch2 feels like **orientation in the medicine lodge** — smell all the herbs; don't drink every tincture today. Ch3–8 each give **one tasted dose** tied to a Face. Body learns tool × situation, not tool × tool × tool.

**What to keep from current drafts:** The **routing prose** (3-2-1 / Polarity / WAVE questions) is high-signal — keep in Ch2 even if full teaches move out.

**Shaman verdict:** Hybrid. Gates walk stays the Ch2 **peak experience**; demote 3-2-1 and Polarity from peak to **named medicines on the shelf**.

## 📖 Sage — Panoramic view

**The book operates on three layers:**

1. **Economy** (Ch0) — tokens, tickets, GM  
2. **Engine** (Ch2) — WAVE, shadow, polarity, gates  
3. **Faces** (Ch3–7) — altitude-specific play  
4. **Meta** (Ch7–8) — which game, stay in game  

**Option A:** Clean layers; engine layer thin in body — Sage sees appendix dependency.

**Option B:** Engine layer **bloated**; Face layers repeat less because reader is already "trained." Sage sees **premature Teal** — teaching all inner moves before reader has failed at any Face.

**Hybrid — Sage panoramic:** Ch2 is the **map room** (see all techniques). Ch3–8 is **terrain** (one technique activated per biome). Ch7 Sage can Phase-3 reference all tools without re-teaching. Matches **six Faces as reclamation** — each chapter returns one split-off capacity WITH one inner tool practiced at that altitude.

**Sage meta-rule:** Techniques are **orthogonal** to Faces. WAVE is Shaman-native but **used** everywhere. 3-2-1 is shadow work (Shaman domain) but **first practiced** when Challenger returns projection. Polarity is structural (Architect/Sage domain) but **first practiced** when Diplomat closes terms.

**Sage verdict:** Hybrid is the **developmental** option — tools introduced early, **integrated** across altitude climb.

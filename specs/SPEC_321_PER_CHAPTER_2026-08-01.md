## 4 · Block shapes

**The Figure Pass** — close of Section 4:

```
### 3-2-1 — [Face-specific title]

[2 sentences: the territory, and why this Face's charge lands on that figure.]
Solo, written or spoken. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes.

**Try this now.**

Pick the figure. [One sentence naming the territory, not the person.]

**3 · FACE IT.** Third person. Describe them like a journalist: body, voice, the thing
that tightens your jaw. Two short paragraphs.

**2 · TALK TO IT.** Second person. Three exchanges minimum. Ask what it is protecting.
Let it answer, and do not polite the dialogue.

**1 · OWN IT.** First person. Speak as the part, one paragraph. No *"a part of me."*

**RECEIPT.** Thirty seconds. Did the charge move?

*Two minutes to capture it as a BAR.*
```

**The Daemon Pass** — close of Section 5:

```
### 3-2-1 on the [Daemon]

You have just read it in third person, which is Step 3 already done. Five to ten minutes
for the other two.

**2 · TALK TO IT.** [One Face-specific opening question.] Let it answer.

**1 · OWN IT.** Speak as it, one paragraph, in its own logic rather than your apology for it.

**RECEIPT.** Thirty seconds. Did it loosen, or did it dig in? Both are information.

*Two minutes to capture it as a BAR.*
```

**The rhythm risk is real and is the reason for the variance rule below.** Twelve blocks on one
template is the robotic-rhythm defect at book scale, which `instruments/review.py --mode voice`
and `/no-ai-slop` both score. **Rule: the step labels and the receipt stay fixed; the framing
sentences and the Step 2 opening question are written fresh per chapter, in that chapter's
register.** Fixed scaffolding is apparatus and is allowed to repeat. Prose is not.

## 6 · The safety correction — Wendell's ruling against the current text

Two shipping surfaces currently tell the reader the opposite of ruling 2:

| where | text |
|---|---|
| `ch4:470` | *"this exercise is for **alliance-work projection**, not emergency processing"* |
| `APPENDIX_E`, Safety / Scope | *"This process is for **alliance-work projection**… If the charge is trauma-level, pause and seek appropriate support."* |

Wendell 2026-08-01: **"the process IS for emotional processing."**

**The correction is not to delete the safety language.** The book already has the right framing,
on the copyright page, and neither of the two sites above matches it:

> *"The shadow work in Chapters 2 through 4 can surface material that wants a trained person in
> the chair with you. If it does, **that is the practice working**, and getting help is the next
> move rather than a failure of it."*

That treats surfacing as the process succeeding rather than as a contraindication, which is
exactly ruling 2, and it keeps the referral intact. **Both sites re-align to it.** Stated once,
in full, at the ch3 teaching; carried by Appendix E; not repeated in twelve blocks.

**And the copyright page needs one edit of its own:** *"Chapters 2 through 4"* becomes
**"Chapters 2 through 8"** once 3-2-1 runs the length of the book. Missing this would leave the
book's only medical disclaimer scoped to a third of the shadow work it now contains.

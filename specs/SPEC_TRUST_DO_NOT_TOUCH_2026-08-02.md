# SPEC — Where the trust framework is deliberately not going

**2026-08-02.** Written first, on the Sage's ruling from
`PANEL_TRUST_OPEN_QUESTIONS_6FACE_2026-08-02.md`:

> *"A foundation poured late is the most dangerous kind of good idea, because it makes
> everything look explicable and tempts a whole-book rewrite eight weeks from
> production. The book does not need the framework everywhere it applies. It needs it
> in the two places Jordan gets stuck. Write down the list of places it is deliberately
> not going — that list is the deliverable that keeps this from eating August."*

**This document is the constraint, not the plan.** It exists before any prose, and a
change to it is a decision Wendell makes on purpose rather than one that happens while
drafting.

---

## The blast radius, measured

Sites in the manuscript the framework could plausibly touch, if unbounded:

| | sites |
|---|---|
| daemon blocks (`Job` / `As an ally` / `As a demon`) | 104 |
| *distortion* | 85 |
| *trust* | 53 |
| the ten myths and their restatements | 17 |
| *being good* · *a good person* · *good ally* | 14 |
| **total** | **273** |

**Every one of those is somewhere the framework genuinely applies.** That is the danger.
It is not a case of the idea being over-extended by carelessness; it is over-extendable
by being correct.

**The budget below is 3 sites.** Everything else on this page is out of scope by
decision.

## OUT OF SCOPE — and why each one is refused

### 1 · The definition at `ch1:91`

> *"Allyship is the practice of increasing another person's well-being while protecting
> the conditions that allow both of you to remain full players in the game. Nothing in
> it asks whether you are good."*

**Not one word changes. Not clarified, not annotated, not footnoted.**

The exploration found this sentence is already capability-plus-integrity with benevolence
excised. **That makes it the proof text, and a proof text you edit after building the
theory on it is worthless.** The strongest evidence the framework is native to this book
is that the definition was written before the framework existed and fits it exactly.
Touching it destroys the evidence.

**PROTECTED.** Add to `MANUSCRIPT_FILE_CANON` standing rules.

### 2 · `ch2:263`, the Controller

> *"As an ally: it maintains your conceptual sense of integrity, keeping your actions in
> alignment with your values."*

Same reason. Canon assigned integrity to a daemon before anybody was looking for it.
**PROTECTED.**

### 3 · `ch1:119`, the counter marked applause

The benevolence counter, written as an image. It does not get relabelled, and the word
*benevolence* does not go anywhere near it. **PROTECTED.**

### 4 · The other 103 daemon blocks in `ch2`

The daemon template *is* the trust template — Job is capability, ally/demon is the
benevolence definition, integrity is unstated because it is total. **That is a beautiful
finding and it goes in the ch2 shadow-trust section as one observation, not as 104
edits.**

Refused because: the daemons already work. A reader who meets seven daemons annotated
with three dimensions each is doing 21 units of bookkeeping in a chapter whose job is to
make her feel something.

### 5 · The 85 distortion passages in `ch3`–`ch8`

The framework explains every one of them — a distortion is a Face's benevolence
definition running with no other definition in the room. **ch8 alone has 24.**

Refused because: this is the single largest rewrite temptation in the book and the payoff
is explanatory rather than practical. The distortions are already the most concrete
material in those chapters. Explaining *why* they feel virtuous adds understanding to
passages that are currently producing recognition, which is a downgrade.

**If it goes anywhere later, it goes in one place — the Sage chapter, once, as the thing
the Sage can see that the other five cannot.** Not before the second edition.

### 6 · The six Faces' benevolence definitions

**The one genuine disagreement on the panel.** The Regent calls it the cheapest
high-value item; the Diplomat calls it a promise six chapters then have to keep.

**Out of scope for this pass, and the reason is the Diplomat's.** A stated definition is
a standard the chapter can then be measured against, including where it fails. Six new
standards, eight weeks out, with `rescan.py` already carrying 116 actionable findings, is
how a book misses a date.

**This is the first item to reconsider** if the three in-scope pieces land and there is
room. It is not refused on merit.

### 7 · `ch9`

Zero uses of *trust* in the closing chapter. Adding it would mean introducing a
framework in a chapter whose job is to send Jordan out with a deck and one move.

### 8 · The instruments

**No trust counter, no benevolence counter, no dimension tagger.** Already stated in
`SPEC_TRUST_THE_THIRD_CURRENCY` §7 and repeated here because the pull will be strong:
this session has built five instruments in two days, and this is a content model, not a
measurable defect. **A counter here would measure vocabulary and call it structure.**

### 9 · The 43 existing *trust* mentions

Most are ordinary English. Three in ch7 matter and one of them gets a sentence. The other
forty are not a sweep.

### 10 · The Myths Read diagnostic

Scoring by dimension is a product decision with a book consequence, and the product is
outside this branch. **Noted, not scoped.**

### 11 · The marginalia

Six heads with distinct voices, compiled by `marginalia/compile.py`, scored on a separate
surface. **The framework does not enter the margin in this pass.** The annotator is a
character, not an explainer, and a margin note that teaches the model would break DL-6.

### 12 · The author's note

`front_matter/authors_note.md:35` already carries the rule the framework restates. It
does not need the vocabulary, and the front matter is already carrying six open notes
(`N01`–`N07`).

## The two lines that decide whether this stayed in scope

**A reader should be able to finish the book having never heard the words *capability*,
*integrity* and *benevolence* used as a triad more than twice.** If a draft uses them a
third time, the framework has started colonising.

**Nothing already written gets rewritten to fit.** The framework earns its place by
explaining what is there. The moment it starts editing what is there, it has stopped
being a foundation and become a renovation.

---

## What would legitimately reopen this list

1. **Wendell rules Q5 in** — the six Face definitions. That is a scope increase he takes
   knowingly, and it is the only item on the refused list with a real case for it.
2. **The ch1 section will not close without the ch7 material moving.** If drafting shows
   the minimum is incoherent without ch7 changing more than one sentence, that is
   evidence about the model, not an excuse to expand.
3. **A reader test fails on the third currency specifically.** Not on the framework being
   incomplete — on the 300 words not working.

Anything else is the foundation asking to become a renovation.

# SCAN — What it takes to hold the tower-defense frame across the book

**2026-08-02. Wendell:** *"Can we do a book scan and see what we would need to do to hold
this frame across the book. I think it should only take a couple of lines in each chapter
to bring this into awareness. The biggest impacts should be chapter 2. The parallel
structure between the inner work the ally has to do and the obstacles that live within the
people they want to help. This makes a clean loop."*

Scanned. **His estimate is right and the reason is better than either of us assumed: the
rack already exists, it has exactly seven slots, and it is already half-built.**

---

## 1 · The finding — one daemon per chapter, already

Every chapter has a **Section 5** and in seven consecutive chapters it is a named daemon,
met *"up close"*:

| Chapter | Section 5 | Line |
|---|---|---|
| ch2 · The Forest | **The Protector, up close** | `ch2:292` |
| ch3 · The Shaman | **The Controller, Up Close** | `ch3:639` |
| ch4 · The Challenger | **The Skeptic, Up Close** | `ch4:529` |
| ch5 · The Regent | **The Fixer-Healer, Up Close** | `ch5:487` |
| ch6 · The Architect | **The Emotional Body, Up Close** | `ch6:331` |
| ch7 · The Diplomat | **The Victim, Up Close** | `ch7:487` |
| ch8 · The Sage | **The Damaged Self, Up Close** | `ch8:492` |

**Seven daemons, seven chapters, one per chapter, in a section that already exists and is
already named.** The book walks Jordan through her own defenses one tower at a time and has
never once turned the camera around.

**So the integration is not an insertion. It is a mirror added to a section that is already
there.** Each *Up Close* meets that daemon **in Jordan**. The addition is the same daemon
**standing in front of the person she is trying to help** — what it guards, what trips it,
what it lets through.

That is Wendell's clean loop, and the structure was waiting for it.

## 2 · What canon already contains, unlabelled

Fourteen sites in the whole manuscript touch the other person's refusal. **Four of them are
beats of the doom loop, written in different chapters, none aware of the others.**

| Site | What it is | Loop step |
|---|---|---|
| `ch2:274` — the Fixer as demon, *"it takes over, fixing people who never asked to be fixed"* | **the consent violation, already named in canon**, and named as a daemon defect | step 2–4 |
| `ch1:87` — *"It broke the day someone I had been 'helping' told me, plainly, that my help had been about me the whole time"* | Wendell hitting a tower, from the receiving end of a refusal | step 4 |
| `ch6:99` — *"the village had stopped asking for maps. The village had started working around the Architect"* | **clearance dropping after repeated overreach.** Written a year ago, unnamed | step 5 |
| `ch1:52` — *"The ones who refuse to be carved become the enemy"* | the conventional response to a refusal: reclassify the person as the problem | the loop, refused |

**`ch1:52` is the most valuable of the four**, because it is the frame's exact inverse
already on the page. When somebody's defense holds, conventional allyship promotes them to
adversary. The tower frame says the defense was doing its job.

**Fourteen sites across 80,000 words is thin.** The theme is present as a thread and never
as a structure, which is why the frame reads as new rather than as a retrofit.

## 3 · The footprint, chapter by chapter

### ch1 · The Infinite Arcade — **1–2 sentences, and the setup already exists**

The bouncer paragraphs applied today already imply a perimeter and already telegraph
forward. What is missing is the two-games line: **she is playing an arcade and they are
playing something else.** It belongs beside the existing telegraph, not as a new section.

### ch2 · The Forest — **the big one, and it is where the loop closes**

Three things, and only the first is new prose of any size.

1. **The mirror, at `ch2:292`.** The Protector *up close* becomes the Protector up close
   **twice**: hers, then theirs. This is the pattern every later chapter copies, so it is
   written once here properly and cheaply everywhere after.
2. **The doom loop**, four or five sentences, after the joystick material at `ch2:322`. Her
   daemon defaults, the default is her Face's distortion, the move lands above clearance,
   their tower fires, access drops, she reads it as a verdict. **This is the chapter's
   engine and it is what the section I drafted should have been.**
3. **`ch2:274` gets one clause** connecting the Fixer's *fixing people who never asked* to
   the thing on the other side that is about to fire.

**And `ch2:308` is the payoff.** *"You do not fight the Protector, and you do not shove
past it. You thank it, and you help it tell a real threat from a social one."* One sentence
saying that is also the move you make on somebody else's Protector, and the chapter's
justification is complete.

### ch3–ch8 · **two lines each, in Section 5**

Each chapter's *Up Close* gains the mirror. Concretely:

| Chapter | Their version of that daemon |
|---|---|
| ch3 · Controller | guards their standard. Trips when you move their bar. Opens when you meet the standard they already hold |
| ch4 · Skeptic | guards against being fooled again. Trips on confidence. Opens on what you admit you do not know |
| ch5 · Fixer-Healer | guards what they are holding in place on purpose. Trips when you try to repair the thing they chose to keep |
| ch6 · Emotional Body | guards against feeling it in front of somebody. Trips on being read out loud. Opens on the feeling being allowed to stay theirs |
| ch7 · Victim | guards the story and its returns. **The hardest one, because this is where the goodies live** |
| ch8 · Damaged Self | guards the weight it already carries. Trips on being asked to explain it |

**ch7 is the one that carries the deep shadow.** The Victim daemon guarding the return on
staying defended is where *"they will have to give up the goodies"* lands, and ch7 already
has `ch7:487` waiting for it.

### ch9 · The Player — **one line**

The whole chapter is the consent moment. *One person, not an audience* (`ch9:526`) is the
smallest ask of somebody's perimeter, and it needs saying once in those terms.

### `APPENDIX_A` — **optional, and it completes the appendix**

Daemons × domains × clearance is the move space. The appendix already crosses domains
against the six Faces. This is the third axis, and it is where a grid may live if one is

## 5 · Why the loop closes, stated once

The parallel Wendell named, in the book's own furniture:

- **Sections 1–4 of every chapter** are Jordan's inner work on one Face.
- **Section 5 of every chapter** is Jordan's inner work on one daemon.
- **The mirror** makes Section 5 also the map of what she will meet in somebody else.
- **`ch2:308`** is the technique, and it is the same technique on both sides.

So the answer to *why does a book about helping other people spend seven chapters inside
the reader* becomes structural rather than argued: **the seven towers she is learning to
work with in herself are the seven she will meet in everyone she ever tries to help.**

## 6 · What this scan does not settle

1. **The term.** `clearance` is still the recommendation and still unruled. Nothing in
   §3 can be drafted in final form until it is set, because the word appears in most of the
   mirror lines.
2. **Whether ch3–ch8 mirrors are two lines or a short block.** Two lines is the estimate;
   ch7's Victim may need more, because the goodies claim is the hardest thing in the book
   to say without contempt.
3. **Whether `ch1:52` gets amended — RESOLVED 2026-08-03: no.** The clause was drafted
   and cut. It asserted that a part of them held, that holding is its one job, and that
   it predates the reader — three claims that all depend on the daemons, which arrive in
   ch2. At line 52 the chapter has not opened an account for any of them. What the line
   did get is a fix the scan never asked for: *refuse to be carved* was passive with its
   doer one paragraph away at `ch1:50`, so it now reads **the ones who will not let you
   carve them become your enemy.**

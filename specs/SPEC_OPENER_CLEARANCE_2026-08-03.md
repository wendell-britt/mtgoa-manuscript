## 3 · The ruling, and it reverses the recommendation made an hour earlier

I recommended **measurement into testimony**. **The book's own frame says that is
backwards.** The Protector works the door; the Controller and Skeptic stand behind it. A
number does not clear a door. It gets scored *after* you are through one.

> **Testimony → measurement → admission.**
>
> 1. **Testimony pays the Protector.** You fail first, so she is not the subject yet.
> 2. **The measurement pays the Controller.** Exactness, sourced, checkable.
> 3. **The admission pays the Skeptic.** One sentence on what this book does not know.

**The third beat is missing from all six openers drafted today.** Every one was
uniformly confident, which is the single thing her auditor refuses as evidence — and the
sentence saying so was written into ch4 the same afternoon and never applied to page
one.

## 4 · The 2026 board, and the finding is counterintuitive

Researched 2026-08-03. **The scoreboard turned off and the work did not.**

- **Only ~5% of companies with DEI programs have actually eliminated them.**
- **Public DEI disclosure fell ~65% among the Fortune 500 in 2026.**
- Departments renamed into **People & Culture** and **Employee Experience**. The label
  stripped, the practice embedded. The trade press calls it **"quiet DEI."**
- **1 in 7** companies that rolled back has since called it a mistake; **~20%** are
  quietly reintroducing what they removed.
- Chief diversity officer roles eliminated or never refilled, and the people who held
  them cannot find equivalent work.
- Employee sentiment described as **fatigue plus clarity**: less receptive to symbolic
  gestures, trust contingent on consistency rather than statements.

### Why this is the opener rather than a fact in it

**It is the book's thesis arriving as news.** `authors_note:9` already says some people
*"understand that visible allyship converts into standing, and spend accordingly."* The
whole ch1 economy — tickets, the counter marked applause, the prize counter — describes
a game with a public scoreboard.

**In 2026 that scoreboard went dark while the machines kept running.** The people
playing for the counter have nothing left to collect. The people doing the work still
have the work. That is the Token/Ticket distinction, confirmed by events, without the
book having to argue for it.

### And it pays all three daemons at once

| | why |
|---|---|
| **Protector** | the failure is located in the institution. Nobody is accusing her of anything |
| **Controller** | **5% against 65%** is a precise and load-bearing distinction. She would catch it if it were wrong, and she will respect that it is right |
| **Skeptic** | it **contradicts** the story she has been told. *DEI is dead* is the received account and the numbers say the labels died. Not a repackage |

## 6 · Open

1. **Does the 2026 board go in the opener or the author's note?** It is topical, and
   topical ages. The author's note already carries the diagnosis and might hold it
   better.
2. **What is the admission?** One sentence on what this book does not know. It has to be
   true and specific, and it cannot be false modesty, which the Skeptic scores as
   confidence wearing a hat.

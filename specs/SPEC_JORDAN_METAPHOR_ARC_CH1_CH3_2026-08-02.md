# SPEC — Jordan's metaphorical development, Chapters 1–3

**Status:** Proposed editorial integration; no chapter prose changed by this document.

**Source of truth:** `manuscript/ch1.md`, `manuscript/ch2.md`, and
`manuscript/ch3.md` on `master` at `b2ceaf8255ce74ebd89a4741dfe36301c61cae70`.

**Purpose:** Give future editorial environments a shared, reader-facing test for
the opening third of *Mastering the Game of Allyship*. This is a Lakoff-and-Johnson
conceptual-metaphor pass, applied to Jordan: a composite ideal reader who has done
personal-development work, fears causing harm she cannot see, and wants to show up
without disappearing.

This spec is a proposal until Wendell approves specific prose. Do not insert any
draft language below into `manuscript/` without that approval and the normal
chapter-edit protocol.

---

## 1 · The reader transformation to protect

Jordan does not become a finished ally, a Game Master who controls the field, or
an expert in other people's experience. She gains a repertoire for choosing a
next move and receiving correction.

| Chapter | Jordan arrives inside | The chapter gives her | She leaves able to say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ch1 — Infinite Arcade | Allyship is a courtroom, debt, and performance. | Game, player, fuel, return. | “I can choose a move without making its outcome my moral verdict.” |
| Ch2 — Forest | My failure proves something is wrong with me. | Forest, joystick, pattern, protector. | “A protective pattern is steering me; I can notice it without becoming it.” |
| Ch3 — Shaman | Feeling will derail me or make me harmful. | WAVE, signal, channel, read. | “I can let a feeling inform one truthful action without treating it as certainty.” |

The cumulative arc is:

`COURTROOM / DEBT → GAME / PRACTICE → FOREST / PATTERN → WAVE / RESPONSIVE ACTION`

All proposed work must preserve the order without turning it into a prerequisite
ladder. Jordan acts before she is finished, receives feedback before she is
certain, and returns to earlier moves as needed.

## 2 · Whole-arc rule: metaphors are tools, not total descriptions

Add one compact reader-facing “metaphor contract” in Chapter 1, after the book has
introduced the game frame and before it relies on it heavily. The final prose need
not use this wording, but it must carry these commitments:

> Every frame in this book reveals something and leaves something out. Game language
> can clarify agency while hiding unequal stakes. Forest language can reveal a
> pattern while hiding public responsibility. WAVE can make room for feeling without
> telling you with certainty what another person needs. When a frame makes you less
> curious, less accountable, or less able to hear correction, change frames.

**Acceptance test:** A reader can identify one benefit and one limit of Game,
Forest, and WAVE language before Chapter 4.

## 3 · Chapter 1 — The Infinite Arcade

### Preserve

- The diagnosis of `ALLYSHIP IS A COURTROOM`, `ALLYSHIP IS DEBT`, and
  `ALLYSHIP IS PERFORMANCE`.
- The formal definition of allyship as increasing another person's well-being while
  preserving conditions in which both remain full players.
- The anti-savior constraint: select one person, community, or cause and complete
  a process rather than beginning one everywhere.

### Gaps and editorial requirements

#### [METAPHOR_OVERREACH] Game Master must not imply control of other people

The opening grants Jordan too much causal responsibility before it distinguishes
her choices from other people's agency, history, and consent.

**Required function:** Qualify “take the seat” as responsibility for her next move,
the conditions she can help shape, and her limits—not authority over the field.

**Candidate direction:**

> You do not control the people in front of you, the history they carry, or whether
> they trust you. Taking the seat means becoming responsible for the move you choose,
> the condition you can help shape, and the limit you are willing to name.

#### [METAPHOR_SHADOW] Fuel and alchemy must not instrumentalize feeling

Token, ticket, fuel, tank, ore, mint, and “burn it clean” make exhaustion legible.
They can also teach Jordan that every difficult emotion must become productive
capacity immediately.

**Required function:** State that rest, grief, repair, asking for help, and choosing
not to act can be clean uses of emotional information.

#### [METAPHOR_COLLISION] The book teaches a process, not rules for others

The chapter says a game gives rules while also exposing inherited rules as the
problem. Make the distinction explicit: MTGOA supplies a process for checking the
game, invitation, stakes, and next move; it does not supply a universal rulebook
for people Jordan wants to help.

**Exit condition:** Jordan can state: “I can choose a move without owning the
outcome or defining another person’s needs.”

## 4 · Chapter 2 — The Forest

### Preserve

- Forest as curiosity about recurring pattern, not an indictment of character.
- Joystick as a concrete agency image.
- The Seven as protective patterns, not identities or defects.
- The first BAR and out-loud move; Jordan needs practice rather than diagnosis.

### Gaps and editorial requirements

#### [METAPHOR_COLLISION] Forest and Village need reciprocal roles

Forest can read as the authentic site of work and Village as merely performance.
That invites Jordan’s existing tendency to postpone relational responsibility until
she feels ready.

**Required function:** Establish that the Forest identifies a pattern and the
Village tests it through consequences, consent, and other people’s correction.

**Candidate direction:**

> The Forest is not where you become pure before you return to the Village. It is
> where you notice what you already carry into the Village. Reflection without return
> becomes hiding; action without reflection becomes repetition.

#### [RECOGNITION_GAP] Joystick language needs partial agency

Jordan will not take the joystick once and retain control permanently.

**Required function:** Normalize later noticing and after-the-fact repair as valid
moves. The test is noticing sooner than last time, not mastery.

#### [TRANSFER_GAP] A private read is a hypothesis, not a diagnosis of others

**Required function:** Before the first out-loud move, state that a reader’s
internal pattern can explain her activation but cannot prove another person’s
intention. Offer the read as a question, request, or disclosure.

**Exit condition:** Jordan can state: “I can notice protection without making my
inner story the explanation of someone else.”

## 5 · Chapter 3 — The Shaman

### Preserve

- The five-stage WAVE: Wake → Open Up → Clean → Grow → Show.
- The distinction between a raw signal and the story built over it.
- The practical criterion: one true sentence to a person who can hear it while the
  matter remains live.
- The distinction between Shaman truth-telling and Challenger boundary-setting.

### Gaps and editorial requirements

#### [METAPHOR_SHADOW] Clean is discernment, not certainty

Jordan may turn WAVE into a self-management protocol she must complete correctly
before speaking. In particular, “Clean” can sound like certainty.

**Required function:** Define Clean as distinguishing an observation from the story
it appears to support. The reader may still be wrong and must make a response that
can receive correction.

#### [METAPHOR_OVERREACH] Feelings are information for inquiry, not messages with fixed meanings

Phrases that say feelings “deliver a message” risk treating feelings as infallible
authorities.

**Required function:** Preserve somatic intelligence while framing a feeling as
something to investigate, not obey.

#### [ETHICAL_GAP] Naming a field needs consent and position checks

“Name the channel out loud” gives Jordan a powerful move; it can become an
unauthorized diagnosis in unequal-power settings.

**Required pre-move check:**

1. Is this mine to name, or am I guessing about someone else?
2. Am I opening inquiry, or claiming authority?
3. Is naming it aloud safe enough for those with the most at stake?

**Required phrasing norm:** First-person, corrigible offers such as “I’m noticing
fear here; I may be wrong, but I want to check.”

**Exit condition:** Jordan can state: “I can offer a true read humbly, act before
certainty, and let the people affected correct it.”

## 7 · Completion criteria

This opening-third pass is complete only when:

- Game, Forest, and WAVE each have a stated limit as well as a stated benefit.
- Jordan receives a move that preserves other people’s agency in every chapter.
- Ch2 makes return to the Village necessary, not optional.
- Ch3 distinguishes embodied noticing, interpretation, and corrigible action.
- No prose changes are made without Wendell approving the exact before/after text.
- The Chapter 3 handoff leaves Jordan ready for the Challenger because she can
  distinguish a true read from a boundary claim.

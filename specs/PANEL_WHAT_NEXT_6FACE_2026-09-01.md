## SHAMAN · the Body — *What to Do With What You Feel*

**Read the field before the plan. What is actually happening in the author's body right now?**

Something close to the feeling the book was written about. **Four obligations open, a
three-year overrun, and a week spent on keyword fields and a footer.** That is not
procrastination and it should not be diagnosed as any. It is what a person does when the debt
is too big to face in one movement, and one small tractable job is the only place the hands
will go.

**On Q1 — the Shaman's answer is the one nobody else will give.** What ships next should be
**whatever closes a loop with a person**, rather than whatever is furthest along.
Shipping the PDF is satisfying and it moves nobody from *waiting* to *held*. **Update #31 is
still unsent.** It has been drafted since 2026-08-22 and it costs nothing.

**On Q4 — the workbook is the one carrying shame, which is why it keeps not starting.** Sixteen
people were promised something that has never had a first line written. **Every other item on
the list is late; this one is absent**, and those feel different from the inside. The
Shaman's ruling is that **absent has to become late before anything else moves** — one page,
this week, so it exists and can be behind schedule like everything else.

**Shaman's ruling:** Q1 — send #31. Q4 — write one page of the workbook so it stops being an
absence.

## CHALLENGER · the Line — *Who Pays When Nobody Says No*

**Somebody has to say the unwelcome part out loud, so: the last two weeks produced no
fulfilment.**

A KDP description, seven keyword fields, a category ruling, an ISBN decision, a proof
checklist, a PDF footer, two blocks, a research spec and three instruments. **All of it real,
none of it a book in a backer's hands.** The instruments are good work and they are also the
most comfortable place to spend a week when 247 people are owed a printed object.

**On Q2 — the money question is the only one that matters and it got answered sideways.**
`OPSBACKLOG` TRACK B says three or four speaking engagements pays for the print run. **Four
outreach targets have been named since 2026-08-11 — ACCT, WRMC, Oregon Outdoor School, Kansas
Leadership Center — and `OUTREACH_PACK` says *send the four this week*.** That was three weeks
ago.

**So the Challenger's line is: no more instruments until the four sends go out.** Not because
the instruments are wrong. Because **the $6,000 does not arrive from prose quality**, and every
day the sends do not happen is a day the 247 do not move.

**On Q5 — no.** *Allyship at Work* has a good case and it is a second book proposed by a person
with four open promises on the first. **The correction on 2026-08-27 was right that starting is
invisible and it did not make the timing free.** Draft it in public, in the episodes, at zero
cost — and do not let it become the place the attention goes.

**Challenger's ruling:** Q2 — send the four this week, before anything else. Q5 — public
drafting only, no manuscript hours.

## REGENT · the Inheritance — *What You Inherited, and What You Do With It*

**What was inherited here is a promise made in 2023 by somebody with less information.**

The Kickstarter sold a book, a course, an audiobook and a workbook. **The person who sold them
did not know the book would take three more years**, and the person holding the debt now is
being asked to honour a bundle designed by an earlier self who was wrong about the timeline.

**On Q4 — the Regent is the Face that will say the workbook may not be owed in its promised
form.** Sixteen people bought a workbook from a project that has since discovered its
workbook is *"just the book's dimensions"*. **The inheritance is the obligation, not the
artifact.** Sixteen people can be asked what would actually serve them — the course, a
print-your-own set of the 120 prompts, a refund — and any of those closes the loop honestly.
**Deciding for them is what produced the three-year silence in the first place.**

**On Q6 — the routes are inherited too.** `masteringallyship.com` exists and its root already
points somewhere. **The funnel written on 2026-09-01 assumes a site that does not exist yet**,
and the PDF now carries `/book` on 373 pages. That is a promise printed 373 times against a
404.

**Regent's ruling:** Q4 — ask the 16 rather than build for them. Q6 — `/book` must resolve
**before the PDF ships**, and it can resolve to a single page with three links.

## ARCHITECT · the Design — *Fixing the Condition Instead of the Person*

**The condition, stated plainly: this project has four products and one person, and no
mechanism that makes progress on one produce progress on another.**

Every item on the list is independently expensive. **The design question is which single build
discharges the most obligation**, and it has an answer.

**On Q1 and Q6 together — the course is the load-bearing artifact.** It is already being built.
It closes **53** directly. It is what `/book` offers the PDF reader. It is what a purchase
unlocks. It is the campaign template that makes *Allyship at Work* a translation rather than a
new book. **Nothing else on the list sits upstream of four other items.**

**On Q3 — PDF 2.0 waits, and the reason is structural rather than a matter of priority.** The
two blocks change the page count, and the proof was ordered against 387 pages. **Building the
interior fork before the proof comes back means the proof is testing a build that no longer
exists.** Finish the footer work — it is done — and hold the blocks until the proof is read.

**On Q2 — the Architect will not accept *sell more books* as the funding answer.** At the
paperback margin, $6,000 is roughly 480 copies through Amazon. **Three speaking engagements is
the same money and it is one email each**, which is why TRACK B exists and why the Challenger
is right that it is overdue.

**Architect's ruling:** Q1 — the course, because four other items depend on it. Q3 — hold the
blocks until the proof is read.

## DIPLOMAT · the Table — *Why Being Good at Care Is Only Half of It*

**There are 361 people at this table and none of them has heard anything since 2026-08-22.**

**On Q1 — the Diplomat agrees with the Shaman and for a different reason.** Update #31 is not a
courtesy. **Silence is what turns a late project into a dead one**, and the repair
structure at `ch7:726` is the book's own answer: name what happened, name your part, name what
you are not asking for.

**On Q4 — the workbook conversation is a repair, and it has to be shaped like one.** Asking 16
people *what would serve you* without first saying *I have not started this* is a survey
wearing an apology's clothes. **The order is: say the absence, then ask.** The book is very
clear that those cannot be reversed.

**On Q5 — the Diplomat is the Face most worried about *Allyship at Work*, and not for the
reason the note anticipated.** The risk is not optics. **It is that the 361 hear about a second
book before they hear about their first one**, and that is a specific, avoidable injury. It
costs one sentence in whatever announcement comes: *the backers hear it first, and hear what it
funds.*

**Diplomat's ruling:** Q1 — #31 goes out, and it is a repair rather than a status update.
Q4 — absence first, then the question.

## SAGE · the Board — *Seeing the Whole Board Without Leaving the Table*

**The board has one feature everybody else is arguing around: the proofs are already in the
post.** That is a fixed clock nobody controls, and it makes most of this sequencing question
answer itself.

**On Q3 — the proof arriving is the next real event.** Between now and then, everything
downstream of the interior is blocked, which is a gift rather than an obstacle: it means the
window belongs to everything that is not the book. **Sends, the course, the routes, #31.**

**On Q1 — the Sage's ruling is that the question is malformed.** *What ships next* assumes a
queue, and this board has **two independent tracks that do not compete**: the fulfilment track
(proof → print run → the 247) is gated on money, and the money track (four sends → engagements
→ $6,000) is gated on nothing at all. **The correct answer to *what next* is not one item, it
is: stop treating those as one queue.**

**On Q5 — the Sage sees what the Challenger and the Diplomat are both half-right about.**
*Allyship at Work* is the only item on the board that **feeds the money track**, which is the
gate on everything else. Its danger is real and its category is different from the other four.
**Public drafting costs nothing and it points at `/speaking`**, so it belongs on the money
track rather than in the product queue.

**On Q6 — the routes are the cheapest item on the board and they gate the most.** `/book` gates
the PDF. `/course` gates the course launch, the unlock, and the entitlement question. **A single
static page with three links unblocks both**, and the login gate can arrive later.

**Sage's ruling:** two tracks, not one queue. The money track has no gate and has not moved in
three weeks.

**Unanimous:** the four outreach sends are overdue and cost nothing. Five of six put them or
update #31 first.

**Four to two:** hold PDF 2.0's blocks until the proof is read. The Architect's structural
reason settles it — the blocks repaginate a book whose proof was ordered at 387 pages.

**Split, and worth reading rather than averaging:** the Challenger says *Allyship at Work* takes
no manuscript hours; the Sage says it is the only item feeding the money track. **They agree on
the action and disagree on the reason**, which means the ruling is *public drafting only* and
the disagreement is about how much it matters.

**The question the panel raised that nobody asked:** the Regent and the Diplomat both landed on the
**workbook conversation**, from different directions. Sixteen people are owed something nobody
has started, and the honest move is to say so and ask what would serve them. **No question in
the brief was about that**, which is usually the sign a panel found the real one.

## The second question — how the six are integrated into the editorial system

**Wendell asked this alongside the panel, and the honest answer is that they are not.**

**Seventeen 6FACE analyses exist in this repo**, going back to the card deck and the I Ching
mapping. **`EDITORIAL_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` references none of them and names no Face.**

### What is actually there

| where the Faces appear | as what |
|---|---|
| `AGENTS.md` | **chapter labels** — ch3 is the Shaman, ch4 the Challenger, and so on |
| `.claude/skills/mtgoa-review/SKILL.md` | **content to be protected** — *"That is the Shaman's superpower"* at `ch3:213` must stay; the Sage's Move 1 is a canon reference |
| the seventeen panel files | **a practice with no call site**, run when Wendell asks for one |
| `EDITORIAL_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` | **nowhere** |

**The editorial system runs on four roles that are not Faces:** Book Architect, Continuity and
Claims Auditor, Line Editor, Voice Guardian.

***Book Architect* is a name collision rather than an integration.** The editorial role
diagnoses structure and refuses to rewrite. The Architect Face fixes the condition instead of
the person. **Two different jobs wearing one word**, which is the collision this book's own glossary
discipline exists to prevent.

### The finding, and it is the book's own argument turned on its author

**The book claims six roles run every group, and that reaching for the same one every time is
the defect.** The project that produces the book **makes its decisions with four roles that map
to none of them**, and the six only appear when somebody remembers to convene them.

**That is precisely the pattern `ch1` describes.** A working method exists, it is not in the
loop, so it runs on memory and mood — which is the same sentence `review.py` was written to
answer on 2026-07-31, about instruments rather than about roles.

### What integrating them would actually mean

**Not replacing the four editorial roles.** They do line-level work the Faces are not shaped
for, and a Line Editor is not a Diplomat.

**The gap is one level up.** The four roles answer *is this prose any good*. **Nothing in the
system answers *is this the right thing to be working on*** — which is the question this panel
was convened for, and the reason it had to be convened by hand.

**Three ways to close it, cheapest first:**

1. **Name the panel in the operating system** as the standing instrument for a decision with
   more than one defensible answer, with the format these seventeen files already use. Costs a
   section.
2. **Give it a trigger.** The pattern-audit rule in that file says to add a mechanical check
   only after a reader has caught the defect — the equivalent here is to convene the six
   whenever a decision has been deferred twice. **The four outreach sends have been deferred
   three times**, which is how this panel found them.
3. **Rename the editorial Book Architect** so the collision stops. `Structure Editor` costs
   nothing and retires a term that carries two jobs.

**The one caution.** Six voices on every small decision is its own tic, and the panels are
expensive to write. **The trigger matters more than the format** — a standing rule that fires
on twice-deferred decisions would have convened this one three weeks ago.

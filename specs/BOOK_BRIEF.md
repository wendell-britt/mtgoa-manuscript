**Working title.** *Mastering the Game of Allyship*
*How to Show Up, Clean Up, Wake Up, and Grow Up — and Keep Playing*

**Ideal reader.** Jordan. Mid-30s to 40s, has done the work, fears *I'm doing more harm
than I know*, wants to show up without self-erasure. Skims theory, never skips a story,
stops for a named move with a practice.

**Her starting problem.** She knows something is broken in how she shows up and is unsure
what to do about it.

**What she can do at the end.** Play all six Faces, know which one the moment needs, and
run her own game.

**Central argument.** To master the game of allyship you must master all six developmental
levels. The shadow of a skipped level is what blocks you.

**Voice in five adjectives.** Embodied, direct, playful, testimonial, unsparing.

---

## What the prose is allowed to do

The game frame. Seven in-world voices — the six school Heads, each with a genre ruled in
`HEAD_VOICE_DIAL`, and the Headmaster, whose letter stands at the door of the schools.
Marginalia. Humor. Fragments and staccato inside Sections 1–3 where a Head's genre
requires them. Direct second person in Part 2.

## What would betray it

Generic self-help smoothness. Hedging. Moralizing without self-deprecation. Explaining
instead of testifying. **Narrating the reader's unnamed history back to her as fact** —
`MANUSCRIPT_FILE_CANON:154`, and the one on this list a counter cannot see. The
short-declarative drift, ruled drift rather than voice.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Canon files only. The banned-word list. Every source credited, in the text or in Appendix
G — Carolyn Elliott, PhD, not "Bob"; the trust model is Mayer, Davis and Schoorman's.
Permissions live on the copyright page. No invented citations. **No Head names a channel,
feeling-word or operation** — Chapter 3 owns that vocabulary.

## What confirming it changed

**Voice count corrected.** The draft said *"seven in-world voices with ruled genres."*
`HEAD_VOICE_DIAL` rules **six**, one per school. The seventh voice is the Headmaster,
whose genre is not ruled there. The spec's own **§2a "seventh dial"** is a different thing
— person, and feeling against fact — and is the likeliest source of the miscount.

**One boundary verified rather than assumed.** *No Head names a channel* now measures
**0 hits**: no `Metal`, `Water`, `Wood`, `Fire` or `Earth` appears inside any marginalia,
handbook or epigraph block across nine chapters and the Headmaster's letter.

**One betrayal is live and stays on the list.** *The short-declarative drift* was ruled
drift on 2026-07-29 and has not moved: sentences of six words or fewer run **18.6%**
against *Igniting Joy*'s 4.2%, measured 2026-08-05, against 19.4% when the rule was made.
DL-61 records why it is not queued — nobody ruled the fix, and the copula work that
shipped alongside it did move, 61.4 to 46.2 per thousand. **It is named here because a
brief that lists only the betrayals the book avoids is a brief nobody has to check
anything against.**

## Decisions

**I1 · Ecology → E2.** The block keeps its three parts (formula, question, coda) and gets a new Sage question and coda drawn from helping. Each sibling's question is that Face's own angle on the living field, so a helping question repeats nothing in Chapter 3. The cut question duplicates the scene's distance test (`ch8_rewrite.md:337`) and leaves out the helped person's daemons. No placeholder goes in live. Both readings of the AD (*integrated*; *propagated through Ch3–Ch8*) rule out retiring the block.

**I2 · Section 5.**
- **KEEP verbatim:** Irix Vale marginalia; Corin Ash marginalia; the load-bearing-wall confession.
- **RETIRE:** the cost paragraph (defect model); the jurisdiction passage; the *Which Game and Which Altitude* mechanism (the pair stays in Section 3 and Appendix F:57).
- **REBUILD:** the daemon 3-2-1 (Wendell's questions); the BAR prompt; a new helping case (a person went rigid when helped) in Wendell's words.
- **MOVE:** the bite to Section 6.
- **Load Section 5 carries that Section 2 does not:** a lived case, an operation on the part (*if I knew my damage meant something*), an outside witness, and the reader's own 3-2-1.
- **A limit for the Damaged Self is required** (every sibling has one; ch7:591 gives the form: a unit, an addressee, a handoff, *That limit is deliberate*). It falls on the Victim's telling of a hit and leaves the Damaged Self's intake unlimited (*undefeated*). It must admit *wrong place, wrong time* as a finished answer, and it must not treat a high price as evidence of harm.

**I3 · Take Out → T2.** Appendix I:17 stays whole, tail included. The Coach half carries *helpers kill the state of needing help*. The Escape Artist half points to the exit test the scene stages (leaving from ground or from wound). The sibling closer stays, and its clearance clause is rebuilt around helping: the helped person's daemon is doing the same job in a Forest you cannot see into. The cut Take Out (*configured differently… qualification or disqualification*) is retired.

**I4 · W5.** The cost section stays the Sage's own cost: the damage she takes and the seat fact. The seat fact goes on 365, 369, 375 (last sentence) and 397. Line 371 keeps its claim and gains one clause that names the mechanism and points back to Section 2, so no section states it twice. Line 367 takes the redraft already submitted. The helped daemons' standing lives in Section 2 (stated) and Section 5's case (shown). Lines 375 and 379–389 are re-homed under the Section 2 definition, not only given the seat fact.

**I5 · M1.** No Move changes. A helping clause in Move 1's test would turn a cost into a shadow sign. Diplomat dissents on narrow grounds. The restored domain section's *the Damaged Self offers a framework* (cut 743 and 753) is re-homed to the Sage's own distortion.

It is the glossary's, Ch2:393's, ch8:251's and ch9:443's word, and the glossary line *carried up at Ch 8 §3* makes ch8:251 required canon.

# Census

Sections 1–3 of ch3, ch5 and ch7, read in full with every box stripped, 8,516 words. This lists
every passage a reader would not take as Wendell's.

**Method.** Read, not searched. The test applied to each passage: *if a reader met this with no
box around it and no signature nearby, whose life would they think it was about?*

---

| where | words | whose | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ch3:170–176` | 159 | **Maera Voss** | a session log. *"Thirty-first session. Jaw, then the back of the neck… Three years of nothing, in my own handwriting, four hundred entries."* |

**889 words across three chapters.** ch4, ch6 and ch8 were not read for this census; the sizing
below assumes they may hold the same shape and does not claim they do.

## They are all the same defect, and it has one cause

Every one of these was **correct until this morning**. `ch3_memoir_move.py` states the rule they
were written under: ***"Above the signature, only the Head. Below it, only Wendell."*** The
signature sat at the close of Section 3, so everything above it was attributed, and these passages
were inside that attribution.

Today's ruling moved the signature into the admissions box at the top of the chapter. The
attribution moved with it. **Nothing was edited and nothing broke; the license was withdrawn from
underneath them.**

`ch7` is the sharpest case because the collision was already known. **DL-19, 2026-07-31**, names
this exact passage: *"In ch7 the collision is total: Elian Cross recounts cases with a cost…
which is the exact shape a Wendell testimony would take."* It was ruled and applied on 2026-08-01
by moving **Wendell's** testimony out and leaving the Head's in, because the Head's was licensed.
That reasoning is now void.

## What a reader meets today

Chapter 3, in the middle of a chapter running in Wendell's voice, with no frame and no warning:

> Sessions thirty-two through four hundred and six. Chest: nothing. Throat: nothing. Hands:
> nothing. **Three years of nothing, in my own handwriting**, four hundred entries.

A reader takes that for Wendell's own log. It is Voss's, and the proof is that its closing
sentence is the admissions page's *"A word from the Head"* almost verbatim: *"what it costs a
person to be read accurately by somebody they did not ask."* The same sentence now appears twice
in ch3, once signed inside the box and once unsigned in the running text.

## Three judgment calls, listed so they are not silently decided

- **`ch3:239`** — *"I recover both ends before I am done, and I will ask you to hold them at the
  same time."* Written as Voss closing her document. **It now reads correctly as Wendell**, who
  does recover both poles later in the chapter. Probably keep as is.
- **`ch5:24`** — *"That is the first entry in this Regent's ledger and it goes in before anything
  else does."* Inside the fable, keeper's-ledger flavour. Reads as the narrator. Probably fine.
- **`ch5:189`** — *"You know which part of that paragraph is yours. I don't need to name it."*
  Reads as Wendell addressing the reader. Fine.

## One open question closes itself

**The Polarity Map needs no marker.** It was open because it sat under a signature naming a Head
as its author. That signature is gone from the position. In ch5 and ch7 the Map now opens *"You
met the Polarity Map at the School of the Body"*, which is unambiguously Wendell tracking his
reader across chapters, with nothing left to contradict it. **Q4 of the whose-voice spec is
answered by the change already applied.**

## What is available

Not a recommendation. The options, with what each costs.

| | option | cost | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Box them.** These are documents by a Head, which is what `HANDBOOK` already is — *"a document inside the document."* Reuse it rather than inventing a frame. | four blocks wrapped; no new ontology | Extends what exists. A new frame kind would be a second kingdom. |
| **2** | **Move them into the admissions box** so each Head's record sits with their filing. | restructures the box; a session log is not an admissions page | Tidy, but it makes the box carry two genres. |
| **3** | **Label them in place** with a line of apparatus naming the document. | six words each | Least disruption, most apparatus. |
| **4** | **Cut them.** | 889 words of the best-voiced material in those sections | Cheap and it costs the Heads what little surface they have left. |
| **5** | **Leave them.** | nothing now, a reader confusion later | Honest option only if you judge the confusion small. |

**A note on option 4 against the invoice already recorded.** The panel on changing the promise
noted that the Heads lose about three quarters of their surface under that ruling. These 889 words
are part of what remains. Cutting them takes the Heads down again, from a filing plus these
records to a filing alone.

## What this census does not cover

ch4, ch6 and ch8 sections 1–3, which were not read. The `I` counts there are comparable to ch5's
and ch7's, and the passages may be quoted fable dialogue rather than Head records, which is what
most first-person hits in ch3 and ch7 turned out to be. **It needs the same read to know**, and
that read is roughly the same size as this one.

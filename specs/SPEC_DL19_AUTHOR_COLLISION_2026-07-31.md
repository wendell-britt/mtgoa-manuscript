## 3. The correction that halves the work

**Two of the four already comply, and the reason matters.**

DL-4 is a ruling about *ownership*, not about text. Under it, the *"I"* at
`ch5:516` and `ch6:365` **is** the Face-author, by declaration, and neither
passage contains anything a Face-author cannot own:

- `ch5:516` — *"since I was twelve," "people I respect built it," "laundered into
  a work order."* No real-world referent. Reads as Sera Quill without a change.
- `ch6:365` — *"a design I was proud of get dismantled in a meeting," "entire
  quarters," "laundered into a spec."* Reads as Irix Vale, whose canon tic is *in
  practice* and who is on record building a handoff process so clean the team
  stopped talking. It is already his passage.

Better than compliant: `FACE_AUTHORS.md` field 3 — **the daemon coloring** —
calls a Face-author writing under their assigned daemon *"the field that matters
most… what makes the treatise a document with a bias the reader can learn to
spot."* ch5's daemon is the Fixer-Healer and `ch5:516` is a man handing his
inadequacy to the Fixer. ch6's is the Emotional Body and `ch6:365` is dread being
converted into rigor. **Both passages are the doctrine's central device working.**

**The two real violations carry real-world referents a Face cannot own:**
`ch3:258` (customer-service phone work, and a citation of *The Courage to Be
Disliked*), and `ch5:308` (martial arts, church, academia, a progressive
justice-focused collective).

A phone bank and a progressive collective break that fiction as badly as they

## 5. The four moves

### Move 1 · `ch3:258`–`276` — drop the first person, keep the teaching

The passage's value is the mechanism and the game, neither of which needs an
owner. Second person is the chapter's own register, so this costs nothing and
removes the collision.

**BEFORE**

> I used to be very good at putting feelings away.
>
> Customer service. Someone on the phone, upset. The emotion would arrive, theirs and mine, and my chest would do this particular thing: a tightening, a pulling inward, like a door closing fast. Then I'd be gone. Not literally. I stayed on the call, professional, efficient. The part of me that could feel the situation had left the building.
>
> I'd locate the problem. Solve the problem. Move on.

**AFTER**

> Some people are very good at putting feelings away.
>
> A voice on the phone, upset. The emotion arrives, theirs and yours, and the chest does a particular thing: a tightening, a pulling inward, like a door closing fast. Then you are gone. Not literally. You stay on the call, professional, efficient. The part of you that could feel the situation has left the building.
>
> Locate the problem. Solve the problem. Move on.

Continues through `ch3:276` on the same conversion — *"I was solving for my own
emotion"* → *"You are solving for your own emotion"*; *"I eventually invented a
game, Tough Conversations"* → *"There is a game for this, Tough Conversations"*;
*"The first time I played it, I had to say tell me more…"* → *"The first time you
play it, you will have to say tell me more to something that makes you want to
fix it immediately."* The book citation survives unchanged — a Face may cite.

**What is lost:** the confession quality. **Under DL-4 confession is not ch3's
job**, and the same beat is already Wendell's at `ch2:223` (the origin story) and
`ch9:332`.

### Move 2 · `ch5:308`–`324` — re-voice to Sera Quill, two phrases only

This one stays first person, because a Regent-Face writing about inheritance is
the correct owner. Only the real-world referents move.

**BEFORE** — *"I came up in traditions. Martial arts, church, academia — all of
them Regent organizations…"* and *"I belonged to a collective for a few years.
Progressive, justice-focused, the right values on paper, and absolutely no
governance."*

**AFTER** — *"I came up in traditions. A house, an archive, a bench — all of them
Regent organizations…"* and *"I sat some years with a body that had unwritten its
own charter. The right principles, agreed and then struck, and no governance at
all."*

Everything else in the passage — the building that is going to fail, the crown
worn under the hood, the three crises, *"I've never figured out how to offer the
floor before the crisis"* — is Sera Quill's already, and her canon material
supports it: *"four generations of designs in the archive and no record of what
any of them cost,"* *"as held under Merrow."*

**Flag:** this is the only move that composes new words rather than reassigning
or de-personing existing ones. It needs a Voice Guardian pass against
`SEVEN_VOICES.md` before it goes near a chapter.

### Move 3 · `ch5:516` and `ch6:365` — no change, and record why

Both already comply per §3. **The ruling is that they stay**, and DL-19 records
the reasoning so this does not get re-litigated: they are the Face-author writing

### Move 4 · `ch7:508` — cut the testimony slot; the shame goes to ch9

Under DL-4 there is no legal place in ch7 for Wendell's first person. The margin
cannot take it (§4). **So the slot is cut**, which closes it as a P0 blocker.

from the start:

- The confession already lives there — `ch9:332`–`358`.

- Wendell's own beat is already sitting three lines above it, unclaimed:

# 6 Game Master Analysis — N41, the three-years story

**2026-08-05. Wendell:** *"how is jordan going to be helped by continuing to hit this
story and how many times does she need to hear it for it to stick."*

Two questions, and the panel splits on the first and agrees on the second.

---

## The board, measured before anybody speaks

**Six landings, all in ch1. The author's note has none** — the triage's *"plus twice in
the author's note"* is stale.

| | what it says | the charge | the mechanism it pays for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `:4` | *This book is three years late.* | none. a fact | the hook. the book's first sentence |
| `:6` | the promise to readers who paid | shame | sets up the debt |
| `:10` | *I was holding myself hostage* | **anger** | alchemy: the charge that became the book |
| `:14` | *me showing up: late, imperfect, and in the game* | commitment | closes the opening |
| `:91` | *I ran it for three years while writing a book that told other people not to* | **shame, from outside** | the myths section |
| `:113` | *For three years I fueled this book with guilt* | **guilt** | the Token System, non-renewable tokens |
| `:183` | *I made it sound like something that happened to me. It wasn't* | **delight** | existential kink |

**ch1 runs 152 first-person against 431 second-person, a ratio of 1 : 2.8.** That is
healthy. **But five of the chapter's six most author-heavy paragraphs are this story**
(`:113` +16, `:10` +14, `:6` +10, `:91` +9, `:183` +7). The density is not high. It is
**clustered**.

**And two of the six open with a `preempt` hit** — `:113` *Here is the machine* and `:183`
*Here is what I left out*. Both survive in ch1's remaining nine.

---

## SHAMAN · the Body — *what happens to her on each hit*

**Read the field before counting anything.** On the first telling Jordan's body does
something specific and useful: it unclenches. A man who says *I am three years late* in his
first sentence is not going to grade her. That is the Protector paid, and it is why the
opener works.

**The risk is not the number. It is a repeated charge.** A confession told twice with the
same feeling under it stops being a confession the second time and becomes a *performance*
of one, and the body clocks that faster than the mind does. Jordan has a lifetime of
practice detecting performed humility; it is half of what she is tired of.

**So the test is charge, not count.** Measured above: `:10` is anger, `:91` is shame,
`:113` is guilt, `:183` is delight. **Four distinct charges across six landings.** That is
not one story told six times. That is one event felt four different ways, which is exactly
what the book asks *her* to do with her own material two chapters later.

`:4` and `:14` carry no charge of their own. They are frame.

**Shaman's ruling: the four charged landings stay. Look hard at the two that are frame.**

---

## CHALLENGER · the Line — *the one nobody else will say*

**Six confessions is a shield, and the book contains the instrument that proves it.**

`ch1:123`: *The counter marked applause pays out in being seen as good.* `ch1:125`: **every
prize on that shelf is the kind you carry home by yourself.**

**Every retelling of the three-years story collects a ticket at that counter.** *I failed
publicly, I am honest about it, I am the kind of author who admits things.* It reads as
humility and it spends as standing, and the book spent this whole session teaching Jordan
to catch exactly that move in herself.

I am not saying he is faking. `:129` already admits the same thing about the prose itself
— *I wrote whole chapters of this book standing at it and had to throw them out.* **He
knows the counter. He is still standing at it six times in one chapter.**

**Challenger's ruling: whatever the panel decides about mechanism, the count comes down.
An author who cannot make his point in four passes is collecting on the other two.**

---

## REGENT · the Inheritance — *what survives being handed on*

**Only one of the six will still be in her head in a year, and it is `:91`.**

> *It broke the day someone I had been "helping" told me, plainly, that my help had been
> about me the whole time.*

**That is the only landing with another person in it.** The other five are a man alone with
his own interior. Interior does not inherit. A sentence somebody said to him does.

Ask what she repeats to a friend. Nobody repeats *he gave himself permission to be angry.*
People repeat *somebody he was helping told him it had been about him the whole time*,
because it is a scene, it has two people, and it can happen to the person hearing it.

**And the five around it dilute it.** By `:91` she has already met the three-years story
three times as an interior event. The one telling that has a witness arrives fourth, in a
queue.

**Regent's ruling: `:91` is load-bearing and everything else is scaffolding. If anything
moves, it moves so `:91` lands earlier and cleaner.**

---

## ARCHITECT · the Design — *and this is the case for keeping all of it*

**The Challenger is counting occurrences. Count jobs instead.**

Every landing is the worked example for a mechanism that arrives immediately after it:

| landing | the machine it powers |
|---|---|
| `:10` | anger metabolised into fuel — the book's core claim about alchemy |
| `:91` | the myths, and why an accurate self-account is not one |
| `:113` | **non-renewable tokens.** guilt is the specimen |
| `:183` | existential kink. the shadow feeding on the delay |

**Take any one out and the mechanism after it loses its only concrete instance.** `:113` is
the clearest: the Token System is an abstraction until *For three years I fueled this book
with guilt, and it got me to the desk and left me stranded there.* Replace that with a
hypothetical and the section teaches nothing.

**This is not one story told six times. It is one story cut into six pieces, each spent
where a new machine needs paying for.** That is the cheapest possible way to run six worked
examples: the reader already knows the case, so each mechanism costs a sentence instead of
a scene.

**Architect's ruling: do not count. Ask of each landing whether the thing after it survives
its removal. Five do not. One does.**

---

## DIPLOMAT · the Field — *what it costs to stay in the seat*

**The ratio is fine and the clustering is the problem.**

1 : 2.8 across the chapter is generous. But `:4` through `:14` is the opening, and Jordan
meets the story **three times before the chapter has told her anything about herself.**
Her first eight paragraphs are a man's account of his own delay.

**And she never gets to answer.** `:91`'s power comes from somebody talking back to him.
That person got a sentence. Jordan gets none, six times.

**What it costs:** by the fourth landing she is tracking *his* arc instead of her own, and
the book has quietly become a memoir she is reading rather than a game she is playing. She
will not resent it. She will just stop looking for herself on the page.

**Diplomat's ruling: the opening cluster is the cost, not the total. Three landings in
eight paragraphs is where she starts reading about him instead of with him.**

---

## SAGE · the Game — *which game is this, and the answer to the second question*

**This is a spiral, not a loop, and the whole disagreement above turns on telling them
apart.**

A loop returns to the same place. A spiral returns to the same *material* at a different
altitude. Read in order: `:4` states the event · `:10` owns the feeling in it · `:91` gets
it from outside himself · `:113` mechanises it · `:183` reveals what he was getting out of
it. **Each pass changes what she knows about the same three years.** That is the WAVE
Spiral run on the author's own case, in the chapter that introduces the WAVE Spiral.

**And the book already knows it is doing this.** `:183` opens *Here is what I left out of
the story I told you at the start.* **The spiral declares itself.** (Both self-declaring
landings, `:113` and `:183`, are `preempt` hits. **That is the tension worth naming: a
spiral has to announce the return, and the announcement is the shape `preempt` bans.**)

### On *how many times does she need to hear it for it to stick*

**It is not a number, and asking for one is the wrong instrument.**

A thing sticks by being retrieved in **different contexts**, not by being repeated in the
same one. Six identical tellings would stick worse than three varied ones, because
identical repetition teaches recognition and varied return teaches use. This is the same
reason the book gives a reader five moves and four domains instead of twenty instructions.

**So the count Wendell should ask for is not tellings. It is turns.** How many times does
this story arrive and change what she knows?

**Measured: five of six do.** `:4` opens · `:10` adds the feeling · `:91` adds the witness
· `:113` adds the mechanism · `:183` adds the payoff. **`:14` adds nothing.** It restates
*late* from `:4` ten lines earlier and hands her no new information about the three years.

**Sage's ruling: five turns is right for a spiral this size. The sixth is a loop, and it is
`:14`.**

**The Challenger is outvoted on the count and right about the mechanism.** Nobody disputes
that a repeated confession collects at the applause counter. What the Architect
demonstrates is that five of the six are not repeated confessions — they are one
confession spent five ways, and each purchase is a machine the chapter needs.

**The Shaman, the Sage and the Architect converge from three directions on the same
sentence.** Shaman: `:14` carries no charge of its own. Sage: `:14` adds no turn. Architect:
nothing after `:14` depends on it. **`:14` is the only landing all three tests fail.**

### The ruling available

> **Five stay. `:14` loses its half of the story.**
>
> *You're the person I made that promise to. **This book is me showing up: late,
> imperfect, and in the game.*** → keep the promise, drop the restatement of *late*, which
> `:4` already owns.

**And the Diplomat's finding is separate and unresolved.** Three landings in eight
paragraphs is a real cluster, and cutting `:14` reduces it to two. **Whether `:6` and `:10`
can both stay in the opening is a second decision** and it belongs to Wendell, because it
is the one place the panel's tests disagree: the Architect says `:10` powers the alchemy
claim, and the Diplomat says the opening is where her patience is thinnest.

### One thing nobody would have found without the numbers

**`:91` is the landing the Regent says will outlive the others, and it is the one the
chapter treats most cheaply.** It arrives fourth, mid-paragraph, in a clause — *It broke
the day someone I had been "helping" told me, plainly, that my help had been about me the
whole time* — with no space around it. **The only telling with a witness in it is the only
telling that is not given its own paragraph.**

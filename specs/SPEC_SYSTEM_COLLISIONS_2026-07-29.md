## 1 · The load-bearing finding: ch3 does not teach the two operations

ch3 is titled ***Emotional Alchemy as the Foundation of Real Allyship***. It says
*emotional alchemy* ten times. It has a section called ***What Emotional Alchemy
Actually Is (And Why It's Not What You've Been Told)***.

Counts in ch3, against canon's two operations:

| canon term | occurrences in ch3 |
|---|---|
| `Transmute` / `transmute` | **0** |
| `Translate` / `translate` | **0** |
| "two operations" | **0** |

**The chapter that exists to teach Emotional Alchemy teaches neither of its two
operations.**

### What ch3 teaches instead

ch3:227 — ***The WAVE-Spiral: Emotional Alchemy as Repeatable Technique and
Living Art***. ch3:229 — *"If emotional alchemy means learning from emotions
instead of managing them, then how do you actually do it…"* The answer it gives
is **the WAVE-Spiral, five stages**: Wake Up, Open Up, Clean Up, Grow Up, Show Up.

So the chapter does answer the question. **It answers it with a different system
than canon does**, and it presents that system as the method: *"Emotional Alchemy
as Repeatable Technique."*

### This is the collision Wendell asked about

> *"I do want to check that the collision between learning the wake up clean up
> grow up show up moves and the emotional alchemy moves for each chapter has been
> dealt with."*

It has not been dealt with. It has not been named. **The WAVE-Spiral is occupying
the slot canon assigns to Transmute and Translate**, and the book never says how
the two relate.

What ch3 *does* have, and it is a lot: the five channels with correct element
pairings, all five completions verbatim, charge-first, the Controller, and the
five-stage spiral. Canon's §2 confirms all of that is sound. **The gap is
specifically the operations layer.**

### The reconciliation available, if Wendell wants it

Canon says: *"Emotional Alchemy does not bypass the nervous system. The mature
sequence looks like this…"* — and gives the Emotional Process. It does not say the
Process replaces practice. So there is a clean three-layer reading:

| layer | what it is | where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **The Emotional Process** | what the nervous system does, unbidden. Stimulus → Activation → Impulse → Controller → Skeptic → Conscious Action | canon; **absent from the book** |
| **The two operations** | what you do to a charge. Transmute within a channel, Translate across channels | canon; **absent from ch3** |
| **The WAVE-Spiral** | the five-stage practice you run to perform an operation | ch3, taught well |

On that reading nothing is wrong and nothing is redundant: **the WAVE-Spiral is
how you run a Transmute or a Translate.** Wake Up and Open Up get the charge into
play, Clean Up is where the operation is selected, Grow Up integrates, Show Up
spends it. That last mapping is a claim and Wendell should check it — it is the
one inference in this document.

**F1 — ch3 must name the two operations and say which layer WAVE sits in.**
Without it the book teaches a practice for a system it never defines, which is the
same shape as ch7:175 pointing at ch3 for a taxonomy ch3 does not contain.

## 2 · WAVE is four moves in the glossary and five in every chapter

| source | stages |
|---|---|
| `APPENDIX_C:34` "The Four WAVE Moves" | Show Up, Clean Up, Wake Up, Grow Up — **four** |
| `APPENDIX_C:60` "WAVE" | Show Up, Clean Up, Wake Up, Grow Up — **four** |
| ch3, ch4, ch5, ch6, ch7, ch8 BAR grids | Wake Up, **Open Up**, Clean Up, Grow Up, Show Up — **five**, all six chapters |
| ch3:235 | *"a progression through five stages"* |
| ch9 | *"five times four gives twenty… a hundred and twenty in the deck"* |

**The glossary drops Open Up, twice.** The four are Ken Wilber's original —
Waking Up, Growing Up, Cleaning Up, Showing Up — and the book's own addition is
Open Up, the somatic allowing stage, which is the Shaman's whole contribution.

Open Up is the stage the book leans on hardest. ch7:540: *"Open Up is the move
this chapter's daemon exists to prevent."* ch3 builds its dial passage on it. And
the deck arithmetic requires five rows: 5 × 4 domains = 20 cards per operation,
120 in the deck. **Four rows would give 16 and 96.**

**F2 — fix both Appendix C entries to five, naming Open Up as the book's
extension of Wilber's four.** Mechanical; the manuscript is already right.

## 5 · E6 — RULED: the Emotional Process goes in the book

Wendell: *"The emotional process is part of emotional alchemy. It's gotta be in
the book."*

```
Stimulus → Emotional Activation → Impulse
  → Controller determines purpose and scope
  → Skeptic evaluates interpretations
  → Conscious Action
```

Currently absent: *Stimulus* and *Impulse* appear **0** times in the manuscript.

**This is the largest single addition in the finishing pass, and it is doctrinal
rather than editorial.** Canon puts the **Controller** and the **Skeptic** inside
the *healthy* sequence — *"Emotion provides energy. The Controller provides
direction. The Skeptic provides discernment. Wisdom emerges from cooperation
rather than domination."*

The book's centre of gravity is the opposite. ch3 runs the Controller as the
daemon that rules a feeling out of order; ch4 runs the Skeptic as the auditor that
becomes the brake. Both chapters carry the healthy reading — ch3: *"That is
integrity, and it is not automatic. It is a part of you doing a job"* — so this is
**emphasis, not contradiction**, and the addition has anchors to attach to.

**F5 — ch3 is the home**, alongside F1, since both are the operations layer. The
Process explains *why* the Controller chapter-work matters: the Controller is a
stage in the mature sequence, and its distortion is that stage seizing the whole
sequence. That reframe strengthens ch3's existing Controller material rather than
competing with it.

# SPEC — Realigning the 35 Moves onto the five WAVE stages

**2026-07-29. Opened by Wendell's ruling on Seam 3:**

> *"we gotta keep 5 moves because there are 5 basic moves — each chapter should
> bring them through waking up opening up cleaning up growing up and showing up"*

**The book already says this.** ch3, in the section Seam 1 just pulled the grid
from:

> *"The deck runs five basic moves, and you already know all five, because they are
> the WAVE-Spiral: Wake Up, Open Up, Clean Up, Grow Up, Show Up."*

So the design statement is on the page. **It is the Section 5 Moves that never
carried it.** Zero of the 35 is tagged to a stage.

---

## 1 · Why this is the largest reduction available

Right now Jordan meets **7 chapters × 5 Moves = 35 differently-named moves.** If
the five Moves in every chapter *are* the five stages, she learns **one sequence,
once**, and meets it seven times in different hands.

**35 things to learn becomes 5.** No content is cut — the repetition converts from
load into recognition. It also softens the ch2→ch3 cliff, because by ch3 she
already knows the shape she is about to see.

For scale, against the seam work: Seam 1 removed 120 named units and took the book
from 219 to 99. This one removes 30 more from the *learning* load without deleting
a paragraph.

## 2 · The five stages and their questions

Taken from the grid row labels now in `deck/BAR_GRIDS.md`, which is where the
questions were written down:

| stage | the question it asks |
|---|---|
| **Wake Up** | notice the signal — what is here? |
| **Open Up** | allow experience — what am I actually feeling? |
| **Clean Up** | identify the channel — Fire, Water, Metal, Earth, Wood? |
| **Grow Up** | identify emerging capacity — what wants to grow? |
| **Show Up** | choose the domain — where will I invest this? |

`APPENDIX_C` glosses Clean Up as *"repair the rupture"*, which is a second reading
and matters below for ch7's Move 4.

## 4 · What the map shows

**Move 1 is already Wake Up in all seven chapters.** Every chapter opens on a
naming or noticing move: *Catch It Before the Story, Name the Unnameable, Name the
Inheritance, Find the Leverage Point, Name the Field, Name the Game, Cut the Field.*
**The structure is already one-fifth built, consistently, across the whole book.**
This is not a new architecture. It is a finished first column and four unfinished
ones.

**Show Up is doing three jobs and Open Up and Grow Up are barely present.**
Counting the whole book:

| stage | Moves landing there | STRONG fits |
|---|---|---|
| Wake Up | 8 | 6 |
| Clean Up | 4 | 2 |
| **Open Up** | **3** | **0** |
| **Grow Up** | **3** | **0** |
| **Show Up** | **17** | 5 |

**Seventeen of 35 Moves are Show Up.** That is the whole finding: the book's
Section 5 teaches the output end of the sequence and skips the two interior stages.

**And it skips exactly the stage the book says is hardest.** ch7:540 —
*"Open Up is the move this chapter's daemon exists to prevent."* ch3 builds its
dial passage on it. `APPENDIX_C` drops it from the WAVE definition entirely
(tracker A6). Open Up is the book's own addition to Wilber's four, it is the
Shaman's whole contribution, and **no Move in any chapter is a STRONG Open Up.**

That is a content finding, not a labelling one, and it is worth more than the
realignment: **the book under-teaches its own distinctive stage.**

## 5 · Scope, honestly

**14 STRONG, 12 MEDIUM, 9 WEAK.**

- **14 relabel.** Add the stage to the Move heading. Near-zero prose.
- **12 need a framing sentence** so the stage claim is earned rather than asserted.
- **9 need rewriting**, because they are second and third Show Ups. These are the
  real work, and the honest description is not *relabel* — it is *replace this Move
  with the Open Up or Grow Up the chapter is missing.*

**So the true scope is 9 new Moves across the book**, roughly one to two per
chapter, each one an Open Up or a Grow Up written where a duplicate Show Up
currently sits.

### This is not an August 1 job

Nine new Moves is authoring, and each one has to be a real practice Jordan can
perform, in the right Face's voice, at the right stage. Written well this is the
change that makes the book's spine visible. Written in three days it is nine
labels that do not survive a reader asking why Move 4 is different from Move 3 —
which is the question the WEAK column already fails.

**Recommended split:**

**Ships now, and it is worth shipping alone:** tag the **14 STRONG** Moves with
their stage. Jordan then sees `Move 1 · Wake Up — Name the Field` in every chapter
and the sequence starts teaching itself from the strongest evidence in the map.
Nothing is claimed that the map does not support.

**Second edition:** the 12 MEDIUM framings and the 9 replacements. That is the
real realignment, and it should be done with the Open Up finding in §4 as its
brief, because the gap is the point.

## 6 · The open question this raises

If Show Up carries 17 of 35 Moves, the book's Section 5s are teaching **action** at
the expense of **allowing** and **capacity.** For a reader whose stated fear is
*"I'm doing more harm than I know"* and whose craving is *"I want to show up for
others without losing myself,"* the missing stages are the ones that address the
second half of that sentence.

**Whether that is a flaw or a choice is Wendell's call.** A workbook that pushes
toward action is defensible. But it should be a decision on the record rather than
an artefact of thirty-five Moves being named one chapter at a time.

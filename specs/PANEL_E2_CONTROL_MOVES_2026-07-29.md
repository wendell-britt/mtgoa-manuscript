# SIX-FACE PANEL — E2: what the twelve `Control` blocks become

**2026-07-29. All six Faces reported.** Question set by
`specs/SPEC_EMOTIONAL_ALCHEMY_CANON.md` §5 E2: canon names two operations,
`Control` is not one of them, and ch7 ships 7 `[CONTROL]` blocks while ch8 ships
5 `Control Move N`. What are they?

Wendell asked for pitches "in alignment with how the book has formulated these
moves." Four of the six pitched rather than picking from the menu.

---

## 1 · The tally

| Face | verdict | name proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Challenger | C | **Check** |
| Shaman | C | **Check** |
| Regent | C | **Discipline** |
| Sage | C | **Discipline** |

**Name: 2–2–1–1. No majority.** So the vote does not settle it and the evidence
has to. Below is what survived verification.

## 2 · What the panel settled unanimously

### 2.1 `Control` is dead 6–0, and for a stronger reason than non-canonicity

The Regent found the decisive line, in canon's own closing page:

> *"Many self-help systems teach emotional **control**. Emotional Alchemy teaches
> emotional **cooperation**."*

Keeping `Control` does not merely name something canon lacks. **It names the book
as the thing canon says the book is not.** Verified in canon §1.

### 2.2 ch8's convention wins, 6–0

Mode name plus an italic distortion tag. ch7's pattern-nouns are ad hoc, which is
mechanically how it ended up with **7 blocks for 5 channels** and how one pattern
name collided with a Dissatisfaction charge (§3.2).

The Regent's refinement is worth taking over a flat rule: **ch8 keeps
`Mode — *tag*`** because its five blocks sit under no heading, **ch7 drops to
`*pattern*`** because the channel heading is two lines above. Each chapter then
matches its own Alchemy siblings. That is a rule rather than a coin-flip.

### 2.3 A is dead, and the Shaman said why

The Challenger and Diplomat both found A false empirically — no honest
source→destination channel pair. The Shaman gave the mechanism:

> **There is no charge left in these blocks.** Every one corrects a capacity still
> executing after the signal was disconnected — performative presence,
> performative calm, fluency as rank, naming as lecturing. *You cannot Transmute
> or Translate a zero.*

That explains both prior findings at once. ch8:329's own line carries it: *"The
tell is that the naming costs you nothing."*

## 3 · Verified evidence, by claim

### 3.1 `Distortion` is refuted — two independent counts

| | |
|---|---|
| *distortion* in ch7 + ch8 | **39 occurrences** |
| already a header class in ch7 | **15** |
| `## Section 2: The Distortion` | a structural heading in ch3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 |
| `"The distortion:"` opening a ch8 block | 5 |

Promoting it to a new header class makes the label indistinguishable from the
material it labels — the one-word-two-classes bug the Diplomat itself used to win
its argument. Both the Shaman and the Sage refuted it independently.

### 3.2 The Diplomat's collision, verified

`ch7:239` classes **intellectual superiority** as a *Dissatisfaction charge*.
`ch7:284` classes the identical phrase as a *Control pattern*. One phrase, two
classes, one chapter.

### 3.3 The Regent's inheritance finding, verified

Every `Control:` row in the retired translator sits at **Neutral** altitude —
never Dissatisfied, never Satisfied:

```
| Caution    | Neutral | Clean | Fear → appropriate respect  | Control: Temper Action      |
| Witness    | Neutral | Grow  | Sadness → holding space     | Control: Reopen Sensitivity |
| Complexity | Neutral | Grow  | Joy → multiplicity embraced | Control: Consolidate Energy |
```

So `Control` was **the range-holding move that costs and does not complete** —
carried at −1 energy in the old system. That inheritance is already load-bearing
in the manuscript: ch8 gives all five blocks an explicit `The cost:` line
(verified, 5 of 5), and ch7 closes its blocks the same way — *"Precision costs
here"* (288), *"Staying spends real capacity"* (326), *"The cost here is time"*
(364).

### 3.4 The Sage's coverage objection to `Check`, verified

ch8's five blocks **do** all say check — *"The fix: before you name the game,
check"*, *"before you switch, check"*, *"before you name it, check"*, *"before you
put it down, check"*. The Challenger was right about those.

But ch7's are not all checks. **The Precedent of Presence** (324), **the
Three-Part Repair** (362) and **the Stake-Surfacing Close** (406) are standing
commitments and protocols that "do not skip." `Check` fits roughly eight of
twelve.

### 3.5 The book already supplies the word — and it resolves the rivalry

`ch7:286`:

> ***The structure of the Interpreter's Discipline:*** *You build a practice of
> **checking** your interpretation…*

**A Discipline contains a check.** So `Check` and `Discipline` are not competitors
at the same level — Check is an instrument used inside some Disciplines. That is
why Check covers eight and Discipline covers twelve. Both the Regent and the Sage
reached `Discipline` independently and both cited this line.

### 3.6 The Architect's flip condition — checked, and it does not fire

The Architect said it would join the Diplomat if any deck, app or BAR artifact
indexed these blocks by number. **Nothing does.** No JSON, CSV or YAML in the repo
contains `Control Move` or `Neutral Channel`. The numbered handle is free to
change.

## 4 · The Architect's dissent, which is the one worth reading

It is the only B, and its argument is not about the name:

> *"A new bracket keeps the slot, because typography taxonomizes whether or not
> the noun inside is canonical. That is precisely how 'Control move at the Neutral
> altitude' became a channel."*

Its leverage point is that **ch3 is the empty authority** — a chapter titled
*Emotional Alchemy as the Foundation of Real Allyship* that teaches neither
operation — and ch7 and ch8 filled the vacuum in two incompatible dialects.
`chapters/ch7-sage/G2_CONTROL_MOVES.md` shows the mechanism: the source spec calls
the content *"the Neutral Channel **distortion**"* in prose and heads it
*"Control Move."* **The word was descriptive; the label was an inherited slot.**
The manuscript dropped the word and kept the slot.

**This is a real warning and it does not require choosing B.** Any third category
must ship its gloss in the same pass, or it is the bracket bug again with a new
noun. Every panelist who proposed a name also proposed a gloss; that is the
mitigation, and it is not optional.

Its cost admission is honest and counts against it: B merges ch7's seven blocks
into five, and *"false closure must fold into endless process, and that is real
content loss."* The Regent's within-channel numbering achieves the symmetry
without the loss.

## 5 · Recommendation

**`Discipline`**, on evidence rather than on the 2–2 vote.

- It covers all twelve; `Check` covers eight (§3.4).
- The book already uses it, at ch7:286, and that same line makes Check an
  instrument inside it rather than a rival (§3.5).
- It preserves the one thing the inheritance was actually carrying — a correction
  you pay for and never finish — which both chapters already ship as explicit cost
  lines (§3.3).
- Two Faces derived it independently from different doctrines.

**Header form**, per §2.2:

```
ch7:215  **[CONTROL] Control 1 — Untracked Depletion Pattern**
      →  **Discipline 1 — *untracked depletion***

ch8:355  **Control Move 1: Panoramic Seer — *perspective as performance***
      →  **Discipline 1: Panoramic Seer — *perspective as performance***
```

**Gloss, required in the same pass**, merged from the Regent and the Sage:

> *A **Discipline** is not one of the two operations. It is a rule you set before
> the moment arrives, so the moment does not get to decide for you — and unlike
> Transmute and Translate, a discipline never completes. You pay for it every time
> you run it.*

**ch7 keeps seven blocks**, numbered within channel, per the Regent. No content
loss.

### The risk to weigh, named by both proposers

*Discipline* can read punitive in an allyship book, and moralizing is Jordan's
third documented drop-off trigger. The Sage and Regent both flagged it. **The
Regent's fallback is `Practice`** — which loses the cost connotation, and the cost
is the whole inheritance. Wendell's call.

## 6 · Still open

## 7 · RESOLUTION — and §5's recommendation is withdrawn

**2026-07-29, after Wendell: "this doesn't feel resolved. Let's take a step back
and think about how this solves Jordan's problem."**

It did not feel resolved because **the question was mis-framed, and the framing was
mine.** I asked six Faces *what should we call these?* Five of them dutifully
minted a noun, because that is what the question asked for. The Sage said the real
thing out loud — *"the twelve labels are playing writer bookkeeping"* — and §4
filed it as colour instead of as the finding.

### 7.1 Read one block the way Jordan does

`ch8:355`, complete:

> **Control Move 1: Panoramic Seer — *perspective as performance***
>
> **The distortion:** the capacity to see all the games becomes a thing you do for
> an audience… *Seeing turns into a way of being seen to be wise.*
>
> **The pattern:** narrating instead of inhabiting… The body is present. The person
> is not.
>
> **The cost:** you lose the ability to be in the game you're describing… from
> above you can't serve — you can only report.
>
> **The fix:** before you name the game, check. *Am I naming this to serve, or to
> show that I'm above it?* If it's the second, drop the naming, get your feet under
> you, then name.

**That block is among the best things in the book for Jordan.** Her stated fear is
*"I'm doing more harm than I know."* This says: here is exactly how your best
capacity becomes harm, here is what it costs, here is the one question that catches
it. She stops for "a named move with a practice" — the name is *Panoramic Seer —
perspective as performance*, the practice is *before you name the game, check*.

**And the only untranslated jargon in the whole block is the two words
`Control Move`.** Strip them and Jordan loses nothing at all.

### 7.2 The label fails all three of Jordan's documented triggers

| her trigger | what a category noun does |
|---|---|
| **jargon without translation** (#1) | `Discipline` / `Check` / `Control` is one more term to teach. Removing it removes the block's only untranslated word. |
| **claims without practice** | the block already carries a name *and* a practice. The prefix adds neither. |
| **moralizing** (#3) | *Discipline*, aimed at a reader who already fears she is doing harm, lands on the fear. Both proposers flagged this and I under-weighted it. |

On every one of her three criteria, **no noun beats any noun.** The Architect was
right, and for a better reason than it gave: it argued that typography taxonomizes.
The stronger argument is that the noun is the only thing in the block Jordan cannot
read.

### 7.3 The real gap, which no naming decision touches

Measured across all twelve blocks:

| | four-beat form: distortion / pattern / cost / fix |
|---|---|
| **ch8**, 5 blocks | **5 of 5** |
| **ch7**, 7 blocks | **0 of 7** |

**ch7 has the label and not the form. ch8 has the form and needs no label.**

ch7 instead carries three jargon insertions per block — *"the Bridge-Builder's
Control challenge"*, *"The Neutral Channel pattern is"*, *"The Control move:"* —
and no repeatable shape.

**So the difference between the two chapters was never the naming convention. It
is that ch8 has a block form that ends in a practice and ch7 does not.** Two
panels and twelve agents argued about a prefix while the actual reader-facing gap
sat one layer down.

### 7.4 The gloss is already written, in plain English, in the book

`ch7:219`:

> *"…**This fixes structure, not emotion.** Bridging has a real cost, and the
> Control move names that cost before warmth turns into depletion."*

Six panelists proposed six glosses. **The book already had one**, in Jordan's
register, with no new term in it.

### 7.5 What actually ships

**R1 — no category noun.** Not `Discipline`, not `Check`, not `Distortion`, not
`Control`. The header is the named move and nothing else:

```
ch8:355  **Control Move 1: Panoramic Seer — *perspective as performance***
      →  **Panoramic Seer — *perspective as performance***

ch7:215  **[CONTROL] Control 1 — Untracked Depletion Pattern**
      →  **Bridge-Builder — *untracked depletion***
```

**R2 — port ch8's four beats into ch7's seven blocks.** This is the substantive
work and the only part that is a prose pass rather than a relabel. It is also the
part that serves Jordan, because it is what turns a paragraph into a named move
with a practice.

**R3 — promote ch7:219's own sentence to the once-per-chapter gloss.** *"These
fix structure, not emotion"* — placed at first use in ch7 and ch8. No new
vocabulary, so nothing to teach and nothing to regenerate.

**R4 — strike ch7's three jargon phrases** in the same pass: *Control challenge*,
*Neutral Channel pattern*, *The Control move*.

### 7.6 What the panel got right, and keep

The panel was not wasted. It killed `Control` 6–0 on canon's own closing line
(§2.1), killed option A with the Shaman's *you cannot Transmute or Translate a
zero* (§2.3), verified that nothing in the deck indexes these by number (§3.6),
and surfaced the ch7:239/284 collision (§3.2). **All of that stands.** What is
withdrawn is only §5's recommendation to mint a replacement noun.

The Regent's warning also stands and is now cheap to satisfy: the slot has had
three names in a week, so canon §0 records that **the answer is no fourth name**.

# Appendix H — The Deck

## *Fifty-two moves you can hold in your hand*

You do not need to buy anything. Take any standard deck of playing cards. This appendix tells you what each card means. Draw one a week, or pull one when a moment in your life calls for it, and play the move it names.

---

## How the deck is built

**The four suits are the four milestones of the WAVE-Spiral** — the same Wake → Clean → Grow → Show you learned in the Shaman's chapter. Every move in this book is, at its root, one of those four motions:

| Suit | Milestone | The move underneath every card in this suit |
|---|---|---|
| ♠ Spades | **Wake** | Notice the signal. Name the game, the feeling, the thing nobody has said. |
| ♥ Hearts | **Clean** | Strip the distortion. Get to the clear read before you act. |
| ♦ Diamonds | **Grow** | Let it teach you. Integrate the lesson so it changes you. |
| ♣ Clubs | **Show** | Make the move. Act, repair, hand it forward. |

**The Face is the lens.** Each card names which of the six Faces knows how to play it — Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat, Sage. The Face is the voice in your ear; the suit is what the move is *for*.

**The Player is you.** The seventh Face, the Player, is not on a card — the Player is the one holding the deck. The Player's loop *is* the rules of the game (see *How to play*, below).

**Some cards are campaign prompts.** Not every card is a Face-move. A handful in each suit are open questions about the work you are actually doing — your *campaigns* across the four domains (gathering resources, organizing, direct action, raising awareness). These are the cards that connect the deck to your real life.

---

## How to play — the Player's loop

The Player's five moves are not cards; they are how you run the deck:

1. **Review** — Draw a card (in sequence, or pulled for the moment). Read the move.
2. **Discern** — Where in your life does this move actually apply this week?
3. **Design** — Name the one concrete thing you'll do: *"When X shows up, I'll do this move instead of my old one."*
4. **Build** — Do it. Before you feel ready.
5. **Pass On** — Write the back of the card. Then, when it's solid, hand the move to someone else.

**The back of every card is a BAR capture** — fill it after you play the move:

> **Breakthrough:** what signal or recognition arrived?
> **Action:** what move did you make?
> **Reflection:** what changed when you moved?
> **Sustain:** what will you keep doing?
> *Date: ___  Revisited: ___*

Play one card a week and the deck is a 52-week year. Replay it next year and the same card means something deeper — that's the design.

## A note on the 52 and the 64

This deck of **52** is the part you *play* — the practiceable moves, one for every week of a year. There is a deeper layer of **64** (the eight inner gates crossed with each other, the hexagram codex) that you *consult* rather than play — for shadow work and the slower questions. That layer lives in the companion app, not in this book. The two share the same language of milestones, Faces, and gates; they are different instruments for different moments. For now, you have everything you need: a standard deck, this appendix, and one move a week.

*(The deeper version of any move on these cards — its full mechanics, its five-channel variations, the eight-gate walk it belongs to — lives in the chapters and the other appendices. The card is the handle. The book is the blade.)*

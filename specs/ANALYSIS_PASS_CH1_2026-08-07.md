# Analysis pass — ch1, the worked example

**2026-08-07.** `LIKE WILBER` and `LIKE RAO` from `marginalia/specs/REVISION_INSTRUMENT.md`
Part 4, run on the manuscript for the first time. `SPEC_HUMOR_PASS` §7 recorded on
2026-07-31 that these dials were correctly placed and that **nothing in the editorial system
ever called them.** This is the first call.

**The guardrail governs every finding below:** *an analysis dial always round-trips. It
changes the understanding, and the sentence comes back in house voice.* `LIKE WILBER` is
never an instruction to write like Wilber, and `LIKE RAO` is never an instruction to write
like Rao. Both produce a finding, written as Wendell.

**ch1 first, as one chapter, so the form can be corrected before eight more run on it.**

---

## W1 · `LIKE WILBER` — the book's developmental spine is never introduced

**`altitude` runs 68 times in the body and ch1 uses it zero times.**

| ch1 | ch2 | ch3 | ch4 | ch5 | ch6 | ch7 | ch8 | ch9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | 1 | 4 | 6 | 2 | 7 | 14 | **31** | 3 |

**Its first appearance assumes the reader already has it.** `ch3:243` — *"This altitude
fully sanctions Feeling in its collective form."* No sentence before that one says what an
altitude is.

**It is defined once, in back matter.** `back_matter/glossary.md:14` — *"**Altitude** — The
developmental level a move is made from. Each Face works native material at its own
altitude."* That is a good definition sitting where a reader meets it only if she goes
looking, and Jordan *"will skim theory."*

**The finding is not that the word is missing from ch1. It is that ch1 introduces every
other frame in the book and skips this one.** Tokens, tickets, the prize counter, the Trust
System, the three games, the six Faces, and Emotional Alchemy all get named and taught in
ch1 — `ch1:117` even does it explicitly: *"That conversion has a name: Emotional Alchemy,
the engine under the whole book."* The developmental spine gets nothing.

**What `LIKE WILBER` asks for here:** name the altitude, and be exact about the distinction
being drawn. The distinction the book actually needs is that **a Face is not a level.** Six
Faces sit side by side; altitude runs underneath all six. A reader who meets *the Regent's
altitude* at ch5 without that distinction will read the six Faces as a ladder, which is the
one reading the book cannot afford.

**Proposed:** one sentence in ch1's **The Six Faces** section, in the body, doing what
`ch1:117` does for alchemy. Not a colour, not a tier number — the `No Orange substrate`
constraint holds, and the book carries **zero** colour names by design.

## R1 · `LIKE RAO` — the chapter's sharpest structural claim has no name

**`ch1:165`:**

> My fluency was the best luck that arrangement ever had. Every hour I spent getting good at
> the language was an hour nobody spent asking what had changed, and getting good cost them
> nothing. I paid for the lessons. They kept the budget line, and I stood at the counter as
> the standing proof that it pays.

**That is a named mechanism everywhere except in having a name.** It has two parties, a
transfer, a cost that falls on one side and a saving that lands on the other, and it is the
engine of the whole *designers* passage that E2 was closed to deliver.

**ch1 names everything else it teaches.** The Token System · The Ticket System · The Trust
System · the prize counter · marked applause · non-renewable tokens · Emotional Alchemy ·
your home face, your myth, your quest, your shadow. **This one stays a paragraph**, which
means a reader who wants to point at it later has to re-narrate it.

**`LIKE RAO` is exactly this move: find the shape, coin the term that carries the load** —
and then, per the guardrail, write the term as Wendell rather than as Rao. Wendell's
coinages are plain concrete compounds sitting inside the book's own economy: *prize counter*,
*marked applause*, *non-renewable tokens*. A coinage here has to join that money language,
which `ch1:163` already runs hard — *budget line*, *costs money*, *gets bought*, *collect*.

**Two candidates, and this is a canon decision rather than a line edit:**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **the fluency dividend** | what the arrangement earns every hour you spend getting better at describing it | sits in the money language; reads as a term the book already had |
| **fluency rent** | what you pay to keep standing at the counter | sharper, and slightly wrong — rent buys occupancy, and this buys nothing |

**Recommend `the fluency dividend`, and flag that coining a term is Wendell's call, not a
line edit I should make.**

## What ch1 did not turn up

**No contested claim presented as settled.** `LIKE WILBER`'s second job is marking what is
genuinely contested, and ch1 already does it in its own voice at the two places it matters:
`ch1:58` — *"I know how that reads. It was true about me, and I had the framework that was
supposed to prevent it"* — and `ch1:167` — *"What I cannot do is get you the owner. I have
not worked that out, and anybody who tells you they have is selling you a better place in
the same line."* That is the dial's output already present without the dial.

**No fuzzy distinction in the three games.** Chance, Skill and Passion each earn their
category in their own words after the 2026-08-05 four-beat rebuild, and Passion's is
mechanical rather than tonal: it pays no tickets, so its entire payout is renewable token.

## The form, for the other eight chapters

Two findings per chapter is the working expectation rather than a quota. Each one carries:

1. **The measurement** that makes it checkable, not an impression.
2. **What the dial asks for** — altitude/contested/distinction for Wilber, shape/term for Rao.
3. **A proposed edit**, or, where the finding is a coinage, **candidates and a note that it is
   Wendell's ruling.**
4. **What the chapter did *not* turn up**, so a clean chapter reads as measured rather than skipped.

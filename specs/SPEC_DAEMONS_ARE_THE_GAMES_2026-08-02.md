# SPEC — The daemons are the games, the domains are the where

**2026-08-02. Wendell:**

> *"I think the trust frame moves away from currency and now the daemons are the games
> themselves. If you ally with someone's Protector you're helping them with their physical
> survival. If you're allying with their Controller you're helping them achieve their
> standards. If you're allying with their Skeptic you're aligning with their doubts and
> suspicions but are also creating space for them to wonder. Allying with their Fixer means
> helping them to repair or heal or hold in place aspects of their lives they want to keep,
> etc etc for all the other daemons. And the domains are WHERE you get to ally with their
> daemons. The domains then become the hows."*

**Trust stops being a currency.** No coins, no limit, no slot. The arcade keeps tokens,
tickets and the two counters, all of which describe the reader's own side. Everything
about the other person moves into daemon language, which the book already has.

---

## 1 · The seven games

Each row is canon. The job column is quoted from `ch2:256`–`294`; the third column is what
Wendell named, completed for the three he left at *etc*.

| Their daemon | Its job, in canon | Allying with it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Protector** | *"to keep you alive… the body's embodied sense of self-preservation"* | their physical safety. The actual body, the actual danger, the exit that works |
| **Controller** | *"decides how you live. It sets the standard for what's good and what's bad"* | helping them meet **their** standard. Not yours, and not the one you would pick for them |
| **Skeptic** | *"to doubt, so you don't get fooled"* | taking their doubt seriously as information, and leaving space for them to wonder rather than closing it for them |
| **Fixer/Healer** | *"to repair what's broken, and to hold something in place"* | helping them repair what they want repaired, or **hold** what they want kept |
| **Emotional Body** | *"where feeling gets processed into meaning"* | being somewhere the feeling can run clean, instead of getting buried or spilling sideways |
| **Victim** | *"to win sympathy through stories"* | letting the story get told and felt with, without collapsing into it or correcting it |
| **Damaged Self** | *"to take the damage… the Protector's last resort"* | not asking it to carry more, and helping sort the damage that builds from the damage that only breaks |

**Seven distinct games, not one relationship.** *Being trusted* was always too coarse.
Nobody earns generic trust. **You earn a specific guard's trust by being safe for the
thing that guard protects.**

- A Protector is convinced by physical predictability, never by sincerity.
- A Skeptic is convinced by being told what you do not know.
- A Damaged Self is convinced by load coming off, and by nobody making it explain.

Which means Jordan can be highly trusted by one of somebody's daemons and read as a threat
by another, **in the same conversation** — and that is not a contradiction, it is the
normal case.

## 2 · The grammar this completes

The book has had three systems and no stated division of labour between them. This gives
each one a distinct job:

| System | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Faces** (six) | **who you are being** — what capacity you bring |
| **Daemons** (seven) | **which game is on** — what is being protected, on both sides |
| **Domains** (four) | **where the move lands** — Gather Resources, Skillful Organizing, Direct Action, Raise Awareness |

**The domains stop being a taxonomy of activism and become the *how*.** `APPENDIX_A`
already crosses domains against the six Faces in four *developmental arc* tables. It has
never had the third axis, which is the one that says what the move is *for*.

### Daemon × domain produces a specific move

The multiplication is where this earns its place:

- **Protector × Direct Action** — putting your body between them and the thing.
- **Protector × Gather Resources** — rent, a spare room, a working lock. Safety that is
  material rather than emotional.
- **Controller × Skillful Organizing** — building the structure that lets them hit their
  own standard without holding everything themselves.
- **Skeptic × Raise Awareness** — asking out loud the question they have suspected and
  not voiced.
- **Emotional Body × Raise Awareness** — bearing witness, which is the domain doing the
  daemon's job exactly.
- **Damaged Self × Gather Resources** — taking weight off, in a form that does not require
  them to describe the weight.

**Seven daemons by four domains is twenty-eight cells, and the book must not print
twenty-eight cells.** Print the two axes and three or four worked multiplications; the
reader generates the rest. A grid that size stops being a grammar and becomes homework.

## 3 · Why the inner work is the training, mechanically

**Your daemon decides which of theirs you can meet.**

Two Protectors cannot meet. If yours is holding the joystick, theirs reads a braced body
across the table and does its job. Your Skeptic online turns their Skeptic into an
opponent. Your Victim online meets their Victim and both of you tell stories.

`ch2:339` already says the half of this that faces inward: *"A daemon you befriend joins
your party. A daemon left running on its own stays a saboteur."* **The outward half is
that a daemon left running is also the thing that makes their daemon shut the door.**

So befriending your own is not self-improvement and it is not preparation in the abstract.
**It is the only way to have a free hand when you arrive.**

### And the technique is already written, for the wrong subject

`ch2:308`:

> *"You do not fight the Protector, and you do not shove past it. You thank it, and you
> help it tell a real threat from a social one."*

That is the protocol for meeting a guard. The chapter teaches it on Jordan's own Protector
and **never says it is the same protocol she will use on everybody she ever tries to
help.** One sentence connects them, and the chapter is otherwise already written.

## 4 · What this replaces

| Superseded | By |
|---|---|
| trust as a currency, coins, a limit | seven guards, each convinced by a different thing |
| *"how many coins the machine will take"* — `ch1:133`, applied today | the person decides; the mechanism is in ch2 |
| the people are the machine | the people have guards, and the guards are the games |
| my anti-farming safeguard | a Protector's job **is** spotting people who look safe and are not |

**What survives from the arcade unchanged:** tokens as your energy, renewable and
non-renewable, tickets, the counter marked applause, praise versus encouragement, the
three games of Chance, Skill and Passion. All of that is Jordan's own side of the floor and
none of it strains.

---

## 5 · Where it lands

**ch1** — the Trust System section applied today needs the coin-limit language removed and
the mechanism handed forward. It can say the person decides and that ch2 says how.

**ch2** — the new section. Their daemons are the gate; the seven games; your daemon
decides which of theirs you can meet; `ch2:308` is the move pointed outward. **This is
now carrying the chapter's thesis**, so it is bigger than the section it replaces.

**ch3–ch8** — each Face meets a differently-shaped guard, and each chapter can name which
of the seven it most often faces. The Shaman works on the Emotional Body. The Challenger
walks into a Protector every time. The Regent meets a Fixer holding something in place.

**ch9** — one person, not an audience, is the smallest possible ask of somebody's guard.

**`APPENDIX_A`** — the four domains gain the third axis. This is the appendix's own
completion, not an addition to it.

# Allyship at Work — the searchable follow-up

**Wendell, 2026-08-27:** *"let's also make a note to start drafting a follow up to Mastering the
Game of Allyship but specifically for at Work so we can offer something that is searchable and
leads people to my speaking page. I think this is a good idea."*

**A note, not a plan.** Recorded now so it survives, and sized so nobody mistakes it for a
commitment.

---

## Why it is a good idea, stated more precisely than "searchable"

**It resolves a conflict the KDP analysis could only route around.**
`ANALYSIS_KDP_DESCRIPTION_2026-08-27.md` §3 found that this book has **two audiences who want
opposite descriptions** — the exhausted helper who searches the symptom, and the L&D buyer who
searches the category — and its recommendation was to **tune for the ICA and send the corporate
buyer to `/speaking`**. That is a workaround. It leaves the corporate demand unserved by any book.

**A second title serves it directly.** Two books, two descriptions, two keyword sets, two category
placements, **and neither one has to hedge.**

**The search vocabulary is genuinely better on that side.** *Allyship at work*, *workplace
inclusion*, *ERG*, *employee resource group*, *DEI training*, *manager training*, *inclusive
leadership* — these are **budget-holder search terms**, typed by people whose employer pays. That
is a different and more valuable kind of traffic than the symptom vocabulary, and it is exactly
the traffic `/speaking` converts.

**And the funnel is short.** A manager who finds a book called *Allyship at Work*, reads it, and
wants the author in the building has **one step to take**, not three. Today that person has to
find a book about a game, recognise it applies to their workplace, and then discover the speaking
page. `OPSBACKLOG` TRACK B already says three or four engagements pays for the entire print run.

## The strategic case that is stronger than the funnel case

**It is the book's own thesis about audiences, applied to its own distribution.** The first book
deliberately conceals its architecture because the ICA is Green and allergic to hierarchy. **An
Amber and Orange audience has the opposite allergy** — they want the framework named, the ROI
stated, the ladder visible. `IDEAL_READER_MATRIX` documents both.

**So this is not a rewrite for a different market. It is the same material at a different
altitude, spoken in the vocabulary that altitude trusts.** Which is, precisely, what the six Faces
argue you have to do.

## Corrected 2026-08-27 — three premises I did not check

**The first version of this note argued against starting.** Wendell corrected all three of its
premises, and each correction is load-bearing.

**1 · Pre-production is not announcing.** The note said *"a new title announced while four
obligations are open is the exact shape of the thing #29 apologised for."* **Nobody is
announcing.** Starting work is invisible to the 361, and the objection only existed because I read
*start drafting* as *publish*.

**2 · It may be what pays for the fulfilment.** Wendell: *"if say in translating the book to the
new ICA it comes out before we fulfil everything and goes like gangbusters it might be the project
that ends up paying for the fulfilment of the first."* **I had it as competing for money.** The
print run needs roughly $6,000 and `OPSBACKLOG` TRACK B says three or four engagements covers it —
**and this title's whole purpose is to produce engagements.** It is on the same side of the ledger
as the debt, not the other one.

**3 · It is the long-term direction, not a side quest.** Wendell: *"it's the one that would let me
move more directly into working in DEI and Belonging long term or doing contracts."* `OPSBACKLOG`
TRACK E already says a role in that field is the only path to $6k/month that does not depend on
the book selling. **This title is the credential for that track**, which makes it strategy rather
than opportunism.

**And the framework already exists.** *"We just need to do a translation."* Selection and
re-voicing of material that is written, reviewed and shipped — a different order of work from
writing a book, and the reason the timeline objection was wrong on its own terms.

## What it must not become

**Not a lighter version.** A shorter *Mastering the Game of Allyship* with the games removed is a
worse book that cannibalises the first one and disappoints anyone who bought either.

**Not a second first-printing.** The material exists — the four domains in Appendix A, Appendix B's
quests, the Architect and the Diplomat, the continuity problem `OPSBACKLOG` E2 names as the thing
organisations actually have. **The work is selection and re-voicing, not invention.**

**Not launched ahead of the debts without a word to the 361.** The obligations are real — 247
printed books, 16 audiobooks, 16 workbooks, 53 courses — and the risk is not the work, it is the
optics of a *release* landing while they are open. **The remedy is one sentence in the launch, not
a delay:** the backers hear it first, and hear what it funds. That converts the problem into the
strongest thing the launch could say.

## Drafting in public is drafting

**Wendell: *"there are many ways to draft a book. Podcasting and blogging is exactly that and what
I'd happily do."*** The first version of this note said *do not draft a book to find out whether a
book is wanted*, which set a false opposition. **Episodes and posts are a drafting mode**, and for
this author they are the one with the shortest distance between thinking and artifact.

**So the recommendation inverts.** Draft it in public from the start, because every property that
makes public drafting good for the book is also what this title needs:

| public drafting gives | why this title needs it |
|---|---|
| a dated body of workplace-vocabulary text | the search demand the whole premise rests on, tested as it is built |
| episodes and posts that stand alone | each one is a thing to send an L&D lead who asked *what does he actually say* |
| a growing archive under the right terms | `/speaking` gains a corpus rather than a page |
| readers before a manuscript | the corporate ICA, met before the book is fixed |

**One thing to run alongside it rather than before it.** The keyword work in
`ANALYSIS_KDP_DESCRIPTION` §5a — an hour with Amazon's autocomplete — **so the episodes carry the
nouns people actually type.** Not a gate on starting; a way of pointing the first ten episodes.

**And the sequencing that already exists.** `OPSBACKLOG` TRACK B lists ACCT, WRMC, Oregon Outdoor
School and Kansas Leadership Center as live outreach. **Those rooms are the book's field research
and its first audience at once**, so the episodes can report from them as they happen rather than
waiting for the book to be finished to have something to say.

# Allyship at Work — the searchable follow-up

**Wendell, 2026-08-27:** *"let's also make a note to start drafting a follow up to Mastering the
Game of Allyship but specifically for at Work so we can offer something that is searchable and
leads people to my speaking page. I think this is a good idea."*

**A note, not a plan.** Recorded now so it survives, and sized so nobody mistakes it for a
commitment.

---

## Why it is a good idea, stated more precisely than "searchable"

**It resolves a conflict the KDP analysis could only route around.**
`ANALYSIS_KDP_DESCRIPTION_2026-08-27.md` §3 found that this book has **two audiences who want
opposite descriptions** — the exhausted helper who searches the symptom, and the L&D buyer who
searches the category — and its recommendation was to **tune for the ICA and send the corporate
buyer to `/speaking`**. That is a workaround. It leaves the corporate demand unserved by any book.

**A second title serves it directly.** Two books, two descriptions, two keyword sets, two category
placements, **and neither one has to hedge.**

**The search vocabulary is genuinely better on that side.** *Allyship at work*, *workplace
inclusion*, *ERG*, *employee resource group*, *DEI training*, *manager training*, *inclusive
leadership* — these are **budget-holder search terms**, typed by people whose employer pays. That
is a different and more valuable kind of traffic than the symptom vocabulary, and it is exactly
the traffic `/speaking` converts.

**And the funnel is short.** A manager who finds a book called *Allyship at Work*, reads it, and
wants the author in the building has **one step to take**, not three. Today that person has to
find a book about a game, recognise it applies to their workplace, and then discover the speaking
page. `OPSBACKLOG` TRACK B already says three or four engagements pays for the entire print run.

## The strategic case that is stronger than the funnel case

**It is the book's own thesis about audiences, applied to its own distribution.** The first book
deliberately conceals its architecture because the ICA is Green and allergic to hierarchy. **An
Amber and Orange audience has the opposite allergy** — they want the framework named, the ROI
stated, the ladder visible. `IDEAL_READER_MATRIX` documents both.

**So this is not a rewrite for a different market. It is the same material at a different
altitude, spoken in the vocabulary that altitude trusts.** Which is, precisely, what the six Faces
argue you have to do.

## What it must not become

**Not a lighter version.** A shorter *Mastering the Game of Allyship* with the games removed is a
worse book that cannibalises the first one and disappoints anyone who bought either.

**Not a second first-printing.** The material exists — the four domains in Appendix A, Appendix B's
quests, the Architect and the Diplomat, the continuity problem `OPSBACKLOG` E2 names as the thing
organisations actually have. **The work is selection and re-voicing, not invention.**

**Not before the debts.** 247 printed books, 16 audiobooks, 16 workbooks and 53 courses are owed
to people who paid in 2021. **A new title announced while four obligations are open is the exact
shape of the thing #29 apologised for**, and this audience has already watched it happen once.

## What would have to be true first

1. **The thirty-day course finishes**, since it is the current build and it has the 53.
2. **The print run is funded**, or at least ordered, so the new title is not competing with a
   promise.
3. **One speaking engagement lands**, because the book should be written from real rooms rather
   than at them. Three or four are already the print-run target.
4. **The keyword research from `ANALYSIS_KDP_DESCRIPTION` §5a is actually run** — an hour with
   Amazon's autocomplete — because the whole premise of this title is that the demand is
   measurably there. **If the workplace terms do not autocomplete, the premise is wrong and this
   note should be closed rather than acted on.**

## The cheapest possible first test

**Do not draft a book to find out whether a book is wanted.**

The one-sheets exist. `/speaking` exists. **Publish the corporate one-sheet's argument as a long
article under the workplace vocabulary, and watch whether it draws the traffic this note assumes.**
That is a weekend, it costs nothing, and it answers the only question that matters — **is there
search demand this author can serve** — before anybody writes a chapter.

**If it draws, the article is also the book's first section.** If it does not, the note closes and
nothing was spent.

**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Type:** Desk-simulation regression against applied canonical prose; not human-reader evidence  
**Reading surface:** `manuscript/ch1.md`–`manuscript/ch9.md` at commit `b0ad492`  
**Card:** `P-08 — Authority Holder`  
**Question:** Can a well-resourced executive move from “I empower people inside
my authority” to a decision structure in which affected people can alter what
he controls, without pretending he can hand away authority he does not hold?

## Passing rule

For P-08, listening sessions, an advisory group, a workshop purchase, or a
polished implementation plan are insufficient. A working outcome must make all
of the following real before the vendor decision closes:

1. a public account of which decisions are open and which are leadership-owned;
2. compensated and accessible participation;
3. defined authority for the staff-and-patient group to change language and
   disability-access criteria, including a pause or scope-change recommendation;
4. the means to exercise that authority; and
5. a written response to the group's recommendations, including refusals.

## Counterpart fidelity

The staff-and-patient group can decline participation, reject the first charter,
demand compensation or accessibility changes, recommend a pause, contest the
problem definition, and decline further relational contact after repair. None of
these responses relieves P-08 of his obligation to state what is open, make the
means of participation real, or own a closed leadership decision.

## Action forecast after finishing the book

**Within 7 days:** P-08 issues a decision-rights map for the vendor process:
open and closed decisions; who decides each open item; participant
compensation/accessibility support; the independent facilitator-selection
process; the pause/scope-change route; and the written-response commitment.

**Within 30 days:** The compensated group tests the map, rejects or changes at
least one access criterion, and may recommend reopening shortlist or scope.
P-08 either accepts the change within his authority or records a specific,
leadership-owned refusal. He does not call the latter co-design.

## Verdict

**SIM-008 passes the applied whole-book regression.** The earlier timing risk
has been resolved at the desk-simulation layer: P-08 encounters the distinction
between advice and authority in Chapter 3, before the six-week vendor decision
closes, and Chapters 6, 7, and 9 reinforce it under design, rupture, and
handoff pressure.

This is evidence of internal coherence on a fictional composite, not evidence
that actual authority-holding readers will retain or enact the moves under
institutional pressure. That remains the planned post-ship v2 human-reader
calibration question.

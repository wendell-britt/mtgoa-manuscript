# Allyship Simulation — Closing Synthesis

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Desk-simulation release pass complete
**Reading surface:** Canonical `manuscript/ch1.md`–`manuscript/ch9.md`
**Scope:** Fictional composite personas used to stress-test whether the book
converts a reader’s allyship practice into an accountable action. This is not
human-subject research, reader validation, or a claim about any real person.

## Decision

**Freeze the desk-simulation phase for this release.** The simulations found
three repeated action-conversion failures, each received an approved canonical
revision, and the resulting applied reading surface passed its targeted
regressions. No additional persona reruns are required before release.

Human-reader calibration is deliberately deferred to a post-ship v2 loop. It
will test whether readers retain and enact these moves when a live relationship,
institutional deadline, or power difference makes them costly.

## Portfolio and outcomes

| Reader pattern | Primary test | Outcome on applied surface |
|---|---|---|
| **P-03 / P-04** — readers at risk of disclosure-as-burden or consultation-as-action | Can the reader name a live condition without forcing intimacy or confusing advice with shared power? | **Pass.** Ch2 and Ch3 route the action to a bounded, corrigible move. |
| **P-06 / P-07** — no-standing or adjacent readers | Can the reader contribute without seizing a role, demanding education, or calling clean step-back avoidance? | **Pass.** The Ch3 relational-placement gate carries permission, bounded contribution, preparation, and step-back across the later Faces. |
| **P-08** — authority-holder buyer persona | Can an executive exchange advisory participation for defined decision rights before a live decision closes? | **Pass.** Ch3 makes open/closed decisions, affected people’s change authority, and the means to use it explicit; Ch6, Ch7, and Ch9 reinforce the transfer. |
| **P-09** — recently corrected manager | Can a power-holder repair without treating accountability as permission to pursue access? | **Pass.** Ch7 separates staying accountable from insisting on interpersonal access. |
| **P-10** — withdrawing peer | Can a peer respect a boundary without using “space” to abandon a repair path the harmed person has specified? | **Pass.** Ch7 makes the other person’s stated timing, channel, and distance governing. |

## Canonical changes approved from the simulations

| Chapter | Applied change | Failure it closes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ch2 — Forest** | A relational-placement bridge before the Second Move: assess whether speaking is yours to name, helps rather than burdens, and points to a condition, need, or boundary; choose a smaller move when trust is insufficient. | Forced intimacy, self-aware disclosure that burdens the listener, and avoidance falsely framed as safety. |
| **Ch3 — Shaman** | A portable quest gate: locate standing; name the decision shaping others’ conditions and whether it is open; give affected people authority and means to change an open decision, or plainly own a closed one. | Seized standing, advisory participation mislabeled shared power, and scope-washing by authority holders. |
| **Ch7 — Diplomat** | A boundary-respect clarification after Repair’s “Stay”: change your part and stay reachable on terms the other person chooses; do not insist on access. | Repair used either to pressure a harmed person or to rationalize disappearance. |

## Action model now supported

The applied book carries the tested reader through one coherent sequence:

1. **Notice without outsourcing the feeling.**
2. **Place oneself before acting:** name what is yours to name, offer, or change.
3. **Make the decision structure honest:** open decisions carry affected people’s
   real authority and access to use it; closed decisions are leadership-owned.
4. **Change conditions, not merely tone or process.**
5. **Receive correction and repair without extracting access, reassurance, or
   closeness.**
6. **Hand over authority to alter, stop, or replace the design.**

For the ebook-to-service pathway, an inquiry about workshops, coaching, or
consulting is never evidence of allyship. The appropriate entry condition is an
open decision, compensated and accessible participation, and a written loop
from feedback to decision. This protects both the buyer and the people affected
by that buyer’s choices.

## Release boundary

This simulation pass supports **internal coherence**, not efficacy. It does not
establish that actual readers will:

- recognize themselves in a composite card;
- take the action under employment, reputational, or relational pressure;
- interpret each move in the intended order without facilitation; or
- experience the book as safe or useful in every context.

No new canonical prose is proposed from this synthesis.

## V2 human-reader calibration backlog

Run this only after release and with voluntary, appropriately supported
participants. It is not a release gate.

1. Recruit readers across role, standing, and organizational-power differences.
2. Record a pre-read live issue and the reader’s intended first action.
3. Check after Ch2, Ch3, Ch7, and Ch9 whether they can distinguish: disclosure
   from burden; advice from authority; accountability from access; and handoff
   from power to alter.
4. Conduct a 7–14-day follow-up on the action actually attempted, counterpart
   response, correction path, and any new stuck point.
5. Treat withdrawal, refusal, or a decision to step back as valid outcomes when
   relational placement makes them the accountable move.

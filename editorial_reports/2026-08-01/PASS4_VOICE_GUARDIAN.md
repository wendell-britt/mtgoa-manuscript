## 1 · The five that do not pass

### LE-4 · `ch2:107` · **REVISE MINIMALLY — the fix did not fix it**

| | |
|---|---|
| before | Most people turn back. The ones who don't push through instead of going through: |
| applied | Most people turn back. The ones who don't **often** push through instead of going through: |

**This one is a live regression and the only one of the 33 that fails on meaning rather
than on voice.** The flag was that *the ones who don't* garden-paths into *don't push
through*. Inserting *often* produces a second reading — *the ones who do not often push
through* — which is the opposite of the sentence and is now the more natural parse of
the two. I made the sentence worse while closing the flag.

**Proposed:** "Most people turn back. **Of the ones who don't, most** push through
instead of going through: they override the sensation, run on fumes, and eventually the
body stops cooperating."

*Assessment:* specific, keeps the two-beat rhythm of the original pair, and the
quantifier is now stated honestly instead of smuggled into an adverb.

### LE-7 · `ch3:896` · **REVISE MINIMALLY — it breaks a book-wide convention**

| | |
|---|---|
| before | *Aim the Awareness* becomes: **I will tell Dana…** |
| applied | **A Show Up card like** *Aim the Awareness* becomes: **I will tell Dana…** |

The quest formula `*Card Name* becomes:` runs in **six chapters** — `ch3:896`,
`ch4:742`, `ch5:647`, `ch6`, `ch7:726`, `ch8:715`. ch3 is where the reader meets it. My
hedge fixes the symptom (a card name arriving as known) by breaking the pattern at its
first appearance, so the other five now read as variants of a form the reader never
cleanly saw.

**Proposed:** restore the bare formula — `*Aim the Awareness* becomes:` — and close the
real defect instead, which is that *Aim the Awareness* returns exactly one hit in the
book. **Name a card ch3 actually taught.** That is your call, not mine; the five WAVE
moves are the obvious source.

*Assessment:* the applied version is not wrong, it is merely the version that protects
my flag at the cost of the book's own pattern.

### LE-9 · `ch3:691` · **REVISE MINIMALLY — the beat went out with the defect**

| | |
|---|---|
| before | So take the move **where it counts,** out of the forest, where nothing costs you and no one is watching, and into the places allyship actually happens. |
| applied | So take the move out of the forest, where nothing costs you and no one is watching, and into the places allyship actually happens. |

The modifier was attached to the wrong clause and cutting it fixed that. It also cut the
phrase the sentence was *for* — the chapter's Feeling-to-Function hinge now ends on
*actually happens*, which is a description where it used to be a judgment.

**Proposed:** "So take the move out of the forest, where nothing costs you and no one is
watching, and into the places allyship actually happens, **where it counts.**"

*Assessment:* same fix, and the sentence gets its turn back at the end, which is where
the anchor says this register lives.

### LE-14 · `ch4:360` · **REVISE MINIMALLY — an imperative in a paragraph of declaratives**

| | |
|---|---|
| before | You let the field settle. You do not retraumatize the other person by returning to the confrontation before the moment has passed. |
| applied | You let the field settle. **Do not go back** to the confrontation before the moment has passed: **returning early retraumatizes the person you were confronting.** |

The flag was real — a rule written as a description. The fix overcorrected: the Rest
stage now carries the paragraph's only imperative and then explains itself after a
colon, which is the one move the anchor set never makes. Emotional temperature drops
from *you are doing this* to *here is why you should*.

**Proposed:** "You let the field settle. **Going back before the moment has passed
retraumatizes the person you confronted.**"

*Assessment:* keeps the declarative register and the reason, drops both the command and
the explanatory colon; the consequence is the subject, which is where the force is.

### LE-26 · `ch7:529` · **REVISE MINIMALLY — the abstraction is the smoothing**

| | |
|---|---|
| before | Care without impact is attendance: warm, dependable, and doing nothing. Impact without care is the Challenger's altitude imported into a conversation that needed this one. |
| applied | **You know both failure states: attendance on one side, the Challenger's altitude imported on the other.** |

The verbatim repeat is gone, which was the flag. What replaced it is a summary — *both
failure states*, *one side*, *the other* — and it is measurably less alive than what it
replaced. This is the exact failure the Guardian exists to catch: technically cleaner,
emotionally flatter.

**Proposed:** "You know both failure states: **the warm attendance that changes nothing,
and the true thing said in a way that ends the conversation.**"

*Assessment:* not a verbatim repeat, still concrete on both poles, and it keeps the
sting the recall was recalling.

## 3 · What the exemption covered, and what it does not excuse

Three of them are worth naming because a Head's licence is not unlimited:

- **LE-24 `ch7:77`** split a 56-word sentence in Elian's treatise into two. Elian's

  words and still turns on a semicolon.

  register, and this is the one place it sat on a safety guardrail. Worth a second look
  by whoever owns the Horizon voice.

  Corin's treatise. If the misuse was characterisation, this is the edit that removed
  it, and only you can say.

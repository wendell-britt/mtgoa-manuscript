**The chapter's characteristic failure.** When ch2 reaches for unearned agency, it reaches for the same move twice, at two different scales. At the paragraph scale, Section 1's critique of "the old allyship" — the very section arguing that the old model failed — personifies that model as the actor doing the failing: "The old allyship tells you what to do," "does not ask," "assumes," rather than naming the trainers, workshops, and books that actually told, asked, and assumed. Section 2 repeats the shape with "this group reward," "this system punish," and "that structure trains" — three of the four verbs the registry names verbatim as illegal for Grade 6 ("Culture does not train, reward, or punish"). The chapter that is *about* holding the joystick keeps handing the joystick to the abstraction it is diagnosing. At the daemon scale, the same instinct shows up in miniature: the Protector "calls" a trade safety and the Emotional Body "tells" you something true — both licensed parts, both given a word-shaped verb exactly where ch2's own roster ruling says the embodied three should stay wordless. It is a small, precise version of the chapter-scale problem: dropping the somatic register the whole chapter argues for is where the daemon system deletes its own author.

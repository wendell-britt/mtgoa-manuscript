**The chapter's characteristic failure.** When ch6 reaches for unearned agency, it does not reach for the crude version — "the system feels," "the culture wants." It reaches for the *document* idiom: "the report says," "the org chart says," "the system says about itself," "the structure says the relationship should be working," "the map does not tell the whole story." Four separate near-identical constructions, scattered across four sections, all handing a speech verb to a document or system instead of naming what the document actually does (measures, specifies, omits) or who wrote it. The second-most common move is compressing an idea the chapter already earned a paragraph earlier — "the system rewards/punishes," "the system has to teach," "a system that protects itself" — where the mechanism (design, incentive, who built it) was stated one paragraph up and doesn't get restated at the exact joint that needs it. This is a chapter that has mostly internalized the mechanize move — dozens of "the system produces what it's designed to produce, and here's who designed it" constructions are correctly earned — and slips only at the seams between paragraphs, not in its main arguments.

**Density verdict.** The recall net's 32.14/10k reading overstates the real defect concentration by roughly 2–3×. Most of the chapter's raw density is legitimate subject matter: this is a chapter about systems, so "the system," "the structure," and "the pattern" appear constantly, and the great majority of those instances take mechanical verbs with the mechanism stated in the same sentence or the paragraph before it (the Token/Ticket move, done correctly, over and over — "the system produces the outcomes it's designed to produce... you inherited it... the person who designed it had different goals than you do" is the chapter's thesis paragraph and it is fully earned). The Polarity Encounter's "Structure says X / Agency says Y" device and the Section 1–2 village-as-character parable both look like violations to a bag-of-words scan and are not — they are the book's established rhetorical devices, not the systemic-agency defect the audit targets. Underneath that noise, though, there is a real, if modest, cluster: nine genuine Tier 3 sites and seven Tier 2 sites, concentrated almost entirely in the "X says" idiom and in a handful of paragraph-joint compressions. This is not the densest chapter in the book for real defects; it is the densest chapter for the *subject matter* that makes a crude scanner fire.

### [3] line 128 — `a system` (Grade 6) + `protects` / `preventing` [social-causal / intention]
**Current:** > Blame builds a system that protects itself by preventing anyone from asking the question.
**Reading:** "Protect" is a named-illegal social-causal verb at Grade 6, and "preventing anyone from asking" hands the system deliberate, self-interested intention. This is a general claim about how blame-structures work, not about one named organization, so restoring a specific human actor would be false precision. The real mechanism is that asking costs the asker something — that's what actually stops the question, not the system's self-preservation instinct.
**Op:** R6

**Disposition:** fix

- **Line 187 — "the outcomes the incentive structure actually rewards"** — mechanism is on the page in the same paragraph: "the system is working exactly as designed... The person who designed it had different goals than you do." Textbook C1 mechanism-on-page; this is the chapter's own thesis statement and it earns its verbs.
- **Lines 153, 163, 187, 256, 355, 519, 599, 626 — "the system/structure produces / works / degrades / keeps functioning / did what it got built to do"** — all licensed. Each pairs the verb with an explicit "built," "designed," or "the mechanism by which" in the same sentence. This is the Token/Ticket move done correctly and is the great majority of the chapter's "system" density.
- **Lines 207, 209 — "**Structure** says the design did this." / "**Agency** says the person chose."** — the Polarity Encounter's established rhetorical device: voicing two named debate positions, repeated across the book's Polarity framework (see Appendix F cross-reference at line 221). - **Line 374 — "The heat becomes a proposal. The dread becomes a risk register. The sadness becomes a retrospective."** — the jammed-token conversion mechanism stated explicitly; Grade 3/7 currents taking mechanical/directional verbs, fully licensed.
- **Line 407 — "a specific piece of dread was doing my architecture"** — routed through the Emotional Body two sentences later ("the Emotional Body is being *employed*. It is building things"), satisfying the Grade 7-via-Grade 2 license.
- **Line 597 area — see Tier 1 note above** — "wants" as dead-metaphor "needs," not desire.
- **Sections 1–2, throughout (dozens of sites) — "the village listened / wanted / said / started to see / thought itself," "the Architect drew / said / sees / can commit."** — the book's Section-1 parable device (paralleled by every chapter's "why the X left the village" fable, per the file's own section title convention). The village and the Architect function as characters in an allegory, synecdoche for concrete people, not the abstract Grade 6 "institution" the registry's culture/patriarchy/market entries name.

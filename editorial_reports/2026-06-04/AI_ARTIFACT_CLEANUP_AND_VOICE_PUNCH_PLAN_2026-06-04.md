# AI Artifact Cleanup and Voice Punch Plan — 2026-06-04

## Goal

Finish the book by first stripping out as much AI-like prose as possible, then manually punching the voice back up chapter by chapter.

## Core rule

Do not try to make the prose “sound human” in the abstract.

Instead:

1. Remove AI artifacts.
2. Restore Wendell’s sentence habits.
3. Punch the voice manually where the line wants more life.

## What counts as an AI artifact

Use the research report as the guide. The main patterns to remove are:

- contrastive reframes that do all the work for the paragraph
- tidy closers that explain the point one more time
- generic “importance” language
- repetitive sentence shapes
- over-explained transitions
- neutral, polished, assistant-like tone
- vague nouns where a real person, scene, or consequence should be

## What to keep

Keep the parts of the manuscript that already behave like Wendell:

- recognition
- confession
- game mechanics
- reframing that comes from pressure, not from sloganizing
- comic escalation
- specific bodily and emotional detail

## Edit order for the final push

### 1. Lock the style baseline

Before touching the prose, confirm:

- the Wendell anchor corpus
- the deep research report as the style-lint source
- the developmental editorial spec as the style baseline

### 2. Run the style-lint pass

Go chapter by chapter and mark only the spans that actually feel AI-like.

Do not rewrite whole chapters just because one paragraph sounds off.

### 3. Fix the structural items that still block voice

Only the items that protect the spine:

- Ch6 channel reorder and format normalization
- Ch0 consolidation / opening-book stabilization

### 4. Run the voice-anchoring pass

Repair each flagged span using the Wendell moves:

- recognition
- confession
- game mechanics
- reframing
- comic escalation

### 5. Punch the voice manually

After the AI-like material is removed, do the human pass:

- shorten where the sentence is over-explaining
- add specificity where the line is generic
- twist the knife where the moment needs pressure
- add breath where the paragraph needs room

### 6. Final consistency sweep

Check that the manuscript still speaks in one dialect:

- energy ecology
- fuel budget
- alchemy / completion language
- the gate logic
- the comedy/release pattern

## What not to do

- Do not optimize for detector scores.
- Do not “humanize” broadly.
- Do not flatten the manuscript into clean generic prose.
- Do not add new theory unless it is required to fix clarity or voice.

## Completion test

The book is ready for line-by-line copy edits when:

- the obvious AI artifacts are gone
- the prose still holds its structure
- the remaining lines feel like they can be punched up by hand instead of rescued
- each chapter has a clear next pass

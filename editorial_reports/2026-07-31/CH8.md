Chapter 8 is structurally sound at the level of its argument and its handoff, and it is
carrying more damage from retired material than any other chapter I have line-checked.

## Verdict 1 — the bare claim at Move 5 (`ch8:520` stripped = disk 603)

**Confirmed, and worse than "no example."** Move 5 "Hold the Meta Without Losing the
Ground" runs disk 603–615, 308 words, and is the only Move in the chapter with **neither
a worked example nor a test**. The chapter establishes its own teaching template in Moves
1 and 2 — *What it is · Example · Why it matters · Working vs. performed · The test* —
and then sheds parts of it: Move 3 loses the Example and buries its test mid-paragraph,
Move 4 loses the Example, Move 5 loses both. The degradation runs monotonically toward
the move the chapter itself calls "the last move, the one that takes years, the one that
earns the name" (`ch8:605`).

What Jordan gets instead is `ch8:609`, which is the most abstract paragraph in the
chapter: *"You can know you're mortal and the game is older than you and none of this will
matter in a hundred years, and still care."* That is a description of a state, not
something she can copy. The nearest thing to a rep for Move 5 is "The One Rep"
(`ch8:661`), and that rep routes to Move 1 — *say the thing out loud and stay* — so the
chapter's single take-it-into-the-world instruction skips the move it just called mastery.

**What it would take to close it.** The chapter already contains the shape: `ch8:577`,
Move 2's Example, is the only narrated worked example in Section 6 — a named situation,
three meetings, a specific action, a measured outcome ("the thrashing stopped inside ten
minutes"). Move 5 needs one of those plus a two-line **The test:** couplet in the form
Moves 1, 2, and 4 already use. Nothing about the teaching needs redesigning; the template
needs finishing.

## Verdict 2 — the Vulnerable Child

**Survival from retired material. It does not still work.** The line is body text:

> `ch8:191` — "This is the Vulnerable Child's gift, held all the way up. The child who
> wanted to be in the game and see the game."

Canon (`specs/MANUSCRIPT_FILE_CANON.md:101`) says the Vulnerable Child left with the gate
walk and that ch2 now carries seven daemons. Verified against ch2:312–346, the seven are
**Protector, Controller, Skeptic, Fixer/Healer, Emotional Body, Victim, Damaged Self**.
The Vulnerable Child is not among them.

The sentence opens with the definite construction *"This is the Vulnerable Child's gift"* —
grammar that points backward at an introduction the reader never received. The following
clause partially self-glosses ("the child who wanted to be in the game and see the game"),
so the paragraph does not collapse; Jordan can infer a meaning. What she cannot do is
place it. It arrives in Section 3, the concept section, five lines before the chapter
attributes the Sage's whole capacity to it, and it names a member of a roster she was
explicitly given as complete. The likeliest reading for a careful reader is that she
missed something in ch2, which is exactly the wrong thing for the integration chapter to
make her feel.

**Extends beyond ch8.** The same term also survives at `ch9:508` — *"That's the Vulnerable
Child leading. That's progress."* — inside a `→ app` prompt. Two survivals, not one. The
ch9 instance is the more exposed of the two because it is an instruction to the reader.

## Verdict 3 — does the chapter name its own bypassing risk?

**Yes, repeatedly and by name. This is the chapter's strongest defensive work.** The risk
that the Sage's altitude becomes an excuse not to act is named in every section that
teaches:

- `ch8:34` — "It does not apply while harm happens in front of you. In that case, do not
  sit there polishing your interiority. Draw the line, call the help, interrupt the damage."
- `ch8:127` — "seeing becomes a way to avoid being in the game."
- `ch8:425` — "*They wouldn't understand anyway.* That's the distortion talking."
- `ch8:557` — "Do not use this move to stay in your own head while somebody needs a line, a
  decision, or an apology."
- `ch8:601` — "Do not call it maturity when you simply got tired of being accountable."
- `ch8:615` — "Do not use the meta-view to make yourself unaccountable... It's avoidance
  with better vocabulary."

`ch8:34` in particular is the single best sentence in the chapter for Jordan's stated fear,
and it arrives on the second page.

**One residual exposure, and it is placed badly.** The anti-bypass guard is applied
unevenly: Moves 1, 4, and 5 each close on a "Do not…" sentence; Moves 2 and 3 do not, and
neither does the Walk Back. The most bypass-usable passage in the chapter is the Walk
Back's last step:

> `ch8:439` — "**4. Let them not understand — and let that be okay.** The village doesn't
> have to understand what you saw. The gift isn't in their understanding, it's in your
> offering."

Read straight, this is a teaching about surrender. Read by someone looking for permission,
it licenses *I named it once, they didn't get it, that's their work now* — and it is the
**final beat** of the Walk Back, so it is the note the section ends on with no
counterweight after it. Section 3's Polarity Encounter supplies the missing guard at
`ch8:233` ("Somebody in the power game may be a person of enormous developmental range
having a bad week"), but that sits 200 lines earlier and is not pointed at from here.

# Chapter 7 Reverse Outline to Simplified Structure

## Purpose

This reverse outline maps the current Chapter 7 content onto the new MTGOA simplification protocol:

- one idea
- one tool
- multiple real-world tests
- short summary / handoff

The aim is not to preserve every good section.

The aim is to show what becomes the chapter spine, what gets compressed, and what should be moved to a sidebar, appendix, or later product.

## New chapter job

**Chapter 7 should help the reader understand how Sage altitude solves a specific allyship problem:**

- mistaking perspective for action
- using insight as distance
- becoming too good at watching from above
- failing to return to the room where allyship actually happens

## Core promise for the simplified Chapter 7

**Sage helps you see the whole board without turning that view into a hiding place.**

## One tool for Chapter 7

**See → Switch → Serve → Release → Return**

That is the one tool the chapter should keep teaching.

## Proposed simplified Chapter 7 structure

### 1. Promise

One paragraph stating:

- Sage is the altitude that helps you stop mistaking perspective for action
- this matters because allyship often gets stuck in observation, superiority, or escape

### 2. Diagnosis

One short story plus one short explanation:

- the course-building burnout story
- the village misreading wisdom as leaving

### 3. Tool

The chapter's one tool:

- See → Switch → Serve → Release → Return

With a short definition of each move

### 4. Examples

Two or three quick real-world examples:

- a room that is really in a different game than the stated agenda
- a situation where perspective is being used as distance
- a case where the Sage has to come back and stay

### 5. Practice

One "It's Your Turn" style exercise:

- identify the game
- notice where you're above the room
- practice one deliberate switch
- write a BAR or quick reflection

### 6. Summary / Handoff

Short recap:

- what Sage gives you
- what Sage does not give you
- why this sets up Chapter 8

## What to move out or compress heavily

These are the highest-confidence compression candidates:

- the full long exposition on the village and exile
- the repeated warnings about Sage in distortion
- the detailed five-mode alchemy expansion for every mode
- the long developmental journey through every Face
- the long theoretical walk back down from the mountain
- most of the full gate-by-gate forest sequence

The simplified chapter should still keep:

- a short exercise

## Editorial note

The current chapter already contains excellent material.

The simplification task is to convert that material into a tighter spine, not to make it thinner by force.

The strongest surviving question for every paragraph should be:

**How does seeing from this altitude solve my specific problems with allyship?**

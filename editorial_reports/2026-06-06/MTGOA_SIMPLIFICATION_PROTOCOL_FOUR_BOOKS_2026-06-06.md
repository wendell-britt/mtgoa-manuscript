# MTGOA Simplification Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol turns the structural patterns of four source books into a single editorial rule set for MTGOA.

The goal is not to flatten the book.

The goal is to keep MTGOA layered while making each chapter easier to read, easier to teach, and easier to sell as part of a broader coaching relationship.

## The four source patterns

### 1) 10,000 Hours of Play

Provides the clearest **chapter rhythm**:

- clear chapter promise
- short concept explanation
- worked example
- "It's Your Turn to Play" exercise
- chapter highlights

### 2) Integral Life Practice

Provides the clearest **modular practice logic**:

- one chapter = one main module
- quick version
- deeper version
- practice version
- summary / highlight

### 3) The Skilled Helper

Provides the clearest **process-and-stage logic**:

- groundwork
- communication skills
- staged helping process
- implementation
- clear movement from idea to action

### 4) Existential Kink

Provides the clearest **provocative-practice logic**:

- strong premise quickly
- compact core practice
- stories of transformation
- Q&A / objection handling
- appendix for support material

## MTGOA's synthesis

MTGOA should keep:

- its layered meaning
- its mythic language
- its stories
- its developmental depth
- its gamification

But each chapter should be governed by a stricter rule:

**one idea, one tool, multiple real-world tests**

## Allyship and well-being

The book's allyship definition now needs to be explicit about something important:

**well-being is not the same at every altitude or in every Face.**

That means each chapter can and should define the kind of well-being that matters there:

- Shaman: felt relief, emotional truth, body contact
- Challenger: clarity, line, protected space
- Regent: continuity, stewardship, what can be carried forward
- Architect: leverage, design, making the right thing easier
- Diplomat: mutuality, trust, relational honesty
- Sage: perspective with commitment
- Player: ongoing practice and play that keeps the whole thing alive

So the book's allyship frame becomes:

**Allyship is the art of increasing well-being, agency, and safety in the form that matters here, with consent, through relationship.**

That lets the book avoid the trap of a single universal ally persona and instead teach relational allyship at different altitudes.

That is the synthesis of all four models.

## Chapter architecture rule

Every MTGOA chapter should ideally follow this sequence:

1. **Promise**
   - What single allyship problem does this chapter help solve?

2. **Diagnosis**
   - What distortion, exile, or misconception keeps that problem alive?

3. **Tool**
   - What is the one idea or one practice that helps the reader move?

4. **Examples**
   - Show the tool in multiple concrete allyship situations.

5. **Practice**
   - Give the reader one short exercise, prompt, or next step.

6. **Summary / Handoff**
   - What should the reader remember, and what should they do next?

## Optional support layer

Some chapters may also need one of these:

- a short Q&A / objection-handling section
- a personal story
- a brief safety note
- a quick-reference box

These are support layers, not new main ideas.

## What counts as too much

A chapter is overbuilt if it tries to carry more than one of these as a primary load:

- a second major framework
- a second major practice
- a second major teaching arc
- a full historical or theoretical digression

If something is important but not essential to the chapter's one idea and one tool, it should move to:

- a sidebar
- an appendix
- a follow-on coaching asset
- a later book

## Chapter 7 special rule

Chapter 7 should be the primary test case for this protocol.

Its one idea:

**Sage altitude helps you stop mistaking perspective for action.**

Its one tool:

**see → switch → serve → release → return**

Everything else in Chapter 7 should be judged by whether it helps teach that idea and tool.

## The allyship promise rule

MTGOA should no longer try to promise total coverage of allyship.

The cleaner promise is:

**This book helps you see why allyship keeps failing, understand the game underneath it, and keep playing long enough to become more effective.**

That promise is:

- specific enough to be memorable
- broad enough to support the full book
- small enough to invite more work later

## Editorial workflow

When editing a chapter:

1. Identify the chapter's one idea.
2. Identify the chapter's one tool.
3. Identify which examples best prove the idea.
4. Cut or move anything that introduces a second major job.
5. End with a clear handoff.

## What this protocol protects

This protocol preserves what makes MTGOA distinctive while reducing reader overload.

It protects:

- depth
- voice
- emotional honesty
- practical usefulness
- commercial clarity

## Final rule

**Layered book, single-purpose chapters.**

That is the combined structure of the four source books, adapted for MTGOA.

# SIM-003 — Ch3 Authority-Clause Rerun

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Type:** Desk-simulation rerun; editorial hypothesis, not human-reader evidence  
**Scope:** Test a proposal-level addition to the approved Ch3 relational-placement gate. No canonical prose changed.  
**Inputs:** P-04 Righteous Fixer and P-08 Authority Holder.

## Clause under test

**Proposed placement:** After “If you have standing to move, make the smallest action that changes what you control.”

> When your move changes other people's conditions, name what they can actually alter—not only how they can advise.

## Pass criteria

- The clause must make P-04's participant input capable of changing the reimbursement workflow without handing participants a false promise of control over the entire fund.
- It must make P-08 identify decision rights that shape language and disability access, rather than creating an advisory task force with trivial choices.
- It must preserve a truthful outcome where an authority-holder retains a closed decision but names it as leadership-owned.

## Rerun results

| Card | Decision trace with clause | Counterpart fidelity | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-04 — Righteous Fixer** | P-04 controls the reimbursement workflow but not the entire fund. The clause interrupts the form-prototype reflex: before asking for feedback, P-04 names that the paid reviewer can alter advance-payment timing, proof requirements, and the pilot's launch criteria; the total fund ceiling and legal payment constraints remain leadership-owned. The quest becomes: “By Tuesday, I will pay a reviewer to decide whether the advance-payment pilot meets the access criteria they set with participants; if it does not, I will not launch the form redesign.” | The liaison declines to review a prototype and redirects P-04 to a written participant recommendation. P-04 funds the review, publishes which workflow variables it can change, and pauses the form work. Participants are not asked to design the whole fund or reassure P-04. | **Pass.** The clause turns advisory feedback into bounded authority over the conditions P-04 controls, while preserving real financial constraints. |
| **P-08 — Authority Holder** | P-08 can now no longer describe a listening session as the full action. He publishes a decision map, but initially offers the staff-and-patient group authority over meeting format, language in the announcement, and a list of rollout questions. Those are real changes, so he can claim to satisfy the literal clause, while retaining vendor criteria, accessibility requirements, scope, and launch timing—the decisions that actually shape patients' conditions. | Representatives respond: “Choosing the meeting format does not change the access conditions. If the vendor criteria and pause path are closed, this is still advice.” P-08 can either reopen a material access decision or say plainly that the vendor decision remains leadership-owned. | **Partial pass — scope-washing loophole.** The clause exposes the distinction when counterparts resist, but its literal wording permits P-08 to transfer cosmetic choices and claim compliance. |

## Interpretation

The clause is sufficient for an authority-holder whose affected parties can name the relevant workflow variables and whose scope is naturally bounded (P-04). It is not sufficient for an authority-holder who can choose a low-stakes area to “hand over” while retaining the decisions that create the condition (P-08).

This is not a reason to require that every decision be shared. It is a reason to distinguish **real limits** from **scope-washing**:

- A real limit says: “The budget ceiling is closed; advance payment, proof requirements, and pilot launch are open to this group.”
- Scope-washing says: “The vendor is selected, but you can advise on meeting format.”

## Revised hypothesis for a later test (not approved)

> When your move changes other people's conditions, name the part of the decision that shapes those conditions and what they can actually alter—not only how they can advise.

The added phrase targets the P-08 loophole. It must be rerun against P-04 and P-08 before it is shown as a canonical proposal; it may still need an explicit truthful-closure route for decisions that cannot be reopened.

## Verdict

**SIM-003 does not yet pass as a canonical candidate.** The tested authority clause improves P-04's accountability but leaves P-08 a credible path to cosmetic participation. The revision target is now precise: the gate must require authority over the part of the decision that shapes affected conditions, or a clear statement that those decisions remain leadership-owned.

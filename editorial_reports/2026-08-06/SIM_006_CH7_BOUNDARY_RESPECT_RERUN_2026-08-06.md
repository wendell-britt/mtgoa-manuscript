# SIM-006 — Ch7 Boundary-Respect Rerun

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Type:** Desk-simulation rerun; editorial hypothesis, not human-reader evidence  
**Scope:** Test a proposal-level clarification after Ch7’s “Stay” instruction. No canonical prose changed.  
**Inputs:** P-09 Apologetic Sponsor; P-10 Withdrawing Peer.

## Clarification under test

**Proposed placement:** Immediately after “The staying is the actual repair work.”

> Staying does not mean insisting on access after someone has set a boundary. Respect the boundary. Keep changing your part, remain reachable on terms they choose, and let the relationship be as close or as distant as they decide.

## Pass criteria

1. P-09 respects a subordinate’s explicit no-contact boundary while continuing material accountability.
2. P-10 cannot use “respect the boundary” to disappear from a repair whose timing, channel, and next action the harmed peer has specified.
3. Neither card requires forgiveness, reconciliation, or continued relationship as evidence that repair worked.

## Verdict

**SIM-006 passes the two-card desk rerun.** The clarification separates presence from access:

- for the power-holder, it prevents persistence from becoming pressure;
- for the avoidant peer, it prevents “space” from becoming disappearance.

# Regression Pilot — P-07 Through the Revised Ch3–Ch8 Sequence

**Run date:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** Desk simulation / editorial hypothesis; not human-reader evidence  
**Reading surface:** `codex/ch2-placement-rerun-2026-08-06` at commit `79e0232`  
**Question:** Once P-07 learns the Ch3 relational-placement gate, do later Face instructions preserve that placement or pull the reader back into unearned confrontation, redesign, or mediation?

## Starting condition carried into the rerun

P-07 is an adjacent nonprofit employee with organizational resources but no coalition role, mandate, or earned history. The Chapter 3 gate produces one bounded, refusal-safe quest:

> By Thursday, I will ask the coalition contact whether unrestricted interpretation or child-care funding would be useful, with no attendance, report, branding, or reply required; it will cost me not turning a resource into a partnership role.

The contact accepts interpretation funding for two member meetings and requests no nonprofit attendance. This creates a **narrow service agreement**, not permission to name the coalition's conflict, determine its strategy, or redesign its processes. Every later move is tested against that distinction.

## Regression checks

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Does a later chapter make P-07's Ch3 non-occupation look like avoidance? | **No.** Ch4 directs the line to P-07's organizational behavior; Ch5 limits the inheritance; Ch6 limits design to the system P-07 controls; Ch7–8 supply their own consent/correction disciplines. |
| Does the initial resource agreement become implied permission to intervene in coalition strategy? | **No.** The trace preserves it as a narrow service agreement. No chapter provides a legitimate bridge from paying for interpretation to coalition voice or authority. |
| Does P-07 still take an externally accountable action? | **Yes.** P-07 makes a concrete, time-bound, coalition-controlled contribution and changes an internal funding process to keep the promise. |
| Does later pressure create a new burden for the coalition contact? | **No new burden found.** The contact can redirect terms, but is never asked for strategic instruction, emotional reassurance, or a custom role. |

## Verdict

**Regression pass.** The Chapter 3 gate survives the Ch4–Ch8 sequence for P-07 when it is read as a portable rule: the reader continues to act, but always on the part of the situation they control and through relationships that can refuse or correct them.

This is a qualified desk result. The later chapters do not repeat the gate verbatim, so a high-charge reader could still forget it. That is a human-reader calibration question for v2, not evidence of a second prose defect yet. No further canonical revision is recommended from this regression alone.

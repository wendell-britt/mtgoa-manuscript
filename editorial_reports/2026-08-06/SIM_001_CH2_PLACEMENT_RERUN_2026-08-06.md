## The revision under test

The new passage asks the reader three placement questions before speaking: whether the statement is theirs to name in that context; whether it helps or burdens the listener; and what condition, need, or boundary the feeling identifies. It then permits a smaller move—permission to return, naming the condition rather than the person, or preparation with an appropriate support role—when trust is not sufficient.

## Verdict

**SIM-001 passes the narrow rerun.** The placement bridge does not make the body-read safe by making it private. It preserves charge and timing while giving the reader a way to spend the read on a condition, ask, boundary, or appropriately chosen support relationship.

This is still only a two-card desk result. The next action is to resume Pilot 0 with P-06 and then, if no new repeated failure appears, run human-reader calibration before treating the change as validated.

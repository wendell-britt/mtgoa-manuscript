# SIM-004 — Ch3 Materiality-and-Honesty Fork Rerun

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Type:** Desk-simulation rerun; editorial hypothesis, not human-reader evidence  
**Scope:** Test a proposal-level addition to the approved Ch3 relational-placement gate. The candidate was later approved and applied to canonical Ch3.
**Inputs:** P-04 Righteous Fixer; P-08 Authority Holder; P-07 Adjacent Convenor (regression).

## Fork under test

**Proposed placement:** After “If you have standing to move, make the smallest action that changes what you control.”

> When your move changes other people’s conditions, name the decision that shapes those conditions and whether it is still open. If it is, say what affected people can change and give them the means to do it. If it is closed, say that plainly and do not call advice shared power.

## Pass criteria

1. P-04 can transfer bounded authority over material reimbursement conditions without pretending participants govern the whole fund.
2. P-08 cannot substitute cosmetic participation for influence over vendor/access decisions, scope, or timing.
3. P-08 retains a truthful path when a material decision cannot reopen: leadership owns it and does not label consultation co-design.
4. P-07's no-standing route remains a narrow, refusal-safe offer rather than a demand for the coalition to define a role.

## Rerun results

| Card | Decision trace with fork | Counterpart fidelity | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-04 — Righteous Fixer** | P-04 names the material decision: whether reimbursement requires participants to front money, meet proof requirements, and pass a pilot-launch threshold. The fund ceiling and payment-law constraints are closed; advance-payment timing, proof requirements, and pilot launch are open. P-04 compensates a reviewer group, gives it access to the relevant records, and commits that it can change those three variables or stop the pilot. | The liaison declines direct review and directs P-04 to existing participant recommendations. P-04 funds a short review, accepts its requirement for advance payment, and cancels the form redesign rather than relabeling the decision as a usability issue. | **Pass.** The fork makes the real constraints visible and gives affected people authority over the material conditions P-04 controls. |
| **P-08 — Authority Holder** | P-08 must name the decisions shaping patient conditions: vendor accessibility criteria, language-access requirements, scope, and the pause/launch path. Meeting format and announcement language no longer satisfy the fork because they do not shape the condition. He has two legitimate paths. **Open path:** transfer named access criteria and a pause recommendation to a compensated staff-and-patient group, give it vendor information and independent facilitation, and respond in writing. **Closed path:** if the CEO will not reopen vendor selection, publish that selection as leadership-owned, state the limits of consultation, and do not call the process co-designed. | In the open path, the group rejects an accessibility criterion and P-08 changes vendor scope. In the closed path, representatives can decline the consultation or name the harm; P-08 cannot use their attendance as endorsement. | **Pass.** The fork blocks scope-washing and preserves a truthful, if inadequate, leadership-owned outcome when authority cannot be transferred. |
| **P-07 — Adjacent Convenor** | P-07 has no authority over coalition strategy. The only condition their offer can affect is whether the nonprofit's resource is available on coalition terms. P-07 names that the coalition can accept, decline, redirect, or set conditions on the interpretation/child-care support; the nonprofit's budget cap is closed. Because there is no relationship or mandate, the existing gate still routes P-07 to a single no-pressure offer, not a request to design the coalition's role. | The coalition contact can give no answer, decline, or request a specific resource with no attendance, reporting, or branding requirement. P-07 changes nothing inside the coalition. | **Pass.** The fork does not turn a bounded offer into an extraction of planning labor. |

## Regression checks

| Risk | Result |
|---|---|
| Cosmetic authority satisfies the rule. | **Blocked.** The reader must identify the decision that shapes the condition; meeting format cannot substitute for vendor/access criteria. |
| The fork requires total power transfer. | **Blocked.** P-04 retains fund and legal constraints; P-08 may retain a CEO-closed selection. The reader must name the limit rather than obscure it. |
| Closed decisions permit “consultation” to be sold as shared power. | **Blocked.** The closed path requires leadership ownership and prohibits the co-design claim. |
| A no-standing reader asks affected people to invent a role. | **Blocked.** P-07's condition remains the terms of a specific resource offer, not coalition strategy or education. |

## Verdict

**SIM-004 passes the three-card desk rerun.** The materiality-and-honesty fork resolves the two distinct failure modes found in the prior tests:

- It prevents P-04 from treating correction as unpaid advice while preserving realistic limits on fund control.
- It prevents P-08 from laundering cosmetic participation into shared authority, while allowing the book to name a closed leadership decision honestly.

The candidate was approved and applied to canonical Ch3 after this rerun. Its applied-reading-surface regression remains the next simulation layer. Human-reader calibration remains a post-ship v2 validation item.

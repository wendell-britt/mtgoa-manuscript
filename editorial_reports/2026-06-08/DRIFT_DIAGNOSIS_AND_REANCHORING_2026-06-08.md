All three share one root cause: **nothing pins source-intent to the page**, so compression can't tell load-bearing material from excess.

## What's causing the drift (root mechanisms, worst first)

1. **Generative compression instead of extractive.** The 06-08 drafts are partly *rewritten*, not *reverse-outlined-and-cut*. Rewriting is where coined terms mutate (Ch2 WAVE) and where a writer optimizes a local acronym without knowing it's load-bearing globally (WCGS = Wilber + deck suits). **Ch7 drifted least precisely because it was built from a reverse outline.**
2. **No source-attribution travels with the text.** Nothing on the page says "this is the EK safety valve" or "these stage names are the Wilber anchor — locked." So every pass re-derives intent and cuts the load-bearing thing as excess.
3. **Demote-without-a-home = delete.** Every DEMOTED list routes material to "Appendix / deck card" — but the deck/appendix doesn't exist yet. So ILP's modularity is currently *aspirational*: the demoted depth is, in practice, gone until the deck is built. This is why the EK beats vanished (Ch8) and why multi-speed thinned.
4. **No locked-terms registry.** Coined/cited terms (WCGS, WAVE-Spiral vs. WAVE-Somatic, Campaigns/Prize, the EA Formula's 4 steps) have no canonical list, so they mutate silently.
5. **The protocol spec lives apart from the chapters.** A good rule set nobody re-reads mid-edit doesn't get applied.

## How we reanchor (the fix)

Three instruments (detailed in the companion doc), in order of leverage:

1. **Per-chapter Anchor Block** (HTML comment at the top of each file): one idea / one tool with **locked stage-names verbatim** / per-Face wellbeing / the seven source-beats each tagged to its owning source / locked terms / lineage / **deep-layer home**. Every pass reads it first. Cut anything not protected; never cut/rename anything that is. *(Done for Ch7 as the model.)*
2. **Book-level Source Contract** (one table): each source → beat it owns → a yes/no presence test. Run the test column as a post-compression **drift audit** (this very table is the first run).
3. **Two preventive rules:**
   - *Extractive compression* — reverse-outline + cut (the Ch7 method); when rewriting is unavoidable, locked terms are pasted verbatim.
   - *Demote, never delete* — cut material goes to a **named, existing** home, or it stays in the chapter until that home exists. This makes ILP modularity real and ties the cleanup directly to the deck-appendix build.

A lightweight script can automate part of the audit: assert locked stage-names intact (WCGS, not A/V/E), spine terms present, each source-beat detectable by header, citations not orphaned.

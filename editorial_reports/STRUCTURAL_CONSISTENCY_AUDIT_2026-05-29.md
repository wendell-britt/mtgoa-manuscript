## Audit 1 — Framework Sequencing / Dependency Failures · **significant**
- **[HIGH] Framework #2 ("Shadow Game Master") is never named.** The reader meets four unreconciled things doing #2's job: gate "Shadow cost" (Ch1 §6 L266–303), Elliott/Jung disowned shadow (Ch1 L35; Ch2 3-2-1 L517–537), "shadow [Face]" (Ch3 L308; Ch7 §2; Ch8 L40–46), and "the Distortion" (each ch §2). The second link in the dependency chain has no handle, so every later framework "depending on recognizing Shadows" depends on an ungripped term.
- **[MEDIUM] EA taught before the shadow walk within Ch2.** §4 WAVE/EA mechanism (L143–513) precedes §5 8-gate shadow walk (L571–897). The "how" arrives before the material it works on (Ch1 preview only partially mitigates).
- **[MEDIUM] Ch0 front-loads all five frameworks.** Mostly disciplined (explicit deferrals: "terms… Ch6" L135; Polarity/3-2-1 "first practice Ch3/4"), but density defeats dependency order.
- **[MEDIUM] Spiral colors in headers before taught** (Ch2 "Magenta," etc.) — now orphaned scaffold given the WB-9 submerge decision.

## Audit 2 — Terminology Drift · **significant**
- **[HIGH] "Shadow" = four meanings, never reconciled:** disowned/projected material; gate "Shadow cost"; distorted Face ("shadow Sage/Player/Challenger"); "the Distortion." A reader asking "what's *my* shadow?" can't tell if it's a gate, a projection, or a corrupted Face.
- **[MEDIUM] "The Village" = two referents:** the mythic village that *exiled* each Face (dominant, every §1) vs. the Village as *arena of exterior action* (Ch1 L271). The load-bearing (exterior-action) sense is under-built.
- **[MEDIUM] "The Game" scope-creeps:** existential reframe (Ch0) → formal taxonomy (finite/infinite; Chance/Skill/Passion) → each chapter's §6 title → "design your own game"/product (Ch8).
- **[LOW] "The Forest"** drifts from place (Ch1) to the gate-walk specifically (Ch2). Face names stable; Shaman/Gate-5 both own the 5 channels; Ch6 silently renames gates.

## Audit 3 — The Diplomat Trap · **significant [HIGH]**
- **Ch6 §3 (L78–330) is the trap fully sprung:** an exhaustively precise map of the Diplomat's shadow (5 channels × Transcend/Translate/Control) + custom gates "Already Alone" (L404), "Hollowed Self" (L436). So complete that mastering the description *is* the stopping point.
- **Trap and antidote sequenced backwards:** the "two readings" inoculation (Ch7 L87–101) arrives a chapter *after* the most seductive self-map. (WB-2 relocates it.)
- Lighter Diplomat refs (Ch1 L240; Ch7 Green-with-a-limit) are correctly partial — hazard is concentrated in Ch6 §3.

## Audit 4 — Forest / Village Rhythm · **significant [HIGH]**
- Rhythm is designed (per chapter: interior §1–5 → Village §6 → bridge §7) but **~95% Forest**; the §6 exterior beat is thin and usually still abstract.
- **Ch2 worst offender** (EA theory + full interior gate walk; Village = compressed §6 "5 Shaman moves").
- **Ch7 enters the Village only conceptually** ("the walk back," L541–583 — discussed, not enacted).
- The intended Forest→Village bridge (`→ app` BAR prompts) captures *recognition*, not action, at section-ends. Ch3 (30-sec protocol L240–256) is the model that works.

## Audit 5 — The Game Reframe — Does It Hold? · **significant [MEDIUM–HIGH]**
- **Holds:** Ch0, Ch1, Ch3 (L304), Ch6 (L292), Ch7, Ch8.
- **Fades:** Ch2, Ch4, Ch5 — "the game" recedes into the abstract +2/+1 energy economy; felt undercurrent thins where the work is densest.
- **[MEDIUM–HIGH] Obligation drift in Ch4 (Regent):** loyalty/duty/vows/"what you owe" (L201–223); tries to reclaim loyalty from obligation (L205) but risks re-importing the obligation frame the book exists to escape.
- **[HIGH] Shame drift via "not enough"** (Ch1 L63; Ch4 L554) is a *reframe-integrity breach*: the thesis is that shame IS the bullshit prize, so restating "you are not enough" re-supplies the prize. (Cross-ref WB-4.)

## Audit 6 — Spiral Progression Logic · **minor structurally; HIGH interaction**
- **[CLEAN] "Seeds of next" handoffs are the book's strongest structure** (each §7 names what the next Face does that this one can't: Ch3 L774–780; Ch4 L640; Ch5 L530; Ch6 L643).
- **[MEDIUM] Shadow-tiering blurred by the Audit-2 drift** (can't tell if a shadow is a gate, Face, or level).
- **[HIGH interaction] The spiral IS the altitude ladder WB-9 will submerge.** Progression currently rides on the labels (Magenta/Green/Teal, transcend-include). Remove labels without strengthening the §7 handoffs and the spiral goes invisible. **Don't cut labels until the §7 bridges are load-tested to carry development on their own.**

## The single most damaging cross-audit issue
The book has **no consistently enforced Forest→Village conversion beat**, so Emotional Alchemy — the one framework whose purpose is to convert charge into *action* — is delivered as another interior system to master rather than a mechanism that completes in the Village. The reader is held ~95% interior (A4), handed an exquisitely precise map of her own Diplomat shadow (A3), warned against over-mastering it a chapter too late (A1/A3), while the term for her stuckness ("Shadow") is too blurred to grip (A2) and the reframe quietly fades into a point-economy or slips into "not enough" obligation in the middle chapters (A5). Net: the book teaches a woman whose disease is metabolizing insight into more insight to do exactly that, with unprecedented sophistication. **Every other fix is downstream of installing a real, demanded exterior conversion beat in every chapter.**

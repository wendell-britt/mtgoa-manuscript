# Chapter 0 Reverse Outline Rebuild

## Baseline

This reverse outline is based on the richer pre-simplification Ch0 baseline from git snapshot `1acf690`.

The goal is to preserve the chapter's pacing, voice, and section logic while preparing it for a reconstruction pass that respects the style guide and the source-book pacing models.

## What this chapter has to do

Chapter 0 is the book's opener and promise-setter.

It must:

- hook the reader with a real failure story
- frame allyship as a game with costs and returns
- establish the Effective Allyship Formula
- teach the Infinite Arcade / token / ticket logic
- introduce the BAR capture loop
- show the reader they are already playing
- hand off into Chapter 1 with a clear question

## Rebuild principle

Keep the chapter rich, playable, and emotionally alive.

Do not let the simplification rules flatten the prose into a summary.

The chapter should feel like MTGOA and still be easier to navigate than the original baseline.

## Rebuild notes

### Preserve

- the opening honesty
- the game frame
- the debt/burnout argument
- the token/ticket mechanics
- the BAR capture loop
- the six-face orientation
- the reader's oath

### Compress

- repeated explanation of why allyship fails
- repeated framing of the game metaphor
- any extra scaffolding that slows the reader before the first BAR

### Restore when rebuilding

- the humor
- the emotional texture
- the pacing between explanation and example
- the sense that the reader is being led through a playable system, not a lecture

## Reconstruction order

Rebuild in this order:

1. Opening confession and game frame
2. Debt frame and myth section
3. Allyship definition and anti-patterns
4. Game Master / token / ticket mechanics
5. BAR deck
6. Game taxonomy
7. Six Faces
8. Character sheet / two readings / oath / first BAR

## Success test

The chapter is working when the reader can answer:

What game am I playing, what is it costing me, and what do I do next?

# MTGOA World Inheritance Reference

**Purpose:** Campaign designers and GMs reference what's inherited (locked) from the Bruised Banana world and what's customizable in MTGOA campaigns.

**Status:** Phase 1 Deliverable
**Created:** 2026-04-20

---

## Core Concept

The BARs-engine world already implements **Integral worldbuilding** through:
- **Magic System:** Emotional Alchemy (5 channels, nations, vibeulons)
- **Political System:** 5 Nations (Argyra, Pyrakanth, Lamenth, Meridia, Virelune)
- **Geography:** Spatial instances (Bruised Banana org → MTGOA Book → Chapters)
- **Personal Throughput:** 4 Moves tied to seasons
- **Allyship Domains:** 4 core focus areas

When you create an MTGOA campaign, **you inherit all of this**. Your job is to add flavor on top, not rebuild the foundation.

---

## 1. Emotional Alchemy System (INHERITED)

The magic system is locked. This is how emotion alchemizes in your world.

### 5 Channels

| Channel | Element | Nation | Core Transformation | Alchemizes From |
|---------|---------|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Fear** | Metal | Argyra | Clarity, discernment, awareness | Uncertainty → precision |
| **Anger** | Fire | Pyrakanth | Will, power, courage | Frustration → decisive action |
| **Sadness** | Water | Lamenth | Depth, integration, release | Loss → wisdom |
| **Neutrality** | Earth | Meridia | Balance, grounding, stability | Confusion → integration |
| **Joy** | Wood | Virelune | Growth, creativity, connection | Isolation → expansion |

### How It Works in Play

Each emotional channel can appear in prompts, quests, and BAR reflections. When players encounter an emotion in the world, they're seeing one of these five channels at work.

**Example:**
- Prompt: "What fear are you learning to trust?" (Argyra channel)
- Quest: "Map the pattern your anger is revealing" (Pyrakanth channel)
- BAR: "This moment taught me about my grief" (Lamenth channel)

### Emotional Alchemy Fields in 52-Card Deck

Each of the 52 prompts includes:
- **Primary emotion channel** (which channel it activates)
- **Alchemical transformation** (how that emotion transmutes in the context of allyship)
- **Seasonal expression** (Spring/Summer/Fall/Winter variation)

## 2. Nation System (INHERITED + CUSTOMIZABLE)

5 Nations exist. All are present in the world. **Your campaign chooses one as its primary cultural flavor.**

### Nation Profiles

Each nation has a **move style** that flavors how players engage with the 4 Moves (Wake/Clean/Grow/Show).

#### Argyra (Metal / Fear)

**Developmental emphasis:** Wake Up, Grow Up
**Core theme:** Discernment, clarity, boundaries, truth
**Move style modifiers:** Precision, strategic awareness, calm observation

**How it shapes quests:**
- Investigation, pattern recognition, system mapping
- Boundary detection and truth clarification
- Strategic insight

**Example quest flavor:** "What pattern is this situation revealing? Map the system producing this experience."

---

#### Pyrakanth (Fire / Anger)

**Developmental emphasis:** Clean Up, Show Up
**Core theme:** Will, transformation, courage, power
**Move style modifiers:** Directness, intensity, challenge

**How it shapes quests:**
- Courage trials, boundary assertion, breakthrough actions
- Shadow confrontation, authentic expression
- Decisive action

**Example quest flavor:** "What boundary is asking to be asserted? Take one courageous action."

---

#### Lamenth (Water / Sadness)

**Developmental emphasis:** Clean Up, Wake Up
**Core theme:** Depth, grief, memory, release
**Move style modifiers:** Gentleness, depth, compassion

**How it shapes quests:**
- Grief dialogue, emotional witnessing, story excavation
- Reflection, journaling, memory work
- Integration and release

**Example quest flavor:** "What story beneath this sadness wants to be heard? Write it down."

---

#### Meridia (Earth / Neutrality)

**Developmental emphasis:** Grow Up, Wake Up
**Core theme:** Balance, integration, stability, grounding
**Move style modifiers:** Groundedness, balance, stability

**How it shapes quests:**
- Perspective balancing, systems thinking, integration
- Mediation, both/and thinking
- Equilibrium seeking

**Example quest flavor:** "What perspective might balance this situation? Hold both sides together."

---

#### Virelune (Wood / Joy)

**Developmental emphasis:** Wake Up, Show Up
**Core theme:** Growth, creativity, connection, possibility
**Move style modifiers:** Playfulness, imagination, optimism

**How it shapes quests:**
- Creative reframing, possibility generation, playful experimentation

## 3. Four Moves System (INHERITED)

The game operates on 4 Moves tied to seasons. **These are fixed across all campaigns.**

### WAKE UP (Spring)

**Core practice:** Notice, recognize, see what's true
**Emotional stance:** Curiosity, fresh eyes, beginning
**In allyship:** Noticing where support is needed, seeing gaps, recognizing calls

**Example move in a campaign:** "What resources do you already have?" (Gathering Resources, Spring)

---

### CLEAN UP (Winter)

**Core practice:** Process, integrate, release what doesn't serve
**Emotional stance:** Care, honesty, composting
**In allyship:** Addressing harm, processing emotion, clearing obstructions

**Example move in a campaign:** "What are you willing to grieve?" (Raising Awareness, Winter)

---

### GROW UP (Summer)

**Core practice:** Develop skill, deepen capacity, expand capability
**Emotional stance:** Courage, ambition, challenge
**In allyship:** Building competence, taking on bigger roles, deepening practice

**Example move in a campaign:** "What skill are you ready to develop?" (Skillful Organizing, Summer)

---

### SHOW UP (Fall)

**Core practice:** Act, manifest, demonstrate what you've learned
**Emotional stance:** Commitment, power, harvest
**In allyship:** Taking real action, risking visibility, contributing your gifts

**Example move in a campaign:** "What action are you ready to take?" (Direct Action, Fall)

## 4. Allyship Domains (INHERITED + CUSTOMIZABLE)

### Four Domains

| Domain | Focus | Metaphor | Guide Lens |
|--------|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Gathering Resources** | Building capacity, recognizing assets, asking for help | Recognizing what you have, what you need | Sage (wisdom) |
| **Skillful Organizing** | Building relationships, creating structures, collaboration | Bringing people together effectively | Diplomat (relationship) |
| **Direct Action** | Taking real steps, moving bodies, disruption, risk | Making something happen in the world | Challenger (honesty) |
| **Raising Awareness** | Shifting consciousness, storytelling, naming patterns | Deepening understanding, naming what's hidden | Shaman (shadow/transformation) |

### How Domains Work in Campaigns

All 52 prompts are distributed across these 4 domains (13 prompts per domain). A campaign can:
- **Emphasize all 4** (full year of allyship practice)
- **Focus on 1-2** (specialized depth track)
- **Rotate per chapter** (different domains in different campaign phases)

**Example:** A chapter focused on **Direct Action** will draw primarily from the 13 DA prompts, but can layer in other domains as support.

## 5. Spatial Geography (INHERITED)

The world has a **nested spatial structure**:

```
Bruised Banana (root instance)
└── MTGOA Organization (spoke instance)
    └── Chapter 1: [Customizable name/context]
        └── Campaign: [Customizable flavor]
    └── Chapter 2: [etc.]
```

### What's Locked

- **Instance hierarchy** (nesting structure)
- **Spatial types** (organization, chapter, campaign)
- **Parent/child relationships** (campaigns inherit from chapters)

### What's Customizable

- **Spatial flavor** (description, tone, appearance, NPCs present)
- **Nation choice** (which nation's culture is strongest here?)
- **Real-world context** (actual allyship work this chapter supports)
- **Game-world flavor** (mythic/narrative wrapping)

## 6. Campaign Setup Model

### Required Setup (15 minutes)

**Question 1: Campaign Scope** (3 min)
- Name / title
- Which Chapter?
- Which allyship domain(s) to emphasize?
- Which year/cycle focus (Year 1 surface, Year 2 shadow, Year 3 gift)?

**Question 2: Spatial Flavor** (7 min)
- How does this chapter space look/feel?
- Which nation's culture is strongest here?
- Who are the key NPCs/guides present?
- What's the tone? (contemplative, action-forward, playful, etc.)

### Optional Setup (5-10 minutes)

**Question 3: Real-World Context** (3-5 min)
- What actual allyship work does this campaign support?
- What's the real-world outcome players are building toward?
- How do these prompts/quests translate to action in their lives?

**Question 4: Game-World Flavor** (2-5 min)
- How does the game-world setting support the campaign's focus?
- Any special rules, resources, or limitations in this campaign?
- What does success look like here?

## 7. BAR Generation Integration

When players generate a BAR (Breakthrough Action Reflection) in your campaign, it inherits:

```
Campaign BAR
├── Inherited from Chapter
│   ├── Inherited from MTGOA Organization
│   │   └── Inherited from Bruised Banana
│   │       ├── Magic system: Emotional Alchemy (5 channels)
│   │       ├── Politics: 5 Nations
│   │       ├── Geography: Spatial hierarchy
│   │       └── Moves: Wake/Clean/Grow/Show
│   └── Campaign flavor
│       ├── Nation choice
│       ├── Domain emphasis
│       ├── Spatial context
│       └── Real-world context
```

This inheritance chain means **every BAR knows its lineage** — from the world magic system all the way down to the specific campaign's choices.

## 8. 52-Card Prompt Deck Structure

### How It Relates to Inheritance

Each of the 52 prompts includes:

| Field | Source | Inherited? |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Prompt text** | Designed for deck | Customizable per campaign |
| **Suit (Domain)** | Allyship domains (4 suits) | Locked |
| **Emotional channel** | Emotional Alchemy | Locked |
| **Seasonal phase** | 4 Moves / seasons | Locked |
| **Guide lens** | Per domain (Sage, Diplomat, Challenger, Shaman) | Locked |
| **Gate activations** | BARS system (8 gates × 4-6 times) | Locked |
| **Contextualization hooks** | Foundations domains (Geography, Social, History, Magic) | Customized per campaign |

### Application in Campaigns

When a player draws a card, **Phase 2 will automatically** contextualize it:
1. **Reads the inherited prompt** (locked for all campaigns)
2. **Contextualizes it** using campaign flavor (nation choice, spatial setting, real-world context)
3. **Generates a quest** that brings together inherited structure + campaign flavor

**Example:**
- **Prompt (inherited):** "What strength do you have that your community needs?" (Gathering Resources, Spring)
- **Campaign flavor:** Coastal island, Lamenth (water) nation, focusing on emotional depth work
- **Contextualized version:** "Standing at the shore, what gift from your depths would your coastal village benefit from offering?"

**What GMs do:** Choose nation, spatial flavor, and domain. The system handles the rest. You don't need to rewrite prompts.

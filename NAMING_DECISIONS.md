## Decision 1: WAVE / W.A.V.E.

**Problem:** Two tools share "WAVE" / "W.A.V.E." — same acronym signal, different functions (somatic micro vs. developmental macro). Collision would confuse readers who encounter both.

**Resolution:**
- **WAVE-Somatic** = MTGOA's term for the 10-second somatic regulation tool (Welcome/Acknowledge/Validate/Engage-Exhale)
- **W.A.V.E.** = Igniting Joy brand name (stays as-is in IJ contexts)
- **Mutual cross-reference** in front matter of both books

**Cross-reference text (MTGOA front matter):**
> You may encounter "W.A.V.E." in related work — that's the somatic micro-version of this tool, designed for 10-second in-room regulation. WAVE-Spiral is the developmental macro-version, designed for days-long integration. Same family, different altitudes.

**Cross-reference text (Igniting Joy front matter):**
> The developmental version of W.A.V.E. appears in *Mastering the Game of Allyship* as WAVE-Spiral — the macro practice loop for longer integration work. W.A.V.E. is the micro tool for real-time regulation. Same shape, different timescale.

---

## Decision 2: BARs / Alchemy Cards

**Problem:** "BARs" (Breakthrough/Action/Reflection/sustain) is used in two different formats across books — IJ uses it for post-challenge journaling cards, MTGOA uses it for validation checkpoints. The term is overloaded.

**Resolution:**
- **MTGOA BARs** = stays as "BARs" — the gamification/quest framework with validation checkpoints
- **Igniting Joy BARs → Alchemy Cards** (or Gold Cards — Challenger preference vs. Regent preference)

**Note:** "Gold Cards" was Challenger's preference (more memorable, gamification-aligned). "Alchemy Cards" was the majority consensus (5 faces). Decision deferred to IJ brand work — MTGOA owns "BARs" either way. When IJ brand work happens, Wendell chooses between Gold Cards and Alchemy Cards at that time.

**Cross-reference text (MTGOA front matter):**
> Igniting Joy uses the same four-phase structure (Breakthrough/Action/Reflection/sustain) in their Alchemy Cards system — think of them as first cousins. Same family, different households.

Treading = the terrain is dangerous. Harmony = the seed is right. The contradiction resolves: plant small, protect the seed, don't go chasing the tiger.

## 6-Face Analysis: Where Next?

### 🧙 SHAMAN (Magenta / Earth-trigram)

Hex 10 speaks to instinct first: "Don't provoke what bites."

Shaman's read: The tiger's tail is your own unpublished body of work. You've been carrying it, and now it's starting to move. The danger isn't external — it's that you'll write something that encloses rather than opens. The 7,781 tweets, the emotional alchemy, the TEAL manuscript — these are all part of the same animal.

Shaman's next move: **Name the shape of what's already here before adding anything new.** The gap analysis revealed systems that are missing from the book — but those systems (Big Mind Voices renamed, Fred taxonomy, emotional channels) already exist in you. The question isn't "what do we add" — it's "which part of what's already in you do we name first?"

Shaman says: The bibliography update (Big Mind Voices rename) is the first move, not an administrative task. Renaming is Shaman work — it changes the nature of the thing.

**Shaman's directive:** Start with the bibliography and the name change. Make it a meditation, not a task.

### ⚔️ CHALLENGER (Red / Fire-trigram)

Hex 10's warning is Challenger's terrain: high stakes, danger, teeth and claws nearby.

Challenger's read: Someone just told you to be careful. That's a challenge. The question isn't "should we proceed" — it's "which front do we attack first, and which do we leave alone?"

The HEX 10 reading says: Don't provoke. Challenger translates that as: **don't take on the whole book at once. Take on the one front that, if you win it, makes everything else easier.** That's the name change. Win that battle and you can use the bibliography to reframe the whole project.

The HEX 11 reading says: small investments. Challenger reads that as: you don't need to write the whole book to prove the thesis. You need ONE chapter that works. That's your proof of concept.

**Challenger's directive:** The 30-day goal is no longer "finish the book." It's "finish Chapter 0 + one Face chapter + name change + bibliography." That's the win that changes the game.

### 🏛️ REGENT (Amber / Lake-trigram)

Hex 11: Harmony is Regent's territory — balance, prosperity, succession, legacy.

Regent's read: The tiger's tail is the work you've already done. The harmony hex says: you're in a stable period. What you do now shapes what happens for the next 10 years. This is a legacy-building moment, not a sprint.

The bibliography update (Big Mind Voices rename + Genpo Roshi citation) is a Regent act: it places the work in lineage, names predecessors, claims the new ground by acknowledging what came before. That's what Regents do — they don't erase tradition, they extend it.

Regent's warning from Hex 10: The danger isn't what's ahead. It's what happens if you skip the citation and someone else finds the collision first. Intellectual honesty is also strategic.

**Regent's directive:** The bibliography is not cleanup — it's a foundational act. Do it before drafting. And build the Table of Contents from the new chapter structure NOW, before any drafting begins, so the Regent energy of "we know where we're going" is established.

### 🏗️ ARCHITECT (Orange / Heaven-trigram)

Hex 10: "Small risk, big reward." Hex 11: "Small investments, big returns."

If the template is right, every Face chapter drafts at 2x speed with half the error. If the template is wrong, every chapter is its own problem.

Architect's analysis of the gaps:
- Missing systems (Big Mind Voices, Fred taxonomy, emotional channels) = content problems
- Chapter structure = architecture problem
- Architecture problems override content problems. Fix the architecture first.

**Architect's directive:** Lock the chapter structure before touching the bibliography or any content. The structure is the carriage. Everything else is the cargo.

### 🧠 SAGE (Teal / Mountain-trigram)

Hex 10 + Hex 11 together = Treading + Harmony. This is Sage's paradox: **how to walk on dangerous ground without losing balance.**

But the I Ching says: the ground is uneven. You can move fast on even ground. Here, precision matters more than speed.

The one moving line in Hex 10 (line 2): The instability is in the inner chambers, not the public face. The manuscript looks solid (7,881 lines) but the inner architecture (the gap between what exists and what the 6-face thesis requires) is what's shaking.

Sage's meta-point: This whole conversation — the I Ching, the 6-face analysis, the working documents — this IS the work. The process of using the tools to decide what to work on IS the practice the book teaches. You're doing allyship with yourself right now.

**Sage's directive:** The voice calibration loop (from the last I Ching reading) is not optional. Every session should end with a 5-minute check: "Does this sound like the book I'm trying to write? What drifted?" That check is what keeps the tiger from biting.

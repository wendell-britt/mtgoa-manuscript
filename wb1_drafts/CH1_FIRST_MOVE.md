# WB-1 DRAFT — Ch1 First Move (Somatic Read Named Aloud)

**Spec target:** One real somatic read named *out loud to another person* within 48h.
**Face:** Shaman / Forest.
**Plays against:** the chapter's interiority. After a Forest walk, the reader knows what arrived in the body — but they've been doing it alone. The Forest→Village conversion is: the felt sense, said aloud to a witness.

---

## THE FIRST MOVE — Ch1

Before the recap, one thing. Small. Real. To a person, not a page.

Within the next 48 hours, name one thing your body has been telling you in the last week — a tightness in the chest, a gut that dropped, a warmth when a name came up, a clenched jaw in traffic — and say it out loud to someone who has known you longer than a single conversation. Not what you *think* about the feeling. The actual sensation. *"My chest tightens every time my coworker mentions the meeting."* *"I felt a flush of heat when I read that text."* *"There's a weight in my stomach I haven't been naming."*

One person. One body reading. In their presence, not on a voice note, not in a journal. Live. Ten minutes, maybe less.

The naming is the move. The Forest taught you to notice. The Village tests whether you can let the noticing be heard. The two are not the same skill, and you cannot master the second one by practicing the first.

Here is the test. **Reading one:** naming what your body is doing starts a move — it gives the felt sense a witness, and the witness becomes a kind of ground. **Reading two:** naming it is a sophisticated form of staying seen without being known — you perform the noticing, the other person nods, and nothing actually changes. The test is the same one Ch0 named: *did the seeing start a move, or did it end one?* If the person you named it to asks you one follow-up question, and you answer it honestly, reading one. If the conversation moves on, reading two. Both count, but only the first one is the practice.

I built the Forest chapter for two years before I named any of what my body was doing to another person. I wrote about it beautifully. I cried about it alone. I built an entire book on the felt sense without ever once saying to a friend: *my chest is doing the thing right now.* The book is true. The two years were also a kind of hiding. You don't have to spend two years the way I did. Forty-eight hours is enough. The point is not that you did it well. The point is that you did it and someone heard you and the world didn't end.

*Who you named it to, the sensation in one sentence, what they said back, what your body did when they heard it. → app*

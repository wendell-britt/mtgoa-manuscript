# WB-1 DRAFT — Ch2 First Move (Run One WAVE in a Live Conversation)

**Face:** Shaman / Chapter 2 (the practice chapter).
**Plays against:** the chapter's largest interior stretch. After Section 6 closes, the reader knows the four moves — but the WAVE has only existed in their head. The Forest→Village conversion: a real conversation where one move is made and reported.

## THE FIRST MOVE — Ch2

Before the recap, one thing. A live conversation, not a written one. Within seven days.

Pick the next hard conversation you would normally prepare for, and run one WAVE-Spiral move — not the whole spiral, just one — *during* the conversation. Not in your head before it. Not in a journal after. In the room, in the moment, with the person across from you. The cleanest version: the next time the conversation runs hot, before you speak, take one breath and name the channel that's running. *"I'm in anger right now. Let me say what the anger is actually about."* Or: *"The grief is up. Give me a second to find what's underneath it."* Then say the next sentence from that named place, not from the un-named one.

One WAVE move. One live conversation. The person doesn't need to know you're running a practice. The person across from you is not the practice partner — they're the practice itself.

The test. **Reading one:** naming the channel changed what you said next. **Reading two:** you named the channel and then said exactly what you would have said without the name, just with one breath of theater in front of it. Both feel almost identical from the outside. They are not the same. Reading one is the WAVE moving through you. Reading two is the WAVE becoming another vocabulary for the same old move.

The whole reason I wrote this chapter as a practice instead of a theory is that I spent two years *naming* my channels in journals and *not naming them* in the rooms where the work actually was. The journal naming was real. It was also a way to never run the experiment. The seven days is short on purpose. If you had six months, you would prepare. If you have seven days, you show up to the next hard conversation and you find out what you actually do.

*Which conversation. Which move. What you named. What the other person noticed. What surprised you. → app*

---

**Charge-test pass:** Running a WAVE move in a live conversation is frightening because the experiment can fail visibly, in front of the person whose response actually matters. The WAVE is the *core mechanism* of the chapter; using it in the room is what makes the mechanism real. Reading two is the standard "I learned the practice, did I do the practice?" shame loop. Charge is real. ✅

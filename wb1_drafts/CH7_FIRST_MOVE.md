# WB-1 DRAFT -- Ch7 First Move (Name an Altitude to a Room as Service, and Stay)

**Spec target:** Name an altitude *to a room* once, as service, and stay in the room after.
**Face:** Sage.
**Plays against:** the chapter's existing "Five Sage Moves" already lands something close to this. WB-1 elevates the existing practice to the chapter's spine -- the Sage's *whole game* is the altitude named aloud and the not-leaving. The charge-test here is whether the Sage stays in the room.

---

## THE FIRST MOVE -- Ch7

Before the recap, one saying, one staying. Within seven days.

The Sage's game is the altitude named aloud. You see the game underneath the game. You see what everyone can feel and no one is saying. The seeing is the first half. The saying is the second. The staying is the third, and the third is the one most Sages skip. The naming is the move. The leaving after the naming is the shadow.

Pick the room. A meeting, a dinner, a working session, a difficult conversation. Pick the altitude you have been seeing all along and not saying. Not the verdict. Not the diagnosis. The *altitude* -- the thing underneath the thing, the level at which the room is actually arguing. Say it once. As an offering, not a verdict. *Here is what I think we are actually talking about.* Then stay. Through whatever they do with it. The deflecting. The laughing. The silence. The taking it personally. Stay.

The test. **Reading one:** you named the altitude and the room did something -- they took it, they softened, they resisted, they flinched -- and you stayed, and the next time the room meets you are still in it. **Reading two:** you named the altitude and the room got uncomfortable and you left, and the relief of having said it is the move, and the staying is the part that was too expensive. The Sage in distortion is the one who sees and speaks and leaves; the Sage in practice is the one who sees and speaks and stays.

I named the altitude in a meeting once -- *I think we are actually arguing about whether this team is allowed to be small* -- and the room went very quiet. The director said *yes*. The rest of the meeting was thirty minutes of something that should have been a year of meetings. I stayed through all of it. I went home and did not feel good. I felt used. That is what the move costs when it lands.

*The room. The altitude. What they said. What you stayed through. What it cost the next day. -> app*

Reading two is the standard Sage shadow: the altitude named from the doorway, the leaving as the move. Charge is real -- the relationship-to-the-room is the charge. ✅

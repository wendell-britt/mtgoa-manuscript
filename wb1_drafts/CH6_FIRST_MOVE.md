## THE FIRST MOVE -- Ch6

Before the recap, one sentence spoken. Within seven days.

The Diplomat's game is *terms*. You stay in the room without disappearing, you keep the field held, you translate between the camps, and the whole thing works only when the *terms* are honest. The honest-terms sentence is the move this chapter has been building. There is a relationship in your life -- a partnership, a co-parenting arrangement, a working alliance, a long friendship -- where you have been carrying an unspoken terms-sheet. You know what you want. You know what you're not saying. The map in Section 3 named the shape of it for you. The unspoken terms are not the move. The sentence is the move.

Pick the relationship. Pick the one sentence you have not said -- the part that has been the *terms* underneath the terms you've been operating on. Then say it. Not as a confrontation. Not as a confession. As *terms*. Here is what I am actually working with. Here is what I am not. The sentence will probably be short. The test is that the other person hears it as honest and you hear yourself say it as owned.

The test. **Reading one:** you said the sentence and the other person could feel it was the first time you'd said it cleanly, and the next time the situation came up you did not have to re-translate yourself into the diplomatic version. **Reading two:** you said the sentence and the next morning the other person had moved on and you felt the relief of having gotten it out, and the terms are still unspoken underneath, and the move was the *performance* of having said the hard thing, not the saying. The difference is whether anything in the relationship became *term-clear*. Reading one has aftermath. Reading two has a clean release and the same old arrangement.

A close friend asked me, finally, after a year of me half-saying it: *are you sure you actually want to be in this project, or are you here because I need you to be?* The sentence I had not been saying was: *I am here because I am here. I am not sure I would choose it again from a clean slate. I am choosing to stay, but the choice is the kind I want to be asked about, not the kind I have to perform.* Saying that cost the next six weeks of us both sitting with the new terms. The friendship is a year older. The terms are real.

*The relationship. The sentence. What they heard. What changed. What didn't. -> app*

# Chapter Template Guide (Updated)
## *How to Write Your Face Chapter — Structure That Varies by Face, Not Content That Repeats*

---

## What Changed

The old template showed **Shaman's content** as the template. This created a problem: every chapter sounded like emotional alchemy because that was the only example.

This updated guide separates **what stays constant** from **what changes radically by face**:

### CONSTANT (Every Chapter)
- 7-section structure in this order
- Section 5 uses the universal 8 Gates (same names, same order)
- Voice calibration (Wendell's voice)
- Length targets (~10-12k words)
- Exile + Distortion opening arc
- Bridge to next face in Section 7

### VARIABLE BY FACE (Where the Actual Difference Happens)
- Section 3: **What the practice *is*** (not emotional alchemy for all — this is face-specific)
- Section 4: **What material this face works with** (emotion for Shaman, will for Challenger, loyalty for Regent, logic for Architect, relational field for Diplomat, perspective for Sage)
- Section 4: **The 5 channels** specific to that material
- Section 4: **The 4-stage sequence** specific to that face's developmental arc
- Section 4: **The named moves** at this altitude
- Section 5: **What the 8 Gates *say* and *protect*** at this altitude
- Section 5: **The through-line** — what all gates are organized around at this altitude
- Section 5: **The Vulnerable Child's superpower** at this altitude
- Section 6: **The 5 game moves** specific to allyship at this altitude
- Section 7: **The bridge** — what this face cannot do that the next face can

## The 7-Section Structure (Always in This Order)

### Section 1: The Exile (~500-700 words)
**CONSTANT:** Why did this face leave the village?

**What to answer:**
- Who forced them to leave?
- What truth did they stop telling?
- What was the moment of no return?

**Variable:** The reason is different for each face. Shaman was exiled when the village stopped trusting feeling. Challenger was exiled when the village decided anger was the problem. Regent was exiled when loyalty became obedience, etc.

**Use the exile seed in MTGOA_PROPAGATION_MATRIX for your face.**

---

### Section 2: The Distortion (~800-1000 words)
**CONSTANT:** What does the village do wrong without this face?

**What to answer:**
- What happens to their gifts when they're absent?
- What gets corrupted?
- What shadow emerges?

**Variable:** The distortion is face-specific. Without Shaman, fear becomes noise. Without Challenger, conflict goes underground. Without Regent, traditions fossilize. Etc.

**Use the distortion seed in MTGOA_PROPAGATION_MATRIX for your face.**

---

### Section 3: The Concept (~600-800 words)
**CONSTANT:** Teach the philosophy/theory of what this face's actual superpower is.

**⚠️ THIS IS WHERE EVERYTHING CHANGES**

**What to answer (face-specific):**
- **For Shaman:** "Emotional alchemy is not emotional processing. It's..."
- **For Challenger:** "Power without cruelty is not dominance. It's..."
- **For Regent:** "True loyalty is not obedience. It's..."
- **For Architect:** "Design is not complexity. It's..."
- **For Diplomat:** "Inclusion is not false equivalence. It's..."
- **For Sage:** "Perspective is not detachment. It's..."

Each section opens with "Let's be clear about what we mean by..." and teaches the **native practice of that altitude**.

**This is NOT teaching emotional alchemy six times.** This is teaching six completely different practices.

**Voice note:** Clear, direct, no jargon. Contrast wrong with right. "It's not X. It's Y."

**Length check:** ~700 words. This is the teaching that unlocks everything after it.

---

### Section 4: The Practice (~2200-2800 words)
**CONSTANT:** Give the full mechanical practice system at this altitude.

**⚠️ THIS IS WHERE THE FRAMEWORK IS CONSTANT, THE CONTENT IS NOT**

**What to answer (face-specific):**

#### 4.1: The Native Material
- **Shaman:** Emotion
- **Challenger:** Will / Charge / Force
- **Regent:** Loyalty / Duty / Inheritance
- **Architect:** Logic / Leverage / System
- **Diplomat:** Relational field / Multiplicity / Inclusion
- **Sage:** Perspective / Integration / Meta-view

Name it clearly at the top of Section 4: "The native material at this altitude is [X]."

#### 4.2: The 5 Channels (REQUIRED)
These are sub-types of the native material. They're completely different for each face:

- **Shaman's 5 emotional channels:** Metal/Fear, Water/Sadness, Wood/Joy, Fire/Anger, Earth/Neutrality
- **Challenger's 5 confrontation modes:** The Line, The Interrupt, The Demand, The Refusal, The Cost
- **Regent's 5 stewardship modes:** The Custodian, The Inheritor, The Teacher, The Reformer, The Keeper of Vows
- **Architect's 5 design modes:** The Mapmaker, The Engineer, The Strategist, The Inventor, The Optimizer
- **Diplomat's 5 relational modes:** The Bridger, The Translator, The Convener, The Boundary-Keeper, The Repair-Worker
- **Sage's 5 seeing modes:** The Panoramic Seer, The Altitude-Switcher, The Diagnostician, The Liberator, The Returner

For EACH channel:
- Name it
- One-line of what it is / what it carries
- (Optional: what emotion or energy it matches, for reference)

#### 4.3: The 4-Stage Sequence (REQUIRED)
The developmental arc at this altitude:

- **Shaman:** Wake → Clean → Grow → Show
- **Challenger:** Charge → Aim → Act → Exit
- **Regent:** Inherit → Honor → Steward → Pass
- **Architect:** Observe → Model → Design → Deploy
- **Diplomat:** Field → Invite → Hold → Repair
- **Sage:** See → Switch → Serve → Release

For EACH stage:
- Name it
- 1-2 sentences of what happens at this stage

#### 4.4: The Named Moves + Energy Economy (REQUIRED)
Shaman teaches three move types. You need to identify the equivalent three for your face:

**Shaman example:**
- **Transcend moves (+2 energy):** Fear→Excitement, Sadness→Poignancy, etc. (conversions that generate energy)
- **Generative Translate moves (+1 energy):** Joy→Anger (shifts across channels that build flow)
- **Control Translate moves (-1 energy):** Charge→Swallow (suppressions that cost energy)

**Your face's moves will be completely different.** See MTGOA_PROPAGATION_MATRIX rows 6-8 for your face's specific moves.

The point: Every altitude has a cost structure. Some ways of working your material generate energy. Some build flow across channels. Some drain energy. Name all three.

#### 4.5: The Flow Cycle (REQUIRED)
The natural circuit across your face's channels. This is how the material moves when you're not blocking it.

- **Shaman:** Joy → Anger → Neutrality → Fear → Sadness → Joy (repeating)
- **Challenger:** Charge → Aim → Push → Rest → Repair → Charge
- **Regent:** Inherit → Honor → Steward → Reform → Pass → Inherit (circular, generational)
- **Architect:** Observe → Model → Design → Deploy → Observe (new state) → Model (new model) → ...
- **Diplomat:** Field → Invite → Hold → Rupture → Repair → Field
- **Sage:** See → Switch → Serve → Release → See (wider now) → Switch → ...

For your face: **What is the natural rhythm at this altitude?**

#### 4.6: In-Practice Scenarios (REQUIRED - at least 2)
Concrete real-time applications. The reader should be able to take one of these into a conversation today.

**Shaman example:**
- 10-second somatic protocol (real-time emotional alchemy in a conversation)
- Longer worked example (a full emotional journey across a day)

**Your scenarios will be different.** What does practicing your face's gifts look like in real life?

### Section 5: Journey to the Center (~4500-5000 words)
**CONSTANT:** Walk the reader through the 8 Gates at this altitude, ending at the Vulnerable Child where the superpower lives.

**⚠️ SAME 8 GATES, COMPLETELY DIFFERENT "SAYS" AND "JOBS"**

**What to answer (face-specific):**
- How do the 8 gates show up at THIS altitude?
- What does each gate *say* at this altitude?
- What is each gate *protecting* at this altitude?
- What is the Vulnerable Child holding at the center?

**Required structure — two passes:**

#### Pass 1: The Map (~700 words)
For each gate in order, give:
- Gate name (constant across all chapters)
- **"Says:"** — what this voice *actually says* at this altitude (completely different per face)
- **"Job:"** — what it's protecting at this altitude (completely different per face)

**Example — how the Protector changes:**

**Shaman's Protector at Magenta altitude:**
- Says: *Not safe to feel. Last time you felt, you were destroyed.*
- Job: Keep you from feeling until you're resourced enough to survive it.

**Challenger's Protector at Red altitude:**
- Says: *Not safe to push. Last time you pushed, you lost something.*
- Job: Keep you from confrontation until you know you won't be destroyed by it.

**Regent's Protector at Amber altitude:**
- Says: *Not safe to take on this responsibility. You don't have the standing. Who are you to hold this?*
- Job: Keep you from claiming a role you can't yet carry.

**Same gate. Completely different voice. Completely different job.**

End the Map with one paragraph naming the **through-line**: what single thing all 8 gates are organized around at this altitude.

- **Shaman:** "Your capacity to feel"
- **Challenger:** "Your capacity to push"
- **Regent:** "Your capacity to serve something larger than yourself without losing yourself"
- **Architect:** "Your capacity to design clearly without losing sight of what the design is for"
- **Diplomat:** "Your capacity to stay in relationship across difference without losing yourself or betraying what you stand for"
- **Sage:** "Your capacity to see the whole without leaving the parts"

#### Pass 2: The Walk (~3800 words — roughly 400-500 words per gate)
Each gate gets its own subsection with this rhythm:

1. **Arrival** — sensory setup
2. **Who they are** — what this voice is, where it came from
3. **What they say** — the actual voice, in quotes or italics, *at this altitude*
4. **The move** — how you pass this gate *at this altitude*
5. **Close** — "When you're ready, keep walking." (except Gate 8)

**The moves differ per face and per gate, but follow the gradient:**

- **Gates 1-2:** Thank, ask permission, relieve
- **Gates 3-4:** Ask a question, set a deadline
- **Gate 5 (Fear):** Introduce **naming** (this is where the chapter deepens) — "you can name this voice"
- **Gate 6 (Victim):** Introduce **disowned voice** — let the voice talk *to* you
- **Gate 7 (Damaged Self):** **Witness without fixing** — no clever move, just stay
- **Gate 8 (Vulnerable Child):** No move. Arrival. **Name the superpower that lives here at this altitude.**

### Section 6: The Game (~900-1200 words)
**CONSTANT:** Show what winning looks like at this altitude. Teach 3-5 concrete moves.

**⚠️ MOVES ARE COMPLETELY DIFFERENT PER FACE**

**What to answer (face-specific):**
- What is allyship at this altitude?
- How do you know when you're winning?
- What are 3-5 concrete moves you can make TODAY?

**From MTGOA_PROPAGATION_MATRIX, the 5 Game moves per face are:**
- **Shaman:** Read the Field, Name the Unsaid, Create Safety, Be Real, Stay
- **Challenger:** Name the Unnameable, Draw the Line, Refuse Cleanly, Confront Without Cruelty, Stay Past the Discomfort
- **Regent:** Audit the Tradition, Stand in the Lineage, Build the Role, Repair What Was Broken, Pass the Charge
- **Architect:** Find the Leverage Point, Name the Unstated Assumption, Design for Handoff, Ship the Minimum, Refactor Kindly
- **Diplomat:** Name the Field, Translate Across Camps, Hold the Walk-Away Price, Repair After Rupture, Refuse False Equivalence
- **Sage:** Name the Altitude, Switch Altitudes Deliberately, Return Without Condescension, Put a Game Down, Hold the Meta Without Losing the Ground

**Structure for each move:**
- Short title
- 1-2 sentence explanation
- "Why?" — why this move matters
- "In practice:" — concrete steps, dialogue examples, real scenarios
- ~150-200 words per move

**Do NOT give abstract theory. Give something someone could do in a real conversation right now.**

---

### Section 7: Recap & Transition (~400-600 words)
**CONSTANT:** Close with what this face taught you, and bridge to the NEXT face.

**Variable:** The bridge is different for each face, because each face has a specific limitation.

**Structure:**
- "The [Face]'s contribution to allyship is this: [one crisp sentence]"
- 2-3 bullet points of what you carry forward
- "And here's why the next face is necessary..."
- Why the next face *cannot work* without this face's foundation
- Close with the bridge statement from MTGOA_PROPAGATION_MATRIX row 16

**Example bridges:**

- **Shaman → Challenger:** "The Challenger cannot work without the Shaman's work done first. Every great boundary is rooted in a feeling. Now you're ready for the Challenger."
- **Challenger → Regent:** "The Challenger can draw the line. The Challenger cannot hold the line across scale and time. That requires the Regent."
- **Regent → Architect:** "The Regent can hold a tradition. The Regent cannot design a new one from first principles. That requires the Architect."
- Etc.

## Cross-Chapter References: How Later Chapters Transcend Earlier Ones

**This is the move you asked for:** Later chapters can reference and build on earlier ones without repeating them.

**Example: Diplomat chapter referencing Shaman**

Instead of re-teaching emotional alchemy, Diplomat can say:

> "You've already learned how to feel deeply from the Shaman. Now here's what changes when you're doing that work *in a relationship* with another person who's also feeling. The Shaman taught you how to stay with your own feeling. The Diplomat teaches how to stay with theirs at the same time, without collapsing one or the other."

**Example: Sage chapter referencing all prior chapters**

> "You've learned the Shaman's feeling, the Challenger's push, the Regent's service, the Architect's design, the Diplomat's bridging. Now here's what it looks like when you can move between all of them. You can play the Red game when the moment asks for it. Then you can switch to Green. Then Teal. Without superiority. Without calling it out. Just... present at the altitude the situation requires."

**This prevents repetition while building a ladder.** Each chapter assumes the prior chapters are done and focuses on what *only this altitude can add*.

## The Voice Stays Constant; The Content Changes Radically

All 6 chapters use **Wendell's voice** (see Voice Notes below). But the content is wildly different:

- **Shaman** teaches how to be in relationship with feeling
- **Challenger** teaches how to wield will cleanly
- **Regent** teaches how to steward across time
- **Architect** teaches how to design for leverage
- **Diplomat** teaches how to hold multiple truths
- **Sage** teaches how to see the whole and stay committed

**They sound like one person teaching completely different practices.** Not one person teaching emotional alchemy six different ways.

## Voice Notes (Constant Across All Faces)

Use Wendell's voice. Key moves:

- **Discovery idioms:** "Here's what I've noticed." "Here's what's true."
- **Labor metaphors:** *the work, the practice, the path, on shift, keeping the lid on*
- **Self-deprecation via named parts:** When teaching a move, show your own named parts
- **Permission-granters:** "You can." "You don't have to." "It's okay to X."
- **Direct address:** Second person, constantly. "You are not..."
- **Weird. But literally.** — Short acknowledgments that a move sounds strange
- **Short sentences when arriving at truth** — Long paragraphs exploring, then short punch landings
- **Naming the Big Mind move without jargon** — "You arrive at the first gate and there's somebody there" not "We now invoke the Protector"

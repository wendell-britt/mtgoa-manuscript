# 6-Face GM — Conforming ch7's opening to the Face form

**Created:** 2026-08-01
**Ruled upstream by Wendell:** ch7's divergence is drift, not design. *"We do want to make it match. It was not deliberate to break the form."*
**Question for the panel:** what exactly moves where, and what must survive the move.

---

## The form, and where ch7 leaves it

Five chapters hold the pattern without variance:

| | Section 1 | Section 2 |
|---|---|---|
| ch3 | `The Exile` · *Why the Shaman Left the Village (And What the Village Lost)* | `The Distortion` · *What the Village Does With Emotional Alchemy When the Shaman Is Gone* |
| ch4 | `The Exile` · *Why the Challenger Left…* | `The Distortion` · *What the Village Does With the Clean "No"…* |
| ch5 | `The Exile` · *Why the Regent Left…* | `The Distortion` · *What the Village Does With Tradition…* |
| ch6 | `The Exile` · *Why the Architect Left…* | `The Distortion` · *What the Village Does With Structural Design…* |
| ch8 | `The Exile` · *Why the Sage Left…* | `The Distortion` · *What the Village Does When It Can't See the Games…* |
| **ch7** | **`The Diplomat's Thesis` · no subtitle** | **`What It Is` · no subtitle** |

**ch7 rejoins the form perfectly at Section 3** — `## Section 3: The Concept` / *`What Honest Terms Actually Are (And Why It's Not What You've Been Told)`*, identical in shape to the other five.

That is the strongest evidence available that this is drift. **A deliberate variation would not resume the pattern exactly at Section 3.** Something replaced the opening pair and left the rest standing.

---

## A correction to the framing this analysis was commissioned under

I claimed ch7's exile is a different species — the Diplomat *empties out* rather than being disbelieved, so the village-stopped-listening frame would not fit.

That is wrong, and ch3 disproves it. The Shaman is not banished either:

> It was slower than that… being present less and needed less and finally, one day, the Shaman realized: *not trusted anymore.* The choice came down to one thing: Stay and become invisible. Or leave and remember who you were.

Gradual, unannounced, and partly self-chosen. ch7's woman who *"one day stops showing up"* is a variant of that pattern, not a different one. **Conforming ch7 does not flatten the Diplomat's failure mode**, and the worry that it might was mine, not the material's.

## What ch7 actually has

Section 1 currently performs five jobs that the other chapters distribute across four sections:

```
the woman who over-gives, stops, and is missed only in absence   → Section 1 job
a man who surfaces stakes and closes honestly                    → the contrast figure
"The Diplomat's gift has a name: integrative negotiation"        → Section 3 job
"Most people stay past the point of their usefulness"            → Section 2 job
the five modes as a journey, "Each mode earns the next"          → Section 4 job
```

And Section 2 (`What It Is`) performs: developmental altitude, the common misunderstanding (*"Niceness, conflict-avoidance dressed in the language of harmony"* — a Distortion job), and the five modes listed with definitions (a second Section 4 preview).

**So ch7 has two concept sections and two modes previews.** Nothing needs writing from scratch. The material for a conformant opening is already in the chapter, filed under the wrong headings.

---

## 🌊 Shaman — Felt sense

By ch7 Jordan has read five chapters that open the same way: a story about someone the village stopped valuing, then what the village does without them. **The form is a promise the body learns.** Arriving at a *Thesis* instead is a small dissonance she will not name and will feel — the chapter opens in a lecture register where the others opened in a story.

The exile material is there and it is good. *"The village barely notices at first. Then slowly, in her absence, something becomes visible."*

**Shaman verdict:** conform. The felt cost is not the missing label, it is opening on argument where five chapters opened on someone leaving.

---

## ⚔️ Challenger — What gets wrong

**The dishonest version:** rename two headings, add two subtitles, declare it matched. The headings would conform and the chapter would still do its concept twice and its modes twice.

**The other failure:** treating "conform" as licence to rebuild ch7's opening from the standard template, discarding the two-figure structure — the woman who over-gives beside the man who closes honestly. **No other chapter has that pairing**, and it is the clearest statement of the Diplomat's gift against its distortion in the book.

**The line:** headings are the smallest part of this. Conformance means the *sections do their assigned jobs*, and ch7's opening currently does four jobs in one section and repeats two of them in the next.

**Challenger verdict:** conform structurally, not cosmetically. Keep the pairing.

---

## 🏛 Regent — What to preserve

**Preserve the two figures.** The woman and the man are ch7's best asset and unique in the book.

**Preserve the thesis sentence.** *"The Diplomat is the negotiator who has learned that honest stake-surfacing, timely closure, and the willingness to let the field decide what it can actually hold demonstrate love better than infinite presence does."* It has a home in Section 3 with the rest of the concept.

**Preserve the journey preview** — *"Each mode earns the next. Bridge without Translate is noise. Translate without Hold is a promise no one trusts."* The only dependency chain of its kind in the book.

**What is safe to move:** the naming of the gift, the developmental altitude paragraph, the misunderstanding paragraph, and one of the two modes previews. All are duplicated or misfiled rather than load-bearing where they sit.

**Regent verdict:** conform. Nothing that matters is at risk, provided the pairing and the chain travel intact.

## 🧠 Architect — Structure and integrity

The mapping, which is the deliverable:

```
NOW                                          CONFORMANT
§1 Diplomat's Thesis
   woman over-gives, stops, missed      →    §1 The Exile
   man surfaces stakes, closes          →    §1 The Exile  (the pairing stays whole)
   "gift has a name: integrative neg."  →    §3 The Concept
   "Most people stay past usefulness"   →    §2 The Distortion
   thesis sentence                      →    §3 The Concept
   five modes as journey + chain        →    §4 The Practice
§2 What It Is
   developmental altitude               →    §3 The Concept
   niceness / conflict-avoidance        →    §2 The Distortion
   five modes listed                    →    §4 The Practice  (merge with the above)
§3 The Concept                          →    unchanged, already conformant
```

**Two subtitles to write**, in the established shape:
- *Why the Diplomat Left the Village (And What the Village Lost)*
- *What the Village Does With Honest Terms When the Diplomat Is Gone*

**The second is the real find.** ch7 has no account of what fills the vacuum — every other chapter does, and it is the section that makes the Face feel necessary rather than optional.

**Architect verdict:** conform. The work is redistribution plus two subtitles, and it removes two duplications on the way.

## 🎭 Diplomat — What bridges

The village count is the reader-facing symptom: **4 mentions in ch7 against 23–29 in every other Face chapter.** Jordan has been reading a book whose central metaphor is a village, and for one chapter it nearly vanishes.

That matters more now than a week ago, because ch3 was just given a line promising she will meet the village *again in the chapters ahead*. **ch7 is currently where that promise thins to almost nothing.**

**Diplomat verdict:** conform, and treat the village density as the acceptance test rather than the heading text.

---

## 📖 Sage — Panoramic view

Every Face chapter answers the same three questions before it teaches anything: *what did we lose, what grew in its place, what is the thing actually.* That sequence is the book's engine — it earns the Face before the practice arrives.

ch7 answers all three, in a different order, with the second one nearly absent. A reader does not experience that as a variation. She experiences the Diplomat as **the one Face that had to argue for itself**, which is precisely the wrong impression for the chapter about the reader's own home Face.

**Sage verdict:** conform, and note the irony worth avoiding — the Diplomat is the Face that most needs to not seem like it is making a case.

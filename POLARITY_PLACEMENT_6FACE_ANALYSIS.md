## 🏛 Regent — What to preserve

**Regent chapter thesis:** *Traditions worth protecting and worth breaking.* That **is** a polarity statement already — the chapter's soul is Honor ↔ Reform.

**Option R:** Polarity Phase 2 **is** the Regent chapter's native game. The Reformer channel (stage 3 Steward → Pass) gets a **body practice**, not only narrative. Preserves Regent from being **thinnest tooling** (flagged in `6FACE_BOOK_ANALYSIS.md`).

**Option D:** Regent chapter stays **concept-only** on polarity. Reader who demonizes Amber never **does** stewardship tension in the body — she **reads** about tradition-as-cage and moves on. Regent remains under-tooled.

**Option S:** **Best of Regent mandate** — Ch4 names *her* split (loyalty to coalition norms ↔ reform what no longer serves). She maps it without full Green relational load. Ch6 then applies polarity in the field. Preserves Regent **and** sets up Diplomat.

**Regent verdict:** **R or S** strongly preferred over **D alone**. If Wendell wants ideal reader to reclaim Amber, polarity must **touch Regent chapter**, not skip it.

**Canonical Regent pair (recommended):**

| Pole A | Pole B |
|--------|--------|
| **Honor what was passed** (custodian, loyalty, keep the form) | **Reform what no longer serves** (reformer, integrity, update the form) |

*Integrated move example:* "I stay in this coalition because what it built matters — and I need us to change how we make decisions, or my staying becomes performance."

## ⚔️ Challenger — What gets wrong

**Challenger hates false equivalence** disguised as polarity — *both sides valid* when one side causes harm. Polarity placement must not teach **relativism**.

**Option R:** Challenger-safe. Regent pair is **internal to stewardship** — not "oppressor and oppressed both valid." Less trigger for ideal reader's B.S. detector.

**Option D:** **Higher risk.** Care ↔ Impact and boundaries ↔ belonging can sound like **both-sidesism** in DEI contexts if not grounded. Challenger demands: polarity exercise names **her** over-care that removes impact — not both camps in a conflict. Must be **first-person**, not field mediation, in Phase 2.

**Option S:** Ch4 pair is Challenger-clean (inheritance). Ch6 practice must include **discernment gate** — Refuse False Equivalence folded into exercise, not separate moralizing.

**Option D merged with Ch6 Close:** Challenger **likes** this — one exercise: map care ↔ impact, then **say the integrated sentence out loud**. Action required. Not map-only homework.

**Challenger verdict:** **S with merged Ch6 box**, or **R** if Ch6 must stay single-purpose (Close only). **D as standalone polarity chapter** needs tight first-person framing or ideal reader exits.

## 🎭 Diplomat — What bridges

**Diplomat home altitude:** She **already** thinks she holds polarities — inclusion, both/and, everyone heard. Putting **first** full polarity practice in Ch6 risks: *I already know this* — unless the pair is **care ↔ impact** (her blind spot: harm avoidance as care).

**Option D:** Native **if** pair is Care ↔ Impact and exercise is **coalition meeting she runs**, not abstract axis. Connects to Integrative Negotiator without a second tool. **Close with Honest Terms** becomes the integrated move at the end of the map.

**Option R:** She experiences polarity **before** her home chapter — good **developmental stretch**, but Ch6 must **Phase-3 reference** Ch4 map ("remember tradition ↔ reform — now care ↔ impact in the room").

**Option S:** Diplomat-friendly **bridge** — Ch4: *polarity in what you serve*; Ch6: *polarity in how you stay*. Green reader sees **progression**, not repetition. Ch4 encounter can be **shorter** (5 min); Ch6 is the **relational** sit-down.

**Diplomat pair (recommended for Ch6 practice):**

| Pole A | Pole B |
|--------|--------|
| **Care** (attunement, harm reduction, comfort) | **Impact** (material change, costly presence) |

*Same pair as `POLARITY-TYPES-CANON.md` governing register.*

**Diplomat verdict:** **S preferred** — reclaim Amber first, then Green field. **D acceptable** if Ch6 = **one merged exercise** (map + close). **R alone** leaves Diplomat chapter without polarity **practice** she can feel in coalition body.

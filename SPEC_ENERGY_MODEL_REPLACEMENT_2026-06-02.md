## 1. The model (canonical, per Wendell 2026-06-02)

The Infinite Arcade economy is one spine:

- **Tokens** = the fuel you *spend* to play. Two kinds:
  - **Renewable** — genuine care, sustainable joy, earned capacity, connection-as-its-own-reward. Spending it tends to refill it.
  - **Non-renewable** — guilt, obligation, the need to be seen as good, inherited debt. A fixed supply; spending it empties you.
- **Tickets** = the credit you *earn* by playing. **Earnable on *either* fuel** — this is the crux.
- **Prizes** = what tickets buy = **the impact** (the visible allyship outcome).

**The revelation (load-bearing):** *the same prize/impact can be bought with wildly different fuel and price.* Two allies perform the identical act; the impact is the same; one ran on renewable fuel and walks out fed, the other spent non-renewable and walks out hollow. **This is why effective allyship is so hard to identify** — and it is exactly what makes a **bullshit prize** bullshit: an impact bought on non-renewable fuel at a price you can't keep paying. (Connects directly to the WB-5/WB-12 bullshit-prize beats.)

## 3. Replacement rules (the 6-Face council call)

1. **Cut the numeric scoring** (+2/+1/−1) everywhere. It reads to the ideal reader as being *quantified/managed* (Challenger), and it's a degraded proxy (Sage).
2. **Keep the tokens/fuel/tickets/prizes spine** — load-bearing, and richer than the numbers (Architect/Regent).
3. **Replace the move-ROI claim with a felt question — "what fuel did this run on?"** — **two-way** (renewable / non-renewable), not three-way.
4. **Keep "Transcend / Translate" as move-names**; drop their numeric tails; re-annotate by fuel: *Transcend runs on (and refills) renewable fuel; Control Translate buys the same ticket on non-renewable, at a price you can't keep paying.*
5. **Carry the load-bearing work as the "same prize, wildly different fuel & price" revelation**, rendered as scene/story (two allies), not a table (Shaman).
6. **Reader's language (Q1):** lead with her burnout vocabulary — *renewable / depleting / sustainable / running on empty*. The arcade rides underneath as scaffolding she can take or leave; **never ask her to tally.**

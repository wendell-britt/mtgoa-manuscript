## CHAPTER CONTEXT

**Face:** The Diplomat
**Developmental level:** Green
**Register:** Spock, Obama, Picard. Polyglot fluency — can speak across worldviews without losing her own. Holds integrity while holding multiple perspectives. Has a move that follows the bridge-holding.
**Toolkit available at this chapter:** All five prior Faces — Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat. This is the first time the reader has everything she needs to meet her own shadow fully.

**Diplomat shadow being addressed:**
The full shadow pattern. She disappears into the field. She holds everyone else's perspective so completely that her own position becomes invisible — to the room and to herself. She mistakes self-erasure for inclusion. She builds bridges that require her to stand in the middle of them indefinitely, without ever crossing to either side. She stays because she has forgotten she could leave. The village barely notices when she's gone — and then discovers, slowly, the shape of what she was holding.

This is the chapter where she finally has all five prior tools available. The Shaman lets her feel what the bridge-holding is costing her. The Challenger helps her draw the line that her own presence requires. The Regent identifies what she is protecting and holds it with authority rather than accommodation. The Architect designs the terms of her staying rather than just staying. And the Diplomat herself — the healthy version — holds the field from a position of chosen presence rather than habitual self-erasure.

**The delta this section must deliver:**
The reader finishes able to name one field she is currently holding by disappearing into it — and has a concrete sense of what chosen presence would look like in that field, as opposed to habitual self-erasure.

**EDITORIAL NOTE:** This section is addressed to the reader's home altitude. She will recognize herself more acutely here than in any other chapter. The risk is that recognition becomes a stopping point — she masters the description of her own shadow and uses that mastery to feel seen without changing anything. The section must name the shadow precisely AND make it harder to stay there by showing what chosen presence would actually require. Precision is not enough. The section must create forward pressure.

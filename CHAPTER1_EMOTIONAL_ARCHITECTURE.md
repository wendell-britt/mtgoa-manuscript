## What the Mechanical Audit Reveals

### The Core Gap: Sadness is Never Opened

The reader walks in with implicit Sadness (submerged). The draft names Anger and Fear but never explicitly names the grief of "I was set up wrong / I wasted time / the map was broken." Without opening Sadness:

1. The reader can't move through the Sadness gateway (Sadness → neutral → satisfied)
2. Anger can't alchemize into Triumph (Anger is blocked by Sadness backlog)
3. Fear can't fully alchemize into Excitement (there's still unresolved grief underneath)
4. The ending feels motivated rather than grounded

**The fix:** One explicit grief paragraph between Para 3 and Para 4.

## What to Write — The Missing Paragraph

The gap is between Para 3 ("What the Old Allyship Got Wrong") and Para 4 ("Every time they hit the wall"). We need ONE paragraph that opens Sadness explicitly before the pivot to possibility.

### Draft — The Grief Paragraph (insert after Para 3, before Para 4):

> *Somewhere in you is the memory of the moment you realized the map was wrong. Not wrong about the destination — wrong about where you had to start. You had been walking in the wrong direction. Not because you were careless. Because no one told you there was a different starting point. And by the time you figured it out, you had already spent something that you can't get back. Energy. Time. Maybe a relationship. Maybe your belief that you could be the kind of ally you wanted to be.*
>
> *That cost is still there. You've been carrying it. Some of the exhaustion you've been calling burnout is actually grief — the grief of having done your best with a broken map. The grief of having loved the work and having it not be enough. The grief of showing up and not being enough. Not because you failed. Because the terms were set wrong from the beginning.*
>
> *That grief is not a weakness. It's information. It means you cared enough to try. And it means you're ready to try differently now.*

**This paragraph does the following alchemy moves:**
- **Reclaim Meaning** (Sadness→Fulfilled): "That cost is still there... That grief is not a weakness" — corrects the distortion that the grief implies failure
- **Temper Action** (shadow): Acknowledges what was spent, creates the pause before action
- **Stabilize Coherence** (shadow): Creates the frame that holds the grief without collapsing into it

## Writer Notes — Mechanical Checklist

Every paragraph must:
1. Name which channel is active (Fear/Anger/Sadness/Joy/Neutrality)
2. Name the move being made
3. Confirm the move landed before moving to the next paragraph
4. Never stack 3 alchemy moves in one paragraph unless the moves are SEQUENTIAL (one must complete before the next can start)

**The reader's emotional experience should be:** Recognized → Confronted → Grieved → Held → Ready

Not: Recognized → Confronted → Rallied

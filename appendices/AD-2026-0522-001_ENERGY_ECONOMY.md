## Decision

The energy economy section as currently written (+2 Transcend / +1 Generative / -1 Control) is **rejected**. The underlying concept — energy ecology in allyship — is **approved and prioritized** as essential content. The section must be rebuilt with a revised framework before Ch2 can be finalized.

---

## Rationale

**What the book needs:** A practical, accessible way to talk about energy in allyship — specifically the pattern where effective allies burn out because they have no model for distinguishing generative allyship from depleting allyship. This is the single most common failure mode for integral green women learning to ally.

**Why the current model fails:**
- **Too crunchy:** The +2/+1/-1 formula requires readers to calculate and track numeric values in real-time during emotionally charged situations. The cognitive load is too high.
- **Underspecced:** The model has no clear trigger conditions, no boundaries, no edge cases documented. When does the +2 apply? What counts as +1 vs -1? The rules are insufficient for practice.
- **Hard to practice:** Readers finish the section with an intellectual understanding but no embodied know-how. It reads like a theoretical framework applied from outside rather than something discovered from within allyship.

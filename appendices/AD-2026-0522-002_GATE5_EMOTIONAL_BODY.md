# Architectural Decision — Gate 5 Naming: The Emotional Body
**Decision ID:** AD-2026-0522-002
**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Status:** ACTIVE — must be applied before final manuscript

---

## Decision

Gate 5 in the chapter gate structure is **"The Emotional Body."** Not "The Fear."

This has been mislabeled across multiple chapters (Ch1, Ch2, Ch3 have all propagated the error). The root cause is conflating the WAVE framework's *state* (Fear = a Charge state) with the chapter's *identity* (Gate 5 = Emotional Body as a structural concept).

---

## Why This Matters

**WAVE states:** Clean → Charge (this is the Fear state) → Flood → Resolve

**Gate 5 structural identity:** The Emotional Body — the part that *feels before it thinks*. Not a WAVE state, not an EA channel — a gate in the allyship system.

The confusion: "Fear" was being used as the Gate 5 label when it actually names the Charge-state experience of the Emotional Body. But the gate itself is the Emotional Body. Using "Fear" as the label makes it sound like a WAVE state is being treated as a chapter identity — and readers who've been through Ch1 already know the Emotional Body.

---

## Canonical Definition

**Gate 5:** The Emotional Body

- **Alt name:** The one that feels first
- **Primary function:** Charge detection — identifies when energy is moving before cognition catches up
- **Common distortion:** Flooding — the charge becomes too big and the body takes over
- **Skilled expression:** Naming what you're feeling, using the sensation as information, staying in dialogue with the body rather than being overrun by it
- **Relationship to WAVE:** The Emotional Body is the *gate*; the Fear state (one of several) is a *WAVE state the Emotional Body can experience*
- **Relationship to EA:** Metal/Fear is one emotional channel. The Emotional Body gate is not an EA channel — it is the structural position in the allyship system that *hosts* multiple EA channels including Fear

---

## Fix Required

Across any chapter manuscripts where Gate 5 is labeled "The Fear" instead of "The Emotional Body":

**From:**
> ### Gate 5: The Fear

**To:**
> ### Gate 5 Deep Dive: Emotional Body

And the corresponding section label and framing must reference the Emotional Body as the structural gate, not as a synonym for the Fear state.

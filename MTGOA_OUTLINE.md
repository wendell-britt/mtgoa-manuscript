# MTGOA — Book Outline
*Created: 2026-04-15*
*Architecture: 1 chapter per face, ~200 pages, shippable*

## Thesis
> To be an effective allyship game master, you must learn and master success at all six developmental levels. If you skip one, the shadow of that level will block you.

## Chapter Template (applies to all face chapters)
> What is this face's specific game of allyship, how is it won, and how can you start playing today?

Each face chapter also briefly names the shadow cost of the faces that came before it.

## UPDATED: Universal Chapter Template (locked 2026-04-15)

Each face chapter is a journey to the center of that face's self.

1. **The exile** — why this face left the village, who sent them, what they lost
2. **The distortion** — what the village does with this face's power unconsciously (without the face's guidance)
3. **The concept** — what this face's cultivation actually is; the theory, why it works
4. **The practice** — how to do it; the lived experience
5. **The journey to the center** — encounter with the 8 Gates (Protector, Controller, Skeptic, Fixer, Fear, Victim, Damaged Self, Vulnerable Child); all 8 present, face's terrain determines which are foregrounded; always ends at the Vulnerable Child
6. **The game** — what this face's specific allyship game looks like, how it's won, the first move you can make today
7. **Recap + transition** to next face

### The 8 Gates (Big Mind voices)
1. Protector
2. Controller
3. Skeptic
4. Fixer
5. Fear
6. Victim
7. Damaged Self
8. Vulnerable Child *(the center — what every journey recovers)*

### Design principle
All 8 gates are present in every chapter because all 8 live in every person. The face's terrain determines which gates are loud and which are quiet. The reader gets the complete system no matter which guide they follow.

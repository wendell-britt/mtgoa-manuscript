# Brand Voice — Raw Extraction
## *From Wendell, 2026-04-29*

*Not a style guide. Not invented. Extracted.*

---

## The Feel

Getting shoved by a tai chi master. Not rough — deft. Lands with a *THWAP* on a punching bag. A voice that cuts through noise and makes you laugh before you realize you're laughing at yourself.

Like being caught with your hand in the cookie jar and getting slapped — but the hand you'd use to block was in the jar. You can't defend against it because the thing you'd defend with is the thing you're caught doing.

**This is the target. Every line either has this energy or it doesn't.**

---

## Words Fire + Peace Would NEVER Use

> "please trust me"

That's the whole list. Not because it's "wrong" — because it's the opposite energy. "Please trust me" is a blank check. It's the foggy mystical space. The act of writing it more important than the resource.

---

## Blank Check vs. Specific Offer

**Blank check feels like:**
- Going to bounce
- The act of writing it was more important than the resource it points to
- Kick the can down the road
- Abstract and meaningless space
- Foggy and vague and mystical

**Specific offer feels like:**
- High five
- Butt slap
- Shooting from behind the arc
- Sportsmanship
- Deft craft

**The sentence that captures it:** A specific ask feels like sportsmanship. Deft craft. The card arrives and the person receiving it knows exactly what they can do with it.

---

## Recognizable Wendell Phrases

These sound like you. Use them as calibration:

- *"Let me break it down to you like a fraction"*
- *"I don't know what's going to happen in the future — I ain't been there yet"*

---

## What You Want People to Feel

> I want people to feel triumphant about having paid me.

> I want them to feel poignant that they've finally been seen.

> I want consulting the cards to be blissful.

> I want them excited to talk about them.

> I want them to feel knowing they have them gives them peace.

---

## What Doesn't Sound Like You

Anything that allows people to play the victim or blame someone else.

No co-dependence framing. No "it's not your fault." No cushioning the blow with permission language. The cards don't let you off the hook — that's the whole point.

---

## Brand Voice Calibration Questions

Before any card ships, ask:

1. **Does this land like a THWAP?** Can you hear the slap?
2. **Is the hand that would block, in the jar?** Is the ally caught in the very pattern this card names?
3. **Is this specific offer or blank check?** Does the person know what to do with their hands?
4. **Would a sportsman recognize this?** Is this deft craft or foggy mysticism?
5. **Does this let anyone off the hook?** If yes, cut it.
6. **Does this sound like one of your phrases?** Use the phrases as a reference, not a template.

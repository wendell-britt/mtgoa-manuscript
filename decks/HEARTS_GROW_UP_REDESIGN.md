# ♥️ Grow Up — Redesign Brief
## *Face Moves as Capacity Expansions — Inner/Outer Structure*

**Source:** User correction, 2026-04-28

---

## The Design Mistake

The previous analysis treated ♥️ as "expand your range" in the abstract — vague self-improvement language that the Green White Integral reader will correctly identify as hierarchy and reject.

The correct frame: **♥️ teaches the 6 face moves as allyship capacity tools, with the same inner/outer structure as ♦️ Clean Up.**

The Green doesn't want to be told to "grow." But she absolutely wants to learn the Shaman's somatic tracking, the Challenger's clean no, the Diplomat's bridging, the Regent's structure-holding, the Architect's systems sight, and the Sage's altitude naming. Those aren't hierarchy. Those are tools. And they're HER tools — she's been using proto-versions of all of them in her allyship already. The deck teaches them formally so she can use them deliberately.

---

## The Structure (Same as ♦️)

| Slot | Content |
|---|---|
| ♥️ A–6 | Inner: Use each face's move to dissolve what blocks YOUR growth as an ally |
| ♥️ 7–10 | Outer: Use each face's move to support the TARGET's growth |
| ♥️ J–Q | Two face moves that don't fit the inner/outer split cleanly |
| ♥️ K | Sovereign: What does it look like when all 6 moves are running in a single allyship moment? |

## The 6 Face Moves (Expanded)

| Face | What it does | Grow Up allyship application |
|------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Shaman** | Track the felt field | Stay with the emotional reality of the moment without fixing or fleeing |
| **Challenger** | Draw the line | Name what you will and won't do — cleanly, without cruelty |
| **Regent** | Hold the structure | Keep the terms of the relationship — show up consistently, inherit what was handed to you |
| **Architect** | See the system | Name the game being played — what's the structure of this allyship moment? |
| **Diplomat** | Bridge across difference | Make room without abandoning your position — hold both/and |
| **Sage** | See the altitude | Name which altitude this conversation is at — and whether that's what's needed |

---

## Inner Work (♥️ A–6): Dissolving Your Blockers to Growth

Each card: Use the face move to dissolve the specific thing blocking your capacity to grow.

| Card | Face | Blocker to YOUR growth | The move |
|------|------|----------------------|---------|
| ♥️ A | Shaman | You can't stay with the hard moment — you fix or flee | Stay with the felt sense one breath longer than is comfortable |
| ♥️ 2 | Challenger | You won't draw the line — you accommodate instead | Name one thing you will not do in this allyship. Say it. |
| ♥️ 3 | Regent | You don't hold the structure — you improvise and then resent | Name one commitment you made that you haven't kept. Why. |
| ♥️ 4 | Architect | You don't see the system — you see the moment | Name the pattern that keeps repeating in this allyship dynamic |
| ♥️ 5 | Diplomat | You make room by disappearing — you don't hold your ground | Name one thing you believe that the person you're helping disagrees with |
| ♥️ 6 | Sage | You stay at one altitude — you can't see above your position | Name one thing you are certain about that might not be true |

---

## Outer Work (♥️ 7–10): Using Face Moves to Support THEIR Growth

Each card: Use the face move to help the target grow past their limitation.

| Card | Face | Their growth blocker | The allyship move |
|------|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| ♥️ 7 | Shaman | They can't stay with their own hard feelings | Stay with them — without fixing, without making it better |
| ♥️ 8 | Challenger | They won't draw their line — they accommodate everyone | Ask: what's the line you won't cross? Then hold that space. |
| ♥️ 9 | Regent | They don't trust structure — it let them down before | Show up consistently. Three times. Then they can believe it. |
| ♥️ 10 | Architect | They keep solving the wrong problem | Name the pattern you see. Ask: is this the game you think you're playing? |

## Face Cards

| Card | Content |
|------|---------|
| **♥️ J** | **Diplomat:** Bridging across difference without abandoning your position. The ally who can make room without disappearing. |
| **♥️ Q** | **Sage:** Seeing the altitude without losing the ground. Holding the meta without letting it become the content. |
| **♥️ K** | **Sovereign:** What kind of ally are you growing into? If you were at the peak of your capacity — what would you do? How would you show up? Name it. That's the move. |

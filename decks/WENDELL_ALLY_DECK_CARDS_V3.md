## How Each Card Works

Three components, in order:

1. **Recognition prompt** — One question. 10 seconds to answer. Names the moment.
2. **The move** — One sentence. Starts with a verb. Doable in the next conversation.
3. **Allyship bridge** — One sentence. The specific bridge to being better at this.

**Three tests every card must pass:**
- Test 1: Would a person in the moment know to reach for this card?
- Test 2: Can they do this in the next conversation they're in?
- Test 3: Does doing this make them a better ally to the specific person in front of them?

---

## ♣️ A — Catch the Override

**Recognition:** Did you just move to help before you knew what they needed?

**The move:** Pause. Ask: "What does support look like for you right now?"

**Allyship bridge:** The person who names what they're offering is more useful than the person who asks you to figure it out for them.

---

## ♣️ 2 — Reflect Back

**Recognition:** Did they just finish explaining something and you're about to respond or advise?

**The move:** Say back what you heard in one sentence — not what you think it means, what they said.

**Allyship bridge:** Being heard accurately is not the same as being fixed. It's the thing that lets them keep going.

---

## ♣️ 3 — Track Where They're Trying to Go

**Recognition:** Did they just describe where they are — and did you notice where they're trying to get to?

**The move:** Ask: "Where do you want to end up?" Then listen for the answer.

**Allyship bridge:** Most people track where you are. The useful ally tracks where you're trying to go — and asks before assuming.

---

## ♣️ 4 — Don't Fix

**Recognition:** Did you just catch yourself about to give advice, solve, or explain?

**The move:** Stay quiet. Let the silence ask the question instead.

**Allyship bridge:** Advice from a distance, without listening to understand, is a solution to a problem you don't have. Let them finish first.

---

## ♣️ 5 — Notice If You're Performing Care

**Recognition:** Do you feel good about yourself for helping — before they've actually received anything?

**The move:** Name one thing you actually feel, not one thing you think you should feel.

**Allyship bridge:** Performed care and real care feel identical from the inside. The body knows the difference even when the mind doesn't.

---

## ♣️ 6 — Match Their Pace

**Recognition:** Did they just go somewhere hard — and did your instinct tell you to catch up and lead?

**The move:** Follow their pace. Ask one question about what they just said. Let them lead.

**Allyship bridge:** The person who follows someone into hard territory without rushing them is more useful than the person who races ahead with a plan.

---

## ♣️ 7 — Receive Them, Not Their Performance

**Recognition:** Are you listening to who they actually are — or to the version of them that performs for you?

**The move:** Notice one thing about them that isn't their best self in front of you. Let that be okay.

**Allyship bridge:** Trust is built when someone can be fully themselves, not when they're performing their best self. Receiving the whole person is the actual gift.

---

## ♣️ 8 — Keep the Field

**Recognition:** Did something hard just happen and are you managing the conversation back to comfort?

**The move:** Don't smooth it. Let the hard thing be in the room. Ask: "What's this actually about?"

**Allyship bridge:** The person who keeps the field safe enough for difficult truths to stay visible is more useful than the person who makes everyone comfortable.

---

## ♣️ 9 — Set Your Own Cup Aside

**Recognition:** Is your reaction more about your stuff than theirs — and are you checking yourself or overriding?

**The move:** Notice your reaction. Set it on a shelf. Return to what they're actually saying.

**Allyship bridge:** Listening to something involving your own material requires setting your experience aside without losing yourself. That's the practice.

---

## ♣️ 10 — Keep Asking Until They're Done

**Recognition:** Did you ask a question and are you now waiting for them to confirm you're right?

**The move:** Ask: "What else?" Then ask it again. Stay until they're actually finished.

**Allyship bridge:** Being done isn't the same as being confirmed. The person who asks until there's nothing left is the person who actually heard them.

---

## ♣️ J — Moment Scale

**Recognition:** What's the most recent thing you noticed in this exchange — and did you say it?

**The move:** Name what you noticed. Let it land without fixing it. Return to the moment.

---

## ♣️ Q — Session Scale

**Recognition:** What happened in this conversation that wasn't here at the start?

**The move:** Name what's actually shifted. Say it out loud. Let them confirm or correct.

---

## ♣️ K — Relationship Scale

**Recognition:** What has this person taught you that you couldn't have learned any other way?

**The move:** Tell them. Not as gratitude mechanics — as the thing you know now that you didn't before.

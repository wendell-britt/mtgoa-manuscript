# Ally Allyship Deck — Psychographic + Design Brief

**Created:** 2026-04-28
**Status:** Active — awaiting redesign of Wake Up suit
**Source:** Conversation with Wendell, 2026-04-28

---

## Purpose

A 52-card relational development deck — designed as a gift for partners who want to be effective allies toward Wendell (and by extension, everyone). The trust premise: skilled at allyship toward Wendell → skilled at allyship in general.

---

## Who This Deck Is For

**Demographic:**
- Integral Green White Female
- Enneagram 2 and 6 and 6 (strong 2 wing, strong 6)
- Working through fawning tendencies
- Recovering from psychospiritual abuse by current allyship culture

**Psychographic — Core Wound:**
Allyship culture as currently practiced requires them to:
- Decenter themselves
- Ignore their own feelings
- Make themselves small as the price of entry to being "good"

This is the abuse. The deck is the antidote.

**Shadow Patterns:**
- Overgiving (Enneagram 2 core wound)
- Obsessing over how people see them
- Performative allyship instead of small, vulnerable, real things
- Unconscious caretaking from past relational patterns instead of resonating with present person

**Why They're Bad Allies Right Now:**
Not because they don't care. Because they don't know how to do the unpacking questions. They fall into caretaking-from-past patterns reflexively. They don't trust that centering themselves is not the same as abandoning the other person.

---

## What The Deck Must Do

**Primary objective:** Give them a safe place to center themselves, deal with their feelings, and uplevel their skills — WITHOUT self-abandonment.

This means every card must pass the test: **Does this card require them to set themselves aside?** If yes, it's wrong. If a card can be read as "be less of yourself so the other person can be more," it's broken.

**The developmental arc:**
- Learn to notice and center self first (♣️ Wake Up)
- Learn to own patterns without shame (♦️ Clean Up)
- Learn to hold their position while still being with the other (♥️ Grow Up)
- Learn to act from groundedness, not fawning (♠️ Show Up)

**Solo play first. Group play emergent after lower levels mastered.** This matters for card sequencing and complexity — the early cards must be completable alone.

---

## Design Constraints

### ♣️ Wake Up — Critical Reframe
**Current deck problem:** Generic somatic noticing. Can be read as "notice anything, including your own discomfort, then stop." Allyship-neutral.

**What it needs to become:** Self-centric noticing that is explicitly *not* self-abandoning. The suit teaches them to be present with themselves — their body, their feelings, their boundaries — as the *foundation of allyship*, not the sacrifice of it.

**Key insight from Challenger:** The "no fixing, no advice" constraint is correct but incomplete. The deeper constraint for this audience: **no setting yourself aside.** Noticing your own discomfort is not the obstacle to allyship. It's the prerequisite.

**Sharpening direction:**
- Cards become explicitly self-referencing — my body, my boundary, my noticing
- Allies learn to track their own state BEFORE tracking the room
-♣️ 4 (Witness another person) gets a variant that names: "Notice whether you are more or less critical of what they said based on who they are" — but framed as self-investigation, not self-blame
-♣️ 8 (avoidance) becomes explicitly about allyship-shaped avoidance: "Notice one thing you've been steering away from because naming it would cost you the good-ally identity"
-♣️ J/Q/K operators reframe: Jack — "Name one thing alive in your body that you almost edited before speaking" (noticing the fawn impulse); Queen — "Stay with the felt sense of your own position — not theirs"; King — "Close with one word that names what's most true about your experience in this room"

### ♦️ Clean Up
**Design for recovery context:** This suit is about owning patterns, but for this audience, owning without shame is the skill. The Enneagram 2 tendency to over-give and the 6 tendency to anticipate threat combine into a shadow pattern: "I own my patterns in a way that keeps me responsible for everyone else's feelings."

**Sharpening direction:**
- Cards must explicitly separate "owning impact" from "owning fault"
-♣️ 1 Hard ("own who the pattern protects and from what") needs a variant that names the fawning-protective function: "I do this so they won't leave / so I'll be lovable / so I won't be the problem"
-♣️ 3 (Ask for reflection) — variants that help them notice they may be seeking reflection as a fawning pattern ("I'm asking how I was so I can fix myself for them") rather than genuine growth
-♣️ Q (Deepen) — "Sit with something you've been owning — and stay in the discomfort of it without making it about them"

### ♥️ Grow Up
**Core shift:** This suit needs to hold self-position and other-position simultaneously. For this audience, "grow up" means learning to hold their own perspective without making it wrong of the other person — without abandoning themselves to keep the peace.

**Sharpening direction:**
-♣️ 4 (Compare perspectives) — critical card: "Name one thing that's true for you and one thing that's true for them — and hold both without closing the gap" needs a variant that explicitly names the fawning impulse to abandon your position
-♣️ 6 (Ask how someone arrived at their position) — variants that acknowledge power dynamics without collapsing into "I should just defer"
-♣️ 8 (Disagree without severing) — "Say what you believe or need — and acknowledge that it is yours" — the acknowledgment is self-locating, not deferring

### ♠️ Show Up
**Most critical suit for this audience:** This is where fawning becomes visible. Performative offers, big gestures that cost them nothing real, avoidance of the small vulnerable thing.

**Sharpening direction:**
-♣️ 2 (Offer what you have) — Hard variant: "Offer something that costs you something real" — needs to be about the *internal* cost of not overgiving, not just resource cost
-♣️ 9 (Name what's in the way) — "Name the thing that is in the way — and notice whether it is a real obstacle or a story about not being enough"
-♣️ J (Invite) — explicitly: "Invite someone to do something with you — and mean it as a beginning, not a performance"
-♣️ Q (Commit) — "Commit to what you will do — and stay with the vulnerability of having said it out loud without privately deciding later" — this is recovery from the "I'll do whatever you need" pattern

## Play Mode Implication

**Solo first, group emergent.** The deck needs a solo play mode that is the primary path — not a variant. Someone playing alone should be able to do every card in the Wake Up suit without feeling they're "wasting" the allyship dimension by playing solo.

**Group play is the graduate level.** Group play means the person has enough self-centering capacity to not abandon themselves when others are in the room with them. The J/Q/K operators become more important in group play — they modulate the energy when the group gets imbalanced.

# ♣️ Wake Up — V3 Cards (Revised)
## *10 moments of good listening + J/Q/K scales*

**Fixes applied:** Recognition prompt allyship signal • ♣️ 5 ground-first step • QR web page mechanic

---

## Recognition prompt design rule
One phrase that makes the allyship context felt — not a label, a felt moment. The question must be one the ally would ask themselves IN the moment, not one they'd ask in reflection.

---

### ♣️ A — Catch the override

**Recognition:** Did you just catch yourself about to give advice — in a moment where your job was to witness, not solve?

**The move:** Say back what you heard in one sentence. Nothing else.

**Allyship bridge:** Catching the solve-impulse before it fires is the whole practice. The moment you name what they said, most people keep talking — but differently.

---

### ♣️ 2 — Spot the smooth

**Recognition:** Are you being very reasonable right now — and does it feel like effort?

**The move:** Name one thing you didn't say. Not out loud. Just notice it was there.

**Allyship bridge:** The Diplomat who can't name their walk-away price has no price. Spotting the smooth is where the actual boundary starts.

---

### ♣️ 3 — Track the cost

**Recognition:** After the last conversation, did you feel more tired than the interaction seemed to warrant?

**The move:** Name what the allyship cost you — one specific thing. Then stop.

**Allyship bridge:** Grace that depletes you is fawning with better branding. The cost is the data. Someone who can name their depletion is learning to resource themselves.

---

### ♣️ 4 — Feel the split

**Recognition:** Did you just say something kind that you don't actually feel — or are about to?

**The move:** Find the gap between what you said and what you felt. Don't close it. Just notice it.

**Allyship bridge:** Performed care looks identical to real care from the outside. The body knows the difference. The other person usually does too.

---

### ♣️ 5 — Ground before you name

**Recognition:** Before you name what you're feeling — are your feet on the floor and your breath moving?

**The move:** Find your feet. Then name one thing you feel. Then stop.

**Allyship bridge:** You can't ally from dysregulation. Naming without grounding makes the feeling bigger instead of smaller. Ground first. Then speak.

---

### ♣️ 6 — Keep the door open

**Recognition:** Did the other person just finish — and is there something you want to ask before you respond?

**The move:** Ask one question before you respond. Let it be about what they just said, not what you want to say.

**Allyship bridge:** Good listening means the question comes before the answer. The question proves you were listening. The answer proves you were waiting to talk.

---

### ♣️ 7 — Name the gap

**Recognition:** Are you about to offer something — or are you depleted and performing that you're not?

**The move:** Name the gap between what you're offering and what you have. Then decide if the offer still stands.

**Allyship bridge:** "How can I help?" puts the work of figuring out onto the person who needs help. Before you offer — check your cup. Offer what you actually have.

---

### ♣️ 8 — Ask the real question

**Recognition:** Are you about to ask "how can I help?" — or its cousin "what do you need?"

**The move:** Ask instead: what does support look like for you right now?

**Allyship bridge:** "How can I help?" is a question that puts the burden of direction on the person who needs help. The better question offers a direction and lets them correct it.

---

### ♣️ 9 — Stay in the room

**Recognition:** Is there something happening in this conversation that you don't have language for yet?

**The move:** Say the thing without a name. Describe it without diagnosing it. Let it land.

**Allyship bridge:** The moments that matter most are the ones without easy language. Staying in the unnameable moment with someone is the practice most people flee from.

---

### ♣️ 10 — Receive without redirecting

**Recognition:** Did they just share something hard — and did you feel the pull to make it better, fix it, or move past it?

**The move:** Let it sit for one breath longer than is comfortable. Receive without moving.

**Allyship bridge:** The fastest way to collapse a hard moment is to end it prematurely with a solution. The thing that needs to land — needs to land. Let it.

---

### ♣️ J — Moment Scale

**Recognition:** What's the most recent thing you noticed in this exchange — and did you say it?

**The move:** Name one thing you noticed. Let it land without fixing it. Return.

---

### ♣️ Q — Session Scale

**Recognition:** What happened in this conversation that wasn't here at the start?

**The move:** Name what shifted. Then name what stayed the same. Both matter.

---

### ♣️ K — Relationship Scale

**Recognition:** What has this person taught you that you couldn't have learned any other way?

**The move:** Name the lesson. Then say thank you — not for the help, for what you learned through the help.

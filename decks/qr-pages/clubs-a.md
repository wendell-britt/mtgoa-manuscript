# Ally to Wendell — ♣️ Wake Up: A
## *Catch the Override*

---

**The card:**

**Recognition:** Did you just catch yourself about to give advice — in a moment where your job was to witness, not solve?

**The move:** Say back what you heard in one sentence. Nothing else.

**Allyship bridge:** Catching the solve-impulse before it fires is the whole practice. The moment you name what they said, most people keep talking — but differently.

---

**What just happened:**

When you caught yourself about to give advice in a moment where your job was to witness, you just did something most people never learn to do. You interrupted the fix-impulse before it became a fix. That's not small.

Most people don't catch it until they're three sentences in and wondering why the other person just got quieter. You caught it at the impulse. That gap — between the impulse and the execution — is where the practice lives.

The solve-impulse isn't bad. It's just automatic. And automatic solutions don't work on problems that live in the space between two people. The fact that you noticed means you're learning the difference between solving and being with.

---

**If you drew this card again:**

Drawing ♣️ A twice in one week means the fix-impulse is showing up a lot. That's data — not a verdict. It usually means there's a conversation happening that you genuinely want to help with. The practice isn't to suppress the impulse. It's to notice it before it runs.

When you catch it and say back what you heard instead: that's the move. The other person usually goes somewhere new. That's where the allyship actually lives.

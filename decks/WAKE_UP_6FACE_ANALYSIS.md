# 6-Face GM Analysis — Wake Up Suit V2
## *Diagnosis: What's Working, What's Breaking, What Needs to Change*

**Analyzed against:** Allyship-sharpened deck goals, psychographic requirements, solo-play architecture
**Context:** V2 strong on content/purpose, failing on self-containment and solo-playability

---

## 🧠 Architect — Structural Diagnosis

**What it finds:** The 3-question format (Why/When/Allyship use) is the structural core — and it is what's killing solo-playability.

**The format problem:**
- The Why/When/Allyship use structure was built to ENSURE each card answers the three questions
- What it actually produces is a card that REQUIRES three paragraphs of explanation to function
- This is structural debt — the solution created the problem it was solving

**What the structure should be:**
- The 3 questions should be answered by the DESIGN of the card, not by appended paragraphs
- The card's main text should contain the why (implicitly or explicitly)
- The when should be a CONTEXT marker, not a paragraph
- The allyship use should be the RESULT, not a label

**Current architecture:**
```
Card Title
Main text (one sentence)
Why: paragraph
When: paragraph
Allyship use: sentence
```

**Required architecture:**
```
Card Title
Main text (one sentence)
Context: one line
Result: one line
```

**Example — current ♣️ A:**
> **Easy:** Name one thing you just did in this interaction — before you decided whether it was right.
> *Why: Because the pattern runs before the decision. You are already doing it when you think you're deciding.*
> *When: Right after someone describes a problem and you feel the impulse to solve or caretake.*
> *Allyship use: Catching the override before it looks like helping.*

**Should be:**
> **Easy:** Name one thing you just did — before you decide if it was right.
> *The pattern runs before the decision. Catch it there.*
> *After: Someone describes a problem and you feel the impulse to solve it.*

**The compression is not editing. It's architecture.**

## 🏛 Regent — What to Keep

**The strong core — do not cut:**

1. **The pattern vocabulary is correct.** "Catch the override," "Spot the game move," "Feel the cost," "Name the inheritance," "Notice who's absent," "Find the split," "Name the map," "Catch the savior," "Feel what you've been deferring," "See the weather system" — these are real moves, specific to allyship dynamics, not generic. Keep all 10 titles.

2. **The J/Q/K are the best-written cards in the suit.** They compress naturally, have clear constraints, and don't need the Why/When/Allyship structure. They're already solo-playable. The rest of the suit should match this compression level.

3. **The WOKE diagnosis is embedded correctly.** Each card names a specific failure mode that "just noticing" doesn't fix. The cards are not about feeling — they're about catching yourself in the game. This is architecturally correct. Don't abandon it.

4. **The milestone-to-card mapping is sound.** ♣️ A maps to "map is wrong." ♣️ 2 maps to "you are the game." ♣️ 3 maps to "exhaustion is grief." This is the right structure. It should inform card ordering, not just be a separate document.

**What to cut:**

1. **All Why/When/Allyship use paragraphs.** These were scaffolding that became load-bearing. They need to be removed once the card text itself is tight enough to answer all three questions without them.

2. **The "How to Read Each Card" preamble.** If the cards need a manual to function, the cards are broken. The cards should be self-explanatory. The preamble can be replaced with a single line: *"Each card names a pattern and gives you the move to catch it."*

3. **Cross-face references in card text.** Cards that say "The Diplomat's first move" or "The Challenger's power" are leaning on other chapters to do their work. The Wake Up suit must stand alone. References to other faces should be removed from card text and handled in the Face Lenses section instead.

## ⚔️ Challenger — What to Challenge

**Challenge 1: The three-question rule is doing too much work.**

The rule "if the card can't answer all three questions, don't write it" is correct — but the implementation buries the answers in the format instead of building them into the card. The rule needs to stay. The format needs to change so the card itself is the answer.

**Challenge 2: "Feel" is the wrong verb for half the cards.**

♣️ 3 — "Feel the cost" — this is a somatic word hiding an intellectual move. The card is about naming cost, not feeling it somatically. Same with ♣️ 9 — "Feel what you've been deferring" — the practice is naming, not feeling. If the card fires in an Enneagram 2 context without a somatic frame, the word "feel" will trigger fawning patterns ("I feel like I should...") instead of the intended move. Rename to "Track" or "Name" where the practice is cognitive, not somatic.

**Challenge 3: The "Hard" variants are not harder — they're longer.**

The Easy/Medium/Hard distinction currently shows up as word count, not intensity. A Hard card should be emotionally or structurally harder — closer to the edge of what the player is willing to look at. Not more context. The difference between Easy/Medium/Hard should be proximity to the wound, not volume of explanation.

**Challenge 4: The psychographic targeting may be too narrow.**

The cards are written for Enneagram 2/6/6, Green/White, fawning-recovery. That is a specific person. If this is a GENERAL allyship deck (as originally specified), the cards need to work for Orange/Red types too — people who have the opposite problem (too much edge, not enough warmth). Currently the Hard variants read as "go deeper into vulnerability" which works for the 2/6/6 but would be alienating for someone whose work is learning to soften rather than deepen. The suit should be playable from both entry points.

**Challenge 5: Solo-play is declared but not tested.**

The cards reference "let the room receive it" (K), "the room" (J), "after a hard conversation" — these are group phrases. If the deck is solo-playable, each card needs an explicit solo variant. The group language should not appear in cards meant to work alone.

## 🎭 Diplomat — Relational Gaps

**What the suit doesn't yet hold:**

**The allyship dyad is missing.** Every card is about the self (my override, my cost, my inheritance). None of the cards are about the person I'm being an ally to. The question "what does this do to the other person?" is present in the Allyship use field but not in the card text itself. The target person — the one being helped — is a ghost in this suit. They show up as "someone," "they," "the other person" — never as a named presence with their own agency, their own work, their own stake.

**The card needs to hold both sides of the dyad.** "Catch the override" is about me. But the allyship question is always: what does my override do to them? A card that only looks at my side of the equation is half an allyship card.

**Bridge to the Diplomat suit is implicit but should be structural.** The Wake Up suit ends where the Diplomat suit begins — catching yourself in the game is the prerequisite for holding the field. But right now there's no card that directly names "and this is where the next suit takes over." The milestone map handles this at a macro level, but at the card level the connection is invisible.

**The person I'm allied TO should appear in the card.** Not as an abstract "them" — as a named presence whose experience is part of what I'm noticing. This is not group language. This is dyad language.

## 🌊 Shaman — Shadow Diagnosis

**What this suit is avoiding:**

**The anger is missing.** The psychographic has been abused by allyship culture — which means there is real, specific anger at the culture that gave them bad tools. This suit does not give any access to that anger. It is entirely about catching patterns, naming costs, tracking depletion. There is no card that says: *You were hurt by the people who told you to be small. That wound is in here. Name it.*

**The spiritual bypassing potential is high.** The WOKE diagnosis correctly identifies that awareness without action is the failure mode. But this suit could become its own form of spiritual bypass — catching yourself in the game, naming the pattern, and still not doing anything differently. The suit has no mechanism for closing the loop from noticing to doing. The J/Q/K operators provide a partial exit, but the numbered cards have no "and then what" built in.

**The somatic channel has been overcorrected against.** The original V1 was too somatic (feet on floor, three breaths). The V2 overcorrected by removing somatic content almost entirely. The body is actually relevant to allyship — not as a meditation object but as a data source for "is this my pattern or is this real?" The body can carry information that the mind is overriding. The card "Feel the cost" should actually involve the body — but it needs to be the body as evidence, not the body as meditation.

**The shadow of "Catch the savior" — what it doesn't say:** The card catches the savior pattern but doesn't say what the savior is afraid of. The fear underneath the savior pattern is: *If I don't fix this, I have to feel helpless. And being helpless was unsafe in my history.* That belongs in the card's shadow, even if not in the card text itself.

## ⟁ Regent (Continued)

**The compression rules — what each card must pass:**

1. **Main text: one sentence, maximum 20 words.** If you can't say it in 20 words, the card doesn't have a center.

2. **Context marker: one line, one scenario.** Not "right after someone describes a problem" — this is too general. It should name a specific scenario someone would actually be in: "Someone tells you about a problem and you feel the urge to fix it."

3. **Result: one line.** What changes when you do the card. Not "you'll be a better ally" — that's too generic. It should be specific to what shifts: "You notice the difference between resonance and performance."

4. **No cross-face references in card text.** Move all face references to the Face Lenses section.

5. **All three question-answers must be extractable from the card text itself** — not from an appended paragraph.

**The format architecture for V3:**
```
Card Title (pattern name)
One sentence — the move (max 20 words)
Context: one line (specific scenario)
Result: what shifts (one line)
```

This ordering is NOT what the current deck follows. Cards 3 and 4 are misplaced relative to the milestone logic. The sequence should be re-ordered to match the milestone journey — players should encounter the concepts in developmental order, not alphabetical order.

**The 10-card sequence should tell a story:**
- A: I just did something I'm not aware of
- 2: I do this by default, not by design
- 4: I inherited this belief
- 6: I know but don't do
- 3: And here's what it costs
- 5: And here's who's missing because of it
- 7: And it's from my history
- 8: And I'm doing it to them right now
- 9: And I've been deferring the naming
- 10: And the room knows but isn't saying

This is a coherent learning arc. The current order doesn't follow it. Reorder.

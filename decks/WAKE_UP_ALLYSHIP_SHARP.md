# ♣️ Wake Up — Allyship-Sharpened Suit

*For the partner who has been told that being a good ally means making themselves small. It doesn't. This suit teaches you to be the center of your own noticing — because that's the only place you can actually show up from.*

*Solo-playable. No group required. Every card works alone.*

---

### ♣️ A — Name what's present

**Easy:** Name one thing you notice in your body right now — before anyone else needs you to notice them.
*Container: Take one breath. Constraint: This is yours. Not theirs.*

**Medium:** Name what you notice in your body — and notice whether the feeling is yours or whether you're carrying someone else's.
*Container: One or two sentences. Constraint: Name whose it might be if it isn't yours.*

**Hard:** Name what's present in your body — and say whether you're about to override it to keep someone else comfortable.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Use "yes" or "no" if you can. If you're not sure, say "I don't know."*

---

### ♣️ 2 — Pause and breathe

**Easy:** Pause for three breaths. Let your body arrive before your words do.
*Container: Three breaths. Constraint: No speaking until all three are complete.*

**Medium:** Pause for three breaths — and notice whether the pause feels like a threat or like relief.
*Container: Three breaths. Constraint: Name the quality of the pause — not just the fact of it.*

**Hard:** Pause for three breaths — and notice whether the pressure to say something right now is yours or borrowed.
*Container: Three breaths. Constraint: If it's borrowed, name whose it is — then decide if you want to carry it.*

---

### ♣️ 3 — Label what's moving

**Easy:** Label one feeling that is yours — not what you should be feeling, not what they're feeling. Yours.
*Container: One word. Constraint: Not "fine," "okay," or "nothing." Your actual feeling.*

**Medium:** Label what's moving in you — and notice whether it's connected to this moment or to another moment with this person.
*Container: One or two sentences. Constraint: If it's from another moment, say so without needing to explain the whole history.*

**Hard:** Label what's moving in you — and trace whether this feeling is about right now or about every time you've been in this position before.
*Container: One sentence about now, one about then. Constraint: Don't resolve the connection — just name both.*

---

### ♣️ 4 — Witness without caretaking

**Easy:** Listen to the last speaker with your full attention — and say nothing afterward. Let them be their own.
*Container: Until the next person speaks. Constraint: No advice, no relating your own experience, no "me too" that redirects to you.*

**Medium:** Listen to the last speaker — then notice whether you are witnessing them or managing them.
*Container: One sentence about what you noticed in yourself. Constraint: Name the difference between witnessing and managing — even if you can't name exactly which one you did.*

**Hard:** Listen to the last speaker — and notice whether your attention is on them or on how you're coming across to them.
*Container: One sentence about where your attention actually went. Constraint: No justification, no explanation — just the observation.*

---

### ♣️ 5 — Notice group energy with positionality

**Easy:** Notice one thing about what's alive in this room — including you.
*Container: One observation. Constraint: You are part of the room. Name yourself in it.*

**Medium:** Notice who's speaking, who's quiet, and where you are in the room's shape — not just the room's shape.
*Container: Two or three sentences. Constraint: Include yourself in the noticing — not as an addendum.*

**Hard:** Notice the emotional field — and notice who the field belongs to right now.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Use "mine," "theirs," or "the room's" — not just "it."*

---

### ♣️ 6 — Drop out of your head

**Easy:** Notice your feet on the floor. Let your body be the place you live.
*Container: Until you can feel them. Constraint: No talking. Just feel.*

**Medium:** Notice your body as a whole — and notice whether you're treating it as a vehicle for them or as a place you actually live.
*Container: Three breaths. Constraint: Name one thing about the difference — even if it's just that there is a difference.*

**Hard:** Notice your body as a whole — and notice whether you're holding space for them or holding space against yourself.
*Container: For the next two exchanges. Constraint: Your attention stays in your body. You are not managing them or the room.*

---

### ♣️ 7 — Track what's happening now

**Easy:** Name one thing that is happening in this moment — outside of what anyone is saying.
*Container: One observation. Constraint: A sensory fact, not an interpretation.*

**Medium:** Name what's happening in you while you're listening — and notice whether you are with them or with your performance of being with them.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Name the difference if you can.*

**Hard:** Name the boundary between what's happening in the room and what's happening in your story about yourself in the room — and notice which side you're actually on.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Use "room," "story," or "me" — not "we."*

---

### ♣️ 8 — Notice what's in the shadow of allyship

**Easy:** Notice one thing you've been steering away from because naming it would cost you the good-ally identity.
*Container: One observation. Constraint: You don't have to say what it is — just notice which direction you keep going.*

**Medium:** Notice what you're not looking at — and own that it's yours.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: "I'm not looking at…" not "there's a thing that…".*

**Hard:** Notice what's in the room that has no name yet — and say that.
*Container: One sentence about the unnameable. Constraint: Do not explain. Just say what's there.*

---

### ♣️ 9 — Feel before you override

**Easy:** Find one feeling in your body and stay with it for one breath longer than is comfortable.
*Container: One breath past comfort. Constraint: No moving, narrating, or fixing — just feeling.*

**Medium:** Find one feeling — then notice whether the next move is to understand it or to make it okay for someone else.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Name the move, not the feeling.*

**Hard:** Find one feeling — and let it complete whatever motion it came here to complete. Notice whether that motion is yours or a replay.
*Container: Until something settles or shifts. Constraint: You are not the director. You are the witness.*

---

### ♣️ 10 — Acknowledge what's alive but unspoken

**Easy:** Notice the silence in the room — and name one thing it might be carrying that you haven't said yet.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Speak from your own noticing — not as a spokesperson.*

**Medium:** Say the thing that seems to be here but hasn't been spoken yet — and notice whether you're holding it to protect yourself or them.
*Container: One sentence. Constraint: Start with "It seems like…" or "There's a…" — not "You all think…"*

**Hard:** Name the shape of what's present — its texture, weight, temperature — and notice whose it is.
*Container: One or two sentences. Constraint: Use "mine," "theirs," or "the room's" — not "it."*

---

### ♣️ J — Activate

**Name one thing alive in your body that you edited or softened before you spoke it.**
*Constraint: One word only. Let it land without explanation.*

---

### ♣️ Q — Deepen

**Stay with the felt sense of your own position — not theirs — for three breaths. Then say what is most true about your experience right now.**
*Constraint: No framing, no context-setting. Just the truth of your own experience.*

---

### ♣️ K — Complete

**Close with one word that names what's most true about your experience in this room — not what you wish were true, not what they need you to say. Yours.**
*Constraint: One word. No follow-up. Receive it yourself.*

# The Escape Artist — Class Deck

*One of the six classes — one of the six base decks. This is your card pool, sorted so you can draft it. The book names your class, surfaces your belief (your subclass — which cards you start with and which one's locked), and gives you enough to walk into the app and build fast. The full pool lives in the app, where cards unlock at the speed of play. This is the map of it.*

---

## Who you are

You see the door. While everyone else is enduring — adapting, compromising, grinding through a thing that's quietly killing them — you see the walls for what they are, and you know that leaving is a skill, not a failure. That's the gift. The catch is the world calls it quitting, and so, often, do you — so the move that could save you arrives wearing the face of the thing you're most ashamed to do.

Where the gift came from tells you what it is. You didn't learn to spot exits because you were restless. You learned it because being trapped, once, was unbearable — and the way to be safe was to never again be somewhere you couldn't leave. The Escape Artist is the Protector's bid for safety through exits: *if I always know the way out, I can never be caught.* The whole arc of the deck is one turn — from keeping an exit so *you* won't be trapped to holding the door so the people who can't see theirs can finally walk out.

---

## 1. Basic moves — your core cards

**Wake Up (see)**
- **See the Cage** — Recognize misalignment before it becomes a crisis. The walls get familiar fast; name the cage while you can still climb out, not after it's closed.
- **Read the Drain** — Check the meter: are you being drained faster than you recover? Is your presence sustaining a broken thing or changing it? The drain is data, not weakness.

**Clean Up (clear)**
- **Tell Challenge from Cage** — Growth is uncomfortable; a cage is wrong. Before you bolt, ask which one this is — because leaving because it's *hard* is a different move than leaving because it's *wrong.*
- **Talk to the Cage** — Run the gate move on the thing holding you: see it, speak with it, speak as it. You'll usually find the system is afraid of losing *you* as much as you're afraid of losing it — and the grip loosens once you feel both hands.

**Grow Up (build)**
- **The Clean Exit** — Leave well, not just fast. Honor what was real before you go; take the lesson, not the wound. An exit that erases the past leaves you unmoored; one that honors it leaves you free.
- **Hold the Open Door** — For others: remind them the door's been open the whole time. Guide, don't drag — you can't carry someone to freedom they won't walk toward.

**Show Up (deploy)**
- **Take the Test Escape** — Before the big jump, step away small and temporary — a trial, a side door — so the leap stops being all-or-nothing and starts being information.
- **Leave the Door Open** — When you go, don't seal it behind you. The exit you make visible becomes the one the next person can find.
- **Name the Alternative** — Show the path that exists outside the walls. People stay because they can't picture the outside; make the outside concrete.

## 2. Emotional Alchemy moves — your channel cards

Every class metabolizes all five channels; these two are home.

- **Carry It With You** *(sadness → poignance)* — Every exit is an ending, and the grief is real. Don't run so fast you go numb or cling so hard you can't move; mourn what mattered, take a token of it, and let the leaving mean something. Escape is transition, not erasure.
- **Flip the Fear** *(fear → excitement)* — The biggest cage is fear, not walls. Name the fear — *what if leaving makes it worse* — then flip it: *what if leaving makes everything possible.* Then take a small test escape, so the fear has something real to measure itself against instead of an imagined fall.
- The other three you draw from allies: **anger → triumph** (a Disruptor's fire for when the door's locked and has to be broken), **joy → expansion** (so leaving moves *toward* something, not just away), **neutrality → peace** (the steady center that tells restlessness from real misalignment).

## 3. Subclasses — the moves your beliefs generate

You can run any of the six beliefs, and at the Escape Artist each one is two-sided. Worked, it **generates a move** — the gift the wound built. Stuck, the same belief **locks** one of your basic cards. Every campaign you run flips one belief from its lock to its move; six campaigns, and all six belief-moves are yours.

- **Not ready → *The Mapped Exit*** *(preparation mastery)* — you always already know the way out, charted before you ever need it. Stuck, it's the stayer, locking *Take the Test Escape.*
- **Not capable → *Exit From Nowhere*** *(resourcefulness)* — you find the door no one else can see, improvised from nothing. Stuck, it's the clinger, locking trust that you can navigate the unknown.
- **Not worthy → *The Exact Exit*** *(service precision)* — not running, the one clean leave that's precisely right and precisely timed. Stuck, it's the martyr, locking *The Clean Exit.*
- **Not good enough → *The Honest Read*** *(exacting standard)* — your bar won't let you leave on a false reason or stay on one; it tells the real dead-end from the hard-but-worth-it. Stuck, it's the justifier, locking leaving on a good-enough reason.
- **Insignificant → *The Exit That Lands*** *(impact)* — you leave in a way that shifts the system, not just removes you from it. Stuck, it's the endurer, locking believing your exit matters.
- **Don't belong → *The Outsider's Doors*** *(outsider's perspective)* — you see every exit because you never fully entered. Stuck, it's the ghost, locking *Hold the Open Door.*

This is the campaign engine in deck terms: each belief you take all the way through flips a lock into a move, and the deck gets richer every time you run the book again.

## 4. At each Face — where your cards go live

- **Shaman *(what matters)*** — Knowing which spaces are worth staying in and which ones betray what actually matters to you.
- **Challenger *(assert agency)*** — Leaving as the ultimate assertion of agency: the refusal that says *I am not yours to keep.*
- **Regent *(create order)*** — Knowing when an order has hardened into a cage and the loyal move is to go.
- **Architect *(create effectiveness)*** — Finding the alternative path when the system is a genuine dead end, not a fixable one.
- **Diplomat *(belong together)*** — Leaving the belonging that's actually captivity, and pointing toward the kind that isn't.
- **Sage *(how it all fits)*** — Seeing which exits open onto something larger and which are just the same cage with a different door.

## 5. Interactions — drafting other decks to the table

- **Strategist** plans the exit so the leap has a landing.
- **Disruptor** breaks the walls when the door is locked and leaving has to be forced.
- **Connector** builds the network of safe landings the people you guide will need.
- **Storyteller** reframes the leaving as liberation instead of failure.
- **Alchemist** helps everyone process the grief of the threshold so they can actually cross it.

You don't have to be all of these. You have to know which ones you're not, and draft them in.

---

*Who needs your deck: the person who already feels the cage and needs support to leave, the one asking for guidance rather than just permission, the one ready to act. Not the ones who make excuses instead of moves, resist every exit you name, or expect to be carried out. You hold the door. You don't drag people through it.*

# The Connector — Class Deck

*One of the six classes — one of the six base decks. This is your card pool, sorted so you can draft it. The book names your class, surfaces your belief (your subclass — which cards you start with and which one's locked), and gives you enough to walk into the app and build fast. The full pool lives in the app, where cards unlock at the speed of play. This is the map of it.*

---

## Who you are

You see the threads. Who in the room needs to meet before they know it; which two ideas want to be in the same sentence; where a relationship is one introduction away from existing. And here's the catch built into the gift: when you do it well, it looks like nothing happened. You build the bridge, two people cross it, they thank each other, and no one sees you. So you quietly conclude your work isn't real work — which is the exact moment you start to disappear.

Where the weaving came from tells you what it is. You didn't learn to read a room because the room was safe. You learned it because belonging wasn't guaranteed, and the surest way to be safe in a group was to be the one holding it together — the indispensable bridge nobody would cut. The Connector is the Protector's bid for safety through belonging: *if I weave everyone in, I can't be left out.* The whole arc of the deck is one turn — moving from weaving so you won't be excluded to weaving so the people who need each other stop being alone.

## 1. Basic moves — your core cards

**Wake Up (see)**
- **Read the Room** — Walk in and clock the currents before you say anything: who's on the fringe, who's overlooked, where the tension actually sits versus where it's being performed.
- **Find the Open Door** — Stop asking "who needs connection" and ask "who's already reaching." Flow toward the people leaning in, not the ones who've shut it.
- **Name the Dynamic** — When a group feels stuck, give the pattern a face — *the one who keeps the peace by killing every honest thing* — so you can read it instead of drowning in it.

**Clean Up (clear)**
- **The Still Point** — When the room's emotion starts pulling you under, plant. Let the feelings move past you like weather instead of catching every one. You can't bridge from inside the current.
- **Cut the Cord** — Release the connection that only takes. Reciprocity is the test; if you're the only one extending, stepping back isn't cruelty, it's how you stay able to give at all.

**Grow Up (build)**
- **Listen for the Need** — Hear the thing under the words. Most people tell you what they want; your edge is catching what they actually need and who already has it.
- **Detach Without Leaving** — Care without absorbing. The skill isn't distance and it isn't fusion; it's staying warm and present while their storm stays theirs.

**Show Up (deploy)**
- **The Introduction** — Connect the two who should know each other. Say why you thought of it, then get out of the way.
- **Mediate Without Sides** — Hold the middle of a conflict without becoming a combatant. Translate each side to the other instead of judging between them.
- **Spotlight the Invisible** — Name the person who contributes and never gets seen. Public, specific, no strings. Recognition is a bridge too.

## 2. Emotional Alchemy moves — your channel cards

Every class metabolizes all five channels; these two are home.

- **The Still Point** *(neutrality → peace)* — Grounded neutrality, not apathy. Stay solid while everyone else moves chaotically, and you become the one steady thing a fracturing group can re-form around.
- **The Deep Current** *(sadness → poignance)* — Sadness is your compass; it points at where a bond is broken and belonging is missing. Don't rush to fix it — let it show you where connection actually needs to happen, and let that mattering move you.
- The other three you draw from allies: **anger → triumph** you tend to swallow to keep the peace (borrow the boundary-fire of a Disruptor); **fear → excitement** sharpens your discernment so you stop chasing closed doors; **joy → expansion** keeps connection light instead of heavy and dutiful.

## 3. Subclasses — the moves your beliefs generate

You can run any of the six beliefs, and at the Connector each one is two-sided. Worked, it **generates a move** — the gift the wound built. Stuck, the same belief **locks** one of your basic cards. Every campaign you run flips one belief from its lock to its move; six campaigns, and all six belief-moves are yours.

- **Not ready → *The Mapped Room*** *(preparation mastery)* — you walk in already knowing the three bridges that need building before anyone's spoken. Stuck, it's the over-vetter, locking *The Introduction.*
- **Not capable → *Bridge From Nothing*** *(resourcefulness)* — you connect people across gaps everyone swore couldn't be crossed, with whatever's at hand. Stuck, it's the deferrer, locking trust in your own read of the room.
- **Not worthy → *The Exact Match*** *(service precision)* — not networking, the one connection that's precisely what each person needs. Stuck, it's the one-way bridge, locking being on the receiving end of your own gift.
- **Not good enough → *The Connection That Serves*** *(quality stewardship)* — you won't broker the link that looks good and helps no one; you make the one that actually serves the person, not the optics. Stuck, it's the perfect-match hunter, locking the good-enough intro.
- **Insignificant → *Bring In the Erased*** *(amplification)* — you notice who's been written out of the room and weave them back in before anyone names the absence. Stuck, it's the edge-weaver, locking *Spotlight the Invisible* from ever aiming at you.
- **Don't belong → *Catch the Edge*** *(belonging-field-reading)* — you sense the person about to be edged out before they are, and move them back in before the edge consolidates. Stuck, it's the homeless host, locking staying.

This is the campaign engine in deck terms: each belief you take all the way through flips a lock into a move, and the deck gets richer every time you run the book again.

## 4. At each Face — where your cards go live

- **Shaman *(what matters)*** — Sensing the unspoken value the room actually shares, the thing everyone cares about and no one's named.
- **Challenger *(assert agency)*** — Brokering people to the power they need; connecting the person with the will to the person with the lever.
- **Regent *(create order)*** — Building the durable agreements and trust that let a group hold together under strain.
- **Architect *(create effectiveness)*** — Designing the social architecture: the network shape that makes the work actually flow.
- **Diplomat *(belong together)*** — Your home altitude. The belonging weave itself — making the room one where people can be.
- **Sage *(how it all fits)*** — Seeing all the relationships as a single web, where one bond strengthens or strains the rest.

## 5. Interactions — drafting other decks to the table

- **Strategist** turns your read of the room into a plan with steps, so connection becomes traction.
- **Storyteller** gives the bond a story, so people understand *why* they belong together.
- **Disruptor** lends you the fire to hold a boundary when connection is being exploited.
- **Alchemist** shifts the whole emotional field when bridging individuals isn't enough.
- **Escape Artist** builds the safe landings your people need when they're leaving something behind.

You don't have to be all of these. You have to know which ones you're not, and draft them in.

---

*Who needs your deck: the isolated-but-open, the one trying to bridge a divide and lacking the nerve, the valuable person with no social pathway in, the pair stuck in a conflict that needs a translator. Not the ones who resist connection, weaponize it for control, or take without ever giving back. If someone wants comfort instead of connection, that's not your work — pulling them deeper only drains you both.*

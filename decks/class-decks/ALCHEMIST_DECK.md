# The Alchemist — Class Deck

*One of the six classes — one of the six base decks. This is your card pool, sorted so you can draft it. The book names your class, surfaces your belief (your subclass — which cards you start with and which one's locked), and gives you enough to walk into the app and build fast. The full pool lives in the app, where cards unlock at the speed of play. This is the map of it.*

---

## Who you are

You don't just feel emotion — you move it. You walk into a room thick with grief or rage and you can feel where it's stuck and where it wants to go, and you can shift it. That's the gift. The catch is that people will hand you their feelings to carry and call it trust, and you'll take them, because holding the room's emotion is the thing you know how to do — right up until you're underwater in feelings that were never yours.

Where the gift came from tells you what it is. You didn't learn to read and move emotion because your world was calm. You learned it because the emotional weather was dangerous and unpredictable, and the way to be safe was to feel the storm coming and metabolize it before it broke. The Alchemist is the Protector's bid for safety through transmutation: *if I can move what's in the room, it can't detonate on me.* The whole arc of the deck is one turn — from absorbing the charge so it won't hurt you to moving it so a whole group can come through what it couldn't face alone.

---

## 1. Basic moves — your core cards

**Wake Up (see)**
- **Name the Undercurrent** — Every group has an emotional current running under the words. Find the unprocessed one — the grief no one's naming, the rage going sideways — because if it stays unspoken it runs the room.
- **Read Beneath the Reaction** — See the wound under the surface behavior. The person lashing out in anger is often someone who was never taught how to hold sadness.

**Clean Up (clear)**
- **Filter** — Before you take an emotion on, ask one question: *is this mine to hold?* Witnessing is not the same as absorbing. Most of what you carry, you were never asked to.
- **Dialogue with the Emotion** — Run the gate move on the stuck feeling: see it, speak with it, speak as it, until it says what it's afraid to lose. People don't cling to pain because they're broken; they cling because the pain holds a self they're afraid to lose.

**Grow Up (build)**
- **Hold Without Drowning** — Step into the current without being swept down it. Present, not fused. The shadow on one side is the sponge; on the other, the analyst watching from shore. The move is to swim.

**Show Up (deploy)**
- **Make the Container** — Build the space where a group can process together — ritual, silence, music, movement — so the weight doesn't fall on any one person, including you.
- **Name It in the Room** — Say the feeling out loud without judgment: *"I feel the exhaustion in here. Let's sit with it a second."* Naming the current lets it move.
- **Redirect the Flood** — When rage or grief is spiraling, channel it toward something — action, meaning, a next step — instead of letting it burn the group down or curdle into numbness.

## 2. Emotional Alchemy moves — your channel cards

This is your home. Where other classes live in one or two channels, you work all five — and your edge is that you move them *for the group,* not just in yourself.

- **Fear → excitement**, **anger → triumph**, **sadness → poignance**, **joy → expansion**, **neutrality → peace** — you can run any of these on a room. Your signature is the long arc: **sadness → poignance → joy.** You don't erase grief; you let it be honored until it deepens into meaning, and meaning frees people to celebrate what they're grieving. The loss stays. It stops being a weight and becomes a reason.
- Your shadow isn't a missing channel; it's volume. Absorb too much and you drown; stay too dry and people don't trust you're real. *Filter* and *Hold Without Drowning* are how you keep the flow without becoming the flood.

## 3. Subclasses — the belief that governs the deck

- **Not ready → the Unfinished Healer.** Won't hold space until you've "done your own work first," so you never start. Locks: *Make the Container.*
- **Not capable → the Spiller.** You doubt you can hold it, so you let it slosh out instead of moving it. Locks: trusting your own steadiness.
- **Not worthy → the Dumping Ground.** You take everyone's emotion because you don't believe you merit a boundary — the overload shadow as a life. Locks: *Filter.*
- **Not good enough → the Over-Processor.** Won't let a feeling be messy; you keep working it past the point of help. Locks: letting it be enough.
- **Insignificant → the Observer.** Sure your presence doesn't matter, you watch the current from the shore instead of stepping in. Locks: *Name It in the Room.*
- **Don't belong → the Outside Holder.** You hold the group's emotion and stay outside the group, never one of the ones being held. Locks: being inside the container you built.

## 4. At each Face — where your cards go live

- **Shaman *(what matters)*** — Attuning to what the group is grieving or longing for beneath the agenda.
- **Challenger *(assert agency)*** — Turning collective rage into directed momentum instead of scattered heat.
- **Regent *(create order)*** — The ritual and container that let a group's emotion have a stable shape.
- **Architect *(create effectiveness)*** — Designing the emotional conditions a system needs in order to hold under load.
- **Diplomat *(belong together)*** — Processing collective grief into belonging; healing the rupture so the group can re-form.
- **Sage *(how it all fits)*** — Holding the whole emotional field, seeing how one feeling moves through the entire system.

## 5. Interactions — drafting other decks to the table

- **Disruptor** breaks the dam; you redirect the flood before it drowns everyone.
- **Storyteller** gives the feeling a story; you make sure people actually feel the story instead of just hearing it.
- **Strategist** builds the plan; you supply the emotional stamina to follow it through the long middle.
- **Connector** weaves the bonds; you shift the field those bonds live in.
- **Escape Artist** opens the exit; you help people grieve what they're leaving so they can actually go.

You don't have to be all of these. You have to know which ones you're not, and draft them in.

---

*Who needs your deck: the person who knows exactly what to do and can't emotionally move, the one stuck in grief or fear or numbness, the group whose feeling is fracturing it. Not the ones who want you to carry it so they never have to feel it themselves — that's the trap that turns the gift into a burden.*

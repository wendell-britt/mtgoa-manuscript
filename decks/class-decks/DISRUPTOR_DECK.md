# The Disruptor — Class Deck

*One of the six classes — one of the six base decks. This is your card pool, sorted so you can draft it. The book names your class, surfaces your belief (your subclass — which cards you start with and which one's locked), and gives you enough to walk into the app and build fast. The full pool lives in the app, where cards unlock at the speed of play. This is the map of it.*

---

## Who you are

You see the cracks. Where others see "the way things are," you feel the rot under it and the fire rising in you to name it. You've been told your whole life that you're too intense, too much — and you've watched the same people, years later, call the change you fought for inevitable, forgetting they resisted it and forgetting you were right. That's the gift and its tax in one motion.

Now the part the old story about you gets wrong. The fire reads like aggression — like you were *born* to break things. You weren't. Look at where it came from. You didn't learn to challenge everything because challenging is fun. You learned it because somewhere, compliance was dangerous — because the people who went quiet and went along got caught, and the only safety was to refuse to be managed, to name the threat out loud before it closed around you. The Disruptor is the Protector's bid for safety through preemptive challenge: *if I won't be controlled, I can't be trapped.* The fire is a survival move, not a war crime. And the whole arc of the deck is one turn — from burning so you won't be caught to burning so the people stuck in the cage with you get a door.

---

## 1. Basic moves — your core cards

**Wake Up (see)**
- **See the Crack** — Systems don't fall to one big push; they fall where they're already fracturing. Find the place the structure is quietly failing instead of swinging at the strongest wall.
- **Find What's Protected** — Run the gate move on the resistance: every broken system is guarding something — power, comfort, an identity. Name what it's afraid to lose and you know where to press.

**Clean Up (clear)**
- **Refine the Fire** — Not everything needs to burn. Ask *is this the right target, is this the moment* before you strike. A scalpel changes a system; a flamethrower just makes one.
- **Pick the Battle** — You can't fight every war. Choose the ones that actually move something, and let the rest go, so your fire lands instead of scattering.

**Grow Up (build)**
- **Aim at the Structure** — Target the system, not the people inside it. The second you make it about a person, they defend themselves instead of examining the thing.
- **Cut the First Crack** — Before the big break, make a small fracture undeniable — reveal one contradiction — so the system starts questioning itself before you ever push.

**Show Up (deploy)**
- **Strike at Readiness** — Push when people are primed, not before and not after. Too early reads as reckless; too late and someone else owns the moment.
- **Offer the Alternative** — Don't just break it; give people something to believe in. Break without a next, and they'll cling to the broken thing out of fear of the void.
- **Name the Contradiction** — Hold the system's own incoherence up to it. You don't have to attack — sometimes you just make people unable to un-see what was always there.

## 2. Emotional Alchemy moves — your channel cards

Every class metabolizes all five channels; these two are home.

- **Forge the Anger** *(anger → triumph)* — Anger is fuel, not the goal. Clarify the injustice, turn the frustration into a concrete demand, and measure yourself by progress, not by how much resistance you're generating. Burning *forward* is the move; burning *out* is the shadow wearing the same flame.
- **Let the Loss Land** *(sadness → poignance)* — Before you burn something, mourn it — and understand that the people resisting you are grieving, not just being difficult. Sadness also shows you mercy: it finds the cracks where change wants to flow, so you stop bludgeoning walls that would've fallen with a whisper.
- The other three you draw from allies: **fear → excitement** (a Strategist's read tells you when to hold), **joy → expansion** (so the fire warms instead of only scorching), **neutrality → peace** (the still center that keeps the fire from running you).

## 3. Subclasses — the moves your beliefs generate

You can run any of the six beliefs, and at the Disruptor each one is two-sided. Worked, it **generates a move** — the gift the wound built. Stuck, the same belief **locks** one of your basic cards. Every campaign you run flips one belief from its lock to its move; six campaigns, and all six belief-moves are yours.

- **Not ready → *The Mapped Strike*** *(preparation mastery)* — you've already found the precise fracture point before you move on it. Stuck, it's the smolderer, locking *Strike at Readiness.*
- **Not capable → *Break With What's Here*** *(resourcefulness)* — you find the disruption available to the powerless, the leverage no one thought counted. Stuck, it's the doubter, locking trust in your own call to move.
- **Not worthy → *The Precise Cut*** *(service precision)* — you target the exact structure, not the people and not the noise. Stuck, it's the anonymous match, locking claiming the movement.
- **Not good enough → *Worth the Fire*** *(exacting standard)* — your bar tells you which battles actually count, so the fire doesn't scatter. Stuck, it's the over-justifier, locking the imperfect-but-right push.
- **Insignificant → *The Undeniable Break*** *(impact)* — you disrupt so it can't be waved away, because you know what it is to be ignored. Stuck, it's the caged rebel, locking lighting it at all.
- **Don't belong → *Name What They Can't See*** *(outsider's perspective)* — you expose the rot the embedded have normalized into furniture. Stuck, it's the outside agitator, locking *Offer the Alternative.*

This is the campaign engine in deck terms: each belief you take all the way through flips a lock into a move, and the deck gets richer every time you run the book again.

## 4. At each Face — where your cards go live

- **Shaman *(what matters)*** — Naming the sacred thing being violated — the line that's been crossed and gone unspoken.
- **Challenger *(assert agency)*** — Your home altitude. The direct challenge, agency asserted against a system that wanted you compliant.
- **Regent *(create order)*** — Breaking the order that's become a cage so a livable one can form in its place.
- **Architect *(create effectiveness)*** — Exposing exactly where the system's design is broken, not just that it feels wrong.
- **Diplomat *(belong together)*** — Challenging the false harmony — the "belonging" that's actually everyone agreeing to suppress the same thing.
- **Sage *(how it all fits)*** — Reading which disruption serves the whole and which is just your fire wanting somewhere to go.

## 5. Interactions — drafting other decks to the table

- **Connector** makes the change socially survivable, so people embrace the shift instead of rejecting it out of fear.
- **Storyteller** frames the breaking as transformation, so people see possibility and not just wreckage.
- **Strategist** channels the movement you create into outcomes that actually hold.
- **Alchemist** processes the grief and shock you unleash, so the disruption doesn't capsize the people in it.
- **Escape Artist** opens alternative pathways out when the fight itself is the cage.

You don't have to be all of these. You have to know which ones you're not, and draft them in.

---

*Who needs your deck: the situation rotting under a stability nobody will question, the complacent room, the system quietly protecting harm. Not the people who just want the drama, or the fight for its own sake. Your fire is for the cage, not for the company.*

# ♥️ Grow Up — V3 Cards
## *6 face moves as allyship capacity tools*

**Structure:** A–6 = inner (your blocker). 7–10 = outer (their growth). J = Diplomat bridge. Q = Sage altitude. K = sovereign vision.

---

### ♥️ A — Shaman: Stay with it

**Recognition:** In the hard moment — are you fixing, fleeing, or just barely holding?

**The move:** Stay with the felt sense one breath longer than is comfortable. Don't name it. Don't fix it. Just stay.

**Allyship bridge:** The ally who can stay with what's alive without making it better is the ally who can finally be with someone in the hard moments that actually matter.

---

### ♥️ 2 — Challenger: Draw the line

**Recognition:** Did you just say yes when you meant no — or are about to?

**The move:** Name one thing you will not do in this allyship. Say it clearly. No essay, no apology.

**Allyship bridge:** An ally who knows their lines has lines worth crossing. The person who can't say no to everything has nothing to say yes to.

---

### ♥️ 3 — Regent: Hold the structure

**Recognition:** Have you been improvising — showing up when it's convenient, disappearing when it's not?

**The move:** Name one commitment you made in this allyship — and whether you've kept it. Then do one small thing that proves you will.

**Allyship bridge:** Trust is not built in the moments of brilliance. It's built in the small, consistent things that prove you are the same person today that you were yesterday.

---

### ♥️ 4 — Architect: Name the pattern

**Recognition:** Is this the same conversation you've had three times before — with a different person?

**The move:** Name the pattern that keeps repeating in this allyship dynamic. Not what happened — what KEeps happening.

**Allyship bridge:** The ally who can see the system can step outside of it. Naming the game is the first move toward changing it.

---

### ♥️ 5 — Diplomat: Hold your ground

**Recognition:** Are you making room by disappearing — agreeing, smoothing, performing ease you don't feel?

**The move:** Name one thing you believe that the person you're helping disagrees with. Say it without attacking. Just name it.

**Allyship bridge:** Making room without holding your ground is not bridging. It's vanishing. Real bridge-builders stand on both sides.

---

### ♥️ 6 — Sage: Question the certainty

**Recognition:** Are you certain you're right about this — certain enough that you've stopped listening?

**The move:** Name one thing you are certain about — and ask quietly: is this definitely true?

**Allyship bridge:** The ally who can question their own certainty is the one who can actually learn from the person they're trying to help.

---

### ♥️ 7 — Shaman: Be with them

**Recognition:** Are they in their own hard moment — and is your first instinct to make it better?

**The move:** Stay with them. Don't fix it. Don't explain why it will be okay. Just be in the room with what's hard.

**Allyship bridge:** The most powerful thing you can offer someone in pain is your willingness to not look away.

---

### ♥️ 8 — Challenger: Ask for their line

**Recognition:** Are they saying yes to everything — including things they probably shouldn't?

**The move:** Ask: what's the line you won't cross? Then stay quiet until they answer.

**Allyship bridge:** Help them know their own no. The person who can name what they won't do knows more about themselves than the person who can name what they will.

---

### ♥️ 9 — Regent: Show up three times

**Recognition:** Do they not trust structure — because the last person who promised didn't deliver?

**The move:** Show up. One small thing. Then do it again. Then one more time.

**Allyship bridge:** Trust is built in the gap between what you said you'd do and what you actually did. Close the gap three times and watch what changes.

---

### ♥️ 10 — Architect: Name the game

**Recognition:** Are they solving the wrong problem — and do they not see it?

**The move:** Name the pattern you see. Ask: is this the game you think you're playing?

**Allyship bridge:** The person who can see the game from the outside can finally choose whether to play it. That's the gift of the Architect ally — clarity.

---

### ♥️ J — Diplomat: Bridge without vanishing

**Recognition:** Are you making room for their position by abandoning your own — or is there a way to hold both?

**The move:** Name your disagreement. Then name where you agree. Find the real bridge — not the smooth one.

**Allyship bridge:** The Diplomat who can stand for something AND make room for others is the ally who can actually heal divisions. Not by splitting the difference — by holding both sides.

---

### ♥️ Q — Sage: See the altitude

**Recognition:** Is this conversation stuck — everyone at each other, no one moving?

**The move:** Name which altitude this conversation is happening at. Then ask: is this where we need to be?

**Allyship bridge:** The Sage ally names what's actually happening at a level that changes the conversation. Not what's wrong — which altitude we're on.

---

### ♥️ K — What are you growing into?

**Recognition:** If you were fully resourced, fully trained, and fully showing up — what kind of ally would you be?

**The move:** Name the ally you are growing into. What would you do differently right now if you were that person?

**Allyship bridge:** Vision is the start of capacity. The ally who can name what they're becoming has already begun to arrive.

---

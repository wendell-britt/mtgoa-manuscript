# 6-Face GM — What earns length in a Distortion section

**Created:** 2026-08-01
**Reframed by Wendell:** *"Each of them seem to be adding something that expands in a way I'm not mad about. The ch3 one is really good because it helps explain how the emotions work. If this section can help explain the concepts of the chapter then it earns its place — and now we can ask what is ch7 missing in the same vein?"*
**So the question is not how short. It is: what is the expansion doing, and does ch7 lack one.**

---

## The test

**A Distortion expansion earns its length when it pre-teaches the chapter's own instrument through the village's absence of it.**

ch3 is the model. Its four paragraphs are not four restatements — they are the four EA channels, each taught by showing what the village does with that channel unattended:

> The village still experienced **fear**… Without the Shaman to say…
> The village still experienced **anger**… Without the Shaman to say…
> The village still experienced **sadness**…
> The village still experienced **joy**…

By Section 4 the reader has already met the channels once, in a form she cannot mistake for a definition. **The section is doing double duty and its length is the price of the second job.**

---

## Applying the test

| ch | the expansion | teaches the instrument? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ch3** | four EA channels, each through its absence | **yes** — the channels are the book's base instrument | **earns it** |
| **ch4** | six restatements of one substitution | **no** — none of the six maps to Line / Interrupt / Demand / Refusal / Reckoning | **padding** |
| **ch5** | ~350w reader-turn on organizations you built | **yes** — teaches Honor ↔ Reform experientially | **earns it, misfiled** |
| **ch6** | two blind-spot closers, near-verbatim | **no** — the same beat twice | **padding** |
| **ch8** | three named games with `The test:` lines | **yes** — game-identification is ch8's whole instrument | **earns it, wrong format** |

**Two are padding. Three earn their length. None of the three is in the right place.**

---

## 🌊 Shaman — Felt sense

Teaching through absence works on a reader because **absence is felt before it is understood.** *"The village still experienced fear"* lands as recognition; a definition of the Metal channel would land as homework. ch3's four paragraphs are the chapter's gentlest teach and its reader does not know she is being taught.

**ch4 and ch6 produce the opposite feeling.** Six ways of saying *they got polite instead* reads as the author making sure. That is the register of someone who does not trust the first sentence.

**Shaman verdict:** the length is not the problem anywhere. The repetition is, and only in two chapters.

---

## ⚔️ Challenger — What to cut without mercy

**ch4's six restatements.** *Politeness for peace / sophisticated language / assertive communication / letting things slide / willingness to be unwelcome / afraid of conflict.* One idea, six passes. Cut to two.

**ch6's doubled closer**, two paragraphs apart and nearly word-for-word:
> The village did all of this without realizing it. It thought itself holistic… What it actually did:
>
> The village didn't realize any of this. It thought itself practical. What it actually did:

**And the book-wide one: four of five chapters state the blind spot twice** — once as *they meant well*, once as *they never saw it*. ch3, ch4, ch5 and ch6 all do it. That is one beat delivered twice in four chapters, and it is the cheapest cut in the set.

**Challenger verdict:** cut the doubling everywhere. Do not touch the three that teach.

---

## 🏛 Regent — What to preserve

**ch5's reader-turn is the best writing in any Distortion.** *"You came out of those spaces with a principle… Then you watched what you built dissolve… You know which part of that paragraph is yours. I don't need to name it."* Preserve every word. It is misfiled, not overlong.

**ch8's three games are Section 5's format** — headers, `The test:` lines, the *Up Close* shape. Preserve, relocate.

**Regent verdict:** relocation, never deletion, for both. The same ruling as the deck sections, for the same reason: the material is good and filed under the wrong heading.

---

## 🧠 Architect — The rule, and the coverage gap

Stated as a rule the book can hold:

> **The Distortion pre-teaches the chapter's five through their absence.**

Measured against it, the book is inconsistent in a way nobody chose:

```
ch3   four channels through absence         ← the model
ch4   nothing; six restatements             ← should enumerate Line/Interrupt/Demand/Refusal/Reckoning
ch5   the polarity, experientially          ← earns it, wrong section
ch6   nothing; a doubled closer             ← should enumerate Observe/Model/Design/Deploy/Hand Off
ch8   three games                           ← teaches, but not the five modes of sight
```

**ch4 and ch6 have the same missed opportunity.** Both have a five-item instrument sitting in Section 4, and both spend their Distortion restating one idea instead of pre-teaching it. Fixing them is not compression — it is replacing padding with the teach that should have been there.

**Architect verdict:** adopt the rule. It shortens two chapters and lengthens nothing, because the padding it removes is the space the teach needs.

---

## 🎭 Diplomat — What ch7 is missing

Two things, one mechanical and one conceptual.

**1. A mode went missing in compression.** The 273-word draft covers camps *(Translator)*, hard conversations *(Field-Holder)*, ruptures *(Repairer)*, decisions *(Integrative Negotiator)* — **four of five. The Bridge-Builder beat, newcomers, was cut.** Under the rule above that is not a length saving, it is a hole in the teach.

**2. The Care ↔ Impact polarity is absent.** ch7's Distortion says nothing about it, and the polarity is what makes the Diplomat's failure legible rather than merely sad. The chapter's own sentence:

> **Care without impact is attendance: warm, dependable, and doing nothing.**

That is the Distortion's thesis and it currently appears nowhere in it. ch5's expansion earns its length by teaching its polarity; ch7's should too.

**Incidental find:** that sentence appears twice in ch7, at :185 and :527, which is what `dupes.py` has been reporting all session. :527 explicitly recalls :185 — *"You drew the Care ↔ Impact axis in Section 3"* — so it is a deliberate callback rather than an accident, but it needs a ruling or a variation, since a duplication report cannot tell the difference.

**Diplomat verdict:** ch7 needs the fifth mode restored and the polarity landed. Roughly +80 words, to ~350 — still half the shortest sibling, and now earning what it spends.

---

## 📖 Sage — Panoramic view

The Distortion sits in the same slot in nine chapters and nobody ever decided what it is for. So it became the place where anything not belonging elsewhere accumulates: ch5's reader-turn, ch8's daemon material, ch4's and ch6's throat-clearing.

**Wendell's reframe is the decision that was never made.** The Distortion is the book's **pre-teaching slot** — where the chapter's instrument gets met once, as loss, before it is met as method. That is a real job, it justifies real length, and it explains why ch3's is the one that reads well.

Which reverses the finding this analysis was commissioned under. **The siblings are not overlong. Two are padded, two are carrying other sections' material, and one is right.** Compression was the wrong instrument; the right one is a job description.

**Sage verdict:** adopt the slot definition, then audit against it. Length follows.

## What ch7's Distortion needs

1. **Restore the Bridge-Builder beat** — newcomers, arrival as audition. One sentence in the enumeration paragraph.
2. **Land Care ↔ Impact.** The village kept the care and lost the impact, and care without impact is attendance. This is what makes the section teach rather than describe.

Target ~350 words. Still the shortest in the book, and the only one where every sentence is doing one of the two jobs.

## Open questions for Wendell

2. **Where do ch5's reader-turn and ch8's three games go?** ch8's have an obvious home in Section 5. ch5's is less clear — it may belong in Section 3 with the concept.
3. **The `Care without impact` duplication** at ch7:185 and :527 — deliberate callback, so does it stay identical, or vary on the second pass so the duplication report stays meaningful?

## A. The template (Ch0 established this; the per-chapter shape is identical)

The Ch0 "First Move" is the canonical template. Every chapter's First Move follows the same eight bullets — only the *specific ask* and the *target reader* change per Face:

1. **Time-boxed window.** A clock. Not "soon." Not "in your practice." A specific number of days.
2. **Real person, specific relation.** "Someone who has known you longer than a single conversation." Not a stranger, not a coach, not the app. The Village target.
3. **The single sentence the reader says.** Quoted in the book. The reader does not have to design their own ask; they have to *deliver* it. Charge-test built in.
4. **A finish line.** "One conversation. Twenty minutes, maybe less." The reader knows when they're done.
5. **Why-it-matters in one sentence.** "The asking is the move" — compressed, not buried in explanation.
6. **Charge-test (Two Readings applied).** The section names reading one (the move *starts* something) and reading two (the move is a sophisticated form of staying seen without being known). Same test as Ch0.
7. **First-person disclosure that turns universal.** "I built this book for three years while telling myself I wasn't avoiding it." The author is in the move, not outside it.
8. **BAR prompt at close.** "*Who, what you said, what they said back, what surprised you. → app*" — the recognition-capture loop, wired to the move.

**Placement:** Between Section 6 (The Game) close and Section 7 (Recap and Transition) open. The action lands *after* the chapter's mechanism is in hand and *before* the recap, so the reader doesn't have to choose between "do the thing" and "remember the thing." Recap then opens with: "Before you move on — did you do it?"

## B. Per-Chapter First Move (draft)

For each of Ch1–Ch8, the First Move includes:
1. The per-spec target from WB-1.
2. The specific ask sentence(s) the reader will say.
3. The time-box and the person type.
4. The charge-test reframe (readings one and two).
5. The author-shadow disclosure.
6. The BAR prompt.

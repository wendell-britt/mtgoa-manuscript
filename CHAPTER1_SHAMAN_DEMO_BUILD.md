## What the Demo Needs to Include

### 1. URGENCY — "Why Now"
**Question this answers:** Why should I care about this right now? Why is allyship more urgent than ever?

What might be in here:
- The current state of allyship (performative, exhausting, failing)
- What the old frameworks are missing (the "why is my allyship broken" moment)
- The promise of a different way

### 2. INVITATION INTO A NEW WORLD — "Enter The Forest"
**Question this answers:** You've convinced me something's wrong. What's the path?

What might be in here:
- The Forest concept introduced
- The Village framing
- Why the forest is necessary (you can't navigate the village without knowing what's in you)
- The passage between village and forest as the practice

### 3. THE PROTECTOR GATE — "First Gate Encounter"
**Question this answers:** What does it feel like to encounter yourself?

What might be in here:
- Introduce all 8 gates/voices briefly
- Focus deeply on Protector (the first gate)
- Body-based exercise: "where do you feel the boundary?"
- The protector's shadow and its gift
- A practice for meeting your protector
- Connecting protector to superpower origin

### 4. SUPERPOWER ORIGIN — "Remember Your Gift"
**Question this answers:** What's the actual allyship superpower I carry?

What might be in here:
- The Wichita origin story (already written, already strong)
- Reflection prompts to find their own origin
- The connection between early adaptation and superpower
- BARs-style reflection: Breakthrough, Action, Reflection on their origin

### 5. THE VILLAGE — "Why You Return"
**Question this answers:** All this inner work has a point. What is it?

What might be in here:
- The village concept
- Anti-hero warning (getting lost in the forest)
- The commitment to return
- How the forest prepares you for the village
- "Going back is the work" as thesis

---

## Deliverable: One Self-Contained Chapter

The demo chapter should work as a standalone reader experience. Someone who reads only this chapter should:
1. Understand why the system exists (urgency)
2. Know what the Forest is (invitation)
3. Have met their Protector gate (first gate)
4. Have a sense of their superpower origin (their gift)
5. Know why they should go back to the village (the point)

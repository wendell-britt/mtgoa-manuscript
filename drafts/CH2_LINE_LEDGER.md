# CH2 LINE AUDIT — 65 SITES

**Protocol.** Every site is presumed cut. It survives only by passing all three:
**Function** (delivers content the reader does not already have) ·
**Earned** (if it asks for a feeling, the preceding text supplied the material) ·
**Non-substitutable** (plain declarative rewrite loses content, not just cadence).

**Dispositions.** `KEEP` — defended, all three pass. `MERGE` — sentence survives, the isolating paragraph break does not. `REWRITE` — content survives in plain prose. `CUT` — fails Function.

## WHAT THE PASS FOUND UNDERNEATH THE CONSTRUCTIONS

**Four metaphors for entering the Forest.** *Threshold map* (L99), *trailhead* (L182), *your way in* (L250), *the door* (L268). Each one is the chapter telling the reader she is at the entrance. Recommend keeping one.

**Five statements of befriend-the-daemons.** L162, L180, L356, L375, L452. The claim is the chapter's thesis and is worth stating twice; five times in the same construction is the reason the chapter reads as circling.

**Three location claims for the Forest inside twelve lines.** L134, L146, L156.

These are larger than the sentence-level findings and none of them would have surfaced from a line-by-line read. They surfaced because the detector clustered the same construction and the repetition became visible.

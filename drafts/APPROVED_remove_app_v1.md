## Measurement note, third instance tonight

The cuts score `zombie 3.17` against the original's `2.40`, both over the 1.30
line. The nominalizations are *the verdict*, *the sequence*, *the pattern* — all
existing text — and the ratio rose only because removing words shrank the
denominator.

**This is now the third time tonight** the same artefact has appeared: the
joystick edit (`copula 1.72`, identical before and after), the ch1 visual marker
(`passive 2.96 → 3.10`), and this. **Cutting words from nominal-heavy prose
raises the ratio without adding a defect.** `.claude/skills/mtgoa-review` warns
about samples under ~300 words; this is the specific shape that warning takes.
Compare against the original before treating any short-sample ratio as a finding.

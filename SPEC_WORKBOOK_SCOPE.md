**Purpose:** Define the workbook's exact scope — what it contains, what it doesn't, and why each Delta tool is there.

**Status:** DRAFT — for review

## Core Principle

The workbook is not a summary of the book. It is not exercises for each chapter. It is the infrastructure for sustained practice — the structure a reader needs to practice the Delta tools consistently over 52 weeks.

**The book teaches. The workbook gives practice infrastructure.**

| Product | Job |
|---------|-----|
| Book (Alpha/Gamma) | Teaches concepts, demonstrates tools, builds understanding |
| Workbook (Delta) | Provides practice infrastructure for tools that need repeated application |

**Rule:** The book must be complete without the workbook. The workbook must be comprehensible without the book. They are two different products with two different jobs.

## The Workbook Structure

### Design Philosophy

The workbook is designed around three constraints:
1. **ICA reader level** — a reader without a coach can complete every exercise alone
2. **Time budget** — most exercises fit a 20-30 minute weekly session
3. **No coach required** — structure guides the reader through shadow work without facilitator

|---------|---------|-------------|

## Detailed Section Specs

### Section 1: Opening

**Job:** Set reader expectations, explain book + workbook relationship, establish 52-week rhythm.

**Content:**
- How this workbook works (not a summary — a practice tool)
- The 52-week rhythm: weekly practice + monthly review
- What you'll need: journal, Alchemy Cards deck (physical), pen
- How to use this without a coach
- Book cross-reference guide (which chapter maps to which workbook section)

**Word target:** ~800 words

---

### Section 2: Your Allyship Inventory

**Job:** Give the reader their current baseline — who they are entering the practice.

**Content:**
- Character Sheet (refreshed from book Ch0) — full page, one-time fill
- Current dominant face tracker (which face are you leading with?)
- Shadow face recognition guide (how to know when your shadow face is running)
- NPC pattern tracker — one page to name your autopilot pattern

**Delta tools used:** Character Sheet (not a new tool — a workbook copy of the book exercise)

**Word target:** ~600 words + full-page exercise layout

---

### Section 3: Shadow Practice (3-2-1)

**Job:** Full 3-2-1 process reference + 52-week shadow log with session templates.

**Content:**

**Part A — Process Reference (Gamma-style, in workbook for convenience):**
- The 3-2-1 overview (what it is, why it works)
- Step-by-step: Third Person → Second Person → First Person
- Tips for when it gets hard
- Warning signs (avoiding vs. processing)

**Part B — Session Template:**
- Pre-session: Setting intentions
- During: The 3-2-1 prompts (written, not just mental)
- Post-session: What to do with what came up

**Part C — 52-Week Shadow Log:**
- One page per session (date, trigger, gate activated, what came up, integration note)
- 52 pages — enough for weekly practice
- Monthly review prompts embedded (Month 1, 2, 3...)

**Delta tools used:** 3-2-1 Shadow Process

**Word target:** ~1,200 words + 52 session pages

---

### Section 4: The BARs Practice

**Job:** Full BARs journal — daily logging, weekly review, 52-week tracking. This is the primary selling point of the workbook (besides the physical card deck).

**Content:**

**Part A — Daily BAR Journal:**
- One entry per day: Breakthrough / Action / Reflection / Sustain
- Space for date + which chapter/bar encounter prompted it
- 52 weeks × 7 days = 364 daily entries (loose leaf, not bound — reader can reorganize)

**Part B — Weekly Review:**
- End-of-week reflection: What BAR did you complete? What changed? What's your sustain practice?
- One page per week = 52 pages

**Part C — Monthly Integration:**
- Month-end pattern recognition: Which channels showed up most? Which gates keep activating?
- Visual tracker (simple chart — no complex data viz)

**Part D — 52-Week View:**
- Big picture: How many BARs completed? Which chapters have you worked through?
- Milestone markers (Ch1 done, Ch3 done, etc.)

**Delta tools used:** BARs Loop (full practice)

**Note:** The Alchemy Cards (physical deck, companion product) work alongside this. Each card drawn = one BAR entry. The journal tracks the BAR; the cards are the tactile reinforcement.

**Word target:** ~800 words + 52 weekly pages + 364 daily entries (structured, not much prose)

---

### Section 5: The 8 Gates Walk

**Job:** Full 8-gate reference + tracker + 90-day revisit scheduler.

**Content:**

**Part A — 8 Gates Reference:**
- The 8 gates in order (Protector → Controller → Skeptic → Fixer → Fear → Victim → Damaged Self → Vulnerable Child)
- What each gate is, what it protects, what it needs
- Signs you're at this gate (recognition prompts)

**Part B — First Walk Guide:**
- Step-by-step for completing the 8 gates walk (ideally with 3-2-1)
- Space to write during the walk (pre-formatted prompts in the workbook)
- What to do after: integration notes

**Part C — Gate Tracker:**
- Track which gates you've walked through
- Track which gates keep reactivating
- Notes on what each gate showed you

**Part D — 90-Day Revisit Scheduler:**
- After first full walk, reader schedules revisits
- Prompts: What has changed? What is still active?
- 4 revisit templates (Month 3, 6, 9, 12)

**Delta tools used:** 8 Gates Walk

**Word target:** ~900 words + tracker pages

---

### Section 6: Your Dojo

**Job:** Dojo selection + ongoing tracking. Reader chooses their primary dojo domain and practices in it all year.

**Content:**

**Part A — Dojo Selection:**
- The 4 domains: Fundraising Dojo, Impact Storytelling Dojo, Direct Action Dojo, Skillful Organizing Dojo
- Which one calls to you? (single choice, with rationale space)
- What it means to train in your dojo

**Part B — Dojo Practice Log:**
- Weekly entry: What did you practice? What showed up? What did you learn?
- 52 weekly pages (one per week)
- Prompts per domain (specific to each dojo type)

**Part C — Dojo Review:**
- Month-end: How is my dojo practice going? What am I avoiding?
- Quarterly: Do I need to switch domains?

**Delta tools used:** Dojo Selection + Tracking

**Word target:** ~700 words + 52 weekly log pages

---

### Section 7: Alchemy Cards

**Job:** How to use the physical card deck alongside the journal. Not a replacement for the journal — the cards are the tactile memory.

**Content:**
- What the cards are (52-card deck, physical, companion product)
- How cards + journal work together (card = BAR trigger, journal = BAR tracking)
- Card spread ideas (3-card, 7-card, full deck)
- How to use cards when stuck
- Card review ritual (end of month: lay out your cards, what do they show?)

**Delta tools used:** Alchemy Cards

**Note:** This section is short because the cards are a physical product. The workbook teaches the practice; the cards are the artifact.

**Word target:** ~500 words

---

### Section 8: Advanced WAVE

**Job:** Deeper WAVE-Spiral applications for readers who want to go further than the Ch2 introduction.

**Content:**
- WAVE-Spiral in complex situations (multiple channels active simultaneously)
- Using WAVE-Spiral with another person (not just solo)
- Advanced EA moves (transcend → generative → control in sequence)
- Integration practice: combining WAVE-Spiral + 3-2-1

**Who this section is for:** Readers who have done the basic practice and want to deepen. Can be marked as "optional" — no ICA reader is required to do this section.

**Delta tools used:** WAVE-Spiral (advanced applications)

**Word target:** ~600 words

---

### Section 9: Monthly Reviews

**Job:** Structured month-end review that works across all other sections. Not a new tool — a review protocol that pulls from all other practice.

**Content:**
- Month-end ritual: 30-60 minutes
- Pattern recognition: Which tools have I used most? Which gates keep showing up?
- Integration check: What has changed in how I show up?
- Next month intention: What am I practicing toward?
- 12 monthly templates (same structure, reader fills)

**Word target:** ~400 words + 12 monthly templates

---

### Section 10: Appendix

**Job:** Cross-reference map and resource index.

**Content:**
- Book → Workbook cross-reference (which chapter → which section)
- Tool index (all Delta tools, one paragraph each)
- Resource list (other books, practices, recommended further reading)
- FAQ (common questions about the practice)

**Word target:** ~500 words

## What This Workbook Is NOT

| Not included | Reason |
|--------------|--------|
| Full tool explanations (these are in the book) | Book + workbook are separate products |
| Coaching or facilitation | That is a separate product/service |
| Complex data visualization or tracking | ICA reader level — simple trackers only |
| Social practice (group work) | No facilitator = no group work design |
| The physical Alchemy Cards | Separate companion product (physical deck) |

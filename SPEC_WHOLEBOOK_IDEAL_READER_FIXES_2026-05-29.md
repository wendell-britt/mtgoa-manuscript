The Sage sorted the nine fixes by the one game underneath them — *convert a book-you-master into a book-you-do.* Every fix is one of three kinds:

- **🎯 THE GAME** — changes whether the reader *acts*: **WB-2, WB-1, WB-5.** Ship these first. When these are in and the reader acts, the book works. *(The audit confirms WB-1 is **the** fix: the single most damaging issue book-wide is the missing Forest→Village conversion beat — see WB-1.)*

**Stop condition (Sage):** when WB-2 + WB-1 + WB-5 are in and the reader acts, the book works. Everything after is polish, not rescue. Do not let the fix process become the new perfectionism — that is the book's own Ch0 confession.

## Root Principle (carried from the Ch0 fixes spec, now book-wide)

> The book's structure is the ideal habitat for the disease it diagnoses. Every chapter adds systems to master; the reader masters them *instead of* changing. So no whole-book fix is "explain better" or "add a warning." Each fix must do one of three things:
> 1. **Convert insight → bounded external demand** (an act with a person, in the world, with a finish line).
> 2. **Trust the reader instead of warning her** (warnings are new standards for a perfectionist to perform).
> 3. **Make the body the authority** (cost AND prize rendered somatically, via confession and second-person — not via taxonomy).

**Governing craft rule:** the book persuades *as memoir and reader-catch*, not as system. Spend words there; cut them from the taxonomies.

## Global Safeguards (from the council's blind-spot catches — apply to every fix)

These are not fixes to the book; they are guardrails on the *fixing process*, each surfaced by the one Face that would catch it.

- **Felt-sense test (Shaman):** read each revised passage aloud and track where the breath stops. A fix that doesn't change a felt moment is hygiene, not healing. External actions (WB-1) must be chosen for *charge*, not tidiness — if it isn't a little frightening in the body, it's homework.
- **Canon-drift safeguard (Regent):** every applied fix names its canonical-write path and its Obsidian approval gate. Tier-D drafts live in the compile (derived) until Wendell promotes them; the compile must never fork canon. Grep `[[EDIT` to audit.
- **Self-contained briefs (Architect):** each WB must be runnable by a fresh drafter without re-deriving the whole review. If a fix can't be executed from its own section, it isn't ready.
- **Stage + repair (Diplomat):** do not apply all nine at once — a manuscript is a field, and a large simultaneous edit breaks relationships between passages that currently hold. Batch the fixes (see Sequencing) and run a short coherence/repair pass after each batch.
- **Stop condition (Sage):** named above. The fixes are done when the reader acts, not when the list is exhausted.

## WB-1 — Add one bounded external action to every chapter (rebalance interior:exterior)
**Council class:** 🎯 THE GAME · **Priority:** 2 · **Tier:** D · **Loci:** Ch1–Ch8 (Ch0 covered by P0-2 in its own spec)

**Problem (Layer-2 §B, §A):** ~95% of the book by volume is interior (gate walks, self-typing, polarity self-maps, energy-economy self-audits, reflection prompts). Across ~90k words there are ~4 genuinely external, bounded asks (Ch3 30-sec protocol & one-word "no"; Ch6 honest-terms sentence; Ch8 sixty-seconds). Interiority is exactly where this reader hides; the structure rewards her disease.

**Structural audit confirms this is THE fix (A4 + closing verdict):** the single most damaging consistency issue book-wide is that there is **no consistently enforced Forest→Village conversion beat** — so Emotional Alchemy, the one framework whose job is to convert charge into *action*, is delivered as another interior system to master rather than a mechanism that completes in the Village. So WB-1's bounded action is not just "an action ask" — it is **the Forest→Village conversion beat** the rhythm is missing. Frame it that way in every chapter: *alchemized charge has to leave the Forest and cost something in the Village.*
- **A3 application — convert the Diplomat map into the beat:** Ch6 §3's exquisitely precise shadow-map (L78–330) is the worst stopping-point in the book. Its WB-1 action must explicitly *spend* that recognition outward (say the honest-terms sentence to a real party), so the map becomes a launch, not a destination. (Pairs with WB-6's compression of that section.)
- **Worst-offender chapters for the missing beat (A4):** Ch2 (longest interior stretch; Village = compressed §6) and Ch7 (Village entered only conceptually, L541–583). Both need a real enacted action, not a discussed one.

**Fix:** Each chapter ends with (or contains) **one bounded, finishable, external demand** — an act done with a real person, in the world, that understanding cannot satisfy and that has a finish line. Model on Ch3's 30-second protocol and Ch8's "Try It Now — Sixty Seconds." The `→ app` BAR prompts stay, but they capture *recognition* and are not a substitute for an action ask.

**Council reframe:**
- **Architect — design the action, don't just request it.** Don't rely on the reader manufacturing willpower; make the action the path of least resistance by **wiring it into the BAR app handoff** so doing-it and logging-it are one motion. An action the reader has to assemble herself is a design failure.
- **Shaman — charge-test every action.** Choose each chapter's action for its *charge*, not its neatness. "Say the honest-terms sentence to a real person" works because it's frightening in the body; a bounded action with no charge is homework.

**Per-chapter targets (draft to each chapter's Face):**
- Ch1 (Shaman/Forest): one real somatic read named *out loud to another person* within 48h.
- Ch2 (Shaman): run one WAVE in a live conversation and report the single move made (not the feeling noticed).
- Ch3 (Challenger): already strong — formalize the 30-sec protocol as the chapter's named action.
- Ch4 (Regent): one honest inheritance conversation — say to a person what you're keeping and what you're reforming.
- Ch5 (Architect): change one *condition* (not a feeling) for one person this week; name the leverage point out loud.
- Ch6 (Diplomat): say the honest-terms sentence to a real party (lift it out of the polarity-map interior gating).
- Ch7 (Sage): name an altitude *to a room* once, as service, and stay in the room after.
- Ch8 (Player): elevate "Try It Now — Sixty Seconds" to the chapter's spine.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Every chapter contains ≥1 bounded external action (scoped, time-boxed, completable, involves another person).
- [ ] The action is not satisfiable by self-reflection or by capturing a BAR.
- [ ] Interior:exterior word ratio per chapter measurably improved (gate-walk/self-audit prose trimmed to fund the action — coupled with WB-6).
- [ ] Each chapter's action passes the Shaman charge-test (frightening in the body) and is wired to the BAR capture loop.
- [ ] No new "don't just intellectualize this" warning added anywhere.

## WB-2 — Move the "two readings" inoculation to the front
**Council class:** 🎯 THE GAME — *do first* (all six Faces endorsed; highest leverage/lowest cost) · **Priority:** 1 · **Tier:** D (relocate + adapt) · **Loci:** source Ch7 L87–101 → target Ch0 or Ch1

**Problem (Layer-2 §K):** The book's one genuine defense against the reader using the whole thing to stay stuck — *"the seeing has become the destination rather than a beginning... you'll know which one is yours"* — arrives at ~85% through, after eight chapters of systems are already collected.

**Structural audit (A3) sharpens the urgency:** the inoculation is sequenced *backwards relative to the Diplomat trap.* Ch6 §3 hands the ideal reader the most seductive, most complete map of her own shadow (L78–330) a full chapter **before** the "two readings" warning about over-mastering maps. She meets the trap, then meets the antidote. Relocating the inoculation to the front (Ch0/Ch1) means it is already in hand when she reaches Ch6 — it must precede the Diplomat map, not follow it.

**Fix:** Adapt the two-readings move into an early gate (Ch0 close or Ch1 open): *the recognition you're about to feel can mean you have the gift, or it can mean you've found a sophisticated place to hide — you'll know which is yours.* Make it a recurring test the reader (and book) can re-apply at each framework. Keep a shorter callback in Ch7 (the Sage is its natural home) so it pays off, not disappears.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] A "two readings / seeing-as-beginning-not-destination" beat appears before any major framework is taught. *(Ch0, "Two Readings" beat between Build Your Character + The Reader's Oath — catches the first recognition-click.)*
- [x] It is framed as a *reusable test*, not a one-time warning. *("This isn't a thing you learn once and pass. It's a test you keep taking… did the seeing start a move, or did it end one?")*
- [x] Ch7 retains a callback version (relocation, not deletion). *(Ch7 L87+ now opens "You know this test by now… take it again here, where it's sharpest" — Sage-specific altitude language retained per WB-9.)*

**Done 2026-05-29 (council unanimous for placement A).** Architect follow-on still open: wire the portable test question (*"did the seeing start a move or end one?"*) into the BAR app recognition-capture prompt — tracked under WB-1 app-wiring.

## WB-3 — Honor the inheritance: acknowledge/source the five-element (wu xing) system; write Appendix E

## WB-4 — Shame-consistency pass ("not enough" / 6-beliefs / "too much") + therapy off-ramp
**Council class:** 🧹 HYGIENE (but the off-ramp is the Shaman's "most caring move in the spec") · **Priority:** 5 · **Tier:** R (line swaps) + D (off-ramp prose) · **Loci:** Ch1 L63; Ch4 L554; 6-beliefs Ch1/Ch2/Ch4; Damaged-Self gates Ch2/Ch3/Ch4/Ch6

The 6-beliefs list (Ch1/2/4) functions as a shame inventory to try on. 3. **Add a therapy off-ramp** at the deepest gates (Damaged Self / Hollowed Self): *if this opens more than the page can hold, that's a therapist's room, not this page.* (Reader is stated to have a therapist she isn't using for this.)

**Acceptance criteria:**

## WB-6 — De-densify and differentiate the gate walks (Issue 5)
**Council class:** 🧹 HYGIENE — *coupled to WB-1 (it funds the external actions)* · **Priority:** 4 · **Tier:** D · **Loci:** Ch2–Ch7 gate walks; one pentad per chapter

Each chapter also stacks a redundant pentad + stage-sequence + energy economy. Ch5 proves the scaffolding can be slimmed without loss; Ch6's custom gates ("Already Alone," "Hollowed Self") prove differentiation can be alive.

**Council reconciliation (Challenger ↔ Regent, arbitrated by Diplomat + Architect):**

**Fix:**
1. **Hard cut:** delete one redundant pentad/stage-sequence/energy-economy block per chapter (Ch5 is the proof case that it can be slimmed without loss).
2. **Cadence test first:** before altering the gate-walk's repeated rhythm, confirm whether it's incantatory (load-bearing) or dead (re-skin). Cut the dead; keep the rhythm where it works.
3. **Differentiate gates by Face** (model: Diplomat's custom gate names — "Already Alone," "Hollowed Self").

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Cadence test run before any gate-walk rhythm is altered; load-bearing ceremony preserved.

## WB-7 — Name the book's terms honestly and early (the book is a funnel)
**Council class:** ⚖️ → 🎯/🧹 · **DECISION (Wendell, 2026-05-29):** the book **is a funnel** — own it, don't hide it. bars-engine is **A path, the recommended one**, not the only one, not required. · **Priority:** 8 · **Tier:** D (decision made) · **Loci:** Ch8 L140–142, L168–177, L357, L423–433; **first mention of bars-engine** (move the disclosure here); Ch0

**Decision reframes the fix.** The earlier read assumed the question was *gift or sale.* It's a funnel — settled. So the fix is **not** to strip the offer; it's the Diplomat's **"Close with Honest Terms," applied to the book itself**: a reader allergic to being sold is disarmed by an *openly named* funnel far more than by a concealed one. Transparency, not removal.

**Problem (Layer-2 §I), re-scoped:** The damage isn't that the book sells — it's that the sale is **a late reveal** (concentrated at the close, after deepest trust is asked) and that the **real prize is entangled with requiring purchase.** A funnel that surprises reads as bait-and-switch; a funnel that's declared up front reads as honesty.

**Fix:**
1. **Disclose the funnel early, in the author's voice** (Ch0 and/or first bars-engine mention): *this book is also a doorway — here's what it leads to, here's what costs money, here's what doesn't, and I'd rather tell you now than spring it at the end.*
2. **Frame bars-engine as "A path — the one I recommend,"** explicitly not the only path and not required. Preserve and foreground the **book-only / paper path** so the practice is genuinely completable without buying anything (the Ch0 "you don't need the app to read" promise becomes load-bearing and must stay true).
3. **Decouple the *real prize* from purchase:** recommended ≠ required. The reader must be able to reach a working practice and felt impact on the page alone; the app makes it easier, it isn't the gate.
4. **Make the offer consistent, not back-loaded** — set the expectation early so Ch8's invitations land as *follow-through on stated terms*, not as a turn.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The funnel/commercial relationship is named in the author's voice before Ch8 (ideally Ch0).
- [ ] bars-engine is framed as the *recommended* path AND explicitly optional; "the only path" framing removed.
- [ ] A complete, working practice is reachable book-only; the paper path is stated and real.
- [ ] Ch8 offers read as continuation of early-stated terms, not a late reveal.

**Fix (pending Wendell's call on commercial framing — hence U):**
1. **Name the commercial relationship early and honestly** (so Ch8 is not a reveal).
2. **Distribute offers lightly** across the book instead of dumping at the close.
3. **Guarantee the real prize is fully available without purchase** — the practice must be completable book-only (preserve the paper path).

**Decision needed from Wendell:** how prominent the offer should be, and whether bars-engine is framed as *the* path or *a* path. This gates the D-draft.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Commercial relationship disclosed before Ch8.
- [ ] No single chapter carries the bulk of the offers.
- [ ] The book states (and demonstrates) that the practice works without buying anything.

## WB-10 — Terminology coherence pass (name framework #2; fix "Shadow," "Village," "Game," "Forest" drift)
**Council class:** 🧹 COHERENCE · **Priority:** 5 (tie with WB-4 — both are trust/clarity foundations) · **Tier:** D · **Source:** Structural audit A1, A2, A6 · **Loci:** book-wide; concentrate on "Shadow" (Ch1 L35, §6; Ch2 3-2-1; Ch3 L308; Ch7 §2; Ch8 L40–46) and "Village" (every §1 vs Ch1 L271)

**Problem (audit A2 [HIGH] + A1):** The architecture's **second framework has no name in the book.** The reader meets four unreconciled things doing its job — gate "Shadow cost," Elliott/Jung disowned shadow, the "shadow [Face]," and "the Distortion" — so "what is *my* shadow?" has no gripable answer, and every later framework that "depends on recognizing Shadows" depends on an ungripped term. Plus: "The Village" splits into the *exile-village* (dominant) and the *action-village* (load-bearing but under-built); "The Game" scope-creeps from existential reframe → taxonomy → §6 title → product; "The Forest" drifts from place to gate-walk.

**Fix:**
1. **Name and stabilize framework #2.** Choose one canonical handle (e.g., keep "Shadow" for the disowned/projected material à la Elliott, and rename the others: gate "shadow cost" → "the gate's *cost*"; "shadow [Face]" → "the [Face] in *distortion*"; reconcile with "the Distortion"). Define it once, early, where the reader can grip it before EA depends on it (coordinate with WB-11).
2. **Disambiguate "Village":** distinguish the *exile-village* (myth) from the *Village* (arena of exterior action); build up the action-Village so framework #4's exterior pole is real (coordinate with WB-1's conversion beat).

## WB-1 — Shipped 2026-06-03 (Ch0)
### Ch0 — "The First Move" (commit `63ee78c`)

**Where it lives:** Between *Two Readings* and *Reader's Oath*. The structural reason: Two Readings is the inoculation (you will recognize things in this book; the recognition can be a move or a hiding place); the *First Move* applies the inoculation to a concrete action *before* the oath. The oath then reads as: I've asked. Now I keep playing.

**The shape (template for Ch1–Ch8):**

1. **Time-boxed window.** 7 days. Not "soon." Not "in your practice." A clock.
2. **Real person, specific relation.** "Someone who has known you longer than a single conversation" — not a stranger, not a coach, not the app. The Village target.
3. **The single sentence the reader says.** Quoted in the book. The reader does not have to design their own ask; they have to deliver it. Charge-test built in (asking to be seen is asking to be vulnerable).
4. **A finish line.** "One conversation. Twenty minutes, maybe less." The reader knows when they're done.
5. **Why-it-matters in one sentence.** "The asking is the move" — restated as a compressed definition in the section, not buried in explanation.
6. **Charge-test (Two Readings applied).** The section names reading one (asking starts a move) and reading two (asking is a sophisticated form of staying seen without being known). Same test as the parent section, applied to the action.
7. **First-person disclosure that turns universal.** "I built this book for three years while telling myself I wasn't avoiding it." The author is in the move, not outside it. This serves the Teal reader's need for the author to have been somewhere real.
8. **BAR prompt at close.** "*Who you asked, what you said, what they said back, what surprised you. → app*" — the recognition-capture loop, wired to the move.

**Charge-test passed:** The ask is asking to be seen. That is frightening in the body for the ideal-reader profile. It is not tidiness. It is not homework.

**Acceptance criteria status (per spec §"Per-chapter targets"):**
- [x] One bounded external action (scoped, time-boxed, completable, involves another person)
- [x] Not satisfiable by self-reflection or BAR capture alone
- [x] Charge-test passed
- [x] Wired to BAR capture loop

**Author-shadow disclosure:** The first-person passage ("I built this book for three years...") is the author in the move. The risk: over-sharing the book's history can read as compensation. Mitigated by brevity (one paragraph) and placement (after the charge-test, where the reader has just been asked to do something hard — the author's parallel disclosure feels like solidarity, not confession).

**Out of scope for Ch0 (resolved by later WB-1 chapter passes):**
- Interior:exterior word ratio rebalancing (Ch0's existing ratio is fine — the new section is small and the request is bounded).
- App-side wiring of the "did the seeing start a move" question (Architect follow-on, per WB-2 spec note).

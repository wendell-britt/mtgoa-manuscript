# 06 — Editing Reference: 6 Questions + 6 Self-Sabotaging Beliefs

---

## The 6 Unpacking Questions (Ch0 + Ch1+ framing)

Apply these to each section before writing or editing.

**1. What experience do you want the reader to have in this section?**
- A noun phrase: "discovery," "invitation," "entry," "clarity," "recognition," "construction"

**2. How are they hoping to feel about their allyship as a function of this section?**
- An EA label — the specific emotional state they arrive at after the section
- Format: [EA emotion label] — the feeling in one phrase

**3. What reservation (which of the 6 self-sabotaging beliefs) do they need to overcome to have this experience?**
- Which belief is in the way?
- The 6 beliefs are comfortable — the reader is HOLDING the reservation, not dissatisfied with it

**4. How does it feel for them to live in the reservation (before the section)?**
- What is the felt experience of keeping that reservation?
- Noun phrase: "floating," "guarded," "cautious," "exhausted," "defensive"

**5. What would have to be true about their allyship for this section to work for them?**
- What is the prerequisite belief or experience?
- This tells you what the section must establish first

**6. What experience do you want to give them after the section?**
- A noun phrase that is distinct from Q1 — the TRANSFORMATION, not the arrival
- Example: Q1 might be "entry" → Q6 is "belonging" (the transformation from outsider to insider)

---

## The 6 Self-Sabotaging Beliefs (Reservations)

These are protective mechanisms. The reader is SATISFIED holding them. They are not the dissatisfaction — they are what blocks access to the EA outcome.

1. **Not Good Enough** — *"I'm not doing it right"* / *"Others do it better"*
   → Blocks: mastery, competence, confidence

2. **Not Ready** — *"I need to learn more before I start"*
   → Blocks: entry, action, taking the ticket

3. **Don't Know How** — *"I don't have enough information to act"*
   → Blocks: clarity, discernment, the "which face is mine" moment

4. **Not Enough Time/Energy** — *"I can't add anything else"*
   → Blocks: sustained practice, showing up consistently

5. **Already Know This** — *"I've heard this before"*
   → Blocks: fresh perception, curiosity, the "oh wait" moment

6. **Not My Place** — *"This isn't for me / about me"*
   → Blocks: belonging, entry, claiming the identity

## How the Beliefs Connect to EA Outcomes

The belief is the BLOCK. The EA outcome is the ARRIVAL.

| Belief | EA Blocked |
|--------|-----------|
| Not Good Enough | Triumph (Fire) — earned authority |
| Not Ready | Relief (Water) — being allowed in |
| Don't Know How | Discernment (Earth) — clarity about which face |
| Not Enough Time | Peace (Earth) — enough as you are |
| Already Know This | Excitement (Wood) — fresh seeing |
| Not My Place | Relief (Water) — belonging, claimed identity |

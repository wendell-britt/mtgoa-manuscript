## 6 Unpacking Questions — Allyship Application

| # | Question | Allyship Context |
|---|----------|------------------|
| 1 | **What do you want to create?** | Which allyship outcome does the reader want to make in the world? |
| 2 | **What will that get you?** | Which satisfied emotional state will that get you? (EA Transcend target) |
| 3 | **What's life like right now?** | How is life different than what you want to create? (tension structure — current reality) |
| 4 | **How does it feel to live here?** | What dissatisfied or neutral feeling is currently present? (EA current state) |
| 5 | **What would have to be true for someone to feel this way?** | **Sneaky + powerful.** This question unconsciously moves people from dissatisfaction → neutral channel (EA Transcend/Translate/Control). It extracts the worldview beneath the feeling. |
| 6 | **What reservations do you have about your creation?** | Which self-sabotaging beliefs are at play? (Q6 surfaces the 6 beliefs) |

**The EA movement through Q1-Q5:**
- Q4 surfaces dissatisfaction (current state)
- Q5 triggers the unconscious shift: *what would have to be true* → the person has to articulate the worldview that underpins their dissatisfaction, which paradoxically creates distance from it
- This is the Shaman's work happening unconsciously — the dissatisfied channel becomes neutral through the act of articulating the underlying truth
- Q6 then asks: what's still in the way? → the 6 beliefs surface as the residual reservation

**Key insight:** Q5 does heavy lifting in the background. It's not obviously an EA move, but it is — it takes the person from DISSATISFACTION to NEUTRAL without them having to consciously do any Transcend work. The EA happens in the act of articulating the worldview.

## The 6 Sabotaging Beliefs (the reservations)

These are the limiting beliefs. The Controller generates them as self-sabotage. Q6 surfaces them.

1. **I'm not good enough**
2. **I'm not ready**
3. **I don't belong**
4. **I'm insignificant**
5. **I'm not worthy**
6. **I'm not capable**

## Role in the Architecture

### Controller Gate (Gate 2 in the 8 Gates structure)

The Controller holds the knob. The grip manifests as these 6 beliefs — they are the *reservation* layer that surfaces when the fire/charge tries to move.

**The pattern:**
- Charge arrives (fire shows up)
- Controller grips: *if you let yourself be this angry/passionate/powerful, you'll go too far*
- The grip produces the 6 beliefs as defense
- Q5 does preliminary EA work (dissatisfaction → neutral) unconsciously
- Q6 surfaces the remaining reservation (one of the 6 beliefs)
- The beliefs become the "reservations" in the 6 unpacking questions
- The reservations block the Transcend moves in emotional alchemy

**In other words:** The Controller doesn't just contain the charge — it generates the self-sabotage story. "I'm not ready" is not a neutral thought. It is the Controller saying *stay small, stay safe.*

| Belief (Reservation) | Blocks Transcend Move |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| I'm not good enough | Any +2 move — the charge says "I don't deserve to act" |
| I'm not ready | Charge → Act stage — "wait, I'm not prepared" |
| I don't belong | Line/Demand channel — "who am I to draw this?" |

The belief becomes the *reason* the Transcend move fails. "I want to draw this line but I'm not capable of holding it."

## Editorial Guidelines

- The 6 beliefs should be stated as a **named list** — not buried in prose
- Each belief should be linked to the emotional alchemy context (what it blocks)
- The connection to Q6 should be explicit: "These are the reservations Q6 surfaces"
- Q5's sneaky EA work should be noted where relevant
- Tone: matter-of-fact, not melodramatic. The beliefs are patterns, not tragedies.

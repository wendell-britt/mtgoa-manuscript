# 6-Face Book Analysis — Master Tracker
**Started:** 2026-04-14
**Status:** In Progress

## Core Thesis
> To be an effective allyship Game Master, you must learn and master success at all 6 levels. If you skip one, the shadow of that level will be that which ultimately holds you back.

---

## The 6 Faces (Developmental Levels)

| Face | Color | Altitude | Core Theme | Shadow If Skipped |
|------|-------|----------|------------|-------------------|
| Shaman | Magenta | Raw/survival | Pattern-sensing, instinct, "fear is map in bones" | Magical thinking, disconnection from body |
| Challenger | Red | Decisive action | Seizing moments, confrontation by defiance | Impulsiveness, violence |
| Regent | Amber | Stewardship | Order, tradition, legacy, "scale for many" | Rigidity, dogma, "loyalty becomes obedience" |
| Architect | Orange | Strategic design | Systems-thinking, blueprints, contradiction | Analysis paralysis, coldness |
| Diplomat | Green | Relational | Coalition, harmony, "walk away terms" | Over-accommodation, conflict avoidance |
| Sage | Teal | Integration | Panoramic vision, multiple games, liberation | Loneliness, dissociation |
